# BD-V-016-01 A bank loan and its deposit

## Alt text

Bayani mai sauƙi kan rancen banki: mai karɓar rance yana neman rance, bankin ya amince da rancen kuma ya ƙara sabon kuɗin ajiya a asusun mai karɓar rancen, sannan daga baya mai karɓar rancen ya mayar wa bankin ainihin kuɗin rancen tare da ruwa.

## Texts

### s1-title

Ku nemi rancen

### s1-text

Mai karɓar rance yana neman rance daga banki.

### s2-title

Ku amince da rancen

### s2-text

Bankin ya amince ya ba da rance.

### s3-title

Ku ƙirƙiri sabon kuɗin ajiya

### s3-text

Bankin ya ƙara sabon kuɗin ajiya a asusun mai karɓar rancen.

### s4-title

Ku kashe ko ku tura kuɗin

### s4-text

Mai karɓar rancen yana amfani da kuɗin ajiyar don biyan wasu.

### s5-title

Ku biya rancen tare da ruwa

### s5-text

Mai karɓar rancen yana mayar wa bankin ainihin kuɗin rancen tare da ruwa.
