# BD-V-022-01 Central ledger and Bitcoin network

## Alt text

Enkampanini yezinkokhelo, inhlangano eyodwa igcina ileja futhi igunyaza ukudluliswa kwemali ngakunye. Ku-Bitcoin, umthumeli usayina umsebenzi wokudlulisa imali, amanodi azimele ayawuhlola bese abavukuzi bewufaka kuleja eyodwa okwabelwana ngayo, ngakho umamukeli akanciki kakhulu esiphathimandleni esisodwa.
