# BD-V-022-01 Central ledger and Bitcoin network

## Alt text

Pogwiritsa ntchito kampani yolipirira, bungwe limodzi limasunga kaundula wa zochitika zandalama ndi kuvomereza kutumiza kulikonse. Pogwiritsa ntchito Bitcoin, wotumiza amasainira chochitika chandalama, makompyuta odziyimira pawokha a pa netiweki amachitsimikizira ndipo okumba bitcoin amachionjezera ku kaundula mmodzi wa zochitika zandalama wogawana, motero wolandira sadalira kwambiri bungwe limodzi lolamulira.
