# BD-V-022-01 Central ledger and Bitcoin network

## Alt text

Kha khamphani ya mbadelo, dzangano ḽithihi ḽi vhulunga bugu ya rekhodo nahone ḽa tendela u rumelwa huṅwe na huṅwe ha tshelede. Kha Bitcoin, murumeli u saina thengiselano, khomphyutha dza netiweke dzi ḓilawulaho dzi a i ṱola nahone vagodi vha i engedza kha bugu nthihi ya rekhodo i kovhekanywaho, zwenezwo zwa fhungudza u ḓitika ha muṱanganedzi nga maanḓalanga a vhukati.
