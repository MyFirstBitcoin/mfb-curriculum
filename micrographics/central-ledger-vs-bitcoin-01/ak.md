# BD-V-022-01 Central ledger and Bitcoin network

## Alt text

Sikatua adwumakuo fam no, ahyehyɛdeɛ baako na ɛkora akontaabu nwoma no so na ɛma kwan ansa na wɔde sika biara amena. Bitcoin fam no, sika menafoɔ no hyɛ sika a ɔde remena no nsɔano, nɛtwɛke no so kɔmputa ahodoɔ a ɛde ne ho hwɛ mu, na atufoɔ de ka akontaabu nwoma baako a wɔn nyinaa de di dwuma no ho, enti ɛho nhia sɛ sika gyefoɔ no de ne ho to tumi baako a ɛdi ne nyinaa so so pii.
