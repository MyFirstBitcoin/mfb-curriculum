# BD-V-022-01 Central ledger and Bitcoin network

## Alt text

Iyo hakoreshejwe ishirahamwe ritunganya ivy'ukwishurana, ishirahamwe rimwe ni ryo ribika igitabu c'ivyanditswe kandi rikemeza iyunguruzwa ryose ry'amahera. Iyo hakoreshejwe Bitcoin, uwurungika ashira umukono ku gikorwa co kurungika amahera, amapfundo y'umuhora yigenga akagisuzuma, abacukura na bo bakacongera mu gitabu kimwe c'ivyanditswe gisangiwe, bigatuma uwuronka atisunga cane ubutegetsi bumwe bukuru.
