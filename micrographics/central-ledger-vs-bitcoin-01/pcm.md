# BD-V-022-01 Central ledger and Bitcoin network

## Alt text

For payment kompani, na one organization dey keep di rekod buk and approve every moni transfer. For Bitcoin, di person wey dey send moni dey sign di transakshon, independent node dem dey check am and miner dem dey add am to one rekod buk wey all of dem dey share, so di person wey dey receive moni no need to depend too much on one central authority.
