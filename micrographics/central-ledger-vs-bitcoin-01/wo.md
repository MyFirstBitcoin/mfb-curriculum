# BD-V-022-01 Central ledger and Bitcoin network

## Alt text

Ci kompani buy def ay pey, benn mbootaay moo yor téereb kont bi te di nangu yónnee xaalis gu nekk. Ci Bitcoin, ki yónnee dafay xaatim ab joxante, ponki lëkkale yu boppam seetlu ko, te gaskat yi yokk ko ci benn téereb kont bu ñépp bokk, ba tax ki war a jot gën a bañ a sukkandiku ci benn kilifa gu digg bi.
