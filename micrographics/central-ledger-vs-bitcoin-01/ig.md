# BD-V-022-01 Central ledger and Bitcoin network

## Alt text

Mgbe a na-eji ụlọ ọrụ ịkwụ ụgwọ, otu ụlọ ọrụ na-edobe akwụkwọ ndekọ ego ma na-akwado nnyefe ego ọ bụla. Mgbe a na-eji Bitcoin, onye na-ezipụ ego na-abịanye aka na azụmahịa, ọnụ netwọkụ nọọrọ onwe ha na-enyocha ya, ndị na-egwupụta bitcoin na-etinyekwa ya n’otu akwụkwọ ndekọ ego a na-ekekọrịta, nke na-eme ka onye nnata ghara ịdabere nke ukwuu n’otu ikike etiti.
