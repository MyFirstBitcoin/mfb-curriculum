# BD-V-022-01 Central ledger and Bitcoin network

## Alt text

Kukkampani yakulipila, bungwe lyomwe ndilyo libamba bbuku lyamali akuzumizya kutumizya mali kulikonse. MuBitcoin, sikutuma usaina citumizyo camali, manodi aayiminina alikke alacilingula alimwi basikumbula balaciyungizya kubbuku lyamali lyomwe ndibabelana, eelyo sikutambula tayeemi kapati kumweendelezi umwi.
