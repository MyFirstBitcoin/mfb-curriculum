# BD-V-022-01 Central ledger and Bitcoin network

## Alt text

Mu kkampuni ekola ku kusasula, ekitongole kimu kye kikuuma ekitabo ky'ebibalirizi era ne kikkiriza buli kusindika ssente. Mu Bitcoin, omusindisi assa omukono ku kikolwa ky'okusindika ssente, ennyingo z'omukutu ezeetongodde ne zikikebera era abasimi ne bakyongera mu kitabo ky'ebibalirizi kimu kye bakozesa bonna, bwe kityo afuna ssente ne yeesigama kitono ku buyinza obw'omu makkati.
