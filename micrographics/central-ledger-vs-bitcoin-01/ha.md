# BD-V-022-01 Central ledger and Bitcoin network

## Alt text

A tsarin kamfanin biyan kuɗi, ƙungiya guda ɗaya ce ke adana kundin lissafi kuma take amincewa da kowane tura kuɗi. A tsarin Bitcoin kuwa, mai aikawa yana sanya hannu a kan ma'amala, kwamfutocin hanyar sadarwa masu zaman kansu suna tantance ta, masu haƙar bitcoin kuma suna ƙara ta a kundin lissafi guda ɗaya na bai ɗaya, don haka mai karɓa ba ya dogaro sosai da hukuma ta tsakiya.
