# BD-V-022-01 Central ledger and Bitcoin network

## Alt text

With a payment company, one organization keeps the ledger and approves each transfer. With Bitcoin, the sender signs a transaction, independent nodes check it and miners add it to one shared ledger, so the receiver depends less on a central authority.
