# BD-V-022-01 Central ledger and Bitcoin network

## Alt text

Ku kampani ya malipilo, ibumba limo lilasunga icitaabo ca ndalama no kusuminisha ukutumina indalama konse. Muli Bitcoin, uwatuma indalama alasaina icicitwa ca ndalama, amanodi yaiminina yalacisuzuma kabili abakumba balacilunda ku citaabo ca ndalama cimo ico bonse babomfya, e co uwapokelela tashintilila sana pa buteko bumo ubwa pakati.
