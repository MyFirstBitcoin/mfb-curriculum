# BD-V-022-01 Central ledger and Bitcoin network

## Alt text

Ki te whakamahi i tētahi kamupene utu, mā te whakahaere kotahi e pupuri te pukapuka kaute, e whakamana hoki ia whakawhitinga. Ki te whakamahi i a Bitcoin, ka waitohu te kaituku i tētahi tauwhitinga, ka taki ngā kōpuku motuhake i taua tauwhitinga, ā, ka tāpiri ngā kaikeri ki te pukapuka kaute tiritahi kotahi, nā reira ka iti ake te whakawhirinaki o te kaiwhiwhi ki tētahi mana whakahaere matua.
