# BD-V-022-01 Central ledger and Bitcoin network

## Alt text

Fa go dirisiwa khamphani ya dituelo, mokgatlho o le mongwe o boloka buka ya direkoto e bile o letlelela phetisetso nngwe le nngwe. Fa go dirisiwa Bitcoin, moromedi o saena tirisano, dinoute tse di ikemetseng di a e tlhatlhoba mme baepi ba e tsenya mo bukeng e le nngwe ya direkoto e e abelanwang, ka jalo moamogedi ga a ikaega thata ka taolo e e mo bogareng.
