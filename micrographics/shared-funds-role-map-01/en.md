# BD-V-096-01 Who controls shared funds

## Alt text

Contributors and beneficiaries depend on whoever controls shared funds. Key control, spending approval, records and review are separate roles; when one person holds several, trust is concentrated, and continuity, privacy, disputes and legal status still need answers.

Key control

### r3

### r4
