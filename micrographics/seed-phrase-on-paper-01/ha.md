# BD-V-038-01 Seed phrase on paper

Jerin kalmomin farfaɗowa jerin kalmomi ne da aka tsara bisa ƙayyadadden tsari; a nan, kalmomin misali goma sha biyu ne masu lambobi da aka rubuta a takarda.
