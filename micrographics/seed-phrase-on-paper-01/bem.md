# BD-V-038-01 Seed phrase on paper

## Alt text

Amashiwi ya kubweseshapo icola ca ndalama ni ndandalika ya mashiwi ayakonkonkana; pano pali amashiwi ikumi na yabili aya ca kumwenako, ayapeelwa amanambala no kulembwa pa pepala. Amashiwi ayalungama, nga yakonkonkana bwino, kuti yabwekesha amaka ya kubomfya icola ca ndalama iciyasuminisha, e ico yafwile ukusungwa mu nkama kabili ukwabula ukulundwa ku intaneti.
