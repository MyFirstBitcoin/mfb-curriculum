# BD-V-085-01 Reassessment, calibration and moderation

## Alt text

Ana gudanar da matakai uku daban-daban a lokaci guda. Ɗaliban da aka tantance da sakamakon Ba tukuna ba suna samun ra'ayi a keɓance, suna yin atisaye kuma suna gabatar da sabuwar shaida; masu tantancewa suna daidaita ma'auninsu ta hanyar ba wa misalai maki kowa da kansa da kuma amincewa kan fassara ɗaya; malamai na iya neman bita ta biyu, wadda ba ta taɓa maye gurbin aikin da aka lura ɗalibi ya yi ba.
