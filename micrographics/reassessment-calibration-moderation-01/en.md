# BD-V-085-01 Reassessment, calibration and moderation

## Alt text

Three separate processes run side by side. Learners with Not yet receive private feedback, practise and provide new evidence; assessors calibrate by scoring examples independently and agreeing an interpretation; educators may ask for a second review, which never replaces observed performance.
