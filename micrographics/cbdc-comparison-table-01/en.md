# BD-V-019-01 Cash, deposits, CBDC and Bitcoin

## Alt text

A table comparing cash, bank deposits, a retail CBDC and Bitcoin on who issues them, their form, access, visibility, supply rules and verification. Digital is not the same as decentralized: a CBDC is still issued by the central bank, and its access, visibility and verification depend on its design.
