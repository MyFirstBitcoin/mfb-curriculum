# BD-V-019-01 Cash, deposits, CBDC and Bitcoin

## Alt text

Jadawalin da ke kwatanta kuɗin hannu, ajiyar banki, CBDC na jama'a da Bitcoin bisa ga wanda ke fitar da su, siffarsu, damar amfani da su, waɗanda ke ganin biyan kuɗi, ka'idojin samar da su da kuma tantancewa. Kasancewa na dijital ba ɗaya yake da rashin cibiyar iko guda ɗaya ba: har yanzu babban banki ne ke fitar da CBDC, kuma damar amfani da shi, waɗanda ke ganin biyan kuɗi da yadda ake tantance shi sun danganta da yadda aka tsara shi.
