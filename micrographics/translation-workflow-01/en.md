# BD-V-097-01 Translation workflow

## Alt text

Translation is a review-and-test process: prepare clear English, translate the meaning, review with a language specialist and an educator, test safety instructions with learners, then confirm the standards are equivalent. Unclear safety instructions go back to the wording.
