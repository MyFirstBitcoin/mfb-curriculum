# BD-V-050-01 On-chain and Lightning payments

## Alt text

Izinkokhelo ezenziwa ku-blockchain ziqoshwa ngokuqondile ku-blockchain ye-Bitcoin, kuyilapho i-Lightning iyinethiwekhi yezinkokhelo eyakhelwe phezu kwe-Bitcoin ukuze kwenziwe izinkokhelo ezisheshayo nezivamile, ezingase futhi zingaphumeleli. Kuzo zombili lezi zindlela, izimali zingagcinwa ngumhlinzeki wesevisi noma zigcinwe ngumnikazi wazo uqobo.
