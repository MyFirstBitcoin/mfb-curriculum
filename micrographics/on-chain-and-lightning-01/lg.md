# BD-V-050-01 On-chain and Lightning payments

## Alt text

Okusasula okukolebwa ku lujegere kuwandiikibwa butereevu mu lujegere lwa bulooka olwa Bitcoin, ate Lightning mukutu gw’okusasula ogwazimbibwa ku Bitcoin okusobozesa okusasula okw’amangu era okukolebwa enfunda nnyingi, naye okuyinza n’okulemererwa. Mu ngeri zombi, ensimbi zisobola okukuumibwa omuntu omulala oba mmwe bennyini.
