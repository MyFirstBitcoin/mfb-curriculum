# BD-V-050-01 On-chain and Lightning payments

## Alt text

Ukwishurana ku ruhererekane rw'amakuru kwandikwa ata guca ku ruhande mu ruhererekane rw'amakuru rwa Bitcoin, mu gihe Lightning ari umuhora wo kwishurana wubakiye kuri Bitcoin, utuma kwishurana kenshi kandi ningoga bishoboka, naho bishobora kandi kutagenda neza. Muri izo nzira zompi, amahera ashobora gucungerwa n'uwundi muntu canke na nyene yo.
