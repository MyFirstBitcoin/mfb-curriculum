# BD-V-050-01 On-chain and Lightning payments

## Alt text

Wɔtwerɛ sika a wɔtua wɔ blɔk ahoma no so no tẽẽ wɔ Bitcoin blɔk ahoma no mu, na Lightning yɛ sika tua nhyehyɛeɛ a wɔasi wɔ Bitcoin so ma sika a wɔtua no ntɛmntɛm ne mpɛn pii, nanso saa sika tua no nso tumi di nkoguo. Saa akwan mmienu yi mu biara mu no, onipa foforɔ bɛtumi ahwɛ sika no so ama ne wura, anaa sika no wura ankasa bɛtumi ahwɛ so.
