# BD-V-050-01 On-chain and Lightning payments

## Alt text

Dem dey record payments wey happen on-chain straight for Bitcoin blockchain, while Lightning na payment network wey dem build on top Bitcoin for quick payments wey person fit make often, but wey fit still fail. For any of the two ways, na either another person dey keep the money for the owner, or the owner dey keep am by themself.
