# BD-V-050-01 On-chain and Lightning payments

## Alt text

Ana rubuta biyan kuɗi na kan sarkar tubalai kai tsaye a cikin sarkar tubalan Bitcoin, yayin da Lightning cibiyar biyan kuɗi ce da aka gina a kan Bitcoin don biyan kuɗi cikin sauri kuma akai-akai, wanda kuma zai iya gaza. A kowace hanya, wani ɓangare na uku zai iya riƙe kuɗin a madadin mai su, ko kuma mai kuɗin ya riƙe su da kansa.
