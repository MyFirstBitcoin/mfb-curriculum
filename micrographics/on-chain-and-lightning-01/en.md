# BD-V-050-01 On-chain and Lightning payments

## Alt text

On-chain payments are recorded directly in Bitcoin's blockchain, while Lightning is a payment network built on Bitcoin for fast, frequent payments that can also fail. Either path can be custodial or self-custodial.
