# BD-V-050-01 On-chain and Lightning payments

## Alt text

Amalipilo ya pa blockchain yalembwa mu blockchain ya Bitcoin mwine, lelo Lightning lupanda lwa kulipililamo ulwasekelwa pali Bitcoin pa kulipila bwangu kabili libili libili, nangu amalipilo aya kuti yafilwa ukucitika. Mu nshila shonse shibili, indalama shenu kuti shasungwa na bambi nangu imwe bene.
