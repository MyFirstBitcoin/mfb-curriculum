# BD-V-050-01 On-chain and Lightning payments

## Alt text

Malipiro a pa unyolo wa mabuloko amalembedwa mwachindunji mu unyolo wa mabuloko wa Bitcoin, pamene Lightning ndi netiweki yolipirira yomangidwa pa Bitcoin kuti malipiro azichitika mofulumira komanso pafupipafupi, ngakhale malipirowo akhozanso kulephereka. Pa njira iliyonse mwa ziwirizi, ndalama zanu zingasamalidwe ndi munthu wina kapena ndi inu nokha.
