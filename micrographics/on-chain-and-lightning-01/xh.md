# BD-V-050-01 On-chain and Lightning payments

## Alt text

Iintlawulo ezikwikhonkco leebhloko zirekhodwa ngqo kwikhonkco leebhloko leBitcoin, ngoxa iLightning iluthungelwano lweentlawulo olwakhelwe kwiBitcoin ukulungiselela iintlawulo ezikhawulezayo, ezenziwa rhoqo nezinokungaphumeleli. Kuzo zombini ezi ndlela, imali inokugcinwa ngomnye umntu okanye igcinwe ngumniniyo ngokwakhe.
