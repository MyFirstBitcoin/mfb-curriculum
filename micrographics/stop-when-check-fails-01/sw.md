# BD-V-090-01 Stop when a safety check fails

## Alt text

Hali isiyo salama au isiyoelezeka husitisha shughuli. Mwalimu huwalinda watu na siri na kuthibitisha tatizo; kosa la tathmini linalohusu usalama hurekodiwa kama Bado, na mwanafunzi huanza upya, hufanya mazoezi na hutathminiwa tena baadaye.
