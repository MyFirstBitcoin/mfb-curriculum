# BD-V-090-01 Stop when a safety check fails

## Alt text

An unsafe or unexplained condition stops the activity. The educator protects people and secrets and verifies the issue; a safety-critical assessment error is logged as Not yet, and the learner resets, practises and is reassessed later.
