# BD-V-083-01 Tasks to competencies

## Alt text

Tattaunawar ilimin ka'ida daga T1 zuwa T3 tana ba da shaidar ƙwarewa daga C1 zuwa C5, kuma hanyoyin aiki a aikace P6a, P6b da P7 suna ba da shaidar C6 da C7. Sannan kowace ƙwarewa tana samun sakamako guda ɗaya na I ko Ba tukuna ba.

## Texts

Ilimin ka'ida (C1 zuwa C5)

### c1-n1

T1 don C1: kwatanta kuɗi da yanayin da ake ciki a yankin

### c1-n2

T2 don C2 da C3: kuɗin dijital, ayyuka da kwatantawa

### c1-n3

T3 don C4 da C5: riƙon kuɗi, tsaro da hanyoyin biyan kuɗi

### c1-f1-label
