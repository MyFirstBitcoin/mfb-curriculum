# BD-V-083-01 Tasks to competencies

## Alt text

Shughuli za nadharia T1 hadi T3 hutoa ushahidi wa umahiri C1 hadi C5, na njia za vitendo P6a, P6b na P7 hutoa ushahidi wa C6 na C7. Kisha kila umahiri hupata tokeo moja la Ndiyo au Bado.
