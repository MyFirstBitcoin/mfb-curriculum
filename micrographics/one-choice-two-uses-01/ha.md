# BD-V-012-01 One choice, two uses

## Alt text

Mai sayayya mai ƙarancin kuɗi yana duba zaɓuɓɓukan da ke gabansa: abincin yau, sufuri, ko ajiyar kuɗi don gaba. Zaɓar ɗaya yana nufin barin sauran, wanda shi ne farashin damar da aka rasa, kuma babu wani zaɓi da aka nuna a matsayin wanda ya dace.
