# BD-V-080-01 A Lightning payment between two phones

## Alt text

The receiver creates a Lightning payment request with the amount on their phone, the sender scans it and pays, and the payment arrives on the receiver's phone a moment later.

### i1

The receiver invoices

### i1-note

The receiver creates a payment request with the amount attached.

### i2

The sender pays

### i2-note

The sender scans the request, checks the amount and pays.

### i3

The receiver is paid

### i3-note

The payment arrives a moment later, and both are happy.
