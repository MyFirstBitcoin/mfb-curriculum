# BD-V-100-01 Three trust models compared

## Alt text

Ana kwatanta Bitcoin, tsarin gama-gari na tsabar kuɗi mai daidaitacciyar ƙima da tsarin gama-gari na wata alamar dijital ta fuskar wanda ke sarrafa adadin da ake samarwa, abin da ke tallafa wa ƙimarsa, abin da ɗalibi zai iya tabbatarwa da kuma sauran ɓangarorin mu'amalar da ke akwai. Walatan dijital da ayyukan hidima suna ƙara nasu haɗarin ga duka ukun, don haka kamannin abin da ke kan allo ba yana nufin tsarin amincewa iri ɗaya ba.
