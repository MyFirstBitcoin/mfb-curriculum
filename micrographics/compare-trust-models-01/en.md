# BD-V-100-01 Three trust models compared

## Alt text

Bitcoin, a generic stablecoin model and a generic other token model are compared by who controls supply, what supports it, what a learner can verify and which counterparties exist. Wallets and services add their own risk to all three, so a similar screen does not mean the same trust model.
