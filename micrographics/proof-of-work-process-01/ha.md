# BD-V-032-01 Proof of work

## Alt text

Mai haƙa yana lissafin hash na tubalin da aka gabatar akai-akai har sai wani sakamako ya yi ƙasa da iyakar da cibiyar sadarwa ta ƙayyade, sannan ya watsa tubalin. Kullin sadarwa suna bincika hujjar da kowace ƙa'ida cikin sauri kafin tubalin ya shiga sarkar tubalai: yin aikin yana da tsada, amma tabbatar da shi yana da sauƙi.

## Texts

### f1-text

Na'urar haƙa tana lissafin hash akai-akai har sai wani sakamako ya cika sharadin iyakar da aka ƙayyade
