# Bitcoin Supply Schedule

## Alt text

Jadawalin layi da ke nuna adadin bitcoin da ke zagayawa yana ƙaruwa zuwa iyakar miliyan 21, yayin da ladan buloki ke raguwa zuwa rabinsa kusan duk bayan shekaru huɗu.
