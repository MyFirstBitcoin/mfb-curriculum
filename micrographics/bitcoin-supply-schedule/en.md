# Bitcoin Supply Schedule

## Alt text

A line chart showing bitcoin's circulating supply rising toward a 21 million cap while the block reward steps down by half about every four years.

## Texts

### intro

New bitcoin enters circulation with every block. Roughly every four years the reward for mining a block is cut in half. Total supply is capped at 21 million.

### chart-alt

Coins in circulation rise toward 21 million as the block reward steps down by half over time.

### legend-supply

Coins in circulation

### legend-reward

Block reward, BTC

### stat-cap

the absolute maximum number of coins that will ever exist

### stat-halving

the reward is cut every 210,000 blocks, about every four years

### stat-final

the year the very last new bitcoin is expected to be mined
