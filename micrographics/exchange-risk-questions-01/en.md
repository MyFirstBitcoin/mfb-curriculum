# BD-V-049-01 Questions before exchanging

## Alt text

Six risk areas to check before acquiring or exchanging bitcoin, each with a question to ask: costs, custody, identity and privacy, withdrawal and settlement, counterparty and safety, and local context.

## Texts

### r1

Costs

### r1-text

What price, spread, and service or withdrawal fees apply?

### r2

Custody

### r2-text

Who controls the bitcoin before and after the exchange?

### r3

Identity and privacy

### r3-text

What personal information is collected, stored, or shared?

### r4

Withdrawal and settlement

### r4-text

Can payment or withdrawal be delayed, limited, or reversed?

### r5

Counterparty and safety

### r5-text

How can I verify the other party or service, and is an in-person exchange safe?

### r6

Local context

### r6-text

Is the route lawful and appropriate where I live, and what records may matter?
