# BD-V-093-01 Module 3 practice circuit

## Alt text

Learners rotate through eight practice stations: custody, public or secret, receive, send, recovery, explorer, scam and pathway. A separate observation lane follows an approved route, so group practice is never mistaken for formal assessment.
