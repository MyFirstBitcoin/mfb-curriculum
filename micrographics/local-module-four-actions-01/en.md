# BD-V-054-01 Your local Module 4

## Alt text

In the local Module 4, the learner moves through four actions: connect an idea from Modules 1 to 3 to a local need, compare Bitcoin with a realistic existing option, practice safely with guidance, and explain what they learned and a responsible next step. Their educator, and sometimes a local expert, guides them throughout.
