# BD-V-052-01 Self-custody responsibilities

## Alt text

Control of keys sits at the center of eight responsibilities: backup, recovery, receiving, sending, tool choice, acquisition path, privacy and scam checks. Each one protects something, and a failure in any of them can cost access, money or privacy.
