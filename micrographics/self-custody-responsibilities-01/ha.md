# BD-V-052-01 Self-custody responsibilities

## Alt text

Ikon maɓallai yana tsakiyar nauyi takwas: yin kwafin ajiya, dawo da damar shiga, karɓa, aikawa, zaɓin kayan aiki, hanyar saye, sirri da binciken zamba. Kowannensu yana kare wani abu, kuma gazawa a kowane ɗayansu na iya jawo asarar damar shiga, kuɗi ko sirri.
