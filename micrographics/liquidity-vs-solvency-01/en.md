# BD-V-017-01 Liquidity risk and solvency risk

## Alt text

Liquidity risk: many depositors queue to withdraw today, and the bank lacks enough cash now even though its loans may be repaid later. Solvency risk: borrowers default on home loans, and the bank's assets become worth less than what it owes.
