# BD-V-081-01 Two completion gates

## Alt text

Theory competencies C1 to C5 and practical competencies C6 and C7 must each receive a Yes before the completion certificate is issued. Not yet means more practice and reassessment; attendance and reflection do not replace either gate.

## Texts

### start

The educator assesses each learner

### b1-head

Theory (C1 to C5)

### b1-n1

Activities, conversations and educator-led interaction

### b1-n2

All five competencies meet standard

### b2-head

Practice (C6 and C7)

### b2-n1

Direct educator observation of C6 and the C7 local task

### b2-n2

Both competencies meet standard

### end

Yes on both gates: completion certificate

### loop

Not yet? Back to practice, then reassessment. Attendance and reflection do not count as evidence.
