# BD-V-004-01 The learning path

## Alt text

Taswirar hanya mai matakai huɗu: Matsala, Fahimtar Kuɗi; Mafita, Yadda Bitcoin ke Aiki; Ɗaukar Mataki, Amfani da Bitcoin Cikin Tsaro; da Haɗawa, Bitcoin a Cikin Al'ummarku, kowanne tare da tambayar da ke jagorantarsa.

## Texts

### i1

Matsala

### i1-note

Fahimtar Kuɗi: Ta yaya tsarin kuɗi ke aiki da sauyawa?

### i2

Mafita

### i2-note

Yadda Bitcoin ke Aiki: Me ya sa aka ƙirƙiri Bitcoin, kuma ta yaya yake magance matsalar?

### i3

Ɗaukar Mataki

### i3-note

Amfani da Bitcoin Cikin Tsaro: Ta yaya zan ɗauki alhakin amfani da Bitcoin da kuma kare shi?

### i4

Haɗawa

### i4-note

Bitcoin a Cikin Al'ummarku: Ta yaya matsalar, mafitar, da ɗaukar mataki cikin tsaro suke da alaƙa da wannan al'umma?
