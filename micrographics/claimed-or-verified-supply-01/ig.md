# BD-V-027-01 Claimed or verified supply

## Alt text

Onye na-ewepụta ego nwere ike ịgbanwe na nzuzo oke ọkọnọ e dere na weebụsaịtị ma ọ bụ n'ebe nchekwa data ya. Iwu ọkọnọ Bitcoin ghe oghe, mbinye aka, akụkọ azụmahịa ghe oghe, ihe akaebe nke ọrụ na nkwenye sitere na kọmputa njikọ na-arụkọ ọrụ ọnụ iji gbochie ịchepụta mkpụrụ ego ọhụrụ ma ọ bụ imefu otu bitcoin ugboro abụọ.

## Texts

### c1-head

Nkwupụta gbasara ọkọnọ

### c1-n1

Onye na-ewepụta ego na-ekwupụta oke ọkọnọ na weebụsaịtị ya.

### c1-n2

Naanị ebe nchekwa data nke onye na-ewepụta ego na-edekọ ya.

### c1-n3

Ọ dịghị onye na-abụghị onye na-ewepụta ego nwere ike ịlele ya.

### c1-n4

Onye na-ewepụta ego nwere ike ịgbanwe ọkọnọ na nzuzo.

### c1-f1-label

Onye na-enyocha ya

### c1-f1

Naanị onye na-ewepụta ego

### c2-head

Iwu ọkọnọ ghe oghe

### c2-n1

Mbinye aka na-egosi onye nwere ike imefu bitcoin ọ bụla.

### c2-n2

Onye ọ bụla nwere ike ịgụ akụkọ azụmahịa ghe oghe.

### c2-n3

Ihe akaebe nke ọrụ na-eme ka idegharị akụkọ azụmahịa rie nnukwu ego.

### c2-n4

Kọmputa njikọ na-ajụ mkpụrụ ego na-adabaghị n'iwu na imefu otu ego ugboro abụọ.

### c2-f1-label

Onye na-enyocha ya

### c2-f1

Onye ọ bụla na-arụ ọrụ kọmputa njikọ
