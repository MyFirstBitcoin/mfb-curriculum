# BD-V-027-01 Claimed or verified supply

## Alt text

A supply limit written on a website or in an issuer's database can be changed quietly by that issuer. Bitcoin's supply rule is public, and signatures, a public history, proof of work and node verification together stop new coins from being invented or the same bitcoin from being spent twice.
