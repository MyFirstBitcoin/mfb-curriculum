# BD-V-027-01 Claimed or verified supply

## Alt text

Malire a kuchuluka kwa ndalama olembedwa pa webusaiti kapena m'nkhokwe ya deta ya wotulutsa ndalamazo akhoza kusinthidwa mwakachetechete ndi wotulutsayo. Lamulo la kuchuluka kwa ndalama za Bitcoin ndi lapoyera, ndipo masiginecha, mbiri yapoyera, umboni wa ntchito ndi kutsimikizira kochitidwa ndi manodi zimagwirira ntchito limodzi poletsa kupanga ndalama zatsopano zosaloledwa kapena kugwiritsa ntchito bitcoin yomweyo kawiri.

## Texts

### c1-head

Zonena za kuchuluka kwa ndalama

### c1-n1

Wotulutsa ndalamazo amafotokoza malire a kuchuluka kwake pa webusaiti yake.

### c1-n2

Ndi m'nkhokwe ya deta ya wotulutsa ndalamazo yokha mmene malirewo amalembedwa.

### c1-n3

Palibe amene sali m'gulu la wotulutsa ndalamazo amene angatsimikizire malirewo.

### c1-n4

Wotulutsa ndalamazo akhoza kusintha kuchuluka kwake mwakachetechete.

### c1-f1-label

Zimatsimikiziridwa ndi

### c1-f1

Wotulutsa ndalamazo yekha

### c2-head

Lamulo lapoyera la kuchuluka kwa ndalama

### c2-n1

Masiginecha amasonyeza amene angagwiritse ntchito bitcoin iliyonse.

### c2-n2

### c2-n3

Umboni wa ntchito umapangitsa kuti kusinthanso mbiriyo kukhale kodula.

### c2-n4

Manodi amakana ndalama zosavomerezeka komanso kugwiritsa ntchito ndalama zomwezo kawiri.

### c2-f1-label
