# BD-V-027-01 Claimed or verified supply

## Alt text

Muganhu wabunji bwa mali uulembedwe awebusayiti naa mububiko bwazibalo bwauufumya mali ulakonzya kucincwa awalo bantu batakuzibi. Mulawo wabunji bwa mali waBitcoin uubonwa abantu boonse, alimwi masigineca, makani akale aabonwa abantu boonse, bumboni bwamulimo akusimikizya kwamanodi, zyoonse antoomwe, zikozyanya kulengululwa kwamali mapya naa kubelesya bitcoin njiyomweyo tobilo.
