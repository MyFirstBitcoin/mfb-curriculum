# BD-V-027-01 Claimed or verified supply

## Alt text

Ka taea e te kaituku te whakarerekē huna i te tepe mō te rahinga moni kua tuhia ki tētahi pae tukutuku, ki tōna ake pātengi raraunga rānei. He tūmatanui te ture mō te rahinga moni o Bitcoin, ā, mā te mahi ngātahi a ngā waitohu, te hītori tūmatanui, te taunakitanga mahi me te manatoko a ngā pona e aukati te hanga moni hou me te whakapau tuarua i te bitcoin kotahi.
