# BD-V-027-01 Claimed or verified supply

## Alt text

Sika no yifoɔ bɛtumi asesa sika dodoɔ a ɛrensene so a wɔatwerɛ wɔ wɛbsaet anaa ne nsɛm siebea no a obiara renhunu. Bitcoin sika dodoɔ ho mmara no da adi ma obiara, na nsaano nkyerɛwee, abakɔsɛm a obiara bɛtumi ahwɛ mu, adwumayɛ ho adanseɛ ne nokorɛ a kɔmputa a ɛwɔ nkitahodie nhyehyɛeɛ no mu hwehwɛ no bom si sika foforɔ a obi bɛbɔ anaa bitcoin korɔ no ara a wɔde bɛdi dwuma mprenu no kwan.

## Texts

### c1-head

Sika dodoɔ ho asɛm a wɔka

### c1-n1

Sika no yifoɔ kyerɛ sika dodoɔ a ɛrensene so wɔ ne wɛbsaet so.

### c1-n2

Sika no yifoɔ no ankasa nsɛm siebea nko ara na ɛkyerɛ saa dodoɔ no.

### c1-n3

Obiara a ɔnyɛ sika no yifoɔ no mu bi ntumi nhwehwɛ mu nhwɛ sɛ ɛyɛ nokorɛ anaa.

### c1-n4

Sika no yifoɔ no bɛtumi asesa sika dodoɔ no a obiara renhunu.

### c1-f1-label

### c1-f1

### c2-head

Sika dodoɔ ho mmara a obiara bɛtumi ahwɛ mu

### c2-n1

Nsaano nkyerɛwee kyerɛ onii a ɔbɛtumi de bitcoin biara adi dwuma.

### c2-n2

### c2-n3

Adwumayɛ ho adanseɛ ma abakɔsɛm no a wɔbɛsan atwerɛ no foforɔ no gye sika pii.

### c2-n4
