# BD-V-027-01 Claimed or verified supply

## Alt text

Igitigiri ntarengwa c'amahera canditswe ku rubuga canke mu bubiko bw'amakuru bw'uwuyasohora kirashobora guhindurwa na we ata wubimenye. Itegeko ry'igitigiri c'amahera ya Bitcoin rirabonwa na bose, kandi imikono yo mu buhinga bwa none, kahise kabonwa na bose, icemezo c'igikorwa n'igenzura rikorwa n'ama node birakorana mu kubuza ko amahera mashasha aremwa ata tegeko rikurikijwe canke ko bitcoin imwe ikoreshwa incuro zibiri.

## Texts

### c1-head

Ivyemezwa ku gitigiri c'amahera

### c1-n1

Uwusohora amahera aravuga ku rubuga rwiwe igitigiri ntarengwa cayo.

### c1-n2

Ico gitigiri canditswe gusa mu bubiko bw'amakuru bw'uwuyasohora.

### c1-n3

Nta muntu wo hanze y'uwuyasohora ashobora kugenzura ico gitigiri.

### c1-n4

Uwusohora amahera arashobora guhindura igitigiri cayo ata wubimenye.

### c1-f1-label

Bigenzurwa na

### c1-f1

Uwusohora amahera wenyene

### c2-head

Itegeko ryeruye ry'igitigiri c'amahera

### c2-n1

Imikono yo mu buhinga bwa none irerekana uwushobora gukoresha bitcoin imwe yose.

### c2-n2

### c2-n3

### c2-n4
