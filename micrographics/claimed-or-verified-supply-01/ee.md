# BD-V-027-01 Claimed or verified supply

## Alt text

Ga ɖeɖe dɔwɔƒe ate ŋu atrɔ ga ƒe agbɔsɔsɔ ƒe se si woŋlɔ ɖe nyatakakadzraɖoƒe alo dɔwɔƒe la ƒe nyatakakadzraɖonu me adzaa. Bitcoin ƒe ga ƒe agbɔsɔsɔ ŋuti se le dutoƒo, eye asiŋɔŋlɔwo, ŋutinya si ame sia ame ate ŋu akpɔ, dɔwɔwɔ ƒe kpeɖodzi kple netwɔk mɔ̃wo ƒe emekɔkɔ wɔa dɔ ɖekae tsia ga yeye siwo se la meɖe mɔ na o ƒe wɔwɔ alo bitcoin ɖeka zazã zi eve nu.
