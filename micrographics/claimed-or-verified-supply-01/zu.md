# BD-V-027-01 Claimed or verified supply

## Alt text

Umkhawulo wenani lemali etholakalayo obhalwe kuwebhusayithi noma esizindalwazini somkhiphi ungashintshwa yilowo mkhiphi ngaphandle kokwazisa muntu. Umthetho wenani lemali etholakalayo le-Bitcoin uvulekele umphakathi, futhi amasiginesha, umlando ovulekele umphakathi, ubufakazi bomsebenzi nokuqinisekiswa ngamanodi, konke ndawonye, kuvimbela ukudalwa kwezinhlamvu zemali ezintsha ezingavumelekile noma ukusetshenziswa kwe-bitcoin efanayo kabili.

## Texts

### c1-head

Isimangalo ngenani lemali etholakalayo

### c1-n1

Umkhiphi usho umkhawulo kuwebhusayithi yakhe.

### c1-n2

Yisizindalwazi somkhiphi kuphela esirekhoda lo mkhawulo.

### c1-n3

Akekho ongaphandle komkhiphi ongawuqinisekisa.

### c1-n4

Umkhiphi angashintsha inani lemali etholakalayo ngaphandle kokwazisa muntu.

### c1-f1-label

Oqinisekisayo

### c1-f1

Umkhiphi kuphela

### c2-head

Umthetho wenani lemali etholakalayo ovulekele umphakathi

### c2-n1

Amasiginesha abonisa ukuthi ubani ongasebenzisa i-bitcoin ngayinye.

### c2-n2

Noma ubani angafunda umlando wokuthengiselana ovulekele umphakathi.

### c2-n3

Ubufakazi bomsebenzi benza ukubhala kabusha umlando kubize kakhulu.

### c2-n4

Amanodi ayazenqaba izinhlamvu zemali ezingavumelekile nokusetshenziswa kwemali efanayo kabili.

### c2-f1-label

Oqinisekisayo

### c2-f1

Noma ubani osebenzisa inodi
