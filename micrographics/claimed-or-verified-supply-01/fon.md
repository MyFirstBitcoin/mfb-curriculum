# BD-V-027-01 Claimed or verified supply

## Alt text

Mɛ e nɔ ɖe akwɛ tɔn ɔ sixu ɖyɔ akwɛ sin sɔha sín dogbó e e wlan ɖo ɛntɛnɛti ji wɛxwé alo ɖo nusisɔnu xɔ tɔn ɔ bo ma ɖɔ na mɛɖe a. Bitcoin sin akwɛ sɔha sín sɛn ɖo gbangba, bo alɔɖowema lɛ, tan e ɖo gbangba ɔ, azɔ wiwa sin ɖijɛ kpo nɔdu lɛ sin dodokpɔ kpo nɔ wa azɔ ɖokpo bo glɔ alɔ akwɛ yɔyɔ dándán alo bitcoin ɖokpo ɔ zizan hwɛ eve.
