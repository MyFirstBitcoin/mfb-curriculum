# BD-V-027-01 Claimed or verified supply

## Alt text

Umda wobonelelo obhalwe kwiwebhusayithi okanye kumthombo wedatha womkhuphi unokutshintshwa ngokufihlakeleyo nguloo mkhuphi. Umgaqo wobonelelo weBitcoin usesidlangalaleni, yaye utyikityo, imbali esesidlangalaleni, ubungqina bomsebenzi kunye nokuqinisekiswa ziinodi zisebenza kunye ukuthintela ukuyilwa kweengqekembe ezintsha okanye ukusetyenziswa kwe-bitcoin enye kabini.

## Texts

### c1-head

Ibango malunga nobonelelo

### c1-n1

Umkhuphi uchaza umda kwiwebhusayithi yakhe.

### c1-n2

Ngumthombo wedatha womkhuphi kuphela owubhalayo.

### c1-n3

Akukho mntu ungaphandle komkhuphi onokuwuqinisekisa.

### c1-n4

Umkhuphi unokulutshintsha ubonelelo ngokufihlakeleyo.

### c1-f1-label

### c1-f1
