# BD-V-027-01 Claimed or verified supply

## Alt text

Supply limit wey dem write for website or inside di database of di body wey dey issue am fit change without anybody knowing, if di body decide to change am. Bitcoin supply rule dey open to everybody, and signature dem, history wey everybody fit see, proof say work don happen and checking by node dem dey work together to stop anybody from creating new coin anyhow or spending di same bitcoin twice.
