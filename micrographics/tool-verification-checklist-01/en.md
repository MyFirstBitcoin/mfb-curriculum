# BD-V-057-01 What to verify in a tool

## Alt text

Ten things to verify before using a local Bitcoin tool: purpose, custody, source, project continuity, recovery, privacy, cost, compatibility, support and law, each with its question. Note the date you checked.

## Texts

### boundary

Write down the date you checked. A new version needs a new check.

### s1-title

Purpose

### s1-text

What problem does the tool solve? Is it necessary?

### s2-title

Custody

### s2-text

Who controls keys and funds?

### s3-title

Source

### s3-text

Who publishes it? Is the listing genuine?

### s4-title

Project continuity

### s4-text

Is it open source, reviewed and maintained?

### s5-title

Recovery

### s5-text

What if the device, service or company disappears?

### s6-title

Privacy

### s6-text

What identity, transaction or device data are collected?

### s7-title

Cost

### s7-text

What fees, spreads, subscriptions or hardware costs apply?

### s8-title

Compatibility

### s8-text

Does it support the wallet, network, address or invoice?

### s9-title

Support

### s9-text

Is safe local help available without sharing secrets?

### s10-title

Law

### s10-text

Are there local restrictions, reporting duties or tax effects?
