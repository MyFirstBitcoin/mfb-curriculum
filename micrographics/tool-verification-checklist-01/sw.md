# BD-V-057-01 What to verify in a tool

## Alt text

Mambo kumi ya kuthibitisha kabla ya kutumia zana ya ndani ya Bitcoin: madhumuni, udhibiti, chanzo, uendelevu wa mradi, urejeshaji, faragha, gharama, uoanifu, usaidizi na sheria, kila moja likiwa na swali lake. Andikeni tarehe mliyofanya ukaguzi.
