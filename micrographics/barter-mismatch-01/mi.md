# BD-V-009-01 Barter mismatch

## Alt text

He raihi tā Amina, ā, e hiahia ana ia ki te hū; he hū tā Luis, ā, e hiahia ana ia ki te hinu tunu kai; he hinu tunu kai tā Mei, ā, e hiahia ana ia ki te mahi whakatika.
