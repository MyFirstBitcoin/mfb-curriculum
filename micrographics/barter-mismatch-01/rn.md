# BD-V-009-01 Barter mismatch

## Alt text

Amina afise umuceri kandi ashaka ibirato, Luis afise ibirato kandi ashaka amavuta yo guteka, Mei na we afise amavuta yo guteka kandi ashaka gusanurirwa. Nta babiri muri bo bashobora guhanahana ivyo bafise ata wundi aciyemwo, kuko ata n'umwe muri abo babiri ashaka ico uwundi atanga.
