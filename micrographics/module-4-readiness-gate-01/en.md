# BD-V-095-01 Module 4 readiness gate

## Alt text

Eight checks cover purpose, competency, educator capability, tools, local rules, safety, access and observation. Every check must be satisfactory for live delivery; a gap leads to a safer route or revised plan and another readiness review.
