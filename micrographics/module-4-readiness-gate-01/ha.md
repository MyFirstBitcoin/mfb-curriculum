# BD-V-095-01 Module 4 readiness gate

## Alt text

Abubuwan tantancewa guda takwas sun shafi manufa, ƙwarewa, iyawar mai koyarwa, kayan aiki, dokokin yankin, tsaro, damar shiga da lura. Dole ne a gamsu da sakamakon kowane tantancewa kafin gabatar da darasin kai tsaye; idan akwai gibi, sai a bi hanya mafi aminci ko a sake fasalin shirin sannan a sake duba ko an shirya.
