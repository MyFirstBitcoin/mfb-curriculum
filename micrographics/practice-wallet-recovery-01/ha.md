# BD-V-043-01 Practice wallet recovery safely

## Alt text

Atisayen matakai shida na dawo da jakar kuɗin horo: ku bi umarnin hukuma, ku duba dacewa, ku yi aikin a keɓance, ku yi amfani da hanyar dawo da ita da aka amince da ita, ku tabbatar cewa jakar kuɗin ita ce wadda ake sa ran samu, sannan ku sake saita komai. Ku yi amfani da kayan da aka tanada, marasa komai ko na kwaikwayo kawai, kuma malamin ba zai taɓa ganin kalmomin dawo da jakar kuɗi ba.

## Texts

### boundary

Kayan da aka tanada, marasa komai ko na kwaikwayo kawai. Malaminku ba zai taɓa ganin kalmomin dawo da jakar kuɗi ba.

Ku bi umarnin

### s1-text

Ku yi amfani da jagorar hukuma ta takamaiman jakar kuɗin da ake amfani da ita wajen horon.

### s2-text

### s5-title

Ku tabbatar da jakar kuɗin

### s6-title

Ku sake saita komai

### s6-text
