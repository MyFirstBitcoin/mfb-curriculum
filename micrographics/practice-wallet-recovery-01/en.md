# BD-V-043-01 Practice wallet recovery safely

## Alt text

A six-step practice for recovering a training wallet: follow official instructions, check compatibility, work privately, use the approved recovery method, confirm the expected wallet, then reset. Use only prepared, empty or simulated materials, and the educator never sees the recovery words.

## Texts

### boundary

Prepared, empty or simulated materials only. Your educator never sees the recovery words.

### s1-title

Follow the instructions

### s1-text

Use the official guide for the exact training wallet.

### s2-title

Check compatibility

### s2-text

Make sure the wallet and its recovery method work together.

### s3-title

Work privately

### s3-text

Keep cameras and observers away from the secret material.

### s4-title

Use the approved method

### s4-text

Recover with the wallet&#x27;s own backup-check or recovery method.

### s5-title

Confirm the wallet

### s5-text

Check the expected receiving information or training history.

### s6-title

Reset

### s6-text

Clear the temporary training environment as instructed.
