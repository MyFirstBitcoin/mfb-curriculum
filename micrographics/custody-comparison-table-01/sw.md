# BD-V-040-01 Custodial and self-custodial compared

## Alt text

Jedwali linalolinganisha matumizi ya uhifadhi kupitia mtoa huduma na matumizi ya kujihifadhia mwenyewe: nani anashikilia funguo, nani anashughulikia urejeshaji, na faida na hasara kuu za kila moja. Hakuna mfumo usio na hatari.
