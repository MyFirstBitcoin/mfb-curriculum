# BD-V-010-01 Selling and buying become separate

## Alt text

Amina tana sayar da shinkafa ga mutum ɗaya don samun kuɗin da kowa ke karɓa, sannan daga baya ta yi amfani da waɗannan kuɗin don sayen takalma daga wani dabam. Sayarwa da saye sun zama ayyuka biyu daban.

Da farko, tana sayarwa

### f2

### f2-text
