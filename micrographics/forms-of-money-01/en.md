# BD-V-013-01 Forms of money that coexist

## Alt text

Six forms of money appear left to right in the order they arose: direct exchange, commodity money, standard coins, representative paper, bank and government money, and digital money. Each new form starts later while the older forms carry on beside it, so many forms coexist rather than one replacing another.
