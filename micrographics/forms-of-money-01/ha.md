# BD-V-013-01 Forms of money that coexist

## Alt text

An nuna nau'o'in kuɗi shida daga hagu zuwa dama bisa tsarin lokacin da suka bayyana: musayar kai tsaye, kuɗin kayayyaki, tsabar kuɗi masu ƙa'ida, takardun kuɗi masu wakilci, kuɗin banki da na gwamnati, da kuɗin dijital. Kowane sabon nau'i yana farawa daga baya yayin da ake ci gaba da amfani da tsofaffin nau'o'i tare da shi, don haka nau'o'i da yawa suna kasancewa tare maimakon wani ya maye gurbin wani.
