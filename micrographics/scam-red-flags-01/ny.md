# BD-V-047-01 Scam red flags

## Alt text

Zizindikiro zisanu zochenjeza za chinyengo: kukupanikizani kuti muchitepo kanthu mwachangu, kudzinamizira kuti ndi munthu wina, kusintha kumene ndalama zikutumizidwa, kulonjeza phindu lotsimikizika ndi kupempha zinsinsi.
