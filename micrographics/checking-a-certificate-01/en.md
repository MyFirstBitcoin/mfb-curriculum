# BD-V-075-01 Checking a certificate

## Alt text

To check a Bitcoin Diploma certificate, find the educator or organization that issued it and ask them. My First Bitcoin does not run a central verification service; only the issuer can confirm, correct or withdraw a certificate.

## Texts

### boundary

My First Bitcoin does not run a central verification service.

### s1-title

Find the issuer

### s1-text

Every certificate names the educator or organization that issued it.

### s2-title

Ask the issuer

### s2-text

Send your question to that issuer, not to My First Bitcoin.

### s3-title

Rely on their answer

### s3-text

Only the issuer can confirm, correct or withdraw a certificate.
