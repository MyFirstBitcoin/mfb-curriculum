# BD-V-026-01 Participant powers and limits

## Alt text

Users, nodes, miners, developers and services each have some power around Bitcoin's shared rules and public ledger, but none can change the ledger or the rules for everyone alone.

## Texts

### i1

Users

### i1-text

Can transact without an account. Cannot change rules for everyone.

### i2

Nodes

### i2-text

Can check every transaction and block. Cannot force rules on others.

### i3

Miners

### i3-text

Can propose blocks. Cannot make an invalid block acceptable.

### center

Shared rules and public ledger

### i4

Developers

### i4-text

Can write open-source software. Changes need voluntary adoption.

### i5

Services

### i5-text

Can become points of dependence. Cannot rewrite the ledger.
