# BD-V-025-01 Bitcoin origin timeline

## Alt text

Bitcoin's origin in four dated events: on 31 October 2008 Satoshi Nakamoto published the whitepaper, describing direct online payments without a financial intermediary; on 3 January 2009 the Genesis Block started the Bitcoin network, with a headline about bank bailouts; on 12 January 2009 Satoshi sent bitcoin to Hal Finney in the first recorded person-to-person transaction; on 22 May 2010 a user paid 10,000 BTC for two pizzas, now Bitcoin Pizza Day.
