# BD-V-025-01 Bitcoin origin timeline

## Alt text

Asalin Bitcoin a cikin abubuwa huɗu masu kwanan wata: a ranar 31 Oktoba 2008 Satoshi Nakamoto ya wallafa takardar bayanin fasaha, wadda ta bayyana biyan kuɗi kai tsaye ta intanet ba tare da mai shiga tsakani na harkokin kuɗi ba; a ranar 3 Janairu 2009 Tubalin Farko ya ƙaddamar da cibiyar sadarwar Bitcoin, tare da kanun labari game da tallafin ceton bankuna; a ranar 12 Janairu 2009 Satoshi ya aika bitcoin zuwa ga Hal Finney a mu'amala ta farko kai tsaye tsakanin mutum da mutum da aka rubuta; a ranar 22 Mayu 2010 wani mai amfani ya biya 10,000 BTC don pizza biyu, ranar da yanzu ake kira Ranar Pizza ta Bitcoin.

### i1

31 Oktoba 2008

### i1-note

An wallafa takardar bayanin fasaha: biyan kuɗi ta intanet ba tare da mai shiga tsakani ba

### i2

### i2-note

Tubalin Farko: cibiyar sadarwar Bitcoin ta fara aiki

### i3

12 Janairu 2009

### i3-note

Mu'amala ta farko kai tsaye tsakanin mutum da mutum, daga Satoshi zuwa Hal Finney

### i4

22 Mayu 2010

### i4-note

10,000 BTC don pizza biyu: Ranar Pizza ta Bitcoin
