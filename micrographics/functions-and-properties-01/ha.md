# BD-V-008-01 Functions and properties of money

## Alt text

Matakai biyu: ayyukan kuɗi guda uku (hanyar musanya, ma'aunin lissafi, ma'ajiyar ƙima) sun ginu a kan halaye bakwai (ɗorewa, sauƙin ɗauka, yiwuwar rabawa, musanyuwa da irinsa, ƙaranci, karɓuwa da yiwuwar tantancewa).

## Texts

### l1

Ayyuka

### l1-text

Hanyar musanya, ma'aunin lissafi, ma'ajiyar ƙima.

### l2

Halaye

### l2-text

Mai ɗorewa, mai sauƙin ɗauka, mai yiwuwa a raba, mai musanyuwa da irinsa, mai ƙaranci, karɓaɓɓe, mai yiwuwa a tantance.
