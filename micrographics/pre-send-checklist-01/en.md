# BD-V-045-01 Pre-send checklist

## Alt text

Before approving a payment, the sender checks the recipient and purpose, the payment method, the destination, and the amount, unit, fee or expiry, using a small test when unfamiliar. If anything differs, they stop and verify; they approve only when everything matches, then check the result.
