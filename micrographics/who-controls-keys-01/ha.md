# BD-V-039-01 Custodial and self-custodial use

## Alt text

A tsarin riƙon wani, mai ba da sabis ne yake sarrafa maɓallan kuma yake amincewa da kashe kuɗi, don haka ma'aunin alƙawarinsa ne. A tsarin riƙon kai, mai amfani ne yake sarrafa maɓallan da kwafin ajiya; dukkan hanyoyin biyu suna da abubuwan da suka dogara da su da kuma haɗarin gazawa.
