# BD-V-039-01 Custodial and self-custodial use

## Alt text

Katika uhifadhi wa huduma, huduma inadhibiti funguo na kuidhinisha matumizi, hivyo salio ni ahadi yake. Katika kujihifadhi, mtumiaji anadhibiti funguo na nakala rudufu; njia zote mbili zina utegemezi na hatari za kushindwa.
