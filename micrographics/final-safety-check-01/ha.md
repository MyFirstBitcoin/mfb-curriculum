# BD-V-072-01 Final safety check

## Alt text

Jerin dubawar tsaro na ƙarshe da aka yi wa alamar kammalawa: ku kare sirruka, ku tabbatar da bayanan biyan kuɗi, ku yi gwaji da ƙaramin adadi, kuma ku dakata idan ana matsa muku. Idan wani abu bai bayyana ba, ku dakata ku ƙara koyo kafin ku ɗauki mataki.

## Texts

### screen-title

Dubawar tsaro ta ƙarshe

### f1-label

✓ Ku kare sirruka

### f1

Kada ku taɓa raba jimlar dawo da walat ko maɓallin sirri.

### f2-label

✓ Ku tabbatar da bayanan biyan kuɗi

### f2

Ku duba mai karɓa, hanyar sadarwa, adireshi ko takardar neman biyan kuɗi, adadin kuɗi da kuɗin hidima.

### f3-label

✓ Ku yi gwaji da ƙaramin adadi

### f3

Ku fara aika ƙaramin adadin kuɗi idan tsarin sabo ne a gare ku.

### f4-label

✓ Ku dakata idan ana matsa muku

### f4

Kada ku ɗauki mataki saboda tsoro, gaggawa ko alƙawuran riba.

### note

Idan wani abu bai bayyana ba, ku dakata ku ƙara koyo kafin ku ɗauki mataki.
