# Types of Money

## Alt text

A taxonomy of money split into two branches: physical money (commodity, representative, and fiat) and digital currencies (electronic money, CBDCs, stablecoins, and cryptocurrencies), each with a small icon and one-line description.

## Texts

### physical

Physical

### commodity

Commodity

### commodity-desc

A valued object accepted in trade<br>gold, silver, gunpowder.

### representative

Representative

### representative-desc

A claim on a commodity<br>a certificate redeemable for silver.

### fiat

Fiat

### fiat-desc

Government-issued bills and coins<br>Federal Reserve notes.

### digital

Digital

### electronic

Electronic money

### electronic-desc

Digital dollars and euros, moved over payment rails.

### cbdc

CBDCs

### cbdc-desc

A nation's currency issued and controlled by its central bank.

### stablecoins

Stablecoins

### stablecoins-desc

Built to hold a steady value, usually pegged to the dollar.

### crypto

Cryptocurrencies

### crypto-desc

Digital currencies<br>some decentralized, some not.
