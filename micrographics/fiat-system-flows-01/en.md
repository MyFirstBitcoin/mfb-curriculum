# BD-V-015-01 How a fiat system connects

## Alt text

The actors in a fiat system and what passes between them: the central bank sets policy and settles banks, commercial banks hold deposits that are their liability to customers, payment services carry instructions, the government taxes and spends, and citizens and businesses pay and get paid.

## Texts

### r1

Central bank

### r1-text

Manages monetary policy and supports payment settlement.

### r2

Commercial bank

### r2-text

Owes customers their deposits; a deposit is the bank&#x27;s liability.

### r3

Payment services

### r3-text

Carry payment instructions that change bank deposits.

### r4

Government

### r4-text

Collects taxes and spends, under its own laws.

### r5

Citizens and businesses

### r5-text

Hold deposits, pay, get paid and pay taxes.

### r6

### r6-text

Exact institutions and rules differ from place to place.
