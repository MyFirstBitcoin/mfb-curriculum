# BD-V-024-01 Five-part answer to double spending

## Alt text

Five parts combine to answer the double-spend problem: a peer-to-peer network, digital signatures, blocks and mining, independent verification, and a shared history with the most accumulated proof of work. Together they let the network reject a second spend without a central authority.

## Texts

### i1

Peer-to-peer network

### i1-text

Transactions are announced directly to participants

### i2

Digital signatures

### i2-text

Only the private key holder can authorize a spend

### i3

Blocks and mining

### i3-text

Miners use proof of work to add the next block

### center

The network rejects a second spend, with no central authority

### i4

Independent verification

### i4-text

Nodes check transactions and blocks against the rules

### i5

Shared history

### i5-text
