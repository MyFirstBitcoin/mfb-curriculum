# BD-V-033-01 Seven properties of Bitcoin as money

## Alt text

Bitcoin combines seven properties of money in one asset: durability, portability, divisibility, fungibility, scarcity, verifiability and acceptability. Each has practical limits, such as keeping keys safe and acceptance that is still low in many communities.
