# BD-V-098-01 Adapting practice or assessment

## Alt text

Practice activities can be adapted to the learners and setting. An activity used for assessment must keep the competency, evidence, independence, safety and minimum standard; if equivalence is uncertain, use the adaptation for practice and choose a clearer assessment task.
