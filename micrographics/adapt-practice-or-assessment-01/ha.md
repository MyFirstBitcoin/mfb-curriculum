# BD-V-098-01 Adapting practice or assessment

## Alt text

Ana iya daidaita ayyukan atisaye da masu koyo da kuma yanayin wurin. Aikin da ake amfani da shi don tantancewa dole ne ya kiyaye ƙwarewar da ake aunawa, shaida, dogaro da kai, aminci da mafi ƙarancin ma'auni; idan babu tabbas cewa wanda aka daidaita ya yi daidai da na asali, ku yi amfani da shi don atisaye kuma ku zaɓi aikin tantancewa mafi bayyana.
