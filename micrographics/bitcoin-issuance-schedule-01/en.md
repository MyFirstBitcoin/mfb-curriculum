# BD-V-028-01 Bitcoin issuance and halvings

## Alt text

The block subsidy starts at 50 bitcoin and halves every 210,000 blocks, while total issued bitcoin rises toward the 21 million limit. Transaction fees are paid on top and are not new bitcoin; no price forecast is implied.

## Texts

### supply

Total bitcoin issued

### limit

21 million limit

### subsidy

New bitcoin per block

### era

Halving every 210,000 blocks (roughly four years)

### fees

Transaction fees are paid on top and are not new bitcoin.
