# BD-V-028-01 Bitcoin issuance and halvings

## Alt text

Tallafin buloki yana farawa da bitcoin 50 kuma yana raguwa zuwa rabi a kowane buloki 210,000, yayin da jimillar bitcoin da aka fitar take ƙaruwa tana kusantar iyakar miliyan 21. Ana biyan kuɗaɗen mu'amala a ƙari, kuma ba sababbin bitcoin ba ne; wannan ba yana nufin an yi hasashen farashi ba.

## Texts

### supply

Jimillar bitcoin da aka fitar

### limit

Iyakar miliyan 21

### subsidy

Sabbin bitcoin a kowane buloki

### era

Raguwa zuwa rabi a kowane buloki 210,000 (kimanin shekaru huɗu)

### fees

Ana biyan kuɗaɗen mu'amala a ƙari, kuma ba sababbin bitcoin ba ne.
