# BD-V-082-01 Planning cohort capacity

## Alt text

To plan a cohort, multiply the number of learners by the likely support and observation time, then add time for setup, feedback, access needs and reassessment. If the educators do not have enough time, change the plan: fewer learners, more educators or more time.

## Texts

### s1-title

Count the learners

### s1-text

Note how many learners are in the cohort.

### s2-title

Estimate the time

### s2-text

Multiply by the likely support and observation time per learner.

### s3-title

Add extra time

### s3-text

Allow for setup, troubleshooting, feedback, access needs and reassessment.

### s4-title

Share the work

### s4-text

Divide the total among the educators assessing.

### s5-title

Check the fit

### s5-text

Make sure the educators have enough time.

### s5-aside

Not enough? Extend the session, add an after-class session or a follow-up.

### loop

Changed the plan? Work it out again.
