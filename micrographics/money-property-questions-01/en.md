# BD-V-007-01 Questions about money's properties

## Alt text

Seven properties of useful money, each with a question to ask when comparing forms of money: durability, portability, divisibility, fungibility, scarcity, acceptability and verifiability.

## Texts

### i1

Durability

### i1-text

Can it remain usable over time?

### i2

Portability

### i2-text

Can people move or transfer it easily?

### i3

Divisibility

### i3-text

Can it represent both small and large amounts?

### i4

Fungibility

### i4-text

Are equal units normally interchangeable?

### center

Useful money

### i5

Scarcity

### i5-text

Can its supply increase quickly, and who can change it?

### i6

Acceptability

### i6-text

Will other people receive it in exchange?

### i7

Verifiability

### i7-text

Can people check that it is genuine and readily identify equivalent units?
