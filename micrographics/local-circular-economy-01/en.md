# BD-V-065-01 A local circular economy

## Alt text

In a Bitcoin circular economy, bitcoin passes from person to person in exchange for goods, services and time. A customer pays a merchant, the merchant pays a service provider, the service provider pays a worker, and the worker spends it again as a customer.

## Texts

### i1

Customer

### i1-text

The customer pays a merchant in bitcoin for goods.

### i2

Merchant

### i2-text

The merchant pays a service provider in bitcoin for a service.

### i3

Service provider

### i3-text

The service provider pays a worker in bitcoin for their time.

### i4

Worker

### i4-text

The worker spends that bitcoin again as a customer.
