# How Transations Work

## Alt text

Tsarin matakai bakwai a tsaye da ke nuna biyan kuɗi da bitcoin daga Alice zuwa Bob: ƙirƙirar ma'amala da sanya mata hannu, yaɗa ta, tantancewa daga mahaɗan cibiyar sadarwa, mempool, haƙa ta cikin tubali, da ƙara tubalin zuwa sarkar tubalan yayin da tabbaci ke ƙaruwa.

### s1-desc
