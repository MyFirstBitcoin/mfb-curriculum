# BD-V-048-01 Ways to receive bitcoin

## Alt text

Mutum ɗaya zai iya samun bitcoin ta hanyoyi huɗu daban-daban: samun sa ta hanyar aiki ko tallace-tallace, musayar tsabar kuɗin gida da wani mutum, amfani da dandalin musaya ko dillali, ko amfani da ATM. Kowace hanya tana ƙarewa da riƙe bitcoin da kanku ko samun ragowar kuɗi a wurin mai ajiyarsu, kuma ba a fifita wata hanya a kan sauran ba.

## Texts

### start

Mutum ɗaya da ke son samun bitcoin

### b1-head

### b1-n1

### b2-head

### b2-n1

### b3-head

### b3-n1

### b4-head

ATM

### b4-n1

### end
