# BD-V-048-01 Ways to receive bitcoin

## Alt text

One person can receive bitcoin in four different ways: earning it through work or sales, exchanging local cash with another person, using an exchange or broker, or using an ATM. Each route ends in self-custody or a custodial balance, and none is recommended over the others.
