# BD-V-031-01 Roles in Bitcoin

## Alt text

Swiphemu swa mune eka Bitcoin: vatirhisi va amukela, va hlayisa ni ku rhumela bitcoin, tinodi ti tiyisisa milawu hi toxe, vaceli va aka tibuloko leti ringanyetiweke hi vumbhoni bya ntchito, naswona vatumbuluxi va sofwere va ringanyeta sofwere. Ku hava xiphemu xin’we lexi lawulaka netiweke.

## Texts

### r1

Mutirhisi

### r1-text

Mutirhisi u amukela, a hlayisa ni ku rhumela bitcoin.

### r2

Nodi

### r2-text

Nodi yi tikambela milawu hi yoxe naswona yi hundzisela rungula leri landzelaka milawu.

### r3

Muceli

### r3-text

Muceli u aka tibuloko leti ringanyetiweke naswona u endla vumbhoni bya ntchito.

### r4

Mutumbuluxi wa sofwere

### r4-text

Mutumbuluxi wa sofwere u kambisisa, a tsala ni ku ringanyeta sofwere.
