# BD-V-031-01 Roles in Bitcoin

## Alt text

Izindima ezine ku-Bitcoin: abasebenzisi bamukela, bagcine futhi bathumele i-bitcoin, amanodi aqinisekisa imithetho ngokuzimela, abavukuzi bakha amabhulokhi ahlongozwayo ngobufakazi bomsebenzi, kanti abathuthukisi baphakamisa isofthiwe. Ayikho indima eyodwa elawula inethiwekhi.

## Texts

### r1

Umsebenzisi

### r1-text

Umsebenzisi wamukela, agcine futhi athumele i-bitcoin.

### r2

Inodi

### r2-text

Inodi izihlolela imithetho bese idlulisela idatha evumelekile.

### r3

Umvukuzi

### r3-text

Umvukuzi wakha amabhulokhi ahlongozwayo futhi enze ubufakazi bomsebenzi.

### r4

Umthuthukisi

### r4-text

Umthuthukisi ubuyekeza, abhale futhi aphakamise isofthiwe.
