# BD-V-031-01 Roles in Bitcoin

## Alt text

Amabanga ane muri Bitcoin: abakoresha barakira, bakabika kandi bakarungika bitcoin, amahuriro arigenzurira ko amategeko yubahirijwe, abacukuzi bategura ibice vy'amakuru bishobora kwemerwa bakoresheje icemezo c'igikorwa, abahinguzi ba porogarama na bo bagatanga ivyiyumviro vya porogarama. Nta na rimwe muri ayo mabanga ryihariza ububasha bwo kugenzura umuhora.

## Texts

### r1

Umukoresha

### r1-text

Umukoresha arakira, akabika kandi akarungika bitcoin.

### r2

Ihuriro

### r2-text

Ihuriro ririgenzurira ko amategeko yubahirijwe kandi rigahanahana amakuru yemewe.

### r3

Umucukuzi

### r3-text

Umucukuzi ategura ibice vy'amakuru bishobora kwemerwa kandi agakora akazi gatanga icemezo c'igikorwa.

### r4

Umuhinguzi wa porogarama

### r4-text

Umuhinguzi wa porogarama arasuzuma, akandika kandi agatanga ivyiyumviro vya porogarama.
