# BD-V-031-01 Roles in Bitcoin

## Alt text

E whā ngā tūranga i Bitcoin: ka whiwhi, ka pupuri, ka tuku bitcoin ngā kaiwhakamahi, ka manatoko motuhake ngā kōpuku i ngā ture, ka hanga ngā kaikeri i ngā poraka kaitono mā te taunakitanga mahi, ā, ka tūtohu pūmanawa ngā kaiwhakawhanake. Kāore he tūranga kotahi e whakahaere ana i te whatunga.

## Texts

### r1

Kaiwhakamahi

### r1-text

Ka whiwhi, ka pupuri, ka tuku bitcoin te kaiwhakamahi.

### r2

Kōpuku

### r2-text

Ka arowhai te kōpuku i ngā ture mōna ake, ā, ka tuku whakamua i ngā raraunga whaimana.

### r3

Kaikeri

### r3-text

Ka hanga te kaikeri i ngā poraka kaitono, ā, ka whakatutuki i te taunakitanga mahi.

### r4

Kaiwhakawhanake

### r4-text

Ka arotake, ka tuhi, ka tūtohu pūmanawa te kaiwhakawhanake.
