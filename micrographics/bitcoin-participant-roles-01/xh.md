# BD-V-031-01 Roles in Bitcoin

## Alt text

Iindima ezine kwi-Bitcoin: abasebenzisi bafumana, bagcine baze bathumele i-bitcoin, iinodi ziqinisekisa imithetho ngokuzimeleyo, abagodi bakha iibhloko ezicetywayo ngobungqina bomsebenzi, yaye abaphuhlisi bacebisa isoftwe. Akukho ndima inye ilawula uthungelwano.

## Texts

### r1

Umsebenzisi

### r1-text

Umsebenzisi ufumana, agcine aze athumele i-bitcoin.

### r2

Inodi

### r2-text

Inodi izijongela ngokwayo imithetho ize idlulisele idatha evumelekileyo.

### r3

Umgodi

### r3-text

Umgodi wakha iibhloko ezicetywayo aze enze ubungqina bomsebenzi.

### r4

Umphuhlisi

### r4-text

Umphuhlisi uphonononga, abhale aze acebise isoftwe.
