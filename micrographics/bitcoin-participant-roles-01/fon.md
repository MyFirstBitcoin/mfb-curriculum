# BD-V-031-01 Roles in Bitcoin

## Alt text

Azɔ ɛnɛ ɖo Bitcoin mɛ: nuzantɔ lɛ nọ xɔ bitcoin, bo nọ hɛn ɛn bo nọ sɛ ɛ ɖo mɛ; nɔdu lɛ nọ kpɔn sɛn lɛ ɖokpo na yeɖe; blɔku ɖotɔ lɛ nọ ɖo blɔku he ye na ɖo nukɔn lɛ bo nọ do azɔ wiwa tɔn kun; bo lɔjisiyɛ wlatɔ lɛ nọ ɖo lɔjisiyɛ ɖo nukɔn. Azɔ ɖokpo ɖevo ma nọ ɖu rɛzo ɔ ji a.

## Texts

### r1

Nuzantɔ

### r1-text

Nuzantɔ nọ xɔ bitcoin, bo nọ hɛn ɛn bo nọ sɛ ɛ ɖo mɛ.

### r2

Nɔdu

### r2-text

Nɔdu nọ kpɔn sɛn lɛ ɖokpo na eɖe bo nọ sɛ nyatakɛn he sɔgbe lɛ ɖo nɔdu ɖevo lɛ.

### r3

Blɔku ɖotɔ

### r3-text

Blɔku ɖotɔ nọ ɖo blɔku he e na ɖo nukɔn lɛ bo nọ do azɔ wiwa tɔn kun.

### r4

Lɔjisiyɛ wlatɔ

### r4-text

Lɔjisiyɛ wlatɔ nọ gbadopɔn lɔjisiyɛ, bo nọ wlan ɛn bo nọ ɖo ɛ ɖo nukɔn.
