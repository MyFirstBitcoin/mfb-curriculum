# BD-V-031-01 Roles in Bitcoin

## Alt text

Fo tiri wroko ini Bitcoin: gebroikiman e kisi, hori nanga seni bitcoin, node e tesi den regla fu densrefi, digriman e meki kandidati-blok nanga wroko-beweysi, nanga software-mekiman e poti software na fesi. No wan fu den grupu disi e basi a netwerki ensrefi.

## Texts

### r1

Gebroikiman

### r1-text

Wan gebroikiman e kisi, hori nanga seni bitcoin.

### r2

Node

### r2-text

Wan node e tesi den regla fu ensrefi nanga seni data di klopu go moro fara.

### r3

Digriman

### r3-text

Wan digriman e meki kandidati-blok nanga du wroko-beweysi.

### r4

Software-mekiman

### r4-text

Wan software-mekiman e luku software bun, skrifi en nanga poti en na fesi.
