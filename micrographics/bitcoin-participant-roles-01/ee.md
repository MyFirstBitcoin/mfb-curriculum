# BD-V-031-01 Roles in Bitcoin

## Alt text

Dɔ ene le Bitcoin me: zãlawo xɔa bitcoin, dzraa wo ɖo eye wodɔa wo ɖa, kadodoƒewo ŋutɔwo léa ŋku ɖe sewo dzi, blɔk kulawo wɔa blɔk siwo woate ŋu axɔ to dɔwɔwɔ ƒe kpeɖodzi me, eye sɔftwɛ wɔlawo ɖoa sɔftwɛ ŋu aɖaŋu. Dɔ ɖeka aɖeke mela ɖe kadodo blibo la dzi o.

## Texts

### r1

Zãla

### r1-text

Zãla xɔa bitcoin, dzraa wo ɖo eye wòdɔa wo ɖa.

### r2

Kadodoƒe

### r2-text

Kadodoƒe ŋutɔ léa ŋku ɖe sewo dzi eye wòɖoa nyatakaka si sɔ ɖe sewo nu la ɖa.

### r3

Blɔk kula

### r3-text

Blɔk kula wɔa blɔk siwo woate ŋu axɔ eye wòwɔa dɔwɔwɔ ƒe kpeɖodzi.

### r4

Sɔftwɛ wɔla

### r4-text

Sɔftwɛ wɔla léa ŋku ɖe sɔftwɛ dzi, ŋlɔa wo eye wòɖoa wo ŋu aɖaŋu.
