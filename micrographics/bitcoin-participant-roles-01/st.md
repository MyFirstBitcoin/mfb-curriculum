# BD-V-031-01 Roles in Bitcoin

## Alt text

Likarolo tse 'nè ho Bitcoin: basebelisi ba amohela, ba boloka le ho romela bitcoin, linoute li netefatsa melao ka boikemelo, baepi ba haha liboloko tse ka amoheloang ka bopaki ba mosebetsi, 'me baqapi ba sisinya software. Ha ho karolo e le 'ngoe e laolang marang-rang.

## Texts

### r1

Mosebelisi

### r1-text

Mosebelisi o amohela, o boloka le ho romela bitcoin.

### r2

Noute

### r2-text

Noute e itlhahlobela melao ebe e fetisa lintlha tse nepahetseng.

### r3

Moepi

### r3-text

Moepi o haha liboloko tse ka amoheloang ebe o etsa bopaki ba mosebetsi.

### r4

Moqapi

### r4-text

Moqapi o hlahloba, o ngola le ho sisinya software.
