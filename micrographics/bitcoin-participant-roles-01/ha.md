# BD-V-031-01 Roles in Bitcoin

## Alt text

Matsayin mahalarta iri huɗu a Bitcoin: masu amfani suna karɓa, suna ajiyewa kuma suna aika bitcoin, ƙullai suna tabbatar da bin ƙa’idoji da kansu, masu haƙowa suna gina tubalan da ake neman amincewa da su ta hanyar tabbacin aiki, kuma masu haɓaka manhaja suna ba da shawarwari kan manhaja. Babu wani matsayi guda ɗaya da yake sarrafa hanyar sadarwar.

## Texts

### r1

Mai amfani

### r1-text

Mai amfani yana karɓa, yana ajiyewa kuma yana aika bitcoin.

### r2

Ƙulli

### r2-text

Ƙulli yana bincika ƙa’idoji da kansa kuma yana isar da sahihan bayanai.

### r3

Mai haƙowa

### r3-text

Mai haƙowa yana gina tubalan da ake neman amincewa da su kuma yana aiwatar da tabbacin aiki.

### r4

Mai haɓaka manhaja

### r4-text

Mai haɓaka manhaja yana nazari, yana rubutawa kuma yana ba da shawarwari kan manhaja.
