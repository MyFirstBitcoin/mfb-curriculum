# BD-V-031-01 Roles in Bitcoin

## Alt text

Ọrụ anọ na Bitcoin: ndị ọrụ na-anata, na-ejide ma na-eziga bitcoin, ọnụ netwọk na-enyocha iwu n'onwe ha, ndị na-egwupụta na-ewu blọk ndị a na-atụ aro site n'iji ihe akaebe ọrụ, ndị nrụpụta sọftụwia na-atụkwa aro sọftụwia. Ọ dịghị otu ọrụ n'ime ha na-achịkwa netwọk ahụ.

## Texts

### r1

Onye ọrụ

### r1-text

Onye ọrụ na-anata, na-ejide ma na-eziga bitcoin.

### r2

Ọnụ netwọk

### r2-text

Ọnụ netwọk na-enyocha iwu n'onwe ya ma na-ebufe data ziri ezi.

### r3

Onye na-egwupụta

### r3-text

Onye na-egwupụta na-ewu blọk ndị a na-atụ aro ma na-arụ ọrụ iji nye ihe akaebe ọrụ.

### r4

Onye nrụpụta sọftụwia

### r4-text

Onye nrụpụta sọftụwia na-enyocha, na-ede ma na-atụ aro sọftụwia.
