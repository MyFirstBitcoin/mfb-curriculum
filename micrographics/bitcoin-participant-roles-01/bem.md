# BD-V-031-01 Roles in Bitcoin

## Alt text

Imilimo ine muli Bitcoin: ababomfya bapokelela, basunga no kutuma bitcoin, amanodi yashininikisha imikalile ya makonko pa lwawo, abemba bapanga amabuloko ayengasuminishiwa ukupitila mu bushininkisho bwa mulimo, kabili abapanga amaporogilamu balangisha amaporogilamu ayengabomfiwa. Takuli mulimo umo uwatungulula umulumikizano onse.

## Texts

### r1

Uubomfya

### r1-text

Uubomfya apokelela, asunga no kutuma bitcoin.

### r2

Nodi

### r2-text

Nodi ilashininikisha iyo ine imikalile ya makonko no kutumya ilyashi ilyalungama.

### r3

Umwimba

### r3-text

Umwimba apanga amabuloko ayengasuminishiwa no kucita ubushininkisho bwa mulimo.

### r4

Uupanga amaporogilamu

### r4-text

Uupanga amaporogilamu alasansha, alemba no kulangisha amaporogilamu ayengabomfiwa.
