# BD-V-031-01 Roles in Bitcoin

## Alt text

Dwumadie ahodoɔ nnan wɔ Bitcoin mu: wɔn a wɔde di dwuma no gye bitcoin, sie, na wɔsane de kɔma afoforɔ; nkitahodie afiri biara hwɛ sɛ wɔadi mmara no so a ɛmfa ne ho nto afiri foforɔ so; bitcoin tutufoɔ de adwumayɛ ho adanseɛ yɛ blɔk a wɔbɛsɔ ahwɛ; na kɔmputa so dwumadie yɛfoɔ de kɔmputa so dwumadie to dwa. Saa dwumadie yi mu baako mpo nni nkitahodie no so tumi.

## Texts

### r1

Ɔdefoɔ

### r1-text

Ɔdefoɔ gye bitcoin, sie, na ɔsane de kɔma afoforɔ.

### r2

Nkitahodie afiri

### r2-text

Nkitahodie afiri ankasa hwɛ sɛ wɔadi mmara no so, na ɛde nsɛm a ɛdi mmara no so kɔma afiri afoforɔ.

### r3

Bitcoin tutufoɔ

### r3-text

Bitcoin tutufoɔ yɛ blɔk a wɔbɛsɔ ahwɛ na ɔyɛ adwumayɛ ho adanseɛ no.

### r4

Kɔmputa so dwumadie yɛfoɔ

### r4-text

Kɔmputa so dwumadie yɛfoɔ hwehwɛ kɔmputa so dwumadie mu, ɔkyerɛw bi, na ɔde bi nso to dwa.
