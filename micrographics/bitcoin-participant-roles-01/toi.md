# BD-V-031-01 Roles in Bitcoin

## Alt text

Milimo iine muBitcoin: babelesi balatambula, balabamba alimwi balatuma bitcoin, manodi alalisinizizya milawo, bagodi balayaka mabuloko aasololekwa kwiinda mukucita bumboni bwamulimo, alimwi bapangi bamapulogilamu balasololeka mapulogilamu. Taakwe mulimo omwe weendelezya netiweki.
