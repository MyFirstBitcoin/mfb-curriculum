# BD-V-060-01 Ways to use Bitcoin in daily life

## Alt text

Hanyoyi bakwai daban-daban da za a iya amfani da Bitcoin a rayuwar yau da kullum: samun kuɗin shiga, yin tanadi, karɓa, aikawa, kashe kuɗi, ba da gudummawa, da sanya farashi da lissafin kuɗi. Kowace hanya ce ta amfani da shi, ba matakin da dole ne a bi ba.
