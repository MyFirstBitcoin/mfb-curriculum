# BD-V-035-01 A wallet manages keys

## Alt text

Wallet dey hold di keys. With dem, una fit receive and send bitcoin, while di bitcoin itself dey recorded for di ledger wey everybody dey share, no be inside di phone or device.

## Texts

### start

Una wallet dey hold di keys

### b1-head

Receive

### b1-n1

Share address wey una go use receive

### b2-head

Send

### b2-n1

Sign with una key

### end

Di bitcoin dey remain for di ledger wey everybody dey share
