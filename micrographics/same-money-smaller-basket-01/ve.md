# BD-V-018-01 Same money, smaller basket

## Alt text

Yunithi dzenedzo dza 100 dza tshelede dzi renga bhasikete yoṱhe ya thundu dza mutheo musi i tshi ḓura 100, fhedzi a dzi koni musi bhasikete yeneyo i tshi ḓura 110. Vhuimo ha mitengo ho gonya nahone maanḓa a u renga a tshelede o fhungudzea.
