# BD-V-018-01 Same money, smaller basket

## Alt text

Nanga a srefi 100 moni-yuniti yu kan bai a heri basketi nanga den moro fanowdu sani te a e kosti 100, ma no te a srefi basketi e kosti 110. Den prisi go hei, èn a krakti fu a moni fu bai sani go lagi.
