# BD-V-088-01 The learning rhythm

## Alt text

The educator sets purpose, activates prior ideas, explains, checks, guides application, invites independent production, debriefs, invites reflection, and connects to the next concept. Unclear responses lead back to explanation or practice.

## Texts

### s1-title

Purpose

### s1-text

Tell learners why the idea matters.

### s2-title

Activate

### s2-text

Ask what they already think.

### s3-title

Explain

### s3-text

Give the shortest accurate explanation.

### s4-title

Check

### s4-text

Ask a question that reveals understanding.

### s4-aside

Unclear? Go back to explain or practise.

### s5-title

Apply

### s5-text

Use an activity, scenario or decision.

### s6-title

Produce

### s6-text

Ask learners to explain or perform with less support.

### s7-title

Debrief

### s7-text

Name evidence, limits, safety and trade-offs.

### s8-title

Reflect

### s8-text

End the module with a brief closing question.

### s9-title

Connect

### s9-text

Show how it prepares the next part.
