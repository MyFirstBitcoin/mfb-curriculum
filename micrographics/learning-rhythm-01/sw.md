# BD-V-088-01 The learning rhythm

## Alt text

Mwalimu anaweka lengo, anaamsha mawazo ya awali, anaeleza, anahakiki, anaongoza matumizi, anakaribisha uzalishaji wa kujitegemea, anatathmini, anakaribisha tafakari, na anaunganisha na dhana inayofuata. Majibu yasiyoeleweka yanarudisha kwenye maelezo au mazoezi.
