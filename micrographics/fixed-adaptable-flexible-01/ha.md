# BD-V-087-01 What stays fixed and what can change

## Alt text

Ana iya sauya misalan yankin da hanyoyin koyarwa cikin ƙayyadaddun iyakokin ƙwarewa, shaidar koyo, aminci da kammalawa. Sauye-sauyen da suka shafi tantancewa suna buƙatar wata hanya makamanciya da aka amince da ita, wadda ke kiyaye aikin da ɗalibi zai yi da kuma manufar aminci iri ɗaya.
