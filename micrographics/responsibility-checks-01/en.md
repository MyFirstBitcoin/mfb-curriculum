# BD-V-053-01 Self-custody responsibility checks

## Alt text

A table of six self-custody tasks, what to protect or check for each, and what can happen if it goes wrong: backup, receiving, sending, custody, tool choice and acquisition.

## Texts

### corner

Task

### col1

What to protect or check

### col2

If it goes wrong

### row1

Backup

### c1-1

Keep the seed phrase offline and private.

### c1-2

No company can restore a lost backup.

### row2

Receiving

### c2-1

Share only the address or payment request.

### c2-2

Shared secrets let others spend your bitcoin.

### row3

Sending

### c3-1

Check method, destination, amount and final screen.

### c3-2

A sent payment is usually hard to reverse.

### row4

Custody

### c4-1

Know who holds the keys.

### c4-2

A service can limit or delay withdrawals.

### row5

Tool choice

### c5-1

Check the download source and recovery method.

### c5-2

A fake wallet can steal your keys.

### row6

Acquisition

### c6-1

Compare total cost, identity data and limits.

### c6-2

Hidden fees or a dishonest party can cost money.
