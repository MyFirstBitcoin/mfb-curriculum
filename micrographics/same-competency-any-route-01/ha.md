# BD-V-089-01 Same competency, any route

## Alt text

Kayan aiki na ainihi, kwaikwayo da takarda, da hanyoyin da aka amince da su waɗanda suka dace da buƙatun ɗalibai za su iya tallafa wa ƙwarewa iri ɗaya muddin ɗalibi yana yanke shawarwarin da ake buƙata kuma malami zai iya tantance hujjoji masu daidaitaccen inganci. Idan babu wannan daidaito, ku shirya wata hanya kafin bayar da takardar shaida.

## Texts

### b1-head

### b1-n1

Walat, mai binciken sarkar tubalai ko biyan kuɗi ta intanet

### b2-head

### b2-n1

### b3-head

### end
