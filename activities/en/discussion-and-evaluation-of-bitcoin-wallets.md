# Discussion and evaluation of Bitcoin wallets

_Discussion, Sorting · Individual, Whole class_

Learners compare the wallets listed on bitcoin.org against the criteria covered in the chapter, choose the one that fits their own needs, and defend the choice to the group.

#### Materials

* A phone, tablet or computer with internet access, one per learner or pair

#### Procedure

Go to the following website: [https://bitcoin.org/en/choose-your-wallet](https://bitcoin.org/en/choose-your-wallet)

Use your new knowledge of Bitcoin wallets to select the one best suited to your needs based on the criteria we discussed today.

#### Notes

The criteria are the ones the chapter has just covered: security, privacy, convenience, compatibility, fees, control, reputation, and whether the wallet's software is open source. Ask a few learners to name the wallet they picked and which criterion decided it, so the class hears that the answer depends on the person and not on a ranking.
