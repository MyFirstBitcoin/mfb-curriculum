# Generate SHA 256 Hash

_Experiential · Individual_

Learners hash words and sentences of their own choosing in an online SHA256 generator and see for themselves that a hash is a one-way digital fingerprint.

#### Materials

* A phone, tablet or computer with internet access

#### Procedure

Curious about how hashing works? Open [tools.keycdn.com/sha256-online-generator](https://tools.keycdn.com/sha256-online-generator) to instantly generate a SHA256 hash from any word, sentence, or input you choose. Hash functions are like digital fingerprints: they're one-way, meaning once something is hashed, it can't be reversed. Try it out and see for yourself!

#### Notes

Two things are worth making visible while learners experiment, both of them from this chapter: the same input always produces the same hash, and changing a single character produces a completely different one. Ask two learners to hash the same word and compare, then ask one of them to change one letter.
