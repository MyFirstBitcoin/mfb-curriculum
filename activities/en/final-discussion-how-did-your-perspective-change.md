# Final Discussion, How Did Your Perspective Change?

_Discussion, Reflection · Individual, Whole class_

Learners answer the four questions from the very first activity again, compare their new answers with the originals, and decide what their next step is.

#### Materials

* The answers each learner wrote during module 1, for comparison

#### Procedure

Please answer the following questions:

* Why do we need money?
* What is money?
* Who controls money?
* What gives money its "value"?
* Write down the questions that were selected during Module 1 and answer them now.

Then:

1. Go back to the first activity in Module 1 and compare your new answers to your old answers.
1. Compare and discuss the original answers and questions. Did something change?
1. Ask yourself this final question: What is my next step? And how can I use this new knowledge to empower myself?

If you're ready to take the next step, check out the additional resources in the following section, which contain the best resources for further learning and success.
