# 12.6 Difficult terms to check locally

Pay particular attention to:

* money versus [currency](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency)
* [asset](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset), claim, [liability](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liability), [liquidity](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liquidity), and [solvency](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#solvency)
* [inflation](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#inflation) and [purchasing power](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power)
* [decentralization](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#decentralization) and peer-to-peer
* [proof of work](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work) and verification
* wallet, [custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), and [self-custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#self-custody)
* [private key](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key), [seed phrase](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), and recovery
* address, invoice, and payment request
* [confirmation](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#confirmation) and finality
* on-chain and Lightning
* [privacy](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy) and anonymity
* [KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#kyc) and [no-KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#no-kyc); and
* trade-off, risk, and uncertainty.

Use the definitions in the Lexicon.

My First Bitcoin prefers in-person Bitcoin Diploma sessions because direct educator contact and greater opportunity for practical activities can make learning more engaging and natural. This is a preference, not a requirement. Online delivery can be more affordable, scalable, and achievable for many people, so educators should support either mode appropriately.
