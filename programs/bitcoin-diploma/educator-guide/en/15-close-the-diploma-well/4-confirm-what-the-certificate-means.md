# 15.3 Confirm what the certificate means

Explain the difference between the two certificate types:

* **Certificate of participation:** confirms that the learner attended or took part in the Bitcoin Diploma, without confirming that they passed the assessment.
* **Certificate of completion:** confirms that the educator assessed the learner and judged that they passed all required competencies.

The educator assesses the learner, accredits the result, and distributes either certificate. The certificate records the named educator's judgment. My First Bitcoin does not centrally evaluate learners, issue certificates, provide a central verification service, or guarantee the learner's competency. The current certificate template uses the wording **My First Bitcoin Diploma**; an educator who has completed the Educator Training Program may show the ETP badge beside their name.

Before issuing a completion certificate, confirm that your selected tasks and local variants, observation capacity, accessibility routes, Assessment Grid, and proportionate local privacy, correction, and appeals arrangements are ready. If you offer an issuer-specific verification method, identify it clearly and minimize personal data; do not imply that it is central My First Bitcoin verification.

After you have confirmed each learner's certificate type and assessment status, use the [Certificate Tool](https://tools.myfirstbitcoin.org/certificate) to produce participation or completion certificates in a batch. Check names, certificate types, and issuing details before distributing them. The tool helps prepare certificates; it does not assess learners, decide completion, or change the educator-issued certification model.

Independent review, attendance, confidence, and activity participation do not by themselves establish completion. A learner with a **Not yet** result should receive a clear, respectful opportunity for more practice and reassessment as they build competency. Do not create public “graduates” and “failures” inside the room.
