# 15.1 Capstone discussion

Use think–pair–share before whole-group discussion:

* What can you now explain about money that you could not explain before?
* How does Bitcoin respond to one money or digital-payment problem?
* Which new responsibility does that response create?
* Which practical check are you better prepared to perform?
* Which scam or error signal would make you stop?
* What did the local module show, and what did it fail to prove?
* Which claim do you now evaluate differently?
* What requires further learning?

Ask learners to support answers with a course concept and an example, not a slogan.
