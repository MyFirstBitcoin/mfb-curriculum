# 15.2 Final personal learning reflection (`F2`)

Each learner articulates either:

* a personal reason to continue learning about Bitcoin; or
* a reason for remaining uncertain.

The response should:

* use at least one course concept accurately
* connect to an observed, local, or realistic money experience
* identify one relevant benefit, risk, question, or trade-off; and
* avoid an investment or [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) claim.

Use the course-ending reflection formatively to support `F2`. Preserve the learner's right to remain uncertain: give feedback on grounded explanation and connection to experience, not enthusiasm, agreement, or an intention to acquire bitcoin. It does not currently contribute to certification.

Examples:

> I want to learn more about [self-custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#self-custody) because I can now explain the difference between an account balance and control of keys. I am not ready to manage a meaningful amount until I can test recovery safely.

> I remain uncertain that Bitcoin improves local payments because most merchants here do not accept it and conversion adds cost. I want to compare the complete route with our existing mobile-money option.

Keep this reflection conversational. Do not require an engagement promise, a written next-step commitment, or evidence of completing a post-course action. Do not require a commitment to acquire or use bitcoin.
