# 14.1 Use visuals as teaching tools

Use diagrams, tables, timelines, and interface examples to reveal relationships, not decorate a lecture.

Before showing a visual:

* identify the one idea learners should notice
* ask learners to predict the relationship or sequence
* confirm that labels match the current Student Guide and local language
* explain symbols and reading order
* check that the visual works in grayscale and at phone or print size; and
* prepare a spoken or text equivalent.

After showing it:

* ask a learner to explain the relationship
* name what the visual simplifies
* correct any misleading inference; and
* connect it to the activity or next section.

Educator-useful visual areas include:

* functions versus properties of money
* [barter](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#barter) and double coincidence
* local fiat roles and bank balance sheets
* [liquidity](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liquidity) versus [solvency](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#solvency)
* [inflation](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#inflation) and [purchasing power](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power)
* Bitcoin roles and [proof of work](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work)
* supply, [halvings](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#halving), and [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi)
* fiat issue / Bitcoin response / responsibility
* wallet, key, address, and seed relationships
* safe recovery and transaction checks
* [confirmations](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#confirmation), explorer [privacy](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy), and address reuse
* scam red flags
* on-chain and Lightning comparison
* Presentation → Practice → Production
* local payment paths, merchant flows, and circular economies; and
* final safety and next steps.

Never display a real learner [seed phrase](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), [private key](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key), payable address, invoice, balance, identity, or transaction. Mark fictional payment data **SAMPLE — DO NOT PAY**.

Do not turn a qualified text claim into an absolute visual. A supply chart must not imply [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) growth. A [decentralization](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#decentralization) map must not imply equal influence. A wallet image must not imply bitcoin is stored inside the device.
