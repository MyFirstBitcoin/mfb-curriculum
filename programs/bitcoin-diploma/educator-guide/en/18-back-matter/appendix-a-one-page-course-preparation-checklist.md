# Appendix A: one-page course preparation checklist

### Curriculum

* [ ] I can explain the four-module narrative and learner promises.
* [ ] I reviewed every required assessed competency, its competency criteria, and its evidence type.
* [ ] I know that all four modules are required Bitcoin Diploma learning, how I may adapt **Module 3** and curate **Module 4**, and what belongs to [Advanced Learning](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/advanced-modules/).
* [ ] I reviewed the [Activity Library](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/activities/en/) where useful and selected activities for a clear competency rather than entertainment alone.
* [ ] I prepared a review and bridge for every module.

### Learners and access

* [ ] I know the cohort's language, literacy, numeracy, and device assumptions.
* [ ] Accessibility needs and accommodations are arranged.
* [ ] Learners can use invented scenarios instead of private disclosure.
* [ ] The schedule includes practice, assessment, feedback, and reassessment.

### Local context

* [ ] Local fiat-system and banking information is current and sourced.
* [ ] The official [inflation](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#inflation) measure and basket were checked.
* [ ] [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc) claims are current and design-specific.
* [ ] Local tools, fees, services, resources, and listings are dated.
* [ ] Legal, tax, privacy, and reporting context is framed without advice.
* [ ] Any local expert can explain the no-promotion and privacy boundaries.

### Safety and technology

* [ ] No task requires a real learner [seed phrase](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), key, credential, balance, or history.
* [ ] Training devices and wallets are prepared and reset.
* [ ] Sample payment information is marked **SAMPLE — DO NOT PAY**.
* [ ] Live demonstrations have been tested end to end.
* [ ] Stop conditions and escalation steps are clear.
* [ ] Every live or online dependency has a low-tech fallback.
* [ ] Device, power, internet, charging, and room arrangements are ready.

### Assessment

* [ ] Activities used for assessment give learners a clear and fair chance to show each competency.
* [ ] I have considered cohort size, available time, questions, troubleshooting, feedback, and reassessment.
* [ ] Learners will have supported practice before I make a final judgement.
* [ ] I know what to look for, how to ask a helpful follow-up question, and when to record **Yes** or **Not yet**.
* [ ] Online, low-tech, and accessibility routes are ready where needed.
* [ ] Any notes protect [privacy](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy) and are kept to the minimum useful information.
* [ ] I know how to offer participation or completion certification appropriately.
* [ ] I have confirmed each learner's result before using the [Certificate Tool](https://tools.myfirstbitcoin.org/certificate) and will check certificate details before distribution.
