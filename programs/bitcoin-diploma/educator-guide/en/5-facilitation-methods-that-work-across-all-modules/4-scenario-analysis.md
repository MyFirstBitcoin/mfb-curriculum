# 5.4 Scenario analysis

Use realistic invented characters and local conditions. Ask:

1. What is the person's goal?
1. Which facts are known?
1. Which facts must be verified?
1. Who controls the money, keys, records, and rules?
1. Which options exist?
1. What could fail?
1. What is the smallest safe next step?

Change one condition after the first answer. For example: the internet fails, the address changes, the recipient wants local [currency](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency), the fee rises, or the learner notices an urgent support message. This tests whether the learner can transfer their reasoning.
