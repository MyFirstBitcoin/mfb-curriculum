# 5.1 The learning rhythm

For most concepts, use this sequence:

1. **Purpose:** Tell learners why the idea matters.
1. **Activate:** Ask what they already think or have observed.
1. **Explain:** Give the shortest accurate explanation that unlocks the competency.
1. **Check:** Ask a quick question that reveals what the learner can explain or apply, not only recall.
1. **Apply:** Use a comparison, activity, scenario, demonstration, or decision.
1. **Produce:** Ask learners to explain or perform with less support.
1. **Debrief:** Name the evidence, limitation, safety point, and trade-off.
1. **Reflect:** End the module with the Student Guide's brief closing question. Give learners quiet thinking time; an answer is optional.
1. **Connect:** Show how the idea prepares for the next part of the Bitcoin Diploma.
