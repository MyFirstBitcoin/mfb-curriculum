# 1.6 Educator support

You do not need to prepare or teach in isolation. The [Educator Training Program](https://etp.myfirstbitcoin.org/) supports prospective and active educators in developing their teaching and assessment practice and connecting with the wider education community.

Use local expert support when a module depends on knowledge you do not hold, especially current banking rules, legal or tax context, accessibility, translation, local payment tools, merchant operations, [community custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#community-custody), or safety conditions. An expert should support the learning competency, not use the class to promote a product, service, political position, or investment choice.
