# 7.15 Why people look for alternatives

Compare cash with locally relevant alternatives through the same criteria. Include benefits and risks for precious metals, real estate, stocks, education, land, tools, foreign [currency](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency), or another appropriate example.

Do not recommend an [asset](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset). Do not imply that any option guarantees [purchasing power](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power). Ask:

* Which money function is the person trying to improve?
* What [opportunity cost](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#opportunity-cost) appears?
* What new storage, access, [liquidity](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liquidity), knowledge, legal, or [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) risk appears?

This section prepares learners to evaluate Bitcoin by the same standard rather than treating it as exempt from trade-offs.
