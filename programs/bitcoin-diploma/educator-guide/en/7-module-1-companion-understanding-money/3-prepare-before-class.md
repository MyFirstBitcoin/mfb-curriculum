# 7.3 Prepare before class

* Read the **Module 1** Student Guide sections and identify which definitions need local-language support.
* Prepare the five opening questions for cohort discussion.
* Select one money-properties activity and one modern-money activity; browse the [Activity Library](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/activities/en/) for options to adapt.
* Prepare locally familiar examples of a [medium of exchange](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#medium-of-exchange), [unit of account](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#unit-of-account), and [store of value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#store-of-value).
* Use local examples only to illustrate the shared, neutral concepts; verify changing local facts, avoid recommendations, and preserve the same competency and evidence standard.
* Review the [currencies](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) and payment methods learners encounter locally.
* Be ready to facilitate the local fiat-system scenario. Identify the relevant central bank, commercial banks, payment providers, government roles, and settlement arrangements.
* Verify current local banking rules relevant to the discussion, including reserve or [liquidity](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liquidity) arrangements, [capital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#capital) supervision, deposit protection, withdrawal rules, account-access rules, and consumer protection. Explain only what you have verified and do not provide legal or financial advice.
* Locate the official [inflation](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#inflation) measure, current publication date, basket description, and methodology. Prepare one local item with a dated [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) comparison.
* Check whether a local [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc) exists, is proposed, or is only hypothetical. Do not teach a generic [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc) feature as a confirmed local fact.
* Prepare an offline role map, balance-sheet sketch, price-basket example, and activity fallback.
