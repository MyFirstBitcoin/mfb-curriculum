# 7.2 Assessed competencies and competency criteria

Record each assessed competency. Decimal `C` IDs are competency criteria and rubric look-fors, not separate pass/fail rows.


| Assessed competency | Learner must be able to | Competency criteria to cover | Useful educator evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | Explain why people use money, compare two forms, and explain one modern-money trade-off. | C1.1 Money's purpose, barter limits, functions, and properties. C1.2 Comparison using relevant characteristics. C1.3 Local, observed, or realistic trade-off, such as inflation, banking, or CBDC, without personal disclosure. | Own-words explanation, comparison, and scenario response. |


Use guided discussions to develop and notice the formative competency `F1` across the course. Do not infer it from attendance or confidence; notice clear explanation, relevant evidence, respectful listening, and basic reasoning. `F1` is not a certification gate.
