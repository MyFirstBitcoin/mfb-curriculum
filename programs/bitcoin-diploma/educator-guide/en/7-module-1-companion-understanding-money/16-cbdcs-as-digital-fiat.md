# 7.16 CBDCs as digital fiat

Use five questions:

1. Who issues it?
1. Who can change its supply rules?
1. Who controls access or permissions?
1. What transaction or identity information is visible to whom?
1. Can users independently verify the system's rules and balances?

Explain that a [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc) is a central-bank [liability](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liability) in digital form. “Digital” does not automatically mean decentralized, scarce, private, permissionless, or openly verifiable.

[CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc) designs differ. Do not claim that every [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc) has or lacks a feature. Separate confirmed local design, published proposal, commentary, and speculation.
