# 7.20 Review and bridge to Module 2

Ask each learner to complete:

> Money can break or become less useful when ________, because ________. One example is ________. The relevant trade-off is ________.

Summarize that people may seek different monetary arrangements when they want different trade-offs around supply, access, control, settlement, [privacy](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy), and trust.

Then use the **Module 1** Student Guide closing question. Keep the response private or optional, following the light module-closing reflection guidance above.
