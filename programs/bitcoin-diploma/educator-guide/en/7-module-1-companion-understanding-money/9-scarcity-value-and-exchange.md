# 7.9 Scarcity, value, and exchange

Use one limited local resource to distinguish four ideas:

* **[Scarcity](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#scarcity):** not every desired use can be satisfied.
* **[Opportunity cost](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#opportunity-cost):** choosing one option gives up the best available alternative.
* **[Time preference](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#time-preference):** circumstances affect how strongly a person prefers [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) now rather than later.
* **Trade-off:** a choice gains one benefit while accepting a cost or giving up another.

Ask learners to explain why rarity alone does not create [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value). A rare item with no use or demand may remain unwanted.

Avoid moral judgments about [time preference](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#time-preference). A person meeting an urgent need is not failing a lesson about saving.
