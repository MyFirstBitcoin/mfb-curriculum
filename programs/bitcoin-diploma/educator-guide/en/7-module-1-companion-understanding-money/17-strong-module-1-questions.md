# 7.17 Strong Module 1 questions

* Why did money come into existence?
* Can something perform one money function well and another poorly?
* Which monetary property matters most for this particular purpose?
* Does [scarcity](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#scarcity) alone create [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value)?
* Which people, records, and rules make an ordinary local payment work?
* What does a bank owe a depositor?
* How are [liquidity](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liquidity) and [solvency](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#solvency) different?
* Why might personal [purchasing-power](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power) experience differ from an official measure?
* Which evidence would support a claim about local [inflation](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#inflation)?
* How is a [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc) similar to and different from [bank deposits](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bank-deposit), cash, and Bitcoin?
* Why might someone seek an alternative to cash, and which new risk appears?
