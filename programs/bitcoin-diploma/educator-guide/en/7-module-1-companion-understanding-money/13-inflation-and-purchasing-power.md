# 7.13 Inflation and purchasing power

Teach [price inflation](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#inflation) as a sustained rise in the general [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) level. One product becoming more expensive is not, by itself, proof of general [inflation](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#inflation).

Ask learners to identify possible causes of a [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) change:

* money and credit conditions
* supply disruption
* changes in demand
* [currency](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) weakness
* taxes or regulation
* changes in wages, transport, energy, or production costs; and
* local shortage or quality change.

Avoid assigning every [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) change to one factor.

#### Activity: purchasing-power investigation

**Before class:** Choose one familiar item and find reliable records of its current and historical [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) if possible. Locate the official [inflation](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#inflation) measure and its basket or methodology. You can use the [Bitcoin Diploma AI assistant](https://ai.myfirstbitcoin.org) to develop questions about the methodology, then check the explanations and figures against the original statistical publication.

**Learner task:** Compare the item's [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) across time, ask whether income changed similarly, and name other possible causes. Then compare the item with the official measure.

**Discussion:**

* Which goods and services are included in the official basket?
* How are weights chosen?
* Has the basket or method changed, and is the reason documented?
* Why can a learner's experience differ from the average?
* What evidence would be needed before alleging error or manipulation?

Prepare for this discussion. Review the local official measure well enough to explain what it does and does not claim. A basket change is not automatically manipulation, but it can affect comparison and should be transparent.
