# 7.7 Functions and properties of money

Teach the difference explicitly:

* A **function** describes what money does: [medium of exchange](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#medium-of-exchange), [unit of account](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#unit-of-account), or [store of value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#store-of-value).
* A **property** describes a quality that can make it useful: [durability](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#durability), [portability](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#portability), [divisibility](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#divisibility), [fungibility](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fungibility), [scarcity](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#scarcity), [acceptability](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#acceptability), or [verifiability](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#verifiability).

Use different purposes. Ask which properties matter for:

* buying food today
* saving for one year
* paying someone far away
* keeping an emergency reserve; and
* recording business [prices](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price).

Then ask whether the same form of money performs equally well for every purpose and in every place.

#### Activity: money-properties comparison

**Purpose:** Prepare the criteria learners will reuse for Bitcoin in **Module 2**.

**Setup:** Select three or four familiar forms, such as local cash, a bank balance, a useful commodity, a precious metal, or another locally relevant [asset](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset). Do not pre-label a winner.

**Task:** Teams compare each form against the seven properties. Every judgment must include a reason, condition, or example.

**Debrief:** Ask which property changed most when the purpose changed. Preserve the comparison for **Module 2**.

**Watch for:** Treating a property as absolute. Cash may be portable across a neighborhood but difficult to transfer internationally. A bank balance may be accepted through local payment rails but dependent on account access.
