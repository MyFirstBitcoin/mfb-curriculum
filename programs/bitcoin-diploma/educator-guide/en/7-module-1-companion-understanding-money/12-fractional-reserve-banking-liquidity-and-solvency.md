# 7.12 Fractional-reserve banking, liquidity, and solvency

Draw a simplified balance sheet:

* the customer deposit is a [liability](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liability) the bank owes
* a bank loan is an [asset](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) to the bank and a [liability](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liability) to the borrower
* approving a loan commonly creates a matching deposit
* banks must manage [liquidity](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liquidity), [capital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#capital), funding, [credit risk](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#credit-risk), regulation, and settlement; and
* exact rules vary by jurisdiction.


<!-- micrographic: bank-loan-deposit-01 -->


Do not use a repeated-physical-deposit “money multiplier” story as if it describes every modern banking system mechanically.

Distinguish:

* **[Liquidity risk](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liquidity-risk):** the bank cannot meet payments or withdrawals when due even if its [assets](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) may have long-term [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value).
* **[Solvency risk](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#solvency-risk):** the bank's [assets](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) are insufficient to cover its obligations.


<!-- micrographic: liquidity-vs-solvency-01 -->


Ask:

> Can an institution be solvent but temporarily illiquid? Can it have cash today but still be insolvent? What is different?

#### Local banking discussion

Prepare to explain relevant local rules and institutions, such as:

* who supervises commercial banks
* whether a reserve requirement exists and what it means
* [liquidity](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liquidity) and [capital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#capital) requirements at a beginner level
* [deposit-insurance](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#deposit-insurance) eligibility and limits
* withdrawal or transfer limits
* the central bank's lender-of-last-resort role
* account-access and identity rules; and
* consumer complaint or resolution pathways.

Use current authoritative sources. State the date checked. If you are unsure, say what must be verified rather than improvising a legal or regulatory answer.

Do not imply that every bank is fraudulent, every bank is about to fail, or regulation removes all risk.
