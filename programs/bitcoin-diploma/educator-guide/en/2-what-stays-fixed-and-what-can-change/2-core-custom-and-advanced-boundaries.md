# 2.2 Four-module Bitcoin Diploma and Advanced Learning boundaries

**The Bitcoin Diploma** follows the four-stage learning arc **Problem → Solution → Action → Connect**. **Modules 1–2** develop the Problem and Solution through explanation, comparison, and reasoning. **Module 3** develops safe Action. **Module 4** is the educator-curated Connect stage, where learners link the shared learning to a local need or opportunity, realistic alternative, safe performance, and responsible next step.

**Module 4** is required Bitcoin Diploma learning, curated by educators as the local subject-matter experts. The scenario or tool may change, but every learner seeking certification must meet `C7` through a local activity that covers the purpose of `C7.1` and `C7.2`.

[Advanced Learning](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/advanced-modules/) is optional follow-on learning beyond the Bitcoin Diploma. Detailed [mining](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work), transaction mechanics, Lightning operations, [multisignature](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#multisignature) design, institutional [custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), organizational treasury, regulation, accounting, tax, compliance, investment analysis, policy, and deep technical topics do not belong in the required beginner experience.

When an interesting question would pull the group into advanced material, acknowledge it and protect the course sequence. A useful response is:

> That is an important question. It needs more foundation than we have built so far, so I am placing it in our follow-up list. Let us first complete the idea that will help us answer it well.
