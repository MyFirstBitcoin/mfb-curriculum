# 8.8 Digital signatures

At beginner level, teach:

* the [private key](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) authorizes
* the signature can be verified without revealing the [private key](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key)
* the signature does not encrypt the transaction; and
* control of the [private key](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) creates both authority and responsibility.

Do not demonstrate or calculate [cryptography](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cryptography). The practical consequences belong in **Module 3**.
