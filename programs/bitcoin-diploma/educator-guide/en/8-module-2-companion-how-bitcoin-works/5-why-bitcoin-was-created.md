# 8.5 Why Bitcoin was created

Start with the goal: direct online payments without a central financial [intermediary](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#intermediary). Then ask what makes that difficult.

A central digital-payment system can keep one authoritative [ledger](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger) and reject a second spend. A decentralized system needs independent participants to agree on a valid history without trusting one database owner.


<!-- micrographic: central-ledger-vs-bitcoin-01 -->
[With a payment company, one organization keeps the ledger and approves each transfer. With Bitcoin, the sender signs a transaction, independent nodes check it and miners add it to one shared ledger, so the receiver depends less on a central authority.](/micrographics/central-ledger-vs-bitcoin-01/en.md)


Ask:

> If I can copy a digital file perfectly, what prevents me from sending the same digital unit to two people?

Clarify that the problem is not copying a picture of a coin. It is deciding which transfer of a scarce digital unit is valid.
