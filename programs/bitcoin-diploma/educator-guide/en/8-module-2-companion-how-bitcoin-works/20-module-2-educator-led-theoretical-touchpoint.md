# 8.20 Module 2 educator-led theoretical touchpoint

Use a short task that asks the learner to connect at least three elements. It supplies educator-led theoretical evidence. For example:

* explain the [double-spend problem](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#double-spending)
* distinguish the roles of a [miner](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) and [node](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node)
* explain how [node](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) verification supports credible supply; and
* apply monetary properties to one benefit and one trade-off.

For `C3`, use a short guided scenario that asks who controls issuance, what can be verified, which counterparty risk exists, and why [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) speculation is outside the Bitcoin Diploma's responsible-learning purpose.

Listen for the learner's own words. A fluent repetition of “21 million” or “decentralized” is not enough without the mechanism or qualification.
