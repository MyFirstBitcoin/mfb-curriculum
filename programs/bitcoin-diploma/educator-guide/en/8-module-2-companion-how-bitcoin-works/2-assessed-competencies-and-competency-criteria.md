# 8.2 Assessed competencies and competency criteria

Record one result for each assessed competency. Decimal `C` IDs are competency criteria and rubric look-fors, not separate pass/fail rows.


| Assessed competency | Learner must be able to | Competency criteria to cover | Useful educator evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C2 | Explain Bitcoin foundations and rules at a beginner level. | C2.1 Digital copying, double spending, and shared verification. C2.2 Roles, proof of work, supply, halving, and sats. C2.3 Key money properties, “don't trust, verify,” and how a Bitcoin property could help or limit its use as money. | Connected explanation, explanation of participant roles, and an example of how a Bitcoin property could help or limit its use as money. |
| C3 | Compare Bitcoin with one other digital asset using guided criteria. | One comparison using control, issuance, verification, custody, or counterparty risk; state a trade-off and distinguish learning from speculation or promotion. | Individual Bitcoin-only comparison without token promotion. |


Use guided discussions to develop and notice the formative competency `F1` across the course. Do not infer it from attendance or confidence; listen for clear explanation, relevant evidence, respectful listening, and basic reasoning. `F1` is not a certification gate.
