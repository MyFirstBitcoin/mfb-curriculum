# 8.16 Responsible learning versus speculation

Explain the boundary:

* Education asks how the system works, what can be verified, what risks exist, and what the learner can safely demonstrate.
* Responsible use begins with purpose, [custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), verification, small tests, and informed choice.
* Trading attempts to profit from [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) changes and may involve leverage, rapid decisions, platform risk, and loss.
* Token promotion creates conflicts of interest and may reward persuasion over clear reasoning.
* Speculation can cause learners to confuse [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) movement with technical or social [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value).

The Bitcoin Diploma does not teach or assess [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) prediction, portfolio construction, trading, investment returns, or token selection.
