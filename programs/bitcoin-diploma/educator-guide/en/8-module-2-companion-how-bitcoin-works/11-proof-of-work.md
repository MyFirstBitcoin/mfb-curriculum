# 8.11 Proof of work

Teach the relationship, not the mathematics:

* [miners](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) make many attempts to find a result below a network [target](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#target)
* finding a result requires real computation and energy
* [nodes](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) check the result quickly
* [proof of work](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work) helps order transactions and makes history costly to rewrite; and
* [difficulty](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#difficulty) measures the work required to satisfy Bitcoin's proof-of-work target; the target adjusts so blocks arrive about every ten minutes on average, not exactly every ten minutes.

Avoid saying [miners](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) solve useful outside equations. Avoid claiming the energy cost is automatically justified or automatically wasteful.

Use claim, evidence, and trade-off:

* What energy is used?
* Where does it come from?
* What security function does it perform?
* Which alternative system is being compared?
* Which facts are current and need dated evidence?

Detailed energy statistics and [mining](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work) economics are not required Bitcoin Diploma content. Use them only when the educator has verified current sources and the discussion supports a competency.
