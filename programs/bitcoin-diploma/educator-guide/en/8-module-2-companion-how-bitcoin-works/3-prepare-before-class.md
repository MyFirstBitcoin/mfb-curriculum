# 8.3 Prepare before class

* Reuse the monetary-properties comparison from **Module 1**.
* Prepare a plain-language double-spend scenario.
* Prepare role cards for user, [node](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node), [miner](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner), developer, and optional service provider.
* Prepare a [node](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) rule card that includes valid signature, no double spend, valid [proof of work](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work), and allowed issuance.
* Prepare a [halving](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#halving) or issuance visual without a [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) chart.
* Prepare [sat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi) conversion examples that do not require current market [prices](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price).
* Select a historical transaction or dated explorer screenshot if showing public data.
* Review the FAQ and misconception tables below.
* Prepare a brief guided comparison for Bitcoin, a generic stablecoin model, and another issuer-controlled digital [asset](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset). Do not browse or promote particular tokens during class.
* Decide which technical questions belong on the Advanced list.
