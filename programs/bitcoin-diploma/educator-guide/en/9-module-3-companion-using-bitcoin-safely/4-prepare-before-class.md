# 9.5 Prepare before class

* Read the **Module 3** Student Guide and practise every demonstration.
* Select the exact prepared wallet, simulation, test environment, or printed sequence.
* Verify the tool's source, version, network, [custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) model, recovery process, and reset process.
* Decide whether learners will use a simulation, test environment, empty training wallet, physical model, or an educator-approved low-stakes live setting. For any live setting, prepare the risk explanation, voluntary-choice process, and no-funds equivalent.
* Prepare sample on-chain addresses, [Lightning invoices](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#lightning-invoice), *[txid](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction-id)*s, QR codes, and screenshots marked **SAMPLE — DO NOT PAY**.
* Prepare public/secret sorting cards.
* Prepare a deliberately public address or historical transaction for the address-history activity. Never use learner-linked data.
* Prepare a fake [seed phrase](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) or disposable empty training wallet for recovery practice.
* Prepare scam and clipboard-replacement scenarios.
* Review the [Activity Library](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/activities/en/) for suitable practice ideas, adapting any wallet or payment activity to the safety and assessment conditions here.
* Prepare an acquisition-pathway comparison relevant to the location without naming a preferred provider.
* Define the helpful steps, support, safety boundaries, reset method, and any optional notes for each activity used for assessment.
* Arrange a private space where learner screens and evidence cannot be observed unnecessarily.
* Ensure every live task has a printed or no-funds equivalent.
