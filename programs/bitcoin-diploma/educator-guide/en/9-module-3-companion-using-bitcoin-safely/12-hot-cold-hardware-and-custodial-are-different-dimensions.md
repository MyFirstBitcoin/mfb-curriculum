# 9.13 Hot, cold, hardware, and custodial are different dimensions

Avoid a common category error:

* **Custodial / self-custodial** describes who controls the keys.
* **Hot / cold** describes whether signing keys are exposed to an internet-connected general-purpose environment.
* [**Hardware wallet**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#hardware-wallet) describes a dedicated device design.
* **On-chain / Lightning** describes a payment path, not [custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) by itself.

A Lightning wallet can be custodial or self-custodial. A [hardware wallet](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#hardware-wallet) still needs a recovery process. A cold arrangement can be badly designed. A [hot wallet](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#hot-wallet) can be appropriate for small everyday use.
