# 9.4 Time and educator capacity

This module needs more individual observation than **Modules 1 and 2**. A two-hour session may introduce and practise the content, but it may not provide enough time to assess every learner reliably.

Before class, estimate:

* the number of individual tasks requiring observation
* the realistic time per learner
* device-sharing delays
* accessibility and language support
* the number of educators or assessors; and
* time for feedback and reassessment.

Use smaller stations, another educator, or additional sessions when helpful. Group practice is valuable, but give each learner an individual opportunity to show the competency. A follow-up appointment or class is acceptable when a learner needs more time.
