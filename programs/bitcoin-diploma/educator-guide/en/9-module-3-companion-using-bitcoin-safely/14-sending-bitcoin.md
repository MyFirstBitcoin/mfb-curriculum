# 9.15 Sending bitcoin

Use a pause-and-check sequence:

1. Confirm recipient and purpose through a trusted channel.
1. Request fresh payment information when appropriate.
1. Confirm on-chain or Lightning and the correct network.
1. Scan or enter the destination.
1. Compare it with the trusted receiving display after entry.
1. Confirm the amount and unit.
1. Review the fee, timing, expiry, and final summary as relevant.
1. Use a small test when the process or amount is unfamiliar.
1. Approve only after all information matches.
1. Verify the result in the appropriate wallet or transaction view.

Teach learners to stop if the recipient changes the destination unexpectedly, discourages verification, creates urgency, or asks for recovery information.

#### Activity: clipboard substitution

Give learners an intended address card and a pasted-address screen with a mismatch. The correct outcome is to detect the mismatch and stop, not to complete the transaction.

Change the scenario:

* Only the middle differs.
* A support message says the change is normal.
* The recipient claims the payment is urgent.
* The QR code and written address disagree.

Ask what independent channel can verify the destination.
