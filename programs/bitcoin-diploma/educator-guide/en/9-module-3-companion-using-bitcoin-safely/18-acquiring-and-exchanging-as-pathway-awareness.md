# 9.3 Assessed competencies and competency criteria

Record one result for each assessed competency. Decimal `C` IDs are competency criteria and rubric look-fors, not separate pass/fail rows.


| Assessed competency | Learner must be able to | Competency criteria to cover | Evidence emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C4 | Explain wallets, custody, and a safe response to risk. | C4.1 Wallets, addresses, keys, seed phrases, public versus secret information, and custody choices. C4.2 Hardware-wallet and hot-wallet overview. C4.3 Recognize a risk and know when to stop, verify, or seek help. | Individual explanation and safety scenario. |
| C5 | Compare common ways to pay with or access bitcoin in guided scenarios. | C5.1 On-chain and Lightning distinctions and pathway selection. C5.2 Acquisition or exchange pathways and relevant risks. | Educator-led comparison scenario. |
| C6 | Safely complete supported first access and demonstrate recovery readiness. | C6.1 Navigate a prepared wallet, complete or simulate receive or send and supported first access or receipt, and check destination, amount, network, and status. Use guided discussions to develop and notice the formative competency `F1` through clear explanation, relevant evidence, respectful listening, and basic reasoning. `F1` is not a certification gate.
