# 9.8 Addresses and payment requests

Teach an on-chain [Bitcoin address](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-address) as public receiving information generated from wallet keys. It is not a password, account login, or proof that the person requesting payment is trustworthy.

Teach these beginner habits:

* obtain the address from the intended receiving wallet
* confirm Bitcoin and the correct network
* distinguish an on-chain address from a [Lightning invoice](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#lightning-invoice) or address
* copy or scan rather than type manually
* compare after copying
* verify the full address on a trusted display for important payments
* use a fresh address for a new receipt when the wallet provides one; and
* use a small test when the amount or process is unfamiliar.

Do not tell learners that matching only the first and last characters is sufficient for every risk. It is a useful quick check, while full verification on a trusted display gives stronger assurance for important payments.

#### Activity: public or secret?

Prepare cards for:

* on-chain address
* address QR code
* [Lightning invoice](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#lightning-invoice)
* [_txid_](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction-id)
* [public key](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#public-key)
* [private key](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key)
* [seed phrase](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase)
* wallet password or PIN
* balance screenshot; and
* security or recovery plan.

Learners sort them into:

* public receiving or [ledger](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger) information
* secret spending or access information; and
* context-dependent information that may be public technically but private personally.

Debrief that “public on the protocol” does not mean “safe to associate with a person's identity.” A [_txid_](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction-id) or address can reveal activity and relationships.
