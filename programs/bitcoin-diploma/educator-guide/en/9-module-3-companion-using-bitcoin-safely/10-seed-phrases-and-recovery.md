# 9.11 Seed phrases and recovery

A [seed phrase](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), also called a [recovery phrase](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), is one recovery method used by many wallets. It is an ordered list of words that can recreate the keys for compatible wallet designs. It is not a universal feature of every wallet and is more powerful than an ordinary account password.

Teach the consequences:

* correct words in the correct order can restore control
* anyone who obtains the phrase may gain control
* losing it can remove the recovery path
* [wallet compatibility](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#wallet-compatibility) matters; and
* a seed that may have been exposed should be treated as compromised.

#### Never do this in class

* Do not ask a learner to read recovery words aloud.
* Do not walk around while learners record real words.
* Do not let learners photograph, message, email, print, cloud-store, or submit a phrase.
* Do not use one learner's real funded wallet for a public recovery demonstration.
* Do not enter a [hardware-wallet](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#hardware-wallet) phrase into a website or ordinary computer form.
* Do not retain training phrases that later protect learner funds.

#### Activity: recovery-readiness simulation

**Presentation:** Show a complete recovery check using prepared recovery words or a disposable empty training wallet. The words may restore that empty training wallet, but must not protect a personal or funded wallet. Explain compatibility, [privacy](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy), expected result, and reset.

**Practice:** Learners sequence prepared cards or restore a disposable training wallet in a private, controlled environment. They must keep the material out of view and identify unsafe requests.

**Production:** The learner explains or demonstrates how to:

1. use official instructions for the exact wallet.
1. confirm recovery compatibility.
1. work privately without cameras or observers.
1. use the trusted backup-check or recovery method.
1. verify the expected receiving information or training history.
1. clear the temporary device; and
1. return the fictional material securely or destroy it as instructed.

> **Callout – Treat an exposed seed phrase as compromised**
>
> If a real seed may have been exposed, stop using it as a safe backup, create a new trusted wallet with new recovery information, and move funds after carefully verifying the destination. The learner should seek help without revealing either phrase.


Inheritance, [multisignature](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#multisignature) design, advanced passphrases, and high-value recovery planning may require advanced security and professional legal advice. They are outside required Bitcoin Diploma performance.
