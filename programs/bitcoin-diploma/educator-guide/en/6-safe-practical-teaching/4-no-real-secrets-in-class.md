# 6.4 No real secrets in class

<!-- card: callout -->
> **No real secrets in class**
>
> Use prepared recovery words, clearly marked training material, or an empty disposable wallet for recovery practice. Never use a learner's personal or funded wallet, or create a process in which anyone can observe lasting recovery information.
>
> Do not ask learners to prove [self-custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#self-custody) by showing a [seed phrase](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase). Proof of competence is safe handling and explanation, not disclosure.
