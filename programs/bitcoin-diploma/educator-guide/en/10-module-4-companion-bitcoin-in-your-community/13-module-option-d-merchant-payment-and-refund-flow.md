# 10.13 Module option D: merchant payment and refund flow

**Purpose:** Show that merchant adoption requires operations beyond displaying a receive button.

Cover:

* display [currency](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) and exchange-rate timing
* on-chain, Lightning, or both
* [custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) and staff permissions
* receive-device security
* customer and merchant confirmation
* receipts and records
* refunds using a newly verified return request
* conversion or movement of received funds
* accounting and tax review
* internet or wallet failure; and
* support and escalation.


<!-- micrographic: merchant-payment-operations-01 -->


**Production:** The learner performs or directs a payment and refund scenario. They must authenticate the customer, request fresh return information, check the destination and amount, identify who pays any fee, and record the result without exposing unnecessary data.

**Debrief:** Why is sending a refund automatically to an assumed original address unsafe? Who becomes responsible when several staff share one wallet?
