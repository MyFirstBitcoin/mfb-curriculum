# 10.21 Review and course bridge

For review, each learner or team may present:

* local need
* current alternative
* Bitcoin path
* [custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) at each stage
* strongest benefit
* most serious risk
* main failure point
* locally verified fact
* evidence observed; and
* safe next step.

Ask another learner to pose one verification question and identify one trade-off.

Team presentation does not provide individual certification evidence. Each learner seeking certification must produce individually identifiable practical evidence against the `C7` rubric, including `C7.1` and `C7.2`, under direct observation by an educator.

Then use the **Module 4** Student Guide closing question. Keep the response private or optional, following the light module-closing reflection guidance above.

Close with:

> Bitcoin can function as money when people voluntarily use it to earn, save, receive, send, spend, [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price), or settle value. The Connect stage begins with a real need, compares a realistic alternative, makes [custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) and costs clear, respects local law and safety, and leaves people free to decide.
