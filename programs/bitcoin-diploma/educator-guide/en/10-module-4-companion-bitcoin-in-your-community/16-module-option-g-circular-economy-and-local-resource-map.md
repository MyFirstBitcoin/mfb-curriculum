# 10.16 Module option G: circular economy and local resource map

**Purpose:** Evaluate whether a voluntary local loop solves a real problem.

Map:

1. who can earn bitcoin.
1. where it can be used.
1. which merchants can pay suppliers or workers.
1. who must exchange to or from fiat.
1. who controls keys at each stage.
1. who provides education and technical support.
1. what happens when internet or electricity fails.
1. what [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price), [custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), fraud, and privacy risks participants carry; and
1. whether the loop depends on giveaways or one organizer.


<!-- micrographic: local-circular-economy-01 -->


For resource discovery, verify:

* when a listing was last checked
* whether the merchant or group still operates
* which payment method is accepted
* whether an alternative payment is available
* whether publication has consent; and
* whether the contact is offering education or promoting an investment or private exchange.

**Production:** The learner verifies one prepared resource, adds it to the map with a date and safety note, and explains how it supports or fails to support a local loop.

**Safety:** A map listing, meetup membership, or community reputation is not proof of trustworthiness. Do not publish home locations or private contacts.
