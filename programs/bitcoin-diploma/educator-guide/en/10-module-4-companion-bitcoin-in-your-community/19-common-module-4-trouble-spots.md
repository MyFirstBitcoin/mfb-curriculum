# 10.19 Common Module 4 trouble spots

* Starting with a fashionable tool instead of a need.
* Treating a named wallet, exchange, map, merchant, or community as an endorsement.
* Using outdated fees, versions, [custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) claims, legal information, or listings.
* Comparing only an advertised fee rather than total received and the complete path.
* Ignoring [custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) during conversion, funding, or withdrawal.
* Treating one successful payment as proof of security or usefulness.
* Assuming Lightning convenience means [self-custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#self-custody).
* Treating [KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#kyc) or [no-KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#no-kyc) as universally superior.
* Ignoring accounting, tax, employment, privacy, or consumer context.
* Pressuring merchants, workers, or learners to participate.
* Counting adoption or transaction volume without testing whether a real problem is solved.
* Building around one trusted organizer without naming the [custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) dependence.
* Having no offline, no-funds, refund, failure, or support process.
* Assuming that a local example applies globally.
