# 10.17 Community custody and shared funds

If one person or group controls bitcoin for others, they are acting as a [custodian](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custodian). Explain the added responsibilities:

* authorization rules
* separation of duties
* backup and succession
* records and transparency
* [privacy](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy) and access control
* dispute and loss handling
* legal status; and
* concentration of trust.

Do not turn [community-custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#community-custody) design into a beginner implementation exercise. Use a scenario and seek appropriate legal and security review.
