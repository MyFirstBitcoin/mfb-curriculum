# 13.6 Adapt an ordinary learning activity

You may change:

* names, places, objects, [currency](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) units, and local payment methods
* group size and roles
* oral, written, visual, or tactile response format
* live tool versus prepared screen
* timing and scaffolding; and
* the debrief example.

Preserve:

* the concept being learned
* the safety boundary
* the learner action
* the critical comparison or decision; and
* the connection to the module competency.
