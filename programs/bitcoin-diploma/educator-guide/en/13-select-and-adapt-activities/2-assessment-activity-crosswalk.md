# 13.2 Assessment activity crosswalk

Use the linked activities below as possible learning or individual-evidence contexts for the stated competency. An activity remains formative unless it gives the educator a fair opportunity to notice the relevant learner response or action. Adapt the activity when needed and record the result in the Assessment Grid.


| Assessment Grid competency | Protocol task | Activities that can support the task | Required addition or limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 Money and modern-money trade-offs | T1 | Iterative Barter Game, Why Do We Need Money?, Barter Game | Add an individual comparison and a verified local or realistic modern-money scenario; activity participation alone does not cover all three look-fors. |
| C2 Bitcoin foundations and rules | T2 | Consensus, Transactions in Action, Is Bitcoin Sound Money?, Verification | Add an individual copying/verification explanation, a beginner-level explanation of Bitcoin's shared rules and participant roles, and an explanation of how a Bitcoin property could help or limit its use as money. |
| C3 Guided digital-asset comparison | T2 | No current activity is suitable as assessment evidence. | Use the protocol's guided, non-promotional comparison and distinguish learning or responsible use from trading, promotion, or speculation. |
| C4 Wallets, custody, and safety | T3 | Evaluate Bitcoin Wallets, Creating Your First Bitcoin Wallet | Require individual custody-and-secrets explanation, wallet-type distinction, and safe response to a scam or error scenario. |
| C5 Payment and access pathways | T3 | Exploring the Mempool, Lightning Relay Race | Require an individual on-chain and Lightning comparison plus a non-recommendatory pathway decision. The Relay Race needs its safety changes before use. |
| C6 Safe access and recovery readiness | P6a and P6b | Lightning Relay Race, Creating Your First Bitcoin Wallet | Observe a prepared first-access/receipt task and a separate recovery-readiness simulation before recording one result. Never collect, view, or record a learner's real secret or balance. |
| C7 Connect: local Bitcoin-as-money task | P7 | No current activity is suitable as assessment evidence. | Select and approve a local Connect task under the protocol before delivery. It must include direct observation, safe verification, a realistic alternative, and individual explanation. |
