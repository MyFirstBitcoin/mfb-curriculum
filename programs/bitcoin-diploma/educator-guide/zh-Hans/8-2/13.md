# 8.13 聪与单位偏见

每位学习者都应了解：

> 1 比特币 = 100,000,000 [聪](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi)

请使用几个换算示例：

* 0.1 比特币 = 10,000,000 [聪](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi)
* 0.01 比特币 = 1,000,000 [聪](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi)
* 0.001 比特币 = 100,000 [聪](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi)；以及
* 1 [聪](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi) = 0.00000001 比特币。


<!-- micrographic: bitcoin-and-sats-01 -->


请提问：为什么把一个比特币分成更小的单位并不会产生更多的比特币？可以将其与把一份已计量的物品切分成更小单位作比较，但不要声称这种实物类比能够解释该协议。

单位[偏差](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bias)是一种错误印象，即认为看起来更便宜的整单位自然更具[价值](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value)。请勿以当前代币[价格](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price)作为教学切入点。
