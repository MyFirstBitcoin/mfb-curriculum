# 8.7 讲解综合回应

请使用一个由五个部分组成的框架图：

1. 钱包使用[私钥](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key)，通过[数字签名](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-signature)来授权一笔交易。
1. 该交易通过[广播](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#broadcast)传播至[点对点网络](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#peer-to-peer-network)。
1. [矿工](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner)收集交易，并利用[工作量证明](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work)来提出区块。
1. [节点](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node)独立验证交易、区块、[工作量证明](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work)以及发行规则。
1. 参与者最终会在拥有最多[累计工作量](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#accumulated-work)的有效历史记录上达成一致。

没有任何单一部分能够独自造就比特币可信的[稀缺性](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#scarcity)。请避免说「[区块链](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#blockchain)能解决一切」。
