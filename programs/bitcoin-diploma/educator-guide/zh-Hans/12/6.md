# 12.6 需在本地核查的疑难术语

请特别注意：

* 货币与[通货](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency)
* [资产](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset)、债权、[负债](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liability)、[流动性](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liquidity)和[偿付能力](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#solvency)
* [通货膨胀](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#inflation)与[购买力](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power)
* [去中心化](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#decentralization)与点对点
* [工作量证明](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work)与验证
* 钱包、[托管](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody)和[自我托管](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#self-custody)
* [私钥](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key)、[助记词](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase)和恢复
* 地址、发票和付款请求
* [确认](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#confirmation)与最终性
* 链上与闪电网络
* [隐私](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy)与匿名
* [KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#kyc)与[无KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#no-kyc)；以及
* 权衡、风险和不确定性。

请使用词汇表中的定义。

My First Bitcoin更倾向于线下面授的比特币文凭课程，因为与教育者的直接接触以及更多开展实践活动的机会，能让学习更具吸引力、更加自然。这是一种偏好，而非硬性要求。线上授课对许多人而言可能更经济、更易扩展、也更容易实现，因此教育者应根据实际情况妥善支持这两种方式。
