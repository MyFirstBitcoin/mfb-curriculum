# 2.4 Digitalna oskudnost i nezavisno proverljiva ponuda

Digitalne informacije je obično lako kopirati.


<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Digitalna oskudica**
>
> Digitalna [oskudica](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#scarcity) znači da sistem može da razlikuje važeću jedinicu od nevažeće kopije i da spreči da ista jedinica bude potrošena dva puta.


Bitcoin stvara digitalnu [oskudicu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#scarcity) kombinovanjem potpisa, javne [transakcione](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) istorije, [dokaza o radu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work) i nezavisne, na pravila usmerene [provere](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#verification). bitcoin se može preneti, ali mreža odbija pokušaj da se isti bitcoin ponovo potroši.

[Oskudica](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#scarcity) se može nezavisno proveriti kada ljudi mogu sami da provere javna pravila i kada nijedan pojedinačni [izdavalac](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) ne može neprimetno da promeni ponudu za sve. Zato se ograničenje ponude Bitcoina mora proučavati zajedno sa [decentralizacijom](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#decentralization) i [čvorskom](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) [proverom](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#verification).
