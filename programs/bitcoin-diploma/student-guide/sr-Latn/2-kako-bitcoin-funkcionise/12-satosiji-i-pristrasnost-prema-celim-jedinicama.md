# 2.12 Satošiji i pristrasnost prema celim jedinicama

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/98c8cef9bc6e6d0955458f6fb4a71f1de5ed5225-1536x1024.svg)



Jedan bitcoin (1 BTC) može se podeliti na **100.000.000 [satošija](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi)**. Jedan [satoši](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), često skraćeno nazvan [**sat**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), najmanja je jedinica zabeležena na osnovnom sloju Bitcoin mreže.

**Ne morate da kupite ili koristite ceo bitcoin.** Bitcoin softver može da prikaže iznos u bitcoinima ili [satovima](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), baš kao što [valuta](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) može da koristi glavnu jedinicu i manje jedinice. Mnogi ljudi koriste [satove](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi) za svakodnevne iznose jer je cele brojeve lakše čitati. I zahtevi za plaćanje na lancu i zahtevi za plaćanje putem Lightning mreže mogu se prikazati u [satovima](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi).

**Jedinična [pristrasnost](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bias)** je pogrešan utisak da niža [cena](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) po celoj jedinici čini [imovinu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) jeftinijom ili pristupačnijom. Broj jedinica je stvar izbora pri projektovanju. Posedovanje 100.000 [satova](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi) i posedovanje 0,001 BTC predstavljaju isti iznos.

Kada upoređujete [cene](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price), usredsredite se na udeo u ukupnoj ponudi, iznos [vrednosti](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) koji je u pitanju i rizike. Nemojte pretpostavljati da [imovina](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) sa mnogo jedinica koje deluju jeftino nudi bolju [vrednost](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) od [imovine](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) podeljene na manje jedinice.
