# 2.12 聪与单位偏差

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/98c8cef9bc6e6d0955458f6fb4a71f1de5ed5225-1536x1024.svg)



一个比特币（1 BTC）可以分割为**100,000,000[聪（satoshi）](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi)**。[聪](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi)常简称为[**sat**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi)，是比特币基础层上记录的最小单位。

**您无需购买或使用一整个比特币。**比特币软件可以用比特币或[聪](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi)来显示金额，就像一种[货币](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency)可以使用一个主单位和若干更小的单位。许多人在日常金额中使用[聪](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi)，因为整数更易于阅读。链上支付请求和闪电网络支付请求都可以用[聪](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi)来显示。

**单位[偏差](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bias)**是一种错误的感觉，即认为每个完整单位的[价格](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price)较低，会让一项[资产](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset)更便宜或更容易获得。单位的数量只是一种设计选择。拥有100,000[聪](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi)与拥有0.001 BTC描述的是同一金额。

在比较[价格](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price)时，应关注其占总供应量的份额、所涉及的[价值](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value)大小以及风险。不要认为一项[资产](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset)拥有许多看似便宜的单位，就能提供更好的[价值](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value)，优于另一项被划分为更小单位的[资产](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset)。
