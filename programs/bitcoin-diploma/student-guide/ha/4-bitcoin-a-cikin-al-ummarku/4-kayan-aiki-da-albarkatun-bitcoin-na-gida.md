# 4.4 Kayan aiki da albarkatun Bitcoin na gida

<!-- micrographic: tool-verification-checklist-01 -->
[Abubuwa goma da za ku tabbatar kafin amfani da kayan aikin Bitcoin na gida: manufa, riƙon dukiya, tushe, ci gaban aikin, farfadowa, sirri, kuɗin amfani, dacewa, taimako da doka, kowanne da tambayarsa. Ku rubuta ranar da kuka bincika.](/micrographics/tool-verification-checklist-01/ha.md)


Masu amfani da Bitcoin a yankinku na iya amfani da walat, dandamalin musaya, kasuwannin ciniki kai tsaye tsakanin mutane, kayan aikin 'yan kasuwa, taswirori, tarurruka, na'urori, da albarkatun ilimi.


<!-- card: light -->
> **Ku binciki misalin yankinku**
>
> Kayan aiki ko sabis na yankinku misali ne na wani takamaiman lokaci don koyo, ba tabbacin tsaro, samuwa, ƙarancin kuɗin amfani, ko dacewa ba. Ku tabbatar da tushensa, [riƙon kadarori](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) da tsarinsa, hanyar dawo da damar shiga, [sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy), kuɗin amfani, dacewa da sauran na'urori da manhajoji, taimakon da ake bayarwa, da halin da yake ciki a yankinku a yanzu kafin amfani da shi a zahiri.



<!-- micrographic: local-tool-record-01 -->
[Takardar bayanan kayan aiki na gida wadda ba a cika ba: kayan aiki da sigarsa, ranar da aka bincika, tsarin riƙon kadarori, babban amfani, babbar fa'ida da haɗari, hanyar maidowa da madadi.](/micrographics/local-tool-record-01/ha.md)


Ku yi amfani da wannan kwatancin kafin ku zaɓi kayan aiki:

Kayan aikin da aka ambata da sunayensu kuma aka yi amfani da su a aji misalai ne na yankinku, ba shawarwarin amincewa daga My First Bitcoin ba. Ku rubuta ranar da aka binciki kayan aiki. Kayan aikin da ya dace a bara na iya samun masu shi, kuɗin amfani, fasaloli, ko haɗurra daban a yau.
