# 4.8 Hanyoyin shiga da fita a cikin al’umma

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/16d4da9fcdbc76a89acbef6bf760d3199ea7a843-1536x1024.svg)

Hanyar [**shiga**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#on-ramp) ita ce hanyar sauya kuɗin gida, aiki, ko wani [kadara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) zuwa bitcoin. Hanyar [**fita**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#off-ramp) ita ce hanyar sauya bitcoin zuwa kuɗin gida, kayayyaki, ko ayyuka. Hanya ɗaya na iya yin waɗannan ayyuka biyu.

Hanyoyin da ake da su a yankinku na iya haɗawa da:

* samun bitcoin ta hanyar aiki
* saye ko sayarwa kai tsaye da wani mutum
* amfani da dandalin musaya ko dillali
* amfani da ATM ko ƙaramin wurin musaya
* karɓar bitcoin ko kashe shi wajen ɗan kasuwa
* amfani da sabis na biyan kuɗi da ke sauyawa tsakanin bitcoin da kuɗin hukuma
* shiga tsarin tattalin arzikin gida inda bitcoin ke yawo tsakanin mahalarta

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/8ad6c3106c57d796cf1b415d6a8104b043e6a215-776x978.svg)

Ku kwatanta **dukkan matakan hanyar**, ba kuɗin hidima na farko kawai ba. Wani sabis na iya tallata ƙarancin kuɗin hidimar saye amma ya yi amfani da marar kyau [farashin musaya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#exchange-rate) ko ya caji kuɗin cirewa. Hanyar mu'amala kai tsaye tsakanin mutane na iya zama kamar tana kiyaye sirri amma tana buƙatar lokaci, amincewa, da tsaron jiki.

Ku bincika ko hanyar tana ba da damar cire bitcoin zuwa walat da kuke sarrafawa. Adadin bitcoin da ake nunawa a cikin sabis mai rufaffiyar tsari na iya zama haƙƙin neman bitcoin daga sabis ɗin kawai, maimakon adadin da ke kan sarkar da mabuɗanku ke sarrafawa.

Don musaya ido-da-ido, ku yi amfani da wurin jama'a da ya dace, ku guji ɗaukar tsabar kuɗin da ba ku buƙata, ku zo da amintaccen mutum idan ya dace, ku tabbatar an biya kafin ku tafi, kuma ku bi dokokin yankinku. Kada ku bayyana inda kuke adana bitcoin ko nawa kuke da shi.
