# 4.1 Bitcoin a Cikin Al’ummarku

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/119ea5325cea83e0cdbf15651f5a19becfb07cdf-1536x1024.svg)

Wannan shi ne matakin **Haɗawa** na Difloma ta Bitcoin. Za ku haɗa matsalolin tsarin kuɗi na zamani da [abubuwan da ake samu da waɗanda ake rasa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off), da yadda Bitcoin ke magance su, da alhakin yin amfani da shi cikin aminci daga **Sassa na 1–3** da wata buƙata ko dama a yankinku, da wani zaɓi mai yiwuwa a zahiri, da kuma wani aikin gwaji mai aminci. Ba a sa ran ku fara amfani da Bitcoin ko ku yanke hukuncin cewa shi ne zaɓi mafi kyau a kowane yanayi a yankinku ba.

Bitcoin ƙa'idar sadarwa ce guda ɗaya ta duniya, amma mutane suna amfani da ita a yanayi daban-daban na yankunansu. Samun intanet, wayoyi, dokoki, haraji, [nau'o'in kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency), bankuna, 'yan kasuwa, kuɗaɗen caji, harshe, tsaro, da tallafin fasaha duk suna shafar abin da zai zama mai amfani.

Malaminku ne ke zaɓar ayyukan koyo a wannan kashi. Manufar ba ita ce nuna yadda kowace manhaja ko na'ura ke aiki ba. Manufar ita ce haɗa ra'ayoyin da ke cikin **Sassa na 1–3** da zaɓuɓɓukan da ake fuskanta a zahiri a cikin al'ummarku. Malami na iya gayyatar ƙwararre daga yankinku idan ana buƙatar ilimi na musamman.

Ku yi amfani da **Kashi na 3**—jerin abubuwan dubawa na tsaronsa—a dukkan sassan da suka shafi yankinku. Yin amfani da bitcoin ko wata manhaja ko na'ura da aka ambata a cikin aikin gwaji ƙarƙashin kulawa ba yana nufin cewa My First Bitcoin ko malaminku suna ba da shawarar amfani da shi akai-akai, a kai a kai ba tare da tsayawa ba, ko wajen mu'amaloli masu ƙima mafi girma ba. Manufar ita ce a kwatanta wannan zaɓi da [abubuwan da ake samu da waɗanda ake rasa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) yadda ya kamata domin ku iya yanke shawarar kanku.

| --- | --- |
| Matsala ko dama a yankinku |  |
