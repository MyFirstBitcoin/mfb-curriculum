# 4.13 Bita: Haɗa wannan da al'ummarku

[Teburin bitar misalin yankin: ga kowane jigo, daga ainihin buƙata zuwa mataki na gaba da ya dace a ɗauka cikin taka tsantsan, akwai tambayar da za ku yi da kuma fili na zaɓi don rubuta bayanan kulawarku.](/micrographics/community-review-table-01/ha.md)


Ku kammala wannan bita da kanku, sannan ku kwatanta dalilanku da na ƙungiyarku ko rukunin karatunku. Ku yi la'akari da misalin yankinku da kuka nazarta: wace buƙata ta gaske ya biya, wane aikin kuɗi aka yi amfani da shi, kuma an tura [ƙima](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) a kan sarkar, ta hanyar Lightning, ko ta hanyoyin biyu? Ku tambayi wanda yake da iko da maɓallan, mene ne babbar fa'ida da haɗari mafi tsanani, da yadda dokokin yankin, ababen more rayuwa, harshe, ko al'ada suka shafi sakamakon. Mataki na gaba mai nuna ɗaukar nauyi shi ne wanda ya dace da yanayin yankin, ya bayyana farashi da [riƙon kadarori](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) a sarari, ya rage kurakurai da zamba, kuma za a iya tantance shi bisa hujjoji.

**Amsar Kashi na 4:** Bitcoin na iya aiki a matsayin kuɗi idan mutane suka yi amfani da shi bisa son ransu don samun kuɗi, yin ajiya, karɓa, aikawa, kashewa, [sanya farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price), ko kammala biyan [ƙima](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value). Matakin Haɗawa yana farawa da buƙata ta gaske, yana kwatanta wata hanyar da za a iya amfani da ita a zahiri, yana bayyana [riƙon kadarori](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) da farashi a sarari, yana mutunta dokokin yankin da tsaro, kuma yana ba ɗalibai isasshen ilimi don su zaɓa cikin 'yanci.

#### Muhimman abubuwan da za a ɗauka

* Amfani da Bitcoin a matsayin kuɗi ya danganta da yanayin yanki: hanyar da za ta yi amfani ta dogara da buƙatun al'umma, ababen more rayuwa, dokoki, farashi, kayan aiki, harshe, da yanayin tsaro.
* Wani aikin da malami ya tsara na **Kashi na 4** a matakin Haɗawa ya kamata ya danganta darussan Matsala, Mafita, da Aiki da wata matsala ko dama ta gaske, wata hanyar da za a iya amfani da ita a zahiri, yin aiki cikin aminci, da hujjar koyo.
* Taron horaswa na yanki ya kamata ya bi matakai cikin aminci: lura da yadda ake yin aikin, yin atisaye tare da taimako, sannan bayyanawa ko kammala wani takamaiman aiki ba tare da fallasa bayanan sirri ba.
* Kafin ku yi amfani da wani kayan aiki ko sabis na yankinku, ku bincika manufarsa, [riƙon kadarori](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) bisa tsarinsa, tushensa, tsarin dawo da damar amfani, [sirrin bayanai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy), farashi, dacewa da sauran kayan aiki, taimako, da dokokin yankin da ake bi a yanzu.
* Biyan kuɗi a kan sarkar da ta hanyar Lightning suna da bambance-bambance wajen [daidaita fa'ida da illa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) a fannonin kammala biyan kuɗi, sauri, kuɗaɗen caji, [riƙon kadarori](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), da wuraren da matsala za ta iya tasowa; ku tabbatar da hanyar biyan kuɗi da mai karɓa yake sa rai.
* Hanyoyin musayar kuɗi na yanki, [tantance abokin ciniki (KYC)](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#kyc) da zaɓuɓɓukansa, hanyoyin gudanar da hada-hadar 'yan kasuwa, da misalan amfani a cikin al'umma ya kamata a kwatanta su bisa cikakken farashinsu, [riƙon kadarori](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), sirrin bayanai, tsaro, da [daidaita fa'ida da illa ta fuskar doka](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off).
* Ƙaƙƙarfan tsarin tattalin arziki mai zagayawa ko kyakkyawan misalin amfani a cikin al'umma yana kasancewa bisa son rai, yana magance matsala ta gaske, yana kare mahalarta, kuma ba ya sa ɗalibai su dogara da sabis guda ɗaya, mai shiryawa guda ɗaya, ko wani alkawari.
