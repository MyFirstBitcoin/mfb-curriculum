# 4.6 Amfani da Bitcoin a Rayuwar Yau da Kullum

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/b86058296c3087bf7e2280da71b62bd19050e17b-1536x1024.svg)


<!-- micrographic: daily-bitcoin-uses-01 -->


Bitcoin na iya aiki a matsayin kuɗi ta hanyoyi da dama. Kashewa ɗaya ne kawai daga cikinsu.

**Samun kuɗin shiga:** Ma'aikaci, mai ƙirƙira, kasuwanci, ko ɗan al'umma yana karɓar bitcoin a madadin [darajar](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) da ya samar. Samun kuɗin shiga na iya kawar da buƙatar yin saye dabam, amma dole mai karɓa ya amince da batutuwan farashi, [sauyin farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#volatility), bayanan da za a adana, da [riƙon ajiya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody).

**Tanadi:** Mutum yana riƙe bitcoin don amfani a nan gaba. Adadin da za a samar wanda za a iya hasashensa na iya jan hankali, amma ta fuskar musaya da kuɗin gwamnati, [darajar](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) na iya faɗuwa ƙwarai kuma tsaron maɓallan sirri ya zama muhimmi.

**Karɓa:** Mutum yana karɓar kyauta, gudummawa, mayar da kuɗi, kuɗin da aka aiko, ko biyan bashi. Dole mai karɓa ya bayar da madaidaiciyar buƙatar biyan kuɗi kuma ya tabbatar da kammaluwar biyan.

**Aikawa:** Mutum yana tura [daraja](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) ga iyali, abokai, kasuwanci, ko ƙungiya. Dole ya kwatanta hanyar biyan kuɗi, sauri, caji, [sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy), da rashin yiwuwar soke biyan bayan ya kammala.

**Kashewa:** Mutum yana musanya bitcoin da kayayyaki ko ayyuka. Ya kamata ya gano [farashin musaya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#exchange-rate), manufofin ɗan kasuwa, hanyar biyan kuɗi, da tasirin harajin yankin da ya shafi ma'amalar.

**Sanya farashi da lissafin kuɗi:** Mutum ko kasuwanci yana rubuta [daraja](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) a cikin bitcoin ko [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi). A wurare da dama, har yanzu ana buƙatar, a cikin kuɗin gwamnati, [darajar](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) a lokacin biyan kuɗi don lissafin kuɗi ko haraji.

**Bayar da gudummawa:** Mutum yana tallafa wa ƙungiya ko wani mutum kai tsaye. Ya kamata ya tabbatar da buƙatar biyan kuɗin mai karɓa ta wata majiya amintacciya.

Ga kowane irin amfani, ku yi tambayoyi uku:

1. Wace matsala da ake da ita ce [Bitcoin yake magancewa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-address) a nan?
1. Shin Bitcoin ya fi wata hanyar da ake da ita a wannan yanayin?
1. Wane sabon nauyi ko haɗari ne Bitcoin yake haifarwa?

Ku guji farawa da kayan aiki. Ku fara da buƙata.
