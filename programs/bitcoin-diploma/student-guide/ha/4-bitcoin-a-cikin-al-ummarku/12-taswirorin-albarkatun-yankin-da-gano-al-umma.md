# 4.12 Taswirorin albarkatun yankin da gano al’umma

Ana ajiye cikakkun ayyukan da suka shafi albarkatun yanki, jadawalin bayanai, da jerin bayanan da kan sauya a [Cibiyar Albarkatun Koyo](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/resources/?search=map). Taswirori da kundin bayanan al'umma na iya taimaka muku samun 'yan kasuwa, malamai, tarukan ganawa, da tsare-tsaren tattalin arziki masu zagayawa, amma bayanan da ke cikin jerin na iya zama marasa cikawa ko tsofaffi.

Kafin ku dogara da wani bayani a cikin jerin, ku bincika lokacin da aka tabbatar da shi, ku tabbatar da bayanin ta wata hanya dabam, kuma ku zo da wata hanyar biyan kuɗi ko shiga a dama. Al'ummar yankinku na iya ba ku taimako a aikace, amma kasancewa memba ba hujjar amana ba ce. Kada ku bayyana [jerin kalmomin dawo da walat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), adadin kuɗin da kuke da shi, adiresoshin gidajenku, ko matakan tsaronku a taron ganawa. Ku yi taka-tsantsan idan sabon abokin hulɗa ya yi muku tayin sarrafa jarin ku, musayar kuɗi ta sirri, ribar da aka ba da tabbacin samunta, ko taimakon gaggawa.
