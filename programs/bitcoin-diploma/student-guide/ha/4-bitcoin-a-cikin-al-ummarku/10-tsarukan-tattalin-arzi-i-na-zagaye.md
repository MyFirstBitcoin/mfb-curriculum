# 4.10 Tsarukan Tattalin Arziƙi na Zagaye

Wani [**tsarin tattalin arzikin Bitcoin mai zagayawa**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-circular-economy) wata hanyar sadarwa ce wadda mutane ke samun bitcoin, kashe shi, adana shi, da musayarsa a cikin al'umma. [Darajar kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) tana zagayawa tsakanin ma'aikata, abokan ciniki, 'yan kasuwa, masu samar da kayayyaki, malamai, da masu ba da hidimomi, maimakon ta shigo ta hanyar saye sau ɗaya kawai sannan ta fita ta hanyar sauya kuɗin nan take.

My First Bitcoin yana tallafa wa [tsare-tsaren tattalin arzikin Bitcoin masu zagayawa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-circular-economy), kuma da yawa daga cikinsu suna cikin Cibiyar Ilimin Bitcoin. Ku binciki waɗannan al'ummomi a kan [Allon Bayanan Cibiyar](https://network.myfirstbitcoin.org/?min10merchants=1).

Tsarin tattalin arziki mai zagayawa na iya rage dogaro da hidimomin kuɗi da ke nesa, ya kuma ƙulla alaƙa kai tsaye tsakanin mutanen yankin. Haka kuma yana iya gaza idan buƙata ta yi kaɗan, ilimi bai wadatar ba, kayan aikin ba abin dogaro ba ne, tsaro yana da rauni, ko kuma ana matsa wa mutane su yi amfani da bitcoin ba tare da wata buƙata ta gaske ba.

Ku zana taswirar zagayawa mai sauƙi a yankinku:

1. Wa zai iya samun bitcoin?
1. A ina za su iya kashe shi ko amfani da shi?
1. Waɗanne 'yan kasuwa ne za su iya biyan mai samar da kayayyaki ko ma'aikaci da bitcoin?
1. Waɗanne mahalarta ne suke buƙatar musanya bitcoin zuwa kuɗin gwamnati ko kuɗin gwamnati zuwa bitcoin?
1. Wa ke ba da ilimi da tallafin fasaha?
1. Me ke faruwa idan haɗin intanet ya katse?
1. Waɗanne haɗurran [riƙon kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) da [farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) ne kowane mai shiga yake fuskanta?

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/79370421e2d7dc74bd87d0f58ebe565a96a96d52-1536x1024.svg)

Ingantaccen tsarin tattalin arziki mai zagayawa ya dogara ne da shiga bisa son rai. Ya kamata 'yan kasuwa da ma'aikata su iya gane haɗurran biyan kuɗi, kayyade farashi, [riƙon kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), lissafin kuɗi, da [sauyin farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#volatility). Adadin masu fara amfani da tsarin kaɗai ba ya nuna ko hanyar sadarwar tana da amfani.

Mafi ƙaƙƙarfan tsarin zagayawa a cikin al'umma yana warware matsala ta gaske ga mahalarta fiye da ɗaya. Bai kamata ya dogara na dindindin kan kyaututtuka ko kan mai shirya tsarin guda ɗaya da ke sarrafa duk wani biyan kuɗi ba.
