Kowane walat na iya kasancewa ƙarƙashin kulawar wani ko kuma mai shi ne ke kula da shi da kansa, kuma idan biyan kuɗi ya gaza ko lokacinsa ya ƙare, ana sake farawa da sabuwar buƙata.](/micrographics/lightning-payment-sequence-01/ha.md)


Lightning na iya sa yin ƙananan biyan kuɗi cikin sauri ta Bitcoin ya zama mai sauƙin aiwatarwa. Zai iya dacewa da wurin karɓar kuɗi na ɗan kasuwa, biyan kuɗi akai-akai a cikin al'umma, sabis na intanet, ko tura kuɗi inda kuɗin caji na ma'amala a kan sarkar zai yi yawa idan aka kwatanta da adadin da ake biya.

Ya kamata nunin yadda Lightning ke aiki a cikin al'umma ya bayyana:

* ko wani ne ke riƙe da iko da walat ɗin ko mai walat ɗin ne ke riƙe da ikon da kansa
* yadda ake saka kuɗi a walat ɗin
* ko saka kuɗin yana buƙatar a yi a kan sarkar [ma'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) ko a yi amfani da wani sabis
* wace hanyar ajiyar kwafi ko dawo da walat ke kare ragowar kuɗin
* wane irin buƙatar biyan kuɗi ake amfani da shi
* waɗanne kuɗaɗen caji ko iyakoki ne ake amfani da su
* abin da ke faruwa idan biyan kuɗi ya gaza
* yadda mai karɓa ke tabbatar da biyan kuɗin

Wata [**daftar biyan kuɗi ta Lightning**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#lightning-invoice) buƙatar biyan kuɗi ce da aka ƙirƙira domin wani takamaiman biyan kuɗi. Tana iya ƙunsar adadi, bayanan inda za a aika kuɗin, da lokacin ƙarewar wa'adinta. Ku karanta allon [tabbatarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#confirmation) na walat ɗin kafin ku biya.

Wani [**adireshin Lightning**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#lightning-address) alamar shaida ce da mutum zai iya karantawa, wadda wasu sabis ke tallafawa. Yana iya kama da adireshin imel, amma hanya ce ta sauƙaƙa isar da biyan kuɗi, ba asusun imel ba. Ya dogara da mai samar da sabis ko sunan yankin intanet da ke tallafa masa.

Don yin biyan kuɗi tare da jagoranci:

1. Mai karɓa ya ƙirƙiri buƙatar da ta dace.
1. Mai aikawa ya sikanta ta ko ya kwafe ta.
1. Mai aikawa ya duba mai karɓa, adadi, ma'aunin kuɗi, da kuɗin caji.
1. Mai aikawa ya amince ne kawai bayan ya tabbatar da cewa bayanan sun yi daidai.
1. Dukkan ɓangarorin biyu su tabbatar da sakamakon a cikin walat ɗinsu.
1. Ƙungiyar ta bayyana haɗurran [riƙon kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) da gazawa.

Kada ku ɗauka cewa nasarar biyan kuɗi ta Lightning tana tabbatar da cewa walat ɗin yana da aminci don adana kuɗi masu yawa. Sauƙin biyan kuɗi da [riƙon kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) cikin tsaro batutuwa ne daban-daban.
