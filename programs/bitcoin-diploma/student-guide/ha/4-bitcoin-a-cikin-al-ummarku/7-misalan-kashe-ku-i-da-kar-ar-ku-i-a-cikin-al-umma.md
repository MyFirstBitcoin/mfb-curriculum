Ku yi amfani da kayayyaki da ayyukan da aka saba da su a yankinku. Takamaiman abin da za a saya ba shi ne muhimmi ba; tunanin da ke bayan zaɓin hanyar biyan kuɗi shi ne muhimmi.

###### Yanayi na 1: Ƙaramin sayayya

Wani abokin ciniki yana son sayen abin amfanin yau da kullum. Ɗan kasuwar yana karɓar biyan kuɗi ta Lightning da kuma ta kan sarkar Bitcoin.

* Wace hanya ce za ta fi dacewa da ƙaramin biyan kuɗi da ake son yi nan take?
* Wa ke ƙirƙirar buƙatar biyan kuɗin?
* Ta yaya abokin cinikin zai duba adadin da rukunin kuɗin?
* Ta yaya ɗan kasuwar zai san cewa biyan kuɗin ya yi nasara?
* Me zai faru idan walat ɗin abokin cinikin ya nuna “an aika” amma ɗan kasuwar bai ga “an karɓa” ba?

###### Yanayi na 2: Biyan kuɗin aiki

Wani ma'aikaci ya yarda ya karɓi wani ɓangare na kuɗin aikinsa a bitcoin.

* Shin [farashin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) an kayyade shi da [kuɗin gida](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency), bitcoin, ko [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi)?
* A wane lokaci ake kayyade [farashin musaya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#exchange-rate) ɗin?
* Wa zai biya duk wani cajin da ake buƙata?
* Shin mai karɓar yana amfani da [tsarin riƙe kuɗinsa da kansa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#self-custody)?
* Wane bayanin kuɗin shiga ko haraji ake buƙatar adanawa?
* Shin ma'aikacin zai iya kula da adadin kuɗin cikin aminci?

###### Yanayi na 3: Tura kuɗi tsakanin 'yan uwa

Wani mutum yana son aika [ƙimar kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) ga wani ɗan uwansa da ke wani wuri.

* Waɗanne kuɗaɗe da jinkiri ne ke tattare da hanyar da ake amfani da ita yanzu?
* Ta yaya kowane mutum zai samu ko amfani da bitcoin?
* Shin mai karɓar yana son bitcoin ne ko [kuɗin gida](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency)?
* Waɗanne haɗurran musaya da [riƙon kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) ne ke bayyana a kowane mataki?
* Shin hanyar gaba ɗaya tana rage ɓata lokaci ko kashe kuɗi bayan an haɗa duk caji?

###### Yanayi na 4: Mayar da kuɗi

Wani ɗan kasuwa yana buƙatar mayar da [ƙimar kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) bayan an yi [biyan kuɗi a kan sarkar](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#on-chain-payment).

* Me ya sa ya kamata ɗan kasuwar ya nemi sabon adireshin mayar da kuɗi?
* Ta yaya ɗan kasuwar zai tabbatar da cewa lallai buƙatar ta fito ne daga abokin cinikin?
* Wa zai biya [cajin ma'amalar mayar da kuɗin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction-fee)?
* Wane bayani ya kamata ɓangarorin biyu su adana?

###### Yanayi na 5: Gudummawa ga al'umma

Wata ƙungiya a yankin tana son karɓar tallafi a cikin bitcoin.

* Ta yaya masu ba da gudummawa za su tabbatar da sahihancin buƙatar biyan kuɗi?
* Wa ke da iko da walat ɗin karɓar kuɗi da kwafin ajiyarsa?
* Za a karɓi gudummawar kai tsaye a kan sarƙar tubalan bayanai, ta hanyar Lightning, ko ta hanyoyin biyu?
* Waɗanne bayanai ya kamata a bayyana wa jama'a don tabbatar da gaskiya, kuma waɗanne ya kamata su kasance na sirri don kare lafiyar masu ba da gudummawa?
* Waɗanne bayanai ne ake buƙatar adanawa don bin ƙa'idojin yankin na lissafin kuɗi, haraji, ko bayar da rahoto?

Don atisaye na zahiri, ku yi amfani da abu mai ƙaramin daraja ko kwaikwayon yadda ake yin ciniki. Ya kamata ɗalibi ya iya bayyana dukkan matakan kafin ya amince da biyan kuɗi.
