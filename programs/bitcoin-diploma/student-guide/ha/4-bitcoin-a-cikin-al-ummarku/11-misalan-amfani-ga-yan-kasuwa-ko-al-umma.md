# 4.11 Misalan amfani ga ’yan kasuwa ko al’umma

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/9fd6b8ca04a295f860a0ab150d8f05823cbcea16-1536x1024.svg)


<!-- micrographic: merchant-payment-operations-01 -->


Ɗan kasuwa yana buƙatar fiye da maɓallin karɓar kuɗi kawai. Cikakken tsarin biyan kuɗi na iya haɗawa da saita farashi, horar da ma'aikata, rasitoci, mayar da kuɗi, [riƙon amana](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), musayar kuɗi, lissafin kuɗi, haraji, tsaron na'ura, da tallafi.

Kafin fara karɓar Bitcoin, ya kamata ɗan kasuwa ya yanke shawara kan:

* ko [farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) ana nuna su da [kuɗin gida](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency), [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), ko duka biyun
* ko ana biyan kuɗi ta hanyar Bitcoin a kan sarkar, Lightning, ko duka biyun
* ko walat ɗin tana ƙarƙashin riƙon wani ne ko kuma mai ita ne ke riƙon ta da kansa
* wanda zai iya samun damar shiga na'urar karɓar kuɗi
* yadda ake kare kuɗin da aka karɓa ko tura su
* ko ana sauya wani ɓangare na kuɗin zuwa [kuɗin gida](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency)
* yadda ake tabbatar da biyan kuɗi a wurin sayarwa
* yadda ake tafiyar da mayar da kuɗi da gyara kurakurai
* irin bayanan da dokar yankin ta buƙaci a adana
* abin da ma'aikata za su yi idan hanyar sadarwa ko walat ta daina aiki

Hanyoyin amfani a cikin al'umma na iya haɗawa da:

* biyan ma'aikata ko masu ƙirƙira na yankin
* karɓar gudummawa a bayyane
* canja [ƙima](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) tsakanin ƙungiyoyin al'umma
* tallafa wa ƙungiyar ajiyar kuɗi mai tsari bayyananne na [riƙon amana](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody)
* taimaka wa mai aiki daga nesa ya karɓi biyan kuɗi daga ƙasashen waje
* haɗa masu samar da kayayyaki da 'yan kasuwa na yankin
* koyar da amfani da walat cikin aminci
* samar da tallafin gaggawa a wuraren da babu tsarin biyan kuɗi na yau da kullum

[Riƙon amanar kuɗin al'umma](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#community-custody) yana buƙatar kulawa ta musamman. Idan mai shirya al'amura guda ɗaya yana riƙe da kuɗin mutane da yawa, wannan mai shirya al'amuran ya zama [mai riƙon amana](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custodian) kuma wanda ake dogara da shi. [Kuɗin haɗin gwiwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#shared-funds) suna buƙatar ƙa'idoji na bayyane, bayanan da aka adana, matakan sarrafa damar shiga, da nazarin doka inda ya dace.

Ga kowace hanyar amfani, ku ayyana nasara bisa matsalar da aka warware, ba bisa yawan [ma'amalolin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) da aka kammala ba.
