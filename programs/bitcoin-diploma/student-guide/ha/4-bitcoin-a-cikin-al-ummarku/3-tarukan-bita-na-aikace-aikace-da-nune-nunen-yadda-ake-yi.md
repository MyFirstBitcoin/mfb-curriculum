# 4.3 Tarukan bita na aikace-aikace da nune-nunen yadda ake yi

Za ku bi matakai uku: **Gabatarwa**, inda malaminku zai nuna yadda ake gudanar da aikin da haɗurransa; **Atisaye**, inda za ku bi tsarin da aka yi muku jagora ta amfani da kayan horo masu aminci; da **Aiki da kanku**, inda za ku kammala wani aiki ko ku bayyana dalilin wata shawara da kanku.


<!-- micrographic: presentation-practice-production-01 -->
[Ana koyon ƙwarewar aiki a matakai uku, daga hagu zuwa dama: Gabatarwa, inda malami ke nuna yadda ake aiwatar da aikin da haɗurransa, sai Atisaye, wato gwaji ƙarƙashin jagoranci da kayan horo marasa haɗari, sannan Aiwatarwa, inda ɗalibi ke kammala wani aiki ko bayyana dalilin yanke wata shawara da kansa.](/micrographics/presentation-practice-production-01/ha.md)


Taron bitarku na iya haɗawa da:

* gano ko walat tana ƙarƙashin kulawar wani ne ko kuwa mai ita ne ke da cikakken iko da ita
* gano shafin karɓar kuɗi ba tare da ƙirƙira ko tona ainihin [jerin kalmomin dawo da walat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase)
* kwatanta adireshin da ke kan sarkar da [daftarin neman biyan kuɗi na Lightning](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#lightning-invoice)
* bincika [ma'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) a cikin walat ko [manhajar binciken tubalan sarka](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#block-explorer)
* kwatanta kuɗaɗen caji da adadin kuɗin da aka karɓa ta hanyoyin da ake amfani da su a yankin
* gano alamomin zamba
* tsara yadda za a biya ɗan kasuwa ko bayar da gudummawa ga al'umma
* gano da bincika albarkatun da ake da su a yankin don wata takamaiman manufa

Kafin duk wani nuni na aikace kai tsaye, ku tabbatar kun san:

* cewa an bincika kayan aiki da umarnin amfani kwanan nan
* yadda malamin zai bayyana hanyar dawo da walat da abin da zai faru idan wani abu bai yi aiki ba
* ko za a yi amfani da kuɗi na gaske, da haɗurran da suka shafi sirri, tsaro, da asarar kuɗi
* cewa ba za a matsa muku ku girka manhaja ko ku bayyana bayanan kuɗinku ba
* cewa duk adadin kuɗin da za a yi amfani da shi a nunin kaɗan ne, ta yadda rasa shi zai zama darasi, ba bala'i ba
* cewa akwai wata hanyar da ba ta buƙatar intanet ko amfani da kuɗi
* yadda za a dakatar da aikin ajin idan sakamakon ya bambanta da abin da aka tsara

Sakamakon taron bitarku ya kamata ya zama bayani ko ƙwarewa, ba kawai kammala [ma'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) ba.
