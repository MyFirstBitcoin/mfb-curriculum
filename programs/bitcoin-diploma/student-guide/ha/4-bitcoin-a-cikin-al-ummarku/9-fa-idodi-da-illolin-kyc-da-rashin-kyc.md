# 4.9 Fa'idodi da illolin KYC da rashin KYC

[KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#kyc) da [rashin KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#no-kyc) hanyoyi ne da suka bambanta wajen [abin da ake samu da abin da ake rasa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off). Babu ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan sunaye da ke kawar da buƙatar yin nazari cikin tsanaki.


<!-- card: callout -->
> **Sirri ba ya kawar da haɗarin doka ko na lafiyar jiki**
>
> Ba [KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#kyc) ko [rashin KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#no-kyc) ne ya fi dacewa kai tsaye ba. Ku kwatanta ainihin hanyar, ku yi amfani da jagororin yankinku na yanzu idan ƙa'idoji ba su bayyana ba, kuma kada ku karya dokar yankinku ko ku jefa kanku cikin haɗari na jiki domin neman [sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy).


![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/7af8a2eb79ede0b36361dc0d1d6fe8f7ff404da2-1536x1024.svg)

Wani [KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#kyc) sabis na iya buƙatar suna na doka, takardar shaida, adireshi, hoto, bayanin tushen kuɗi, ko wasu bayanai. Wannan na iya ba sabis ɗin damar bin ƙa'idojin yankin, haɗuwa da bankuna, ƙara iyakokin mu'amala, ko taimakawa wajen dawo da damar shiga asusu.

[KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#kyc) kuma yana haifar da haɗarin bayanai. Sabis ɗin na iya adana alaƙa ta dindindin tsakanin bayanan shaida, hanyoyin biyan kuɗi, da bitcoin wajen [mu'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction). Kutse ga bayanai, buƙata ta doka, amfani da bayanai ba daidai ba daga ma'aikatan ciki, ko sauyin manufofi na iya fallasa waɗannan bayanai ko takaita damar amfani da su.

Wata [hanyar rashin KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#no-kyc) na iya tattara bayanan shaida kaɗan. Wannan na iya ƙara wa harkokin kuɗi [sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy). Haka kuma, tana iya ƙunsar abokan mu'amala da ba sa ƙarƙashin sa ido sosai, ƙarancin sauƙin saye da sayarwa, hauhawar [farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price), ƙarancin tallafi, ƙarin haɗarin zamba, ko ƙuntatawa ta doka.

Ku kwatanta ainihin hanyar:


| Tambaya | Hanyar KYC | Hanyar rashin KYC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waɗanne bayanan shaida ake tattarawa? |  |  |
| Wa ke adana bayanan, kuma har tsawon wane lokaci? |  |  |
| Za a iya gano alaƙar mu'amalar a kan sarkar tubalan bayanai? |  |  |
| Wace kariya daga zamba ake da ita? |  |  |
| Wane haɗari ke tattare da abokin mu'amala? |  |  |
| Waɗanne iyakoki da kuɗaɗen caji ake amfani da su? |  |  |
| Wace dokar yankin ce ta shafi wannan? |  |  |
| Me ke faruwa idan an samu saɓani? |  |  |


[Sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy) ba yana nufin ɓoyewa daga kowane haɗari ba. Yana nufin ikon sarrafa bayanan da suka shafe ku. Kada ku karya dokar yankinku ko ku jefa kanku cikin haɗari na jiki domin neman [sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy). Ku nemi jagororin yankinku na yanzu daga ƙwararren mutum idan ƙa'idojin ba su bayyana ba.
