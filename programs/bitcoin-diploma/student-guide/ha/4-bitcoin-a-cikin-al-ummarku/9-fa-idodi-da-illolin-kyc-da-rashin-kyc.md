# 4.9 Fa'idodi da illolin KYC da rashin KYC

[KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#kyc) da [rashin KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#no-kyc) hanyoyi ne da ke da bambance-bambance wajen [abin da ake samu da abin da ake rasa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off). Babu ɗaya daga cikin sunayen biyun da ke kawar da buƙatar yin nazari a hankali.


<!-- card: callout -->
> **Sirri ba ya kawar da haɗarin doka ko na lafiyar jiki**
>
> Babu ɗaya daga cikin [KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#kyc) ko [rashin KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#no-kyc) da ya fi kai tsaye a kowane hali. Ku kwatanta ainihin hanyar, ku bi shawarwarin yankinku na yanzu idan ƙa'idoji ba su bayyana ba, kuma kada ku karya dokar yankinku ko ku jefa kanku cikin haɗarin rauni a ƙoƙarin samun [sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy).


![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/7af8a2eb79ede0b36361dc0d1d6fe8f7ff404da2-1536x1024.svg)

Wani sabis na [KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#kyc) na iya neman suna na doka, takardar shaida, adireshi, hoto, bayanin tushen kuɗi, ko wasu bayanai. Wannan na iya ba sabis ɗin damar bin ƙa'idojin yankin, haɗuwa da bankuna, ƙayyade iyakoki mafi girma, ko taimakawa wajen dawo da damar shiga asusu.

[KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#kyc) kuma yana haifar da haɗarin da ya shafi bayanai. Sabis ɗin na iya adana alaƙa ta dindindin tsakanin shaidar mutum, hanyoyin biyan kuɗi, da bitcoin a cikin [ma'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction). Kutse cikin bayanai, buƙata ta doka, amfani da bayanai ba bisa ƙa'ida ba daga ma'aikatan ciki, ko sauyin manufofi na iya fallasa bayanan ko ƙuntata damar amfani da su.

Wata hanyar [rashin KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#no-kyc) na iya tattara bayanan shaida kaɗan. Wannan na iya inganta harkokin kuɗi ta fuskar [sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy). Haka kuma, tana iya haɗawa da abokan ma'amala da ba a sa musu ido sosai ta ƙa'idoji ba, ƙarancin sauƙin saye da sayarwa, mafi girman [farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price), ƙarancin taimako, ƙarin haɗarin zamba, ko ƙuntatawa ta doka.

Ku kwatanta ainihin hanyar:

[Sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy) ba yana nufin ɓoyuwa daga kowane haɗari ba. Yana nufin ikon sarrafa bayanan da suka shafe ku. Kada ku karya dokar yankinku ko ku jefa kanku cikin haɗarin rauni a ƙoƙarin samun [sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy). Ku nemi shawarwarin yankinku na yanzu daga wanda ya cancanta idan ƙa'idoji ba su bayyana ba.
