# 2.6 Rarraba Iko

Wani [tsari mai cibiyar iko](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#centralized-system) yana da wurin da ke sarrafa shi. Banki yana sarrafa bayanan kwastomominsa a [kundin rajista](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger). Kamfanin biyan kuɗi yana sarrafa hanyar sadarwarsa. Gwamnati tana sarrafa ƙa'idojin fitar da [kuɗinta](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency).


<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Rarraba iko**
>
> Wani [tsari mai rarraba iko](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#decentralized-system) yana raba ayyuka da aiwatar da shawarwari tsakanin mahalarta masu zaman kansu da yawa. Bitcoin ba shi da wani babban ofishi da zai iya buɗe asusu, canja ragowar kuɗin asusu, ko amincewa da zama mamba.


A Bitcoin, [rarraba iko](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#decentralization) ya samo asali ne daga wasu halaye:

* kowa na iya saukewa da bincika [manhajar da lambar tushenta take a buɗe](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#open-source-software)
* masu amfani na iya yin mu'amaloli ba tare da buɗe asusu a [hanyar sadarwar Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-network) ba
* masu zaman kansu daga cikin [na'urorin sadarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) suna tantance [mu'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) da tubalan bayanai
* [masu haƙar ma'adinai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) suna gasa don gabatar da tubalan bayanai
* babu wani [mai haƙar ma'adinai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) da zai iya sa tubalin bayanai marar inganci ya zama abin karɓa ga amintattun [na'urorin sadarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node)
* sauye-sauye ga ƙa'idoji suna buƙatar mahalarta su amince da su da son ransu
* wannan [kundin rajista](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger) ana yin kwafinsa a kwamfutoci da wurare da yawa

[Rarraba iko](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#decentralization) ba daidai yake da samun tasiri iri ɗaya ba. [Haƙar ma'adinai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work), kayan aikinta—tare da ilimin fasaha, kuɗi, samun damar intanet, da haɓaka manhajoji—ba a rarraba su daidai ba. Manyan masu ba da sabis na iya zama wuraren da masu amfani da su suke dogaro da su. Abin da Bitcoin yake iƙirari ya fi wannan taƙaituwa: babu mahalarci guda ɗaya da ke da ikon canja [kundin rajista](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger) ko ƙa'idojin kuɗi ga kowa bisa ra'ayinsa shi kaɗai.

[Rarraba iko](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#decentralization) yana kuma haifar da tsada. Haɗa kai wajen gudanar da ayyuka na iya ɗaukar lokaci mai tsawo. Dole ne masu amfani su tantance bayanai kuma su ɗauki ƙarin nauyi. Babu wata cibiyar taimako ta bai ɗaya ga [hanyar sadarwar Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-network). Waɗannan [sadaukarwar da ake yi don samun wasu fa'idodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) suna cikin tsarin da aka tsara, ba kurakuran da suka faru ba da gangan ba ne waɗanda za a iya kawar da su ba tare da canja tsarin ba.
