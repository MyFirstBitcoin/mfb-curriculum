# 2.18 Bita: Ta Yaya Bitcoin Ke Magance Gazawar Tsarin Kuɗi?

Ku ɗan dakata ku yi la’akari da yadda ƙa’idojin da kowa zai iya gani, fitar da kuɗi mai iyaka, tura kuɗi kai tsaye tsakanin mutane, da kuma ’yancin gudanar da [tantancewa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#verification) suke magance matsalolin da aka gano a **Kashi na 1**: sauya adadin kuɗin da ke akwai, ikon cibiyoyi, ƙuntatacciyar damar amfani, da dogaro da amana. Me ya sa [mahaɗan sadarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) da [masu haƙa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) suke da ayyuka daban-daban, kuma me ya sa dole ne a hana [kashe kuɗi iri ɗaya fiye da sau ɗaya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#double-spending)? Bitcoin yana maye gurbin wani ɓangare na amincewa da cibiyoyi da [tantancewa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#verification) da ɗaukar alhakin kai; ba ya kawar da haɗari ko [buƙatar sadaukar da wani abu don samun wani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off).

**Muhimmin abin tunani:** Bisa ga abin da kuka koya game da Bitcoin a wannan kashi, shin yana da [ƙima](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) a gare ku? Shin [farashin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) bitcoin na yanzu yana wakiltar [ƙimarsa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value)?

#### Muhimman darussa

* An ƙirƙiri Bitcoin ne domin ba da damar tura kuɗi ta hanyar dijital ba tare da wani mai kula da [kundin lissafi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger) na tsakiya ba, tare da hana [kashe kuɗi iri ɗaya fiye da sau ɗaya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#double-spending).
* Nasa [kundin lissafi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger) da kowa zai iya gani, [sa hannun dijital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-signature), [hanyar sadarwa kai tsaye tsakanin mahalarta](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#peer-to-peer-network), [hujjar aiki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work), da kuma ’yancin gudanar da [tantancewa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#verification) suna aiki tare domin adana bayanan bai ɗaya na [ma’amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) a kundin tarihi.
* Bitcoin ba ya ƙarƙashin ikon cibiya guda, domin babu mahalarci guda da zai iya sauya ragowar kuɗaɗe ko ƙa’idojin da suka shafi kowa shi kaɗai.
* Masu amfani suna zaɓar ko za su riƙe ko karɓi bitcoin; [mahaɗan sadarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) suna tantance bin ƙa’idoji; [masu haƙa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) suna gabatar da tubalan bayanai don amincewa da su; masu haɓaka manhaja kuma za su iya ba da shawarar sauye-sauye ga manhaja, amma ba za su iya tilasta su ba.
* Bitcoin yana da jadawalin samarwa da ke a bayyane, mai iyakar BTC miliyan 21, kuma [mahaɗan hanyar sadarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) suna ƙin amincewa da bulolin da suka saɓa wa wannan ƙa'ida.
* Ana iya raba bitcoin ɗaya (1 BTC) zuwa [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi) 100,000,000, don haka mutane za su iya amfani da ƙananan raka'o'i ba tare da buƙatar cikakken bitcoin ɗaya ba.
* Ana iya tantance Bitcoin a matsayin [kuɗi masu inganci](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#sound-money) ta hanyar amfani da halayen kuɗi, tare da la'akari da [abubuwan da ake haƙura da su don samun fa'idodinsa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off): [sauye-sauyen farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#volatility), ƙarancin karɓuwa, biyan kuɗi da galibi yake da wuya ko ba zai yiwu a soke shi ba, da buƙatar mutum ya ɗauki ƙarin alhakin kansa.
