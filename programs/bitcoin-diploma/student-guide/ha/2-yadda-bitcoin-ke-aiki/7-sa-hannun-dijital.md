# 2.7 Sa hannun dijital

[Sa hannun dijital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-signature) suna daga cikin muhimman ɓangarorin tsarin Bitcoin da aka bayyana a takardar bayaninsa. A cikin Bitcoin, jakar kuɗi tana amfani da [maɓallin sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) don ƙirƙirar [sa hannun dijital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-signature) da ke ba da izinin [ma'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction). Jakar kuɗi galibi manhaja ce da ke sarrafa maɓallai kuma ta ƙirƙiri waɗannan sa hannun ga mai amfani. Sai kawai wanda ke riƙe da buƙataccen [maɓallin sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) zai iya ba da izinin kashe kuɗin; hanyar sadarwar za ta iya tabbatar da sahihancin sa hannun ba tare da sanin [maɓallin sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) kansa ba.

Wannan yana ba mutum damar sarrafa bitcoin ba tare da neman izini daga banki ko wani mai gudanarwa na tsakiya ba. Haka kuma yana nufin cewa ikon sarrafa [maɓallin sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) yana da matuƙar muhimmanci: duk wanda ya same shi galibi zai iya ba da izinin kashe kuɗi, yayin da ɓacewar maɓalli na iya sa a kasa dawo da bitcoin ɗin.

Wannan shi ne tushen [riƙon kai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#self-custody): sarrafa maɓallan da kanku maimakon dogara ga wata hidima ta sarrafa su. **Darasi na 3** yana bayyana nauyin da ke kanku na kiyaye tsaron maɓallai, kwafin ajiya, da hanyoyin dawo da su a aikace.
