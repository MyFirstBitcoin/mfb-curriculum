# 2.9 Masu amfani, mahaɗan sadarwa, da masu haƙar ma’adinai

Bitcoin yana aiki ne domin mahalarta daban-daban suna gudanar da ayyuka daban-daban.

* **Masu amfani** suna karɓa, riƙewa, da aika bitcoin. Mai amfani zai iya sarrafa maɓallansa da ke cikin walat ko kuma ya yi amfani da [mai riƙon ajiya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custodian). Masu amfani suna ba cibiyar sadarwar ma'ana ta fuskar tattalin arziki ta hanyar zaɓar ko za su karɓi bitcoin da kuma waɗanne ƙa'idoji ko ayyuka suka amince da su.
* [**Ƙullai**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) suna tafiyar da manhajar Bitcoin kuma suna tabbatar da bin ƙa'idoji da kansu. Wani [cikakken ƙulli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#full-node) yana bincika cewa [ma'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) suna da sa hannu ingantattu, cewa ba a riga an kashe bitcoin ɗin ba, cewa tubalan sun cika sharuɗɗan [hujjar aiki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work), kuma cewa sabbin bitcoin suna bin [jadawalin fitarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuance-schedule). [Ƙullai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) suna raba ingantattun bayanai da sauran ƙullai kuma suna ƙin bayanan da ba su inganta ba.
* [**Masu haƙa**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) suna tattara waɗanda ke jiran aiwatarwa daga cikin [ma'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction), suna gina [tubalan da za a gabatar](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#candidate-block), kuma suna fafatawa ta hanyar [hujjar aiki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work). Idan ya yi nasara, [mai haƙa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) [yana yaɗa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#broadcast) tubali kuma yana iya karɓar abin da aka amince da shi na [tallafin tubali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#block-subsidy) da [kuɗaɗen ma'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction-fee). [Masu haƙa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) suna taimakawa wajen tsara jerin [ma'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) kuma suna sa [kundin lissafin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger) ya zama mai tsadar sake rubutawa.
* **Masu haɓaka manhaja** suna nazari, rubutawa, da ba da shawarwarin sauye-sauye ga manhaja. Ba su ne masu mallakar ƙa'idojin Bitcoin ba. Sauyi ga manhaja yana da tasiri ne kawai idan mahalarta sun zaɓi tafiyar da manhajar da ke bin waɗannan ƙa'idoji.

Waɗannan ayyuka suna ƙayyade iyakokin juna:

* mai amfani ba zai iya kashe bitcoin ba sai da ingantaccen sa hannun da ake buƙata
* wani [mai haƙa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) zai iya gabatar da tubali, amma [ƙullai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) ne suke yanke shawarar ko ya bi ƙa'idoji
* wani [mahaɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) yana tantancewa bisa ƙa'idodinsa, amma [yarjejeniya ta bai ɗaya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#consensus) tana buƙatar ƙa'idoji masu jituwa a duk faɗin hanyar sadarwar
* masu haɓaka manhaja za su iya gabatar da manhaja, amma mahalarta ne ke zaɓar ko za su gudanar da ita

Wasu mutane suna gudanar da ayyuka da dama. Wani [mai haƙa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) zai iya gudanar da wani [mahaɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) kuma ya kasance mai amfani. Ba sai mai farawa ya gudanar da kowane aiki ba kafin ya iya bayyana yadda ayyukan ke daidaita juna.

**Tambayar nan take:** Me ya sa wani [mahaɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) yake tantance [ma'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction)? Ku ambaci aƙalla ƙa'ida ɗaya da wani [mahaɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) yake bincikawa.
