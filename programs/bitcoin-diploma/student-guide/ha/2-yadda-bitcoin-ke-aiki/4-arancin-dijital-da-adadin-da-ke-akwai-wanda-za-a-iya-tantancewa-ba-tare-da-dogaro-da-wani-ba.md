# 2.4 Ƙarancin dijital da adadin da ke akwai wanda za a iya tantancewa ba tare da dogaro da wani ba

Bayanan dijital galibi suna da sauƙin kwafewa.


<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Ƙaranci a tsarin dijital**
>
> A tsarin dijital, [ƙaranci](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#scarcity) yana nufin cewa tsarin zai iya bambance sahihin raka'a da kwafin da ba shi da inganci, kuma ya hana kashe raka'a ɗaya sau biyu.


A tsarin dijital, Bitcoin yana samar da [ƙaranci](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#scarcity) ta hanyar haɗa sa hannaye, bayanan da kowa zai iya gani na [mu'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) da suka gabata, [hujjar aiki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work), da ƙa'idoji da ake yi wa [tabbatarwa mai zaman kanta](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#verification). Ana iya tura bitcoin, amma hanyar sadarwar tana ƙin amincewa da yunƙurin sake kashe bitcoin ɗin nan.

[Ƙaranci](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#scarcity) yana zama abin da za a iya tabbatarwa da kansa idan mutane za su iya bincika ƙa'idojin da kowa zai iya gani da kansu, kuma babu wani [mai fitar da kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) da zai iya sauya adadin kuɗin da ake da shi ga kowa a ɓoye. Shi ya sa dole ne a yi nazarin iyakar adadin Bitcoin tare da [rarraba iko](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#decentralization) da aikin [mahaɗin hanyar sadarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) [na tabbatarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#verification).
