# 2.8 Canja wuri tsakanin takwarori

**Kai-tsaye tsakanin takwarori** yana nufin cewa mahalarta suna hulɗa ta hanyar sadarwa ba tare da buƙatar mai daidaitawa na tsakiya ba. Mai amfani da Bitcoin zai iya ƙirƙira da sanya hannu kan wata [ma'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction), sannan ya [yaɗa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#broadcast) ta zuwa hanyar sadarwar. Mai karɓa ba ya buƙatar asusu a bankin mai aikawa domin Bitcoin da kansa yana samar da tsarin bai ɗaya na kammala biyan kuɗi.

Kai-tsaye tsakanin takwarori ba koyaushe yake nufin cewa kwamfutoci biyu ne kawai suke sadarwa kai-tsaye ba. Wata [ma'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) na iya bi ta walat, masu samar da intanet, da ɗimbin [mahaɗan hanyar sadarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node). Muhimmin abu shi ne cewa babu wani mai tafiyar da Bitcoin na tsakiya da dole sai ya amince da mai aikawa ko mai karɓa.

Masu amfani har yanzu suna iya zaɓar [masu shiga tsakani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#intermediary). Dandalin musaya na iya riƙe bitcoin ga abokan ciniki. Sabis na biyan kuɗi na iya sauƙaƙa takardar neman biyan kuɗi. Walat ɗin da wani sabis ke kula da shi na iya sarrafa maɓallai. Waɗannan sabis na iya kawo sauƙi, amma a wannan yanayin mai amfani ya dogara da ƙa'idoji da tsaron sabis ɗin.

Bitcoin yana ba da damar mallaka da canja kuɗi kai-tsaye; ba ya tilasta wa kowane mai amfani ya guji kowane sabis. Zaɓin [wanda zai riƙe kuɗin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) yana ƙayyade wanda yake da iko a aikace.

Canja kuɗi kai-tsaye tsakanin takwarori na iya zama da amfani idan mutane suna wurare daban-daban ko suna amfani da tsarin banki daban-daban. Hakan kuma yana ɗora alhaki. Sahihiyar [ma'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) da aka aika zuwa adireshin da ba daidai ba, galibi ba za a iya soke ta ba.
