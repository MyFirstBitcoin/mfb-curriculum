# 2.17 Ƙarfafawa, haɗari, da alhaki

Dokokin Bitcoin na bai ɗaya, [cibiyar sadarwa ta tsara-da-tsara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#peer-to-peer-network), da [sa hannun dijital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-signature) suna ba mahalarta muhimman ayyuka. Masu amfani za su iya zaɓar su **karɓa**, **riƙe**, da **aika** bitcoin; [na'urorin tabbatarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) za su iya tabbatar da bin dokokin da kansu; [masu haƙa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) za su iya gabatar da tubalan da za a ƙara; kuma masu haɓaka manhajoji za su iya gabatar da sauye-sauyen manhaja. Babu wani matsayi guda da ke sarrafa cibiyar sadarwar a madadin kowa.



Waɗannan halaye za su iya ƙarfafa mahalarta, amma kuma suna ɗora musu ƙarin alhaki. Idan babu wata hukuma ta tsakiya da za ta amince da [ma'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction), dawo da damar shiga, ko soke sakamakon kurakurai da yawa, dole ne kowane mutum ya iya gane matsayin da yake ɗauka da haɗarin da yake karɓa.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/046836e98fde3a839b4969cfce7f63bfaf59b23f-1536x1024.svg)

Ga mutumin da ya zaɓi [riƙe bitcoin da kansa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#self-custody), mallakar ikon maɓallan yana ba shi ikon sarrafa bitcoin kai tsaye. Babu kamfanin da zai iya daskarar da ragowar kuɗin da ke kan sarkar tubala idan ana sarrafa maɓallansa yadda ya kamata. Haka kuma babu kamfanin da zai iya dawo da wata [jimlar kalmomin dawo da walat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) da ta ɓace.

[Riƙe bitcoin da kai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#self-custody) ba fasalin fasaha kawai ba ne. Yana buƙatar kare kwafin ajiya, tabbatar da adiresoshi, duba yadda walat ke aiki, da tsara yadda za a dawo da damar shiga. Darasi na gaba yana bayyana yadda za a riƙe bitcoin da amfani da shi cikin tsanaki, har da yadda za a tafiyar da waɗannan haɗari da nauye-nauye.

> Ɗaukar alhakin kai ba ya nufin yin komai kai kaɗai ko ƙin karɓar taimako. Ɗalibi mai taka tsantsan zai iya amfana da ilimi, bitar abokan koyo, rubutattun hanyoyin aiki, da ƙananan gwaje-gwaje ba tare da ba kowa damar samun bayanan sirri ba.
