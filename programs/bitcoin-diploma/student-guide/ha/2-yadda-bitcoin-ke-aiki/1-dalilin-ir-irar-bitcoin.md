# 2.1 Dalilin Ƙirƙirar Bitcoin

Biyan kuɗi ta hanyar dijital ya wanzu kafin Bitcoin. Mutane sun iya aika kuɗi ta bankuna, hanyoyin sadarwar katunan biyan kuɗi, da kamfanonin biyan kuɗi ta intanet. Waɗannan tsare-tsare sun sauƙaƙa kasuwanci, amma kowannensu ya dogara da wata amintacciyar ƙungiya don adana [rajistar lissafi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger), amincewa da asusu, kammala tura kuɗi, da warware saɓani.

Wannan dogaro yana haifar da iko da haɗari. Wani [ɗan tsakiya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#intermediary) na iya jinkirta biyan kuɗi, karɓar caji, tattarawa da yin amfani da bayanan sirri ta hanyar da ba ta dace ba, ƙuntata asusu, soke tura kuɗi, ko gaza gudanar da aikinsa. Mai [fitarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) na [kuɗin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) kuma na iya sauya yawansu da ke yawo.

Shekaru da yawa, masana kimiyyar kwamfuta sun yi ƙoƙarin ƙirƙirar kuɗin dijital da ba sa buƙatar wani mai [fitarwa na tsakiya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) ko kamfanin biyan kuɗi. Wannan ƙalubale ne mai wuya. Bayanan dijital suna da sauƙin kwafewa. Idan za a iya kwafe tsabar kuɗin dijital a kashe ta sau biyu, ba za ta iya aiki a matsayin kuɗi ba. Idan kamfani guda ne yake hana kwafewa, wannan kamfanin zai zama cibiyar iko da kuma wurin da gazawarsa za ta jawo gazawar tsarin.

An ƙirƙiri Bitcoin don magance wannan matsala ta hanyar [hanyar sadarwa ta tsara-da-tsara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#peer-to-peer-network). Tsarinsa yana ba mahalarta damar cimma matsaya kan [ma'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) a tarihi guda ɗaya ba tare da wani mai kula na tsakiya da [rajistar lissafi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger) ba. Yana haɗa buɗaɗɗiyar [rajistar lissafi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger) ([sarƙar tubalan bayanai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#blockchain)), [sa hannun dijital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-signature), hanyar sadarwa ta [na'urori masu tabbatar da inganci](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#full-node), [hujjar aiki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work), da abubuwan ƙarfafawa na tattalin arziki.

Bitcoin ba ya kawar da amincewa daga kowane ɓangare na rayuwa. Yana rage buƙatar amincewa da wata hukuma ta tsakiya wajen fitar da kuɗinsa da kammala ma'amalolinsu.

A cikin wannan littafi, **Bitcoin** da babban harafin B yawanci yana nufin hanyar sadarwa, ƙa'idar aiki, ko tsarin gaba ɗaya. Kuma **bitcoin** da ƙaramin harafin b yana nufin ma'aunin kuɗin ko [kadara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset). **BTC** shi ne taƙaitaccen suna da aka saba amfani da shi wajen nuna adadin bitcoin, musamman wajen rubuta [farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) ko takamaiman adadi. Walat ko dandalin musayar kuɗi sabis ne da zai iya haɗuwa da Bitcoin; ba shi ne [hanyar sadarwar Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-network) kanta ba.
