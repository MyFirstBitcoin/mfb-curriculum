# 2.2 Neman kuɗin dijital marasa cibiyar iko

Yawaitar kwamfutocin mutum ɗaya da intanet ya samar da sabbin damar sadarwa. Haka kuma ya samar da sabbin hanyoyin sa ido da sarrafawa. A ƙarshen ƙarni na ashirin, wata ƙungiyar masu tsara manhajoji, masana lissafi, da masu fafutuka da aka sani da [**Cypherpunks**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cypherpunk) ta binciki yadda [ilimin ɓoye bayanai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cryptography) zai iya kare [sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy) da ’yancin ɗaiɗaikun mutane a tsarin dijital.


> **Definition – Ilimin ɓoye bayanai**
>
> [Ilimin ɓoye bayanai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cryptography) yana amfani da hanyoyin lissafi don kare bayanai da tabbatar da cewa wani aiki na gaskiya ne. [Ɓoye bayanai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#encryption) na iya kiyaye sirrin saƙo. [Sa hannun dijital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-signature) na iya tabbatar da cewa saƙo ko umarni ya fito ne daga mai riƙe da maɓallin sirri ba tare da bayyana wannan maɓalli ba.


Ƙungiyar [Cypherpunks](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cypherpunk) ta fahimci cewa sadarwa ta sirri kaɗai ba ta isa ba. Mutane kuma suna buƙatar hanyar musayar [ƙima](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) a intanet. Idan kowane biyan kuɗi na dijital yana buƙatar banki ko kamfani, wannan [ɗan tsakiya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#intermediary) zai iya sa ido kan musayar, ya ba da izininta, ko ya hana ta.

Manufarsu ba kawai samar da kuɗin dijital ba ce. [Kuɗaɗen da aka ajiye a banki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bank-deposit) sun riga sun kasance na dijital. Manufar da ta fi wahalar cimmawa ita ce samar da **kuɗin dijital marasa cibiyar iko guda**: tsarin da babu wani [mai fitar da kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer), sabar, ko mai gudanarwa guda ɗaya da dole mahalarta su dogara da shi.

An gina Bitcoin bisa ayyukan da mutane da yawa suka yi tsawon shekarun da suka kai gommai. Tsarinsa ya haɗa ra’ayoyin da suka gabace shi ta wata sabuwar hanya, ya kuma samar da abubuwan ƙarfafa gwiwa domin wata hanyar sadarwa da kowa zai iya shiga ta ci gaba da aiki.
