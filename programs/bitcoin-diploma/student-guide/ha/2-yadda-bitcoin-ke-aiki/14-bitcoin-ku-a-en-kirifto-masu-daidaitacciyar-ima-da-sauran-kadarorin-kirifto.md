# 2.14 Bitcoin, kuɗaɗen kirifto masu daidaitacciyar ƙima, da sauran kadarorin kirifto

[Kadarorin dijital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-asset) na iya kama da juna a kan allo alhali suna dogara da tsare-tsare masu bambanci sosai. Bitcoin yana da ƙa'idojin adadin da ake samarwa waɗanda kowa zai iya gani, kuma ba shi da [mai fitarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) da zai iya ƙirƙirar sabon bitcoin a wajen waɗannan ƙa'idoji. Stablecoin yawanci yana dogara da wani [mai fitarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer), kadarorin ajiya, haƙƙoƙin da doka ta tanada, ko wani tsari da aka tsara domin bin ƙimar wata [kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency). Sauran kadarorin kirifto na iya samun mutane ko ƙungiyoyi daban-daban da ke sarrafa fitarwa, sauya ƙa'idoji, ko gudanar da muhimman sassan tsarin.

Yayin da kuke kwatanta kowace [kadara ta dijital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-asset), ku tambaya: Wa ke sarrafa fitarwa ko sauye-sauye? Me zan iya tantancewa da kaina? Wa ke riƙe da maɓallan? Wane kamfani, [mai fitarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer), ko wani ɓangaren mu'amala ne dole in amince da shi? Wannan Diploma da ya keɓanta ga Bitcoin yana amfani da kwatantawar ne kawai don fayyace Bitcoin. Ba ya ba da shawarar mallaka, ciniki, ko tallata kowace [kadara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset).

Kwatantawar da ke ƙasa tana bambanta rukunoni da zaɓuɓɓukan tsara su. Ba nuna goyon baya ba ne, ko bayar da shawara, ko iƙirarin cewa kowace [kadara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) tana da aminci, abin dogaro ce, ko ta dace da ku.

> Zamba, iƙirarin yaudara, da ayyuka marasa aminci na iya shafar kowace [kadara ta dijital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-asset) ko hanyar biyan kuɗi. Ku binciki takamaiman [kadara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset), sabis, da [riƙon amana](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) da aka tsara maimakon ɗauka cewa rukuni yana da aminci ko ba shi da aminci saboda rukunin da yake ciki kawai.


**Bitcoin (BTC)** wata [kadara ta dijital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-asset) ce a kan [cibiyar sadarwar Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-network). [Iyakar adadin da za a iya samarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#maximum-supply) da [jadawalin fitarwarsa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuance-schedule) ƙa'idoji ne da kowa zai iya gani. Mutane na iya riƙe bitcoin kai tsaye ta hanyar maɓallai, kuma za su iya tantance ƙa'idojin Bitcoin da kansu ta hanyar gudanar da manhajar da ta dace. Ana iya aika shi ko'ina a duniya ba tare da buƙatar wani mai iko na tsakiya a matsayin [mai fitarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) na Bitcoin domin amincewa da kowace [mu'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction). A kasuwa, [farashinsa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) na iya sauyawa sosai, kuma rasa maɓallai ko bayanan dawo da damar amfani na iya jawo asara ta dindindin; yawanci yana da wuya ko ma ba zai yiwu ba a soke biyan kuɗin da aka yi bisa kuskure. Kuɗaɗen caji, [tabbatarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#confirmation) da tsawon lokacinta, wutar lantarki, hanyoyin sadarwa, bayanan [mu'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) da kowa zai iya gani, da karɓuwarsa a yankinku ma na iya shafar amfani da shi. Mai samar da walat ko dandalin musaya na iya ƙara nasa haɗurran da suka shafi ɓangaren mu'amala da [riƙon amana](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody).

Wasu ayyuka suna amfani da lambar shirin Bitcoin a matsayin tushen farawa amma suna aiki ƙarƙashin ƙa'idoji ko cibiyoyin sadarwa daban. Ana yawan kiran waɗannan rassan da suka ɓulla. Samun tushen lambar shiri ɗaya a tarihi ba ya sa irin wannan reshe ya zama ɓangare na Bitcoin, ko ya ba shi ƙa'idojin Bitcoin, mahalartansa, ko tsaronsa.

**Kuɗaɗen dijital masu daidaitacciyar ƙima**, kamar USD Coin (USDC) da Tether (USDT), alamomin dijital ne da aka tsara don bin wata ƙayyadaddiyar [ƙima](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value), sau da yawa raka'a ɗaya ta [kuɗin ƙasa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) kamar dalar Amurka. Wasu na iya zama masu amfani wajen tura [ƙimar alamar dijital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) tsakanin ayyuka ko hanyoyin sadarwa masu jituwa. Tsarinsu, ajiyarsu, haƙƙoƙin fansarsu, da hanyoyin sarrafa su sun bambanta sosai. Kuɗin dijital mai daidaitacciyar ƙima na iya rasa [ƙimar da aka nufa ya riƙe](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) (“rabuwa da ƙimar da aka ɗaura masa”), kuma masu amfani na iya dogara ga [mai fitar da shi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer), mai kula da [ajiya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custodian), banki, tsarin fansa na doka, mai gudanar da [sarkar bayanai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#blockchain), ko gadar haɗa hanyoyin sadarwa. [Masu fitar da su](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) ko dandamali na iya daskarar da alamomin dijital ko ƙuntata damar amfani da su idan ƙa'idojinsu ko dokoki sun ba da dama. Ingancin ajiya, bayyana bayanai a fili, rashin iya biyan bashi, dokokin sa ido, kurakuran kwangiloli masu sarrafa kansu, zamba, da kuɗaɗen amfani da hanyar sadarwa su ma ƙarin haɗurra ne.

**Sauran kadarorin kirifto** babban rukuni ne na [kadarori](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) da ake amfani da su a wasu hanyoyin sadarwa ko manhajoji. Ƙa'idojinsu na iya ba da damar ayyuka kamar biyan kuɗaɗen hanyar sadarwa, kulle kadarori don tallafa wa hanyar sadarwa, gudanar da mulki, ko amfani da manhaja. Fitarwa, iko, tsarin fasaha, da abin da za a iya tantancewa da kansa sun bambanta daga wata [kadara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) zuwa wata. [Farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) da sauƙin saye ko sayarwa na iya sauyawa da sauri, kuma masu haɓakawa, gidauniyoyi, masu tabbatar da mu'amaloli, masu gudanarwa, ko manyan masu riƙe da kadarori na iya samun gagarumin tasiri, gwargwadon tsarin. Sauye-sauyen ƙa'idoji, fitar da alamomin dijital, kurakuran kwangiloli masu sarrafa kansu, kutse, gazawar gadojin haɗa hanyoyin sadarwa, zamba, gazawar dandamali, da rashin fayyace matsayinsu a doka na iya haifar da haɗarin asara ko rasa damar amfani. Riƙe alamar dijital ba ya ba mai amfani mallakar kamfani, tabbataccen kuɗin shiga, ko haƙƙin fansarta da wata [kadara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset).


| Nau'in kadara | Babban tsari | Babban bambanci |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bitcoin | Ƙa'idojin wadatar kuɗi a bayyane suke kuma babu mai fitar da su na tsakiya. | Ƙa'idojin da ake tantancewa da kansu ne ke tabbatar da iyakarsa ta miliyan 21, ba ajiya ko alkawuran fansa ba. |
| Kuɗaɗen dijital masu daidaitacciyar ƙima | Alamar dijital da aka tsara don bin wata ƙayyadaddiyar ƙima. | Ƙimar da aka nufa ya riƙe ta dogara ne da takamaiman ƙa'idojinsa, ajiyarsa, tsarin fansarsa, da sauran ɓangarorin da ake mu'amala da su; waɗannan sun bambanta daga wani kuɗin dijital mai daidaitacciyar ƙima zuwa wani. |
| Sauran kadarorin kirifto | Kadarorin dijital da ake amfani da su a hanyoyin sadarwa ko manhajoji. | Ƙa'idoji, fitarwa, da iko sun bambanta gwargwadon kadara. |


Kafin ku yi amfani da kowace [kadara ta dijital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-asset), ku bambanta [kadarar](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) kanta da sabis ɗin da ke riƙe muku da ita. Gajeriyar alamar kadara da kuka sani, ko [farashin da yake kama da mai daidaito](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price), ko manhajar da aka tsara da kyau ba sa kawar da buƙatar gano da kwatanta haɗurran [ajiya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), na ɗayan ɓangaren mu'amala, na fasaha, na doka, da na asara.
