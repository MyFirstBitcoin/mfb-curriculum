# 2.16 Jadawalin tarihin asalin Bitcoin

Bitcoin ya bayyana ta hanyar ɗan jerin abubuwan da suka faru a bainar jama'a.


<!-- micrographic: bitcoin-origin-timeline-01 -->


Ku binciki bayanan asali: ku karanta [farar takardar Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-whitepaper) ([PDF na asali](https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf)), ku duba [Toshen Farko](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#genesis-block) ([bayanan toshe](https://mempool.space/block/000000000019d6689c085ae165831e934ff763ae46a2a6c172b3f1b60a8ce26f)) da mu'amalar farko da aka rubuta ta [mutum-da-mutum](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#person-to-person-transaction) ([bayanan mu'amala](https://mempool.space/tx/f4184fc596403b9d638783cf57adfe4c75c605f6356fbc91338530e9831e9e16)), sannan ku karanta [ainihin buƙatar pizza](https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=137.0). [Na'urar binciken toshe](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#block-explorer) hanya ce mai sauƙi ta samun bayanan da kowa zai iya dubawa, ba ita ce babbar hukuma mai iko kan ƙa'idojin Bitcoin ba.

[**Satoshi Nakamoto**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi-nakamoto) sunan ɓoye ne da wanda ya ƙirƙiri Bitcoin ko waɗanda suka ƙirƙire shi suke amfani da shi, kuma ba a san ko su wane ne ba. [Satoshi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi-nakamoto) ya yi sadarwa ta intanet, ya fitar da manhajar tare da buɗe lambar tushenta ga kowa, sannan daga baya ya daina shiga harkar.

Babu wani kamfani na tsakiya da ya mallaki Bitcoin. Manhajar da ƙa'idojin sun ci gaba saboda wasu mutane sun zaɓi gudanar da su, nazartarsu, da haɓaka su. Wannan asali yana da muhimmanci: wanda ya ƙirƙire shi bai ci gaba da zama shugaba na dindindin wanda zai iya fitar da ƙarin tsabar kuɗi ko amincewa da [mu'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction).

Wannan [Toshen Farko](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#genesis-block) da rikicin kuɗi na 2008 suna ba da muhimmin mahallin tarihi ga Bitcoin. Duk da haka, ku tantance Bitcoin bisa tsarinsa da yadda yake aiki, ba bisa labarin wanda ya ƙirƙire shi kaɗai ba.

A watan Oktoba na 2008, [Satoshi Nakamoto](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi-nakamoto) ya sanar da [farar takardar Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-whitepaper). Babbar tambayar da ta gabatar mai sauƙi ce: **ta yaya mutane za su iya biyan kuɗi ta intanet ba tare da barin a kashe rukunin kuɗin dijital ɗaya sau biyu ba ko sanya wani [mai shiga tsakani guda](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#intermediary) a matsayin mai iko?**
