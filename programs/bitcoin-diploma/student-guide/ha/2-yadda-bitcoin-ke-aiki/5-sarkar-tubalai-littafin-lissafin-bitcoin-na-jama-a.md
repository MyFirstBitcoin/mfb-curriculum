# 2.5 Sarkar Tubalai: Littafin Lissafin Bitcoin na Jama’a

> **Definition – Sarkar tubala**
>
> Tsarin Bitcoin na [sarkar tubala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#blockchain) rajistar jama'a ce ta [ma'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) wadda ake kwafewa a kan kwamfutoci masu zaman kansu da yawa. Tubali yana tattara wasu ingantattun [ma'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) a matsayin sashe na gaba da aka gabatar don ƙara wa wannan rajista. Kowane ingantaccen tubali yana haɗuwa da wanda ya gabace shi, yana ba cibiyar sadarwar tarihin bai ɗaya mai jeri don tantancewa.



> **Definition – Na'urar mahaɗi**
>
> [Na'urorin mahaɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) suna bincika ƙa'idoji iri ɗaya da kansu, su kuma daidaita a kan ingantaccen tarihin da ya fi tara [tabbacin aiki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work). Sauya bayanin da aka rubuta a baya yana sauya tubalinsa kuma yana katse alaƙoƙinsa da tubalan da suka biyo baya, don haka dole ne tarihin da ke gogayya da shi ya sake yin wannan aikin kuma ya cim masa kafin [na'urorin mahaɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) su karɓe shi. Wannan tsarin jera bayanai mai tsadar aiwatarwa yana taimaka wa cibiyar sadarwar ta cimma [yarjejeniya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#consensus) ba tare da mai kula da rajista na tsakiya ba. Kwafe-kwafen ba sa sauyawa a lokaci guda; [na'urorin mahaɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) suna karɓar bayanai kuma suna tantance ingancinsu da kansu. Bitcoin na nufin samar da sabon tubali guda ɗaya kusan kowane minti goma a matsakaici. Sake rubuta tarihin yana da wahala da tsada, amma ba yana nufin cewa sauya shi ba zai taɓa yiwuwa ba: ana iya samun tarihohi masu gogayya na ɗan lokaci da kuma sake tsara jerin tubala.
