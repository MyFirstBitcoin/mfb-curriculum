# 2.5 Sarkar Tubalai: Littafin Lissafin Bitcoin na Jama’a

<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Sarkar tubalan**
>
> Bitcoin yana da [sarkar tubalan](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#blockchain) wadda take rajistar jama'a ta [ma'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) da ake kwafewa a kan kwamfutoci masu zaman kansu da yawa. Tubali yana haɗa rukunin ingantattun [ma'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) a matsayin sashe na gaba da aka gabatar domin ƙarawa cikin wannan rajista. Kowane ingantaccen tubali yana da alaƙa da wanda ya gabace shi, yana ba hanyar sadarwar tarihin bai ɗaya mai tsari domin tantancewa.



<!-- card: light -->
> **Yadda na'urorin hanyar sadarwa suke cimma matsaya ɗaya**
>
> [Na'urorin hanyar sadarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) suna bincika ƙa'idoji iri ɗaya da kansu, su kuma haɗu a kan ingantaccen tarihin da ya tara mafi yawan [hujjar aiki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work). Sauya wani bayanin da aka rubuta a baya yana sauya tubalinsa kuma yana yanke alaƙoƙinsa da tubalan da suka zo daga baya. Saboda haka, tarihin da ke gogayya da shi dole ne ya sake yin wannan aikin kuma ya cim masa kafin [na'urorin hanyar sadarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) su karɓe shi. Wannan tsari na jera bayanai mai cin kuɗi da albarkatu yana taimaka wa hanyar sadarwar ta cimma [matsaya ɗaya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#consensus) ba tare da wani mai kula da rajista na tsakiya ba. Kwafe-kwafen ba sa sauyawa a lokaci guda; [na'urorin hanyar sadarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) suna karɓa da tantance bayanai da kansu. Bitcoin yana nufin samar da sabon tubali guda kusan kowane minti goma a matsakaici. Sake rubuta tarihin yana da wahala kuma yana cin kuɗi da albarkatu, amma hakan ba yana nufin sauya shi ba zai taɓa yiwuwa ba: tarihin da ke gogayya da juna na ɗan lokaci da sake tsara jerin tubalan na iya faruwa.
