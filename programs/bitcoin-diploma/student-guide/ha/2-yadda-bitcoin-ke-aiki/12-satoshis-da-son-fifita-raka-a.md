# 2.12 Satoshis da son fifita raka'a

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/98c8cef9bc6e6d0955458f6fb4a71f1de5ed5225-1536x1024.svg)



Ana iya raba bitcoin ɗaya (1 BTC) zuwa **100,000,000 [satoshi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi)**. Shi kuwa [satoshi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), wanda ake yawan taƙaitawa da [**sat**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), shi ne mafi ƙanƙantar raka'ar da ake rubutawa a matakin tushe na Bitcoin.

**Ba sai kun saya ko kun yi amfani da cikakken bitcoin ɗaya ba.** Manhajar Bitcoin na iya nuna adadi a bitcoin ko [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), kamar yadda [kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) zai iya amfani da babbar raka'a da ƙananan raka'o'i. Mutane da yawa suna amfani da [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi) wajen bayyana adadin kuɗin yau da kullum domin cikakkun lambobi sun fi sauƙin karantawa. Ana iya nuna buƙatun biyan kuɗi na kan sarkar da na Lightning duka a [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi).

**Ɗaukar raka'a [da son zuciya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bias)** shi ne kuskuren tunanin cewa ƙarancin [farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) ga kowace cikakkiyar raka'a yana sa [kadara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) ta fi arha ko ta fi sauƙin samu. Yawan raka'o'i zaɓi ne da ake yi yayin ƙirƙira. Mallakar 100,000 [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi) da mallakar 0.001 BTC suna nufin adadi ɗaya ne.

Yayin kwatanta [farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price), ku mai da hankali kan rabon da adadin yake wakilta daga jimillar wadatar, adadin [ƙimar](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) da abin ya ƙunsa, da haɗarurrukan da ke tattare da shi. Kada ku ɗauka cewa [kadara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) mai raka'o'i da yawa da suke kama da masu arha tana bayar da [ƙimar da ta fi kyau](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) fiye da [kadara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) da aka raba zuwa ƙananan raka'o'i.
