# 2.13 Bitcoin a matsayin ingantaccen kuɗin dijital

Kamar yadda **Kashi na 1** ya gabatar, [**kuɗi masu inganci**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#sound-money) na nufin kuɗin da yake da wuya a ƙara adadinsu, kuma halayensu suna ba da damar amfani da su cikin aminci na dogon lokaci. Kalmar tana bayyana hukunci bisa wasu ƙa'idojin tantancewa, don haka ya kamata a bincike ta maimakon karɓarta a matsayin suna kawai.

Bitcoin ya haɗa halaye waɗanda a baya ba su taɓa kasancewa tare a cikin wata [kadara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset):

| Hali | Bayani cikin sauƙaƙan harshe |
| --- | --- |
| Dorewa | Ana adana kwafin kundin lissafin a na'urorin hanyar sadarwa da yawa. Samun damar amfani da kuɗin ya dogara da kiyaye ingantattun maɓallai. |
| Sauƙin ɗauka | Ma'amalar da aka sanya wa hannu tana iya tura darajar kuɗi ta hanyar sadarwa. |
| Iya rarrabawa | bitcoin guda ɗaya (1 BTC) yana rabuwa zuwa sats 100,000,000. |
| Iya musanyawa da juna | Ana iya kashe adadin kuɗi masu daidaito a ƙarƙashin ƙa'idojin yarjejeniya iri ɗaya, ko da yake tarihin ma'amaloli da ke bayyane ga jama'a na iya shafar yadda wasu masu ba da sabis suke mu'amala da wasu takamaiman kuɗaɗe. |
| Ƙaranci | Samar da sababbin kuɗaɗe yana bin ƙa'idojin da ke bayyane ga jama'a, tare da iyakar 21 miliyan. |
| Iya tabbatarwa | Na'urar hanyar sadarwa za ta iya tabbatar da adadin kuɗin da aka samar, ƙa'idojin ma'amaloli, da hujjojin mallaka. |
| Karɓuwa | Karɓuwarsa na ƙaruwa a wasu wurare, amma har yanzu ta yi ƙasa sosai da ta kuɗaɗen ƙasashe a al'ummomi da yawa. |


Bitcoin yana iya gudanar da ayyukan kuɗi guda uku, amma ba daidai gwargwado a kowane wuri ba:

* A matsayin [**ma'ajiyar daraja**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#store-of-value), ana iya hasashen adadin da za a samar, amma [farashinsa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) a kasuwa yana yawan sauyawa.
* A matsayin [**hanyar musayar kuɗi**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#medium-of-exchange), ana iya tura shi ba tare da banki ba, amma karɓuwarsa, kuɗaɗen caji, da kayan aikin biyan kuɗi sun bambanta.
* A matsayin [**ma'aunin lissafi**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#unit-of-account), ana iya bayyana adadin kuɗi a bitcoin ko [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), amma har yanzu ana sanya farashin yawancin kayayyaki da [kuɗin fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency).

Saboda haka, Bitcoin yana da fa'idodi da kuma [rashin alfanu da ke tattare da su](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off).

**Fa'idodin da za a iya samu** sun haɗa da mallaka kai tsaye, adadin kuɗin da za a iya hasashen samarwarsa, turawa a faɗin duniya, damar shiga ga kowa, ƙa'idoji masu bayyana, da juriya ga yunƙurin hana ma'amaloli daga ɓangare guda.

[**Abubuwan da ake sadaukarwa**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) sun haɗa da [farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) [mai yawan canzawa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#volatility), alhakin kula da tsaron kuɗinku da kanku, kasancewar yawanci yana da wuya ko ba zai yiwu ba a soke ingantaccen biyan kuɗi, sauye-sauyen [kuɗaɗen mu'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction-fee), dogaro da wutar lantarki da hanyoyin sadarwa wajen tura kuɗi ta dijital, damar jama'a ta ganin [mu'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) da aka rubuta, da ƙarancin wuraren da ake karɓarsa.

Ko za ku kira Bitcoin [kuɗi mai inganci](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#sound-money) ya kamata ya dogara da ma'aunanku. Ku kwatanta shi da [kuɗin fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money), karafa masu daraja, da sauran [kadarori](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) ta amfani da ma'aunai iri ɗaya.

**Binciken daraja a cikin al'umma:** Shin bitcoin yana da [daraja](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) a cikin al'ummarku? Ku ware tambayoyi uku a cikin amsarku: A kasuwa, wane [farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) ake iya gani? A ina ake karɓarsa, idan ma akwai? Wane zaɓi na kanku ko [son zuciya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bias) yake shafar hukuncinku? [Farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price), amfaninsa a cikin al'umma, da amincewar mutum suna da alaƙa amma ba abu ɗaya ba ne.
