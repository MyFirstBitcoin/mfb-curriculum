# 2.15 Koyo da amfani cikin alhaki ba ciniki ko tallatawa ba ne

Koyon yadda Bitcoin yake aiki ya bambanta da ƙoƙarin samun riba cikin ɗan gajeren lokaci daga [farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) da ke sauyawa, shawo kan wasu su sayi token, ko ɗaukar hasashen kasuwa don samun riba a matsayin ilimi. Ciniki da hasashen kasuwa don samun riba na iya ƙunsar yanke shawara cikin gaggawa, asara, haɗarin dandalin ciniki, da matsin lambar ɗaukar mataki ba tare da cikakken tunani ba. Tallata token na iya haifar da saɓanin maslahohi da kuma ba da lada ga iya shawo kan mutane maimakon bayani mai haske.

Manufar Difloma ta Bitcoin ita ce ta taimaka muku ku bayyana Bitcoin, ku yi tambayoyi masu zurfi, kuma ku yanke shawarwari masu rage haɗari. Ba ta ba da shawarar zuba jari ko gaya muku abin da za ku saya, ku sayar, ko ku riƙe ba.
