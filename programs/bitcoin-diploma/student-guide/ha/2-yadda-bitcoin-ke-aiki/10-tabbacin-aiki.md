> **Definition – Tabbacin aiki**
>
> [Tabbacin aiki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work) shi ne tsarin da [masu haƙa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) ke amfani da shi don yin gasa kan samun damar gabatar da tubali na gaba.


Wani [aikin hash](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#hash-function) yana mayar da bayanai zuwa sakamako mai tsawon da aka kayyade. Ƙaramin sauyi ga bayanan da aka shigar yana samar da sabon sakamakon da ba a iya hasashensa. Wani [mai haƙa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) yana sauya ƙaramin ɓangaren bayanai a cikin tubalin da aka gabatar, sannan ya yi masa hash akai-akai. Sakamako yana zama ingantacce ne kawai idan adadinsa ya yi ƙasa da adadin da cibiyar sadarwa ta sa a matsayin [iyakar manufa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#target), wato iyakar da ƙa'idoji suka kayyade; ƙaramar [iyakar manufa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#target) tana nufin ƙarancin [ingantattun sakamako](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#valid-result) da ƙarin yunƙuri. Babu wata gajeriyar hanya, don haka [masu haƙa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) na iya buƙatar yin yunƙuri da yawa.

Lokacin da wani [mai haƙa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) ya sami [ingantaccen sakamako](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#valid-result), sai ya [yaɗa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#broadcast) tubalin. [Na'urorin cibiyar sadarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) na iya bincika tabbacin cikin sauri, duk da cewa samunsa ya buƙaci lissafi da makamashi mai yawa. Idan tubalin ya ƙunshi wata [ma'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction), wannan [ma'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) tana samun [tabbatarwarta ta farko](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#first-confirmation): an haɗa bayananta a cikin ingantaccen tubali guda ɗaya. Kowane ingantaccen tubali da aka ƙara bayansa yana ƙara wata [tabbatarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#confirmation), wanda hakan ke sa matsayin [ma'amalar](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) a cikin [sarkar tubalai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#blockchain) ya fi tsadar sake rubutawa. Wannan bambanci yana da muhimmanci: samar da aikin yana da tsada, amma tabbatar da shi yana da sauƙi.

[Tabbacin aiki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work) yana da manufofi da dama:

* yana ba wa [masu haƙa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) masu zaman kansu hanyar yin gasa ba tare da mai tsara jadawali na tsakiya ba
* yana sa sake rubuta jerin [ma'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) ya zama mai tsada
* yana danganta tsaron cibiyar sadarwa da kayan aiki da makamashi na zahiri
* yana tallafa wa hanyar da kowa zai iya amfani da ita don zaɓar tsakanin ingantattun tarihin da ke gogayya da juna: sarkar da ta fi yawan [aikin da aka tara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#accumulated-work)

[Haƙar ma'adinai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work) ba ta warware lissafi mai amfani don wata manufa ta waje. Manufarta ita ce tabbatar da tsaron yadda Bitcoin ke jera [ma'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction). Cibiyar sadarwar tana daidaita ƙimar da ake son cimmawa lokaci zuwa lokaci: idan bulokai suna zuwa da sauri fiye da kima, tana rage ƙimar, idan kuma suna zuwa a hankali fiye da kima, tana ƙara ta. Ana yin hakan ne da nufin samun kusan buloki ɗaya a kowane minti goma a matsakaici, yayin da ƙarfin sarrafa lissafi ke sauyawa.

[Tabbacin aiki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work) yana buƙatar kashe makamashi na gaske. Magoya bayansa suna cewa wannan kashe makamashi ne ke kafa tushen tsaron Bitcoin, kuma yana iya haifar da buƙatar makamashin da za a iya daidaita amfani da shi ko wanda da ba haka ba zai kasance ba a amfani da shi. Masu suka suna tambayar yawan makamashin da ake amfani da shi da kuma tushensa. Tantancewa cikin alhaki tana tambayar wane makamashi ake amfani da shi, daga ina yake fitowa, wane irin tsaro yake samarwa, da kuma waɗanne hanyoyin madadin ake kwatantawa.
