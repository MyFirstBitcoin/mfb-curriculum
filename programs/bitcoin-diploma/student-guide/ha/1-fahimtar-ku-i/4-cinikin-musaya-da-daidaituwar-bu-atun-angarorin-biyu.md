# 1.4 Cinikin musaya da daidaituwar buƙatun ɓangarorin biyu

<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Cinikin musayar kaya**
>
> [Cinikin musayar kaya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#barter) shi ne musayar wani kaya ko wata hidima da wani kaya ko wata hidima kai tsaye. Yana iya tafiya da kyau idan musayar ba ta da yawa, amma yana zama da wahala idan mutane da kayayyaki da yawa suka shiga.


Ku yi tunanin cewa **Amina** tana da shinkafa kuma tana son takalma. **Luis** yana da takalma amma yana son man girki, ba shinkafa ba. **Mei** tana da man girki kuma tana son a yi mata aikin gyara. Amina na iya buƙatar yin musaya sau da yawa kafin ta samu takalman. Kowace musaya tana ɗaukar lokaci kuma tana buƙatar cimma yarjejeniya kan [daraja](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value).

Babu wasu mutum biyu da za su iya yin musaya kai tsaye, domin babu ɗayansu da yake son abin da ɗayan yake bayarwa.](/micrographics/barter-mismatch-01/ha.md)


Domin [cinikin musayar kaya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#barter) ya gudana kai tsaye, dole ne kowane mutum ya so abin da ɗayan yake bayarwa a lokaci guda kuma a adadin da ya dace. Masana tattalin arziki suna kiran wannan [**daidaituwar buƙatun ɓangarori biyu**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#double-coincidence-of-wants).

[Cinikin musayar kaya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#barter) yana kuma haifar da wasu matsaloli:

* ba za a iya raba wasu kayayyaki ba tare da lalata [darajarsu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) ba
* wasu kayayyaki suna lalacewa kafin a iya musanya su
* babu ma'auni guda da kowa ke amfani da shi wajen kwatanta [farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price)
* adana kayayyaki masu yawa na iya zama da wahala
* biyan kuɗi daga baya da lissafin kuɗi suna zama masu sarƙaƙiya

Kuɗin da aka amince da su a ko'ina suna raba sayarwa da saye. Amina za ta iya sayar wa mutum ɗaya shinkafa ta karɓi kuɗi, sannan daga baya ta yi amfani da waɗannan kuɗin wajen sayen takalma daga wani dabam.
