# 1.10 Me ya sa dalar Amurka ta zama kuɗin ajiyar duniya?

Wani **ajiyayyen [kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency)** shi ne [kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) da [manyan bankunan ƙasa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank) da gwamnatoci suke riƙewa kuma suke amfani da shi wajen biyan kuɗaɗe tsakanin ƙasashe. Kafin Yaƙin Duniya na Ɗaya, fam na Birtaniya ne ya fi taka wannan rawa. Yaƙin ya raunana [kuɗaɗen ƙasashen Turai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) da cibiyoyin harkokin kuɗi, yayin da Amurka ta samar da kayayyaki tare da tara [zinariya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold). Wannan ya ƙara yawan amfani da dala a tsakanin ƙasashe.

Bayan Yaƙin Duniya na Biyu, Amurka tana da babban tattalin arziki, kuma yawancin ƙarfinta na harkokin kuɗi bai lalace ba. Sai Yarjejeniyar [Bretton Woods](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bretton-woods) ta 1944 ta tsara wani tsarin bayan yaƙi wanda a cikinsa aka danganta [kuɗaɗen ƙasashe](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) da yawa da dala, sannan aka danganta dala a hukumance da [zinariya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold), wanda ya sanya dala a cibiyar biyan kuɗaɗe tsakanin ƙasashe.

A 1971, Shugaba Nixon ya kawo ƙarshen musayar dala da [zinariya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold) a hukumance da gwamnatocin ƙasashen waje da [manyan bankunan ƙasa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank) suke yi. Wannan ya kawo ƙarshen damar musayar dala da [zinariya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold) [a hukumance](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#convertibility) kuma ya kawo ƙarshen tsarin [Bretton Woods](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bretton-woods). Duk da haka, an ci gaba da amfani da dala sosai saboda rawar da ta riga ta kafu tana takawa a kasuwanci da harkokin kuɗi, girman tattalin arzikin Amurka, da kuma faɗi da yalwar kasuwannin kuɗin Amurka. Har yanzu ita ce ke kan gaba a matsayin ajiyayyen [kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) a yau, duk da cewa [manyan bankunan ƙasa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank) suna kuma riƙe da wasu [kuɗaɗen ƙasashe](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency), kuma kason dala na iya canzawa da shigewar lokaci.
