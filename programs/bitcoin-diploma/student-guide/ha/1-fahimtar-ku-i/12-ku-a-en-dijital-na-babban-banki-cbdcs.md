# 1.12 Kuɗaɗen Dijital na Babban Banki (CBDCs)

<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Kuɗin dijital na babban banki (CBDC)**
>
> Wani [kuɗin dijital na babban banki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc), ko [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc), nau'in dijital ne na [kuɗin fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money) da ake fitarwa a matsayin [wajibin biyan bashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liability) na wani [babban banki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank): haƙƙin karɓar bashi ne wanda [babban banki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank) yake da alhakin biya ga mai riƙe da shi. Ya bambanta da tsabar kuɗi da kuma ajiyar kuɗi ta yau da kullum a bankin kasuwanci, ko da yake yadda ake amfani da su na iya zama iri ɗaya.


[CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc) yana da tsare-tsare mabambanta. Wasu an tsara su ne musamman domin cibiyoyin kuɗi; wasu kuma ana ba da shawarar amfani da su ga jama'a. Da farko ku tambayi wane ne [mai fitar da kuɗin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer): idan ana amfani da [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc), [babban banki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank) na ci gaba da zama [mai fitar da kuɗin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) kuma har yanzu yana iya aiwatar da manufofin kuɗi. Saboda haka, [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc) ba ya kafa ƙayyadadden adadin kuɗin da za su kasance ko kawar da iko daga hukuma ta tsakiya; kawai yana sauya fasaha da yadda ake rarraba [kuɗin fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money).

Na gaba, ku tambayi abin da tsarin ya ba da dama a yi. Dangane da ƙa'idojin, [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc) na iya ba da damar kammala biyan kuɗi cikin sauri, samun damar amfani ga mutane da yawa, ko sabbin fasalolin biyan kuɗi. Haka kuma yana iya ƙara wa cibiyoyi ikon sa ido kan [ma'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction), aiwatar da ƙa'idoji, takaita amfani, ko haɗa bayanan shaidar mutum kai tsaye da biyan kuɗi. Wannan iko ne da yake hannun hukuma ta tsakiya: wata cibiya ce take kafa ko sauya ƙa'idojin da suka shafi hakan, maimakon masu amfani na yau da kullum su yi hakan da kansu.

[Jadawalin da ke kwatanta kuɗin hannu, ajiyar banki, CBDC na jama'a da Bitcoin bisa ga wanda ke fitar da su, siffarsu, damar amfani da su, waɗanda ke ganin biyan kuɗi, ka'idojin samar da su da kuma tantancewa. Kasancewa na dijital ba ɗaya yake da rashin cibiyar iko guda ɗaya ba: har yanzu babban banki ne ke fitar da CBDC, kuma damar amfani da shi, waɗanda ke ganin biyan kuɗi da yadda ake tantance shi sun danganta da yadda aka tsara shi.](/micrographics/cbdc-comparison-table-01/ha.md)


Kasancewa na dijital ba ya nufin kai tsaye cewa babu iko a hannun hukuma ta tsakiya, ko adadinsa yana da iyaka, ko yana kare sirri, ko kuma kowa zai iya tabbatar da ingancinsa da kansa. [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc) na iya amfani da fasahar dijital, amma har yanzu [kuɗin fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money) ne idan [mai fitar da shi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer), da ƙa'idojin adadinsa, da ƙa'idojin samun damar amfani da shi, da izinin biyan kuɗi duk suna ƙarƙashin kulawar hukuma ta tsakiya.

Yayin tattaunawa kan [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc), ku yi tambayoyi:

* Wane ne yake fitar da kuɗin?
* Wa zai iya riƙe su ko amfani da su?
* Waɗanne bayanai ake tattarawa?
* Shin ana iya hana biyan kuɗi, takaita shi, ko soke shi a mayar da kuɗin?
* Shin ƙa'idojin za su iya sauyawa?
* Shin masu amfani na yau da kullum za su iya tabbatar da ƙa'idojin da kansu?
* Me zai faru idan babu wutar lantarki ko damar shiga intanet?
* Waɗanne kariyoyi na doka ake da su?

Kada ku ɗauka cewa kowace shawarar samar da [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc) tana da tsari iri ɗaya. Ku yi amfani da bayanai na yanzu, na yankinku, kuma abin dogaro.
