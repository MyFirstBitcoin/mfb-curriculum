# 1.3 Ayyuka da halayen kuɗi

Kuɗi yawanci suna yin manyan ayyuka uku.

**[Hanyar musaya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#medium-of-exchange):** Mutane suna karɓarsu a matsayin biyan kaya da ayyuka. Wannan yana rage buƙatar yin [cinikin kaya da kaya kai tsaye](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#barter).

**[Na'urar lissafi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#unit-of-account):** [Farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) ana bayyana su da ma'auni ɗaya da kowa yake amfani da shi. Wannan yana ba mutane damar kwatanta farashin kayayyaki daban-daban da adana bayanan harkokin kuɗi.

**[Ma'ajiyar ƙima](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#store-of-value):** [Ƙima](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) za a iya adana ta daga yanzu zuwa nan gaba. Kowane nau'in kuɗi yana da haɗarin da zai iya shafar [ikon saye](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power), amma mutane suna kwatanta yadda nau'ikan kuɗi daban-daban suke kiyaye wannan ikon a cikin wani zaɓaɓɓen lokaci da yanayi.

Abu na iya yin wani aiki fiye da wani. A yawancin tsarin kuɗi na yanzu, ana karɓar [kuɗin ƙasa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) a ko'ina don biyan kuɗin yau da kullum, yayin da mutane za su iya zaɓar wani [kadara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) don ajiyar dogon lokaci.

Kuɗi masu amfani kuma sukan kasance da halaye da dama:

Babu wani nau'in kuɗi da ya cika kowane ma'auni ba tare da wata gazawa ba.

Mutane kuma suna kwatanta kuɗi bisa [**ƙarfinsu**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#sound-money): ko yana da wuya a ƙara yawansu, da kuma ko halayensu suna sa a iya dogaro da amfani da su a tsawon lokaci. “[Kuɗi masu ƙarfi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#sound-money)” hukunci ne na tantancewa, ba lakabin da ke tabbatar da wata hujja ba. Don amfani da wannan kalma yadda ya kamata, ku bayyana ƙa'idojin tantancewa kuma ku kwatanta nau'ikan kuɗi daban-daban bisa ma'auni iri ɗaya.


<!-- micrographic: functions-and-properties-01 -->


[Zinariya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold) tana da ɗorewa kuma ba ta da yawa, amma rarraba ta da jigilar ta don ƙananan biyan kuɗi yana da wuya. [Kuɗin takarda](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#paper-cash) suna da sauƙin ɗauka kuma ana karɓarsu a cikin yankin da ake amfani da su, amma suna iya lalacewa kuma ana iya ƙara yawansu. Kwatancen da ya fi dacewa ya danganta da manufa, wuri, da lokaci.

**Ku yi tunani mai zurfi:** Ku faɗi wani lokaci da kuka zaɓi wani nau'in kuɗi ko [kadara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) maimakon wani. Mene ne manufar? Ku kwatanta halayen zaɓuɓɓukan da kuka iya amfani da su. Waɗanne halaye ne suka fi muhimmanci a gare ku, kuma me ya sa?
