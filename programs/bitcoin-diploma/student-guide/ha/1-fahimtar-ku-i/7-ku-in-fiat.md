# 1.7 Kuɗin fiat

> **Definition – Kuɗin fiat**
>
> [Kuɗin fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money) su ne [kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) da ake fitarwa bisa ikon gwamnati, yawanci ta hannun [babban banki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank), waɗanda ba za a iya fansarsu da wani ƙayyadadden adadin haja kamar [zinariya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold). Gwamnatoci suna tallafa wa amfani da su—kuma a wasu yanayi suna tilasta shi—ta hanyar wajabta biyan haraji da su da kuma kafa ƙa'idojin kuɗin da doka ta amince da su wajen biyan bashi. Takamaiman ƙa'idojin karɓar biyan kuɗi da warware [basussuka](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#debt) sun bambanta daga wannan yankin iko zuwa wancan.


[Kuɗin fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money) suna aiki ne saboda mutane, kasuwanci, bankuna, da gwamnatoci suna karɓarsu. Kowane irin kuɗi na iya samun [tasirin hanyar sadarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#network-effect): mutane suna amfani da su saboda wasu mutane suna amfani da su kuma ana bayyana [farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) da su. Ga [kuɗin fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money), wajibcin biyan haraji, ƙa'idojin kuɗin da doka ta amince da su wajen biyan bashi, da tsarin biyan kuɗi na cibiyoyi na iya ƙarfafa wannan hanyar sadarwa.

> **Light – Ku nazarci biyan kuɗi a yankinku**
>
> Ku zaɓi misali ɗaya na biyan kuɗi da aka saba yi a al'ummarku, kamar biyan kuɗin sufuri, abinci, ko katin waya; karɓar albashi; ko biyan wani lissafin kuɗi. Idan kun fi so, ku yi amfani da wani yanayi da kuka ƙirƙira wanda zai iya faruwa a zahiri. A cikin jimloli uku ko huɗu, ku bayyana:
>
> 1. abin da ake biyan kuɗinsa da kuma wane [kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) ake amfani da shi a matsayin [ma'aunin lissafi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#unit-of-account);
> 1. dalilin da ya sa mai sayarwa ko mai karɓa yake son karɓar wannan [kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency); da kuma
> 1. waɗanne cibiyoyi, bayanan da aka adana, ko ƙa'idoji mutane suke dogaro da su don kammala biyan kuɗin.
>
> Ku kammala da ambaton hanya ɗaya da biyan kuɗin zai zama mafi wahala idan wannan amincewar bai ɗaya ko yarda ta rushe.


Tsarin kuɗin fiat na iya samar da sauƙin biyan kuɗi, rance mai sassaucin sharuɗɗa, da hanyoyin magance rikice-rikicen kuɗi. Wannan sassauci kuma yana haifar da **haɗari**. Cibiyoyi kaɗan na iya sauya yawan kuɗin da ake samarwa da kuɗin samun su, ƙuntata damar samun su, ko yanke shawarwarin da suke rage [ƙarfin saye](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power).

[Kuɗin fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money) suna buƙatar matakai daban-daban na **yarda**. Masu amfani suna amincewa da bankuna su adana sahihan bayanai, da masu ba da hidimomin biyan kuɗi su gudanar da tura kuɗi, da [manyan bankuna](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank) su kula da [kuɗin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency), da gwamnatoci su tabbatar da dorewar cibiyoyi masu bin doka. Waɗannan tsare-tsare na iya aiki na dogon lokaci, amma ƙa'idojinsu ba tabbatattu ba ne kuma ana iya yin sakaci da su ko amfani da su ta hanyar da ba ta dace ba.
