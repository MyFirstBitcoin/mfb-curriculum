# 1.8 Tsarin Banki na Ajiyar Wani Ɓangare

Lokacin da kuka ajiye kuɗin hannu a banki, bankin yana rubuta [wajibcin biyan bashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liability) gare ku: yana bin ku bashin adadin kuɗin da ke asusunku. A al'ada, bankin ba ya riƙe kowane kwabo na duk kuɗin da aka ajiye a matsayin kuɗin hannu. Maimakon haka, yana riƙe [ajiyar kuɗin da za a iya amfani da su nan take](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liquidity)—kuɗin hannu ko [kadarori](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) da zai iya amfani da su da sauri wajen biyan kuɗi—kuma yana amfani da jadawalin kadarorinsa da basussukansa wajen bayar da lamuni da yin wasu zuba jari.

Lokacin da bankin kasuwanci ya amince da lamuni, yawanci yana ƙirƙirar sabon ajiya a asusun mai karɓar lamunin. Wannan yana ƙara yawan [kuɗin ajiya na banki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bank-deposit). Lokacin da aka biya asalin bashin, waɗannan [kuɗin ajiya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bank-deposit) suna raguwa. Abubuwan da ke iyakance ayyukan bankuna sun haɗa da [jari](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#capital) da ƙa'idojinsa, [wadatar kuɗin hannu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liquidity) da buƙatarta, [haɗarin rashin biyan bashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#credit-risk), dokokin sa ido, kuɗin samun jarin gudanarwa, da buƙatar lamuni daga [masu karɓar lamuni da suka cika sharuɗɗa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#qualified-borrower).

[Bayani mai sauƙi kan rancen banki: mai karɓar rance yana neman rance, bankin ya amince da rancen kuma ya ƙara sabon kuɗin ajiya a asusun mai karɓar rancen, sannan daga baya mai karɓar rancen ya mayar wa bankin ainihin kuɗin rancen tare da ruwa.](/micrographics/bank-loan-deposit-01/ha.md)


Bankuna gabaɗaya suna neman [riba](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bank-profit) ta hanyar samun kuɗin ruwa da kuɗaɗen caji daga lamuni da zuba jari, yayin da suke biyan kuɗaɗen gudanarwa, na samun jarin gudanarwa, da na bin dokokin sa ido, tare da ɗaukar asara idan masu karɓar lamuni ba su biya ba. Waɗannan ayyuka da iyakoki sun bambanta daga banki zuwa banki da kuma tsakanin yankunan da ke ƙarƙashin dokoki daban-daban.


<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Tsarin banki na riƙe wani kaso na ajiya**
>
> [Tsarin banki na riƙe wani kaso na ajiya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fractional-reserve-banking) yana bayyana tsarin da bankuna ke riƙe wani kaso kawai na kuɗin ajiyar da suke da wajibcin biya a matsayin ajiyar da za a iya amfani da ita nan take, yayin da suke bayar da lamuni da riƙe wasu [kadarori](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset). Adadin ajiyar da ake buƙata da sauran iyakoki sun bambanta tsakanin ƙasashe kuma suna iya sauyawa da shigewar lokaci.


Wannan yana haifar da muhimman haɗari guda biyu:

* **[Haɗarin rashin wadatar kuɗin hannu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liquidity-risk):** Banki na iya mallakar lamuni na dogon lokaci waɗanda ake sa ran za a biya, amma duk da haka ya rasa isassun kuɗin hannu ko [kadarori](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) da za a iya amfani da su da sauri don biyan buƙatun cire kuɗi da yawa a yau.
* **[Haɗarin rashin ikon biyan basussuka](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#solvency-risk):** Lamunin da ba a biya ba ko faɗuwar [kadara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) [a ƙima](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) na iya sa jimillar [kadarorin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) bankin su kasance da ƙimar da ta gaza bashin da ake binsa. A wannan yanayin, bankin ba zai iya cika dukkan wajibansa ba ko da yana da lokacin sayar da [kadarori](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset).

[Haɗarin ƙarancin kuɗin hannu: masu ajiya da yawa sun yi layi don cire kuɗi yau, kuma bankin ba shi da isassun kuɗin hannu yanzu duk da cewa ana iya biyan bashin da ya bayar daga baya. Haɗarin kasa biyan basussuka: masu karɓar bashi sun kasa biyan bashin gidajensu, kuma ƙimar kadarorin bankin ta yi ƙasa da bashin da ake binsa.](/micrographics/liquidity-vs-solvency-01/ha.md)

Wata [**tururuwar cire kuɗi daga banki**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bank-run) na faruwa ne idan masu ajiya da yawa suka yi ƙoƙarin cire kuɗinsu ko ragowar kuɗin asusunsu a lokaci guda saboda suna tsoron bankin zai durƙushe. [Inshorar kuɗin ajiya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#deposit-insurance) da [babban banki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank) da ke bayar da rance na iya rage firgici, amma sharuɗɗan samun inshora da iyakar kuɗin da take ɗaukar nauyi sun bambanta daga yanki mai tsarin doka zuwa wani. Waɗannan matakan kariya sun dogara ne da hukumomi da ƙa'idoji, kuma suna iya mayar da wasu asara ko haɗurra daga banki guda zuwa tsarin bankuna baki ɗaya.

Dokoki da sa ido na iya rage haɗari, amma ba za su iya hana duk wata damfara, rashin kyakkyawan gudanarwa, cin zarafin iko na siyasa, ko kuskure kawai ba. Waɗannan matsaloli abubuwa ne da za su iya faruwa waɗanda ya kamata a tantance, ba abubuwan da ke faruwa a kowane tsarin bankuna ba.

Babban darasin ba shi ne cewa kowane banki yana gab da durƙushewa ba. Darasin shi ne cewa kuɗin da ke cikin asusunku alkawarin banki ne na biyanku. Wannan ba daidai yake da riƙe [kadarar da ake amfani da ita wajen kammala biyan kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) kai tsaye ba.
