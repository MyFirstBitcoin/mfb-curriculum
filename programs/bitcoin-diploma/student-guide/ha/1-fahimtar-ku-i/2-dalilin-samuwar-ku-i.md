# 1.2 Dalilin samuwar kuɗi

Mutane suna da ƙwarewa, albarkatu, buƙatu, da tsare-tsare daban-daban. Musayar kaya da ayyuka tana ba su damar ƙwarewa a fanni ɗaya: manomi zai iya noma abinci, magini zai iya gina gidaje, kuma ma'aikacin jinya zai iya ba da kulawa. Domin wannan ƙwarewa ta yi aiki a faɗin al'umma, kowane mutum yana buƙatar hanya mai sauƙin amfani don musanya [ƙimar](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) aikinsa da aikin wasu.

Musayar kai tsaye sau da yawa tana da wahala. Mutumin da yake da abin da kuke buƙata wataƙila ba ya son abin da kuke bayarwa. Kuɗi suna warware wannan matsalar daidaita mu'amala.


<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Kuɗi**
>
> Kuɗi wata [kadara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) ce da mutane suke karɓa domin suna sa ran wasu mutane ma za su karɓe ta daga baya. Suna taimaka wa mutanen da ba su san juna ba su daidaita musayar kaya da ayyuka, su rubuta [farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) da [basussuka](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#debt), kuma su canja wurin [ƙima](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) daga wani lokaci zuwa wani da kuma daga wani wuri zuwa wani.


Kuɗi suna taimaka wa mutanen da ba su san juna ba su yi haɗin gwiwa a lokuta da wurare daban-daban, har ma a faɗin al'umma. Misali, ma'aikaci zai iya samun kuɗi yau ya kashe su shekara mai zuwa wajen sayen keken da aka ƙera a wata ƙasa, yana biyan kuɗin aikin mutane da yawa da bai taɓa haɗuwa da su ba. Kuɗi kuma suna samar da hanyar bai ɗaya ta rubuta [farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price), [basussuka](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#debt), kuɗin shiga, kuɗin da ake kashewa, da tanadi.

Kuɗi ba su ne abinci, gida, lokaci, ko ƙwarewar da mutane suke [darajantawa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value). Kayan aiki ne na auna da musayar haƙƙin samun waɗannan abubuwa. Amfaninsu ya dogara da amincewa, ƙa'idoji, karɓuwarsu a cikin al'umma, da halayensu na kuɗi.

**Tambayar nan take:** Me ya sa aka fara amfani da kuɗi? Ku bayyana matsalar musaya ko daidaita mu'amala da suke taimaka wa mutane su warware.
