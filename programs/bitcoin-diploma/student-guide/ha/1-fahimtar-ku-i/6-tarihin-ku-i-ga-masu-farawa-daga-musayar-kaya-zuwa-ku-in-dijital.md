# 1.6 Tarihin kuɗi ga masu farawa: daga musayar kaya zuwa kuɗin dijital

Kuɗi bai bunƙasa ta hanya guda iri ɗaya a ko'ina ba. Al'ummomi daban-daban sun yi amfani da kayayyaki, bayanan da aka adana, da hukumomi daban-daban. Duk da haka, wani tsari na gama-gari yana taimakawa wajen bayyana kuɗin zamani.

> “Kuɗi … ba sakamakon wata yarjejeniya ba ne ko kaɗan … sai dai sakamakon bunƙasar tarihi ne wanda ba a yi da niyya ba.”  
_Carl Menger, “Game da Asalin Kuɗi” (1892)_

* **Musayar kai tsaye da bayanan da aka adana a cikin al'umma:** Mutane sun yi amfani da [cinikayyar canjin kaya da kaya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#barter), kyaututtuka, wajibai, da rubutattun ko haddatattun [basussuka](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#debt). Waɗannan tsare-tsare sun dogara sosai kan dangantaka tsakanin mutanen al'umma.
* **[Kuɗin kayayyaki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#commodity-money):** Al'ummomi sun zaɓi kayayyakin da mutane da yawa suke darajantawa kuma suka fi sauƙin yin ciniki da su. Gishiri, ƙwayoyin ado, bawon halittun ruwa, dabbobin kiwo, jan ƙarfe, azurfa, da [zinariya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold) sun taka rawar kuɗi a wurare daban-daban. Kaya yana da amfani ko [ƙima](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) fiye da abin da [mai bayar da shi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) ya yi alkawari.
* **[Tsabar kuɗi masu daidaitaccen ma'auni](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#standard-coins):** Hukumomi sun auna ƙarfe suka buga masa tambari don samar da daidaitattun raka'o'i. Tsabar kuɗi sun sauƙaƙa ganewa da lissafin kuɗi. Hukumomi kuma za su iya rage yawan ƙarfe mai daraja da ke cikin waɗannan tsabar kuɗi ba tare da sauya abin da aka bayyana a matsayin [ƙimarsu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value). Ana kiran wannan [rage darajar tsabar kuɗi ta hanyar rage ƙarfe mai daraja a cikinsu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#debasement).
* **[Takardun kuɗi masu wakiltar ƙarfe](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#representative-paper):** Sufuri da kare ƙarfe suna da wahala. Wuraren ajiyar kaya da bankuna sun bayar da [takardun shaidar haƙƙin mallaka](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#paper-claim) da za a iya musanyawa da wani ƙayyadadden adadin ƙarfe. Takardun sun fi sauƙin ɗauka, amma masu amfani dole su amince da [mai bayar da su](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) cewa zai adana isasshen ƙarfe kuma ya cika alƙawarin musanya su. A ƙarƙashin nau'o'i daban-daban na [**tsarin ma'aunin zinariya**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold-standard), naúrar [kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) tana da alaƙa bisa doka da wani ƙayyadadden adadin [zinariya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold). Ƙa'idojin sun bambanta bisa wuri da zamani, kuma wasu lokuta gwamnatoci ko bankuna sun dakatar da ko kawo ƙarshen [damar musanya kuɗin da ƙarfe](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#convertibility).
* **Kuɗin bankuna da na gwamnati:** Bankuna sun ƙara fitar da takardun haƙƙin karɓar kuɗi da bayar da bashi. Gwamnatoci sun kafa [manyan bankunan ƙasa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank) da tsare-tsaren [kuɗin ƙasa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency). Kuɗi sun ƙara dogara da bayanan da cibiyoyi suka adana da kuma alkawuransu, maimakon kasancewar wani kaya kai tsaye a hannun jama'a. A ƙasashe da dama, [kuɗin ƙasa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) daga baya sun daina kasancewa abin da za a iya musanyawa da wata [kadara ta zahiri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) kamar [zinariya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold). Wannan rabuwa da jingina ga kayayyaki wata muhimmiyar siffa ce ta [**kuɗin fiat**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money) na yau (duba ƙasa).
* **Kuɗin dijital:** Yawancin kuɗin yau suna zirga-zirga a matsayin bayanan dijital ta bankuna da ayyukan biyan kuɗi. Kati ko waya na aika umarni; ba ya ɗauke da kuɗi a cikin kansa. Yawanci, umarnin yana sauya [ajiyar kuɗi a banki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bank-deposit), wato wajibcin banki da aka rubuta—ko haƙƙin da ake da shi a kansa—na biyan mai asusun wani adadi a cikin [kuɗin ƙasa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency).

Wannan tarihi ya nuna maimaituwar [samun wata fa'ida tare da rasa wata](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off). An samu ƙarin sauƙin amfani, amma sau da yawa masu amfani sun ƙara dogara da manufofin kuɗi, [masu fitar da kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer), bankuna, da [masu shiga tsakani wajen biyan kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#intermediary).
