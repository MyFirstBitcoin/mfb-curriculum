# 1.11 Hanyoyin madadin yin tanadi da adana ƙima

Mutane sukan nemi wasu hanyoyi maimakon riƙe tsabar kuɗi idan sun yi imanin cewa za su rasa [ikon sayayya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power). Abubuwan da aka fi zaɓa sun haɗa da karafa masu daraja, gidaje da filaye, da hannayen jari. An kuma yi amfani da karafa masu daraja a matsayin kuɗi a wurare da lokuta da dama. Kowane zaɓi yana da fa'idodi da haɗurra.


<!-- micrographic: saving-alternatives-table-01 -->


Ita [kadara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) abu ne da mutum zai iya mallaka ko da'awar haƙƙin mallaka a kansa, wanda zai iya kasancewa yana da [ƙima](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value); hakan ba yana nufin tana aiwatar da kowane aikin kuɗi kai tsaye ba. Gida na iya adana [ƙima](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) amma bai dace da sayen abinci ba. Hannun jari na iya zama mai sauƙin sayarwa a wata ƙasa amma wanda ba shi da asusun dillancin hannayen jari ba zai iya samunsa ba. [Zinariya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold) ta kasance ana amfani da ita a matsayin kuɗi tsawon lokuta masu yawa a tarihi amma tantance sahihancinta ko raba ta yayin ƙaramar mu'amala na iya zama da wahala.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/73bd09407fd1c7ac48ccf9725918a1227c355533-1536x1024.svg)

Mutane na iya kuma zaɓar baƙon [kuɗi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency), na gwamnati kuma su zaɓi [takardun bashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bond), kasuwanci masu zaman kansu, filaye, kayan aiki, ilimi, ko wasu [kadarori](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset). Kowane zaɓi ya ƙunshi [farashin damar da aka bari](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#opportunity-cost) da haɗari. Babu wata [kadara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) da ke tabbatar da [ikon sayayya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power) a kowane lokaci da yanayi.

**Lura:** Yadda ake sanya haraji na iya shafar waɗannan shawarwari. Ƙa'idojin da suka shafi kuɗin shiga, ribar da aka samu, dukiya, da [mu'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) na iya sauya tsada ko fa'idar riƙewa, sayarwa, ko amfani da wata [kadara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset). Ƙa'idojin haraji sun bambanta daga wannan yankin iko zuwa wancan kuma suna iya sauyawa da lokaci; wannan kwas ba ya bayar da shawarar haraji.

Bitcoin ya bayyana a matsayin wata hanyar da aka gabatar a madadin sauran: wata ƙarancacciyar [kadara ta dijital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-asset) da za a iya riƙewa da turawa ba tare da wata cibiya da ke zama [mai bayarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer). Don tantance wannan da'awa, dole ne mu yi nazarin dalilin da ya sa aka ƙirƙiri Bitcoin da yadda ƙa'idojinsa suke aiki.
