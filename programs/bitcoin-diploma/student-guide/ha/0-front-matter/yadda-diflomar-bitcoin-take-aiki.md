# Yadda diflomar Bitcoin take aiki

### Abubuwan da ɗalibai za su iya bayyanawa da nunawa a ƙarshe

Bayan kammala Diflomar Bitcoin, ya kamata ku iya:

* bayyana dalilin da ya sa mutane ke amfani da kuɗi, kwatanta nau'o'in kuɗi guda biyu, da bayyana yadda kuɗin zamani ke iya buƙatar [sadaukar da wata fa'ida domin samun wata](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) (`C1`)
* bayyana tushen Bitcoin da ƙa'idojinsa a matakin mafari, ciki har da yin kwafin dijital, haɗin gwiwa wajen [tabbatarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#verification), ayyukan masu amfani, [na'urorin sadarwar Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node), [masu haƙar bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner), da masu haɓaka manhajoji, yadda aka iyakance adadin Bitcoin, da yadda wata siffar Bitcoin za ta iya taimakawa ko takaita amfani da shi a matsayin kuɗi (`C2`)
* kwatanta Bitcoin da wata [dukiyar dijital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-asset) ta amfani da ma'aunai masu dacewa, da bambance koyo daga hasashen neman riba ko tallatawa (`C3`)
* bayyana walat, [riƙon ajiya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) da zaɓuɓɓukansa, bayanan da kowa zai iya gani da na sirri, nau'o'in walat, da matakin da ya dace a ɗauka cikin aminci idan an fuskanci zamba ko kuskure (`C4`)
* kwatanta biyan kuɗi kai tsaye a kan sarkar da kuma ta Lightning, da bayyana haɗurran hanyoyi daban-daban na samun dama ko musaya (`C5`)
* kammala samun damar shiga walat da karɓar kuɗi cikin aminci tare da tallafi da matakan dubawa, sannan ku nuna shirin dawo da damar shiga ta amfani da kayan da aka tanada ko na kwaikwayo (`C6`); da
* kammala aiki guda ɗaya na amfani da Bitcoin a matsayin kuɗi a yankinku cikin aminci, wanda malami ya zaɓa, da bayyana manufarsa, madadinsa, [fa'idodin da ake samu da waɗanda ake sadaukarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off), abin da ka iya gaza, da mataki na gaba (`C7`).

Waɗannan ƙwarewa tushe ne, ba hujjar ƙwarewar gwani ba. Kammala Diflomar ba ya nufin kun shirya sarrafa bitcoin mai yawa, ba wasu mutane shawara, ko warware matsalolin fasaha masu zurfi ba tare da tallafi ba.

### Koyo mai sassauci

Ana iya koyar da abubuwan da ke cikin Diflomar Bitcoin ido-da-ido, ta intanet, ta hanyar darasin da ya haɗa duka biyun, ko da kayan karatu na takarda/PDF. Littafin yana samar da hanyar samun ilimi ta bai ɗaya. Malaminku yana ƙara tattaunawa, ayyukan da ake yi bisa jagoranci, nunin yadda ake yi, bayanai masu dacewa da yankinku, da duk wani tantancewa da ake buƙata.

### Abubuwan da ake sa ran bi game da tattaunawa da sirri

Ku yi amfani da tattaunawa don gwada ra'ayoyi, yin tambayoyi, sauraro da kyau, da bayyana dalilanku. Ba sai kun yarda da kowace da'awa ko kowane mutum a rukuninku ba.

Yayin tattaunawa:

* ku bayyana ra'ayoyi da kalmominku
* ku nemi ƙarin bayani idan wata kalma ba ta bayyana ba
* ku bambance abin da kuka fuskanta da kanku daga hujjoji na gama-gari
* ku ƙalubalanci ra'ayoyi ba tare da kai hari ga mutane ba
* ku bayyana rashin tabbas idan ba ku da tabbaci
* ku tuna cewa damar samun hidimomin kuɗi, tsaro, da dama sun bambanta; kada ku ɗauka cewa yanayin kowa iri ɗaya ne da naku
* ku kare bayanan sirri na kuɗin wasu
* ku ɗauki labaran rayuwar da aka raba a rukunin ɗalibai a matsayin sirri sai dai idan mai labarin ya ba da izinin sake faɗa
* kada ku ɗauki hoto, ku yi rikodi, ko ku raba abin da ke allon na'urar wani, aikinsa, ko bayanan da za su bayyana shi ba tare da izini ba
* kada ku matsa wa kowa ya saya, ya sayar, ko ya bayyana yawan kuɗin da yake da su

Malamin ku zai bayyana duk ayyukan tantancewa da ake buƙata. Ana gina ilimin ka'ida musamman a cikin **Sassa na 1 da 2**, inda malamin ku yake lura da yadda kuke bayani, kwatantawa, ba da hujja, ko amfani da wata dabara. Faɗin cewa kuna da tabbaci kaɗai ba hujja ba ce. Ana gina ƙwarewar aikace-aikace musamman a cikin **Sassa na 3 da 4**, inda malami yake lura da ayyukan da suka dace a yanayi mai aminci tare da tallafi.

Malamin ku yana amfani da jadawalin tantancewa don rubuta sakamako ɗaya ga kowace daga cikin ƙwarewa bakwai da ke sama. `C1`–`C5` ana tantance su ta hanyar bayananku da shawarwarin da kuka yanke; `C6`–`C7` suna buƙatar lura kai tsaye. Aikin walat/rasit da aikin shirin farfaɗowa duk sassa ne da ake buƙata na `C6`.

### Ilimin da ake buƙata tun da farko: babu

Ba a buƙatar wani ilimi tun da farko. Kwas ɗin yana gabatar da kowane kalma da ake buƙata kafin a yi amfani da ita a zurfafe.

Iya karatu na asali da lissafin yau da kullum suna da amfani. Lissafi mafi muhimmanci shi ne cewa ana iya raba bitcoin (BTC) ɗaya zuwa ƙananan raka'a 100,000,000 da ake kira [satoshis](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), waɗanda galibi ake taƙaitawa da [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi). **BTC** shi ne taƙaitaccen sunan da aka saba amfani da shi wajen nuna adadin bitcoin. Na'urar lissafi na iya taimakawa, amma ba a buƙatar ilimin lissafi mai zurfi.

Idan kun riga kun fara amfani da Bitcoin, ku tallafa wa malamin ku, ku raba abubuwan da kuka koya, kuma ku bayyana dabaru ga abokan karatu da harshe mai sauƙi. Koyar da wasu wata hanya ce ta ƙarfafa abin da kuka koya. Idan kuna jin daɗin tallafa wa ɗalibai, [ku ba da ra'ayoyin ingantawa](https://forms.myfirstbitcoin.org/feedback) ko tallafi ga al'ummar yankinku, kuma ku binciki [Shirin Horar da Malamai](https://etp.myfirstbitcoin.org/) don bunƙasa ƙwarewar koyarwa.

### Kayan da ake buƙata da abubuwan da ake tsammani game da fasaha

Don Difloma, kuna buƙatar:

* wannan littafi a bugu ko a tsarin PDF
* littafin rubutu da alƙalami, ko wata hanyar rubuta amsoshi
* malami ko rukunin karatu idan akwai
* lokacin tattaunawa da tunani

Ba a buƙatar wayar kanku ko haɗin intanet na dindindin domin koyo, shirye-shiryen aikace-aikace, tantancewa, ko samun takardar shaida. Kada ku girka [walat ɗin Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-wallet) ko ku ƙirƙiri [jimlar farfaɗowa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) don kawai wani ɗalibi ya ce ku yi hakan. Ku yi amfani da kayan aikin aikace-aikace ne kawai ƙarƙashin jagoranci bayyananne kuma sai idan manufarsu da haɗarinsu sun bayyana.

Idan lokacin yin atisaye da [walat ɗin Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-wallet) ya yi, malaminku zai jagorance ku mataki-mataki. Za ku iya koyo ba tare da amfani da kuɗinku ba. Waɗannan halaye masu sauƙi za su taimaka:

* ku yi amfani da na'urar da kuke da iko a kanta, kuma ku tambayi malaminku idan kuna buƙatar taimako wajen zaɓar ɗaya
* ku nemi malaminku ya taimaka muku bincika tushen saukewa kafin ku girka walat
* ku yi atisaye a muhallin gwaji, ta hanyar kwaikwayo, ko da wasu kayan horo da aka tanada; idan malaminku ya shirya wani aiki na zahiri da ke da ƙaramin haɗarin asara, ku fahimci manufarsa da haɗurransa kafin ku zaɓi ko za ku shiga
* ku yi amfani da misalan [kalmomin dawo da damar shiga](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) da aka tanada don atisaye, maimakon kalmomi daga walat na ainihi na wani
* ku kiyaye ainihin [kalmomin dawo da damar shiga](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) (waɗanda kuma ake kira [jerin kalmomin farfaɗowa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase)) a ɓoye: suna dawo da damar shiga walat, don haka kada ku nuna su ga ajin, ku ɗauki hotonsu, ko ku shigar da su a shafin yanar gizo ko cikin saƙo

### Tsaro, ɗaukar alhaki, da rashin bayar da shawarar harkokin kuɗi


> **Callout – Ku fara da koyo; wannan ba shawarar ɗaukar mataki ba ce**
>
> Difloma ta Bitcoin don ilimi kawai ce. Ba ta bayar da shawarar harkokin kuɗi, saka jari, shari'a, ko haraji. Ba ta yi alƙawarin cewa bitcoin zai ƙaru a [farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) ko ba ku shawarar saya, sayarwa, yin jingina da bitcoin domin aro, ko riƙe kowane adadi na bitcoin.
>
> Diflomar ta shafi Bitcoin kaɗai. Sauran token, kayayyakin ciniki, da tallata kuɗaɗen kirifto gaba ɗaya ba sa cikin abin da ta ƙunsa. Wannan iyaka tana fayyace abin da kwas ɗin ya ƙunsa; ba garanti ba ne game da Bitcoin ko shawarar mallakarsa.
>
> A kasuwar Bitcoin, [farashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) na iya sauyawa da sauri. [Ma'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) na iya zama masu wahalar sokewa ko ma ba zai yiwu a soke su ba. Rasa [jerin kalmomin farfaɗowa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), tona [maɓallin sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key), rashin amincin [mai riƙon amana](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custodian), kuskuren manhaja, ko zamba na iya jawo asarar da ba za a iya dawo da ita ba.


Ku bi ƙa'idoji uku a duk tsawon kwas ɗin:

1. **Ku fara da koyo.** Kada ku ɗauki mataki saboda zumuɗi, tsoro, ko matsin lamba kawai.
1. **Ku fara da kaɗan.** Ku gwada kayan aiki da hanyoyin da ba ku saba da su ba da adadin da za ku iya jure rasawa.
1. **Kada Ku Dogara Kawai, Ku Tabbatar.** Ku bincika adiresoshi, adadin kuɗi, kuɗaɗen caji, tushe, da mutane kafin ku amince da wani mataki.

Ɗaukar alhaki kuma ya haɗa da sanin lokacin neman taimako. Ku nemi taimako kafin ku tura kuɗi masu yawa a gare ku, amma kada ku taɓa bayyana [jerin kalmomin farfaɗowa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) ko [maɓallin sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) ga mutumin da yake taimaka muku.

### Difloma mai sassa huɗu

Difloma ta ƙunshi tsarin koyo mai matakai huɗu: **Matsala → Mafita → Aiki → Haɗawa**. Dukkan sassan huɗu suna cikin Difloma. **Sassa na 1 da na 2** suna zurfafa fahimtar Matsala da Mafita da kowa yake koya ta hanyar bayani, kwatantawa, da tunani mai hujja. Malamai za su iya daidaita misalan aikace-aikace, kayan aiki, albarkatu, da hanyoyin koyarwa a **Sassa na 3 da na 4** tare da kiyaye ƙa'idodin aminci da ƙwarewa da ake buƙata. **Sashe na 4** shi ne matakin Haɗawa wanda malami yake tsarawa, inda ɗalibai suke danganta abin da kowa ya koya da wata buƙata ko dama ta yankinsu, wani zaɓi da za a iya aiwatarwa, aiki cikin aminci, da mataki na gaba da za a ɗauka cikin alhaki.


<!-- micrographic: learning-path-roadmap-01 -->


**Sashe na 4** sashe ne na dole a cikin Difloma, wanda malami yake tsarawa. Malamai su ne ƙwararru kan ƙalubalen yankin ɗalibansu, mafita, kayan aiki, albarkatu, dokoki, harshe, ababen more rayuwa, da buƙatu. Ayyukan matakin Haɗawa da malami yake tsarawa na iya haɗawa da samun kuɗi, tanadi, karɓa, kashewa, [Mataki na 2](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#layer-2) a matsayin mafita, hanyoyin musaya na cikin gida, 'yan kasuwa, ko tattalin arzikin da kuɗi ke zagayawa a cikinsa.

[Koyo Mai Zurfi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/advanced-modules/) ba ya cikin Difloma. Yana iya nazarin ilimin tattalin arziki mai zurfi, [ilimin ɓoye bayanai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cryptography), [haƙar bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work), [sirrin bayanai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy), [ma'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) da yadda aka tsara ta, ayyukan Lightning, amfani a kasuwanci, ko tsaro. Ana ware kayan koyo masu zurfi domin Difloma ta masu farawa ta kasance mai sauƙin fahimta da aminci.

Kasancewar batu ba ya cikin Difloma ba yana nufin ba shi da muhimmanci ba ne. Wataƙila kawai yana buƙatar ƙarin lokaci ko ilimin da aka riga aka samu.

### Yadda aka tsara hanyar koyo

Sassan huɗu suna samar da hanyar koyo ta Matsala → Mafita → Aiki → Haɗawa da ake buƙata, ba ƙayyadadden jadawalin aji ba. **Sassa na 1 da na 2** suna gina ƙwarewar da ta dogara da ka'ida ta hanyar bayani, kwatantawa, da tunani mai hujja. **Sashe na 3** yana gina ƙwarewar aiwatar da aiki cikin aminci. **Sashe na 4** malami ne yake tsara shi amma na dole ne: yana danganta abin da kowa ya koya da wata buƙata ta yankinsu, wani zaɓi da za a iya aiwatarwa, aiki mai aminci, da mataki na gaba da za a ɗauka cikin alhaki.


<!-- micrographic: four-module-table-01 -->


Difloma tana ɗaukar aƙalla sa'o'i takwas. Zaman darasi huɗu na sa'o'i biyu-biyu su ne mafi gajeren tsarin da aka tsara, ba iyakar lokaci ba. Malaminku na iya raba sashe zuwa zaman darasi fiye da haka, sake komawa ga wani ra'ayi da aka koya a baya, ko sauya jerin ƙananan sassa idan rukuninku yana buƙatar ƙarin lokaci don tattaunawa, aikace-aikace, bita, ko tantancewa. Babu iyaka ga tsawon lokacin da za a ɗauka: malami ne yake da alhakin bai wa ɗalibai lokacin da suke buƙata, koyar da dukkan ƙwarewar da ake buƙata, da fayyace alaƙar da ke tsakanin sassan.
