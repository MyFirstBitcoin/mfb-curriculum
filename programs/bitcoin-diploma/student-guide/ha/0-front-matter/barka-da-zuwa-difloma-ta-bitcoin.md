# Barka da zuwa Difloma ta Bitcoin

### Game da Mu

My First Bitcoin ƙungiya ce ta ilimi mai zaman kanta wadda ba ta neman riba, wadda John Dennehy ya kafa a 2021. Muna wallafa ilimi kan Bitcoin kyauta, mai buɗaɗɗen tushe, ga ɗalibai da malamai a faɗin duniya.

An fara aikin da ƙananan azuzuwan karatu na ido-da-ido a El Salvador a 2021, bayan ƙasar ta zartar da dokar amincewa da bitcoin a matsayin kuɗin biyan kuɗi na doka. A 2022, rukunin farko na ɗaliban Difloma na Bitcoin ya fara karatu a wata makarantar gwamnati a San Marcos. Wannan gwajin farko ya bunƙasa ya zama manhajar karatu mai buɗaɗɗen tushe wadda malamai masu zaman kansu za su iya fassarawa, daidaitawa da koyarwa ta hanyar [Cibiyar Haɗin Gwiwar Ilimin Bitcoin](https://myfirstbitcoin.org/about/network). My First Bitcoin [yana da tarihin da ke a fili](https://myfirstbitcoin.org/about) wanda ke rubuta muhimman matakan ci gaban aikin da yadda ƙungiyar ke ɗaukar alhakin ayyukanta.

Aikinmu mai zaman kansa ne, al'umma ce ke jagorantarsa, kuma ya mayar da hankali kan Bitcoin. Muna mai da hankali kan fahimtar harkokin kuɗi, tunani cikin tsanaki, da matakan tsaro na aikace - ba cinikayya, caca kan sauyin farashi, ko tallata wasu token ba. Ba ma neman ku amince da wata akida. Maimakon haka, muna samar da ra'ayoyi, tambayoyi, da ilimin aikace domin ku iya cimma naku matsayar.

### Manufarmu: ilimin Bitcoin mai zaman kansa da buɗaɗɗen tushe

Manufarmu ita ce ƙarfafa mutane ta hanyar ilimin Bitcoin mai zaman kansa, marar son kai, kuma mai buɗaɗɗen tushe.

Ilimi mai zaman kansa ba ya ƙarƙashin ikon gwamnati, kamfani, ko wata hidimar kuɗi. Ilimi marar son kai yana bayyana hujjoji da [ribobi da abubuwan da ake sadaukarwa wajen zaɓi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) ba tare da tilasta wa ɗalibi cimma wata matsaya da aka riga aka ƙayyade ba. Kowa na iya karanta ilimi mai buɗaɗɗen tushe, ya raba shi, ya yi masa bita, ya fassara shi, ya kuma inganta shi bisa sharuɗɗan lasisinsa.

Wannan hanya tana da muhimmanci domin kuɗi yana shafar kowa. Ya kamata mutane su iya nazarin tsarin da suke amfani da shi ba tare da neman izini ba. Ya kamata kuma su iya bincika kayan karatun, su yi tambaya kan ikirarin da ke cikinsu, su kuma taimaka wajen inganta su.

### Abubuwan da muke tsayawa a kai: sauƙin samun dama, son sani, tunani mai zurfi, al'umma, da ikon kai

**Sauƙin samun dama** yana nufin cewa ana iya koyar da abubuwan da ke cikin Difloma na Bitcoin ido-da-ido, ta intanet, ko ta hanyar rarraba bugu/PDF. Ƙarancin kayan fasaha bai kamata ya hana shiga koyo ko samun takardar shaida ba. Misalan da suka shafi muhalli na iya sauyawa, amma ƙwarewar da ake buƙata da ma'aunin tantance ta ba sa sauyawa.

**Son sani** yana nufin kasancewa a shirye ku tambayi dalili, ku amince da abin da ba ku sani ba, ku kuma binciki amsar da ba ku saba da ita ba kafin ku karɓe ta ko ku ƙi ta.

**Tunani mai zurfi** yana nufin yin tambayoyi masu bayyana, bincika hujjoji, da kwatanta [ribobi da abubuwan da ake sadaukarwa wajen zaɓi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off). Bitcoin yana yin manyan ikirari game da kuɗi da mallaka. Waɗannan ikirari sun cancanci nazari cikin tsanaki. A yayin wannan kwas, ku bambanta tabbatattun bayanai da ra'ayoyi, da kuma abubuwan da za su iya faruwa da waɗanda aka tabbatar za su faru.

**Al'umma** tana nufin cewa ilimi ya fi amfani idan mutane suka tattauna ra'ayoyi suka kuma haɗa su da rayuwar muhallinsu. Malaminku da abokan karatunku na iya samun gogewar da babu wani littafi na duniya da zai iya ƙunsarta gaba ɗaya. Ku saurara da kyau, ku yi tambayoyi, ku kuma bayyana dalilan tunaninku cikin girmamawa.

**Ikon kai** yana nufin samun iko na gaske kan zaɓuɓɓukanku da albarkatunku. Da Bitcoin, samun ƙarin iko na iya rage dogaro da cibiyoyi. Haka kuma yana ƙara alhakin da ya rataya a wuyan mutum. Iko ba tare da ilimi ba na iya zama haɗari, don haka matakan tsaro na aikace muhimmin ɓangare ne na Difloma na Bitcoin.

### Ku ci gaba da koyo bayan Difloma na Bitcoin

Littafinku, malaminku, da ayyukan rukuni suna samar da cikakkiyar hanyar koyo don Difloma na Bitcoin. Kuna iya shiga cikakke kuma ku sami takardar shaida ba tare da na'ura taku ko haɗin intanet ba.

Idan kun shirya ci gaba da koyo, ku binciki [Gabatarwa ga Bitcoin](https://myfirstbitcoin.org/learn/programs#intro) ko [tafiyar ɗalibi](https://journey.myfirstbitcoin.org/). Waɗannan ƙarin kayan koyo ne na zaɓi, kuma ba sa cikin tantancewa ko bayar da takardar shaida. Ku kwatanta bayanansu da wannan jagora, ku kuma tattauna duk abin da bai bayyana ba da malaminku. Kada ku taɓa shigar da [jimlolin dawo da walat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), [maɓallan sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key), ko bayanan kuɗinku na sirri cikin wani kayan aiki na intanet.

### Ku yi rajista don Difloma na Bitcoin

Malaminku na iya gayyatarku ku [yi rajista don Difloma na Bitcoin](https://forms.myfirstbitcoin.org/register-student/bd). Rajista tana taimaka wa My First Bitcoin wajen bayar da tallafi da damar koyo a nan gaba, da kuma inganta Difloma ga ɗalibai irinku.

### Dalilin samar da Difloma na Bitcoin

Yawancin mutane suna amfani da kuɗi kafin su koyi yadda kuɗi yake aiki. Suna iya samun sa, adana shi, aron sa, da kashe shi ba tare da nazarin wanda yake fitar da shi ba, ko yadda adadinsa ke sauyawa, ko dalilin da ya sa [ƙarfin sayensa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power) yake sauyawa.

Bitcoin yana gabatar da wani tsari dabam. Ba shi da wani [mai fitar da kuɗi na tsakiya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer), yana bin ƙa'idojin da kowa zai iya gani, kuma yana ba da damar mallakar dukiyar dijital kai tsaye. Waɗannan ra'ayoyi na iya zama da wahalar bayyanawa a sarari ba tare da fara nazarin kuɗi da matsalolin da za su iya tasowa a tsarin kuɗi na zamani ba.

Saboda haka, Difloma na Bitcoin yana bin hanya mai sauƙi:

1. Bayyana matsalar da aka tsara Bitcoin don magancewa.
1. Bayyana babban maganin da Bitcoin yake bayarwa.
1. Bayyana tare da aiwatar da alhakin riƙewa da amfani da bitcoin.
1. Bincika yadda Bitcoin zai iya aiki a matsayin kuɗi a cikin al'ummarku.

Manufar ba ita ce a mai da ku ƙwararru cikin sa'o'i takwas ba. Manufar ita ce a ba ku ingantaccen tushe, a taimaka muku ku guji kurakuran da masu farawa suka saba yi, sannan a shirya ku don ci gaba da koyo.

### Yadda za ku iya amfani da wannan kwas a matsayin mafari

Ku ɗauki Difloma na Bitcoin a matsayin farkon tafiyar koyo mai tsawo. A ƙarshe, ya kamata ku sami isasshen ilimi don yin tambayoyi masu zurfi da kuma gane batutuwan da suke buƙatar ƙarin nazari.

Za ku iya amfani da kwas ɗin don:

* bayyana kalmomin da aka saba amfani da su game da kuɗi da Bitcoin
* kwatanta tsarin kuɗi na zamani da Bitcoin cikin harshe mai sauƙi
* tattauna ikirari ta amfani da hujjoji da [la'akari da abin da ake samu da abin da ake rasa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off)
* tantance walat da [hanyoyin riƙon ajiya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) da za a iya zaɓa
* gane haɗurran tsaro da aka saba fuskanta
* zaɓar mataki na gaba mai aminci a tafiyar koyo
* haɗa kai da ƙungiyar koyo ta yankinku
* shirya don nazari mai zurfi


<!-- card: callout -->
> **Ilimi yana zuwa kafin aiki**
>
> Ba sai kun saya ko kun sami bitcoin ta wata hanya ba don kammala Difloma na Bitcoin. Haka kuma, ba sai kun girka [walat na Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-wallet) ko kun yi amfani da kuɗinku ba. Ana iya tantance ƙwarewar aikace-aikacen da ake buƙata ta amfani da walat ɗin atisaye da aka tanada, kwaikwayo, yanayin gwaji, samfurin zahiri, ko wani aiki mai ƙarancin haɗari da malami ya amince da shi.


Idan lokacinku yana da iyaka ko kuma kuna son fara sanin Bitcoin, ku fara da [Gabatarwa ga Bitcoin](https://myfirstbitcoin.org/learn/programs#intro). Wannan kwas ne mafi gajarta da yake gabatar da muhimman abubuwan farko. Bayan Difloma na Bitcoin, za ku iya ci gaba zuwa [Shirin Horar da Malamai](https://etp.myfirstbitcoin.org/) idan kuna son haɓaka ƙwarewar koyarwa. Waɗannan hanyoyin koyo ne daban-daban na zaɓi: Gabatarwa ga Bitcoin taƙaitaccen kwas ne na farko, Difloma na Bitcoin yana gina tushe mai faɗi ga masu farawa, yayin da Shirin Horar da Malamai yake haɓaka ƙwarewar koyarwa. Babu ɗayansu da ake buƙata don kammala Difloma na Bitcoin. Don Difloma na Bitcoin, kuna buƙatar wannan Jagorar Ɗalibi, malami, da kayayyaki da albarkatun da malaminku ya ƙayyade. Ku binciki [tafiyar ɗalibi](https://journey.myfirstbitcoin.org/) don ganin yadda waɗannan damammaki suke da alaƙa da juna, sannan ku zaɓi matakinku na gaba.
