# Barka da zuwa Difloma ta Bitcoin

### Game da Mu

My First Bitcoin ƙungiyar ilimi ce mai zaman kanta wadda ba ta neman riba, kuma John Dennehy ne ya kafa ta a 2021. Muna wallafa kayan koyon Bitcoin kyauta masu buɗaɗɗen tushe ga ɗalibai da malamai a faɗin duniya.

An fara aikin da ƙananan azuzuwan ido-da-ido a El Salvador a 2021, bayan ƙasar ta zartar da dokar da ta amince da bitcoin a matsayin kuɗin biyan kuɗi na doka. A 2022, rukunin ɗaliban farko na Difloma na Bitcoin ya fara karatu a wata makarantar gwamnati a San Marcos. Wannan gwajin ya bunƙasa ya zama manhajar karatu mai buɗaɗɗen tushe wadda malamai masu zaman kansu za su iya fassarawa, daidaitawa, da koyarwa ta hanyar [Cibiyar Sadarwar Ilimin Bitcoin](https://myfirstbitcoin.org/about/network). My First Bitcoin yana da [tarihi a bayyane ga jama'a](https://myfirstbitcoin.org/about) da ke rubuta muhimman nasarorin aikin da yadda ƙungiyar ke ɗaukar alhakin ayyukanta.

Aikinmu mai zaman kansa ne, al'umma ce ke jagorantarsa, kuma ya fi mayar da hankali kan Bitcoin. Muna mai da hankali kan ilimin harkokin kuɗi, tunani mai zurfi, da tsaro a aikace - ba ciniki, hasashen neman riba, ko tallata wasu token ba. Ba ma neman ku amince da wani imani. Maimakon haka, muna ba ku ra'ayoyi, tambayoyi, da ilimin aiki domin ku iya cim ma naku sakamakon tunani.

### Manufarmu: ilimin Bitcoin mai zaman kansa da buɗaɗɗen tushe

Manufarmu ita ce ƙarfafa mutane ta hanyar ilimin Bitcoin mai zaman kansa, marar son kai, kuma mai buɗaɗɗen tushe.

Ilimi mai zaman kansa ba ya ƙarƙashin ikon gwamnati, kamfani, ko wani mai ba da sabis na kuɗi. Ilimi marar son kai yana bayyana hujjoji da [fa'idodi da illolin zaɓuɓɓuka](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) ba tare da tilasta wa ɗalibi cim ma wani sakamakon tunani da aka riga aka ƙayyade ba. Kowa na iya karantawa, rabawa, nazari, fassarawa, da inganta ilimi mai buɗaɗɗen tushe bisa sharuɗɗan lasisinsa.

Wannan hanya tana da muhimmanci domin kuɗi suna shafar kowa. Ya kamata mutane su iya nazarin tsarin da suke amfani da shi ba tare da neman izini ba. Haka kuma, ya kamata su iya bincika kayan koyarwa, tambayar sahihancin abubuwan da suke ikirari, da taimakawa wajen inganta su.

### Abubuwan da muke tsayawa a kai: sauƙin samun dama, tunani mai zurfi, al'umma, da ikon mallakar kai

**Sauƙin samun dama** yana nufin cewa ana iya koyar da abubuwan da ke cikin Difloma na Bitcoin ido-da-ido, ta intanet, ko ta rarraba kayan karatu a bugaggen tsari/PDF. Ƙarancin fasaha bai kamata ya hana shiga koyo ko samun takardar shaida ba. Misalan da suka shafi muhalli na iya sauyawa, amma ƙwarewar da ake buƙata da ma'aunin tantance ƙwarewar suna nan yadda suke.

**Son sani** yana nufin kasancewa a shirye ku tambayi dalili, ku amince da abin da ba ku sani ba, kuma ku binciki amsar da ba ku saba da ita ba kafin ku karɓe ta ko ku ƙi ta.

**Tunani mai zurfi** yana nufin yin tambayoyi bayyanannu, bincika hujjoji, da kwatanta [fa'idodi da illolin zaɓuɓɓuka](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off). Bitcoin yana yin manyan ikirari game da kuɗi da mallaka. Waɗannan ikirari sun cancanci nazari mai zurfi. A lokacin wannan kwas, ku bambanta gaskiya da ra'ayi, da kuma abin da zai iya faruwa da abin da aka tabbatar zai faru.

**Al'umma** tana nufin cewa ilimi yana ƙara amfani idan mutane suna tattauna ra'ayoyi kuma suna danganta su da rayuwar yankinsu. Malaminku da abokan karatunku na iya samun gogewar da babu wani littafin duniya da zai iya ƙunsarta gaba ɗaya. Ku saurara da kyau, ku yi tambayoyi, kuma ku bayyana dalilan tunaninku cikin girmamawa.

**Ikon mallakar kai** yana nufin samun iko na gaske a kan zaɓuɓɓukanku da albarkatunku. Tare da Bitcoin, samun ƙarin iko na iya rage dogaro da cibiyoyi. Haka kuma yana ƙara nauyin da ke kanku. Iko ba tare da ilimi ba na iya zama haɗari, don haka tsaro a aikace muhimmin ɓangare ne na Difloma na Bitcoin.

### Ku ci gaba da koyo bayan Difloma na Bitcoin

Littafinku, malaminku, da ayyukan rukuni suna samar da cikakkiyar hanyar koyo don Difloma na Bitcoin. Kuna iya shiga sosai kuma ku sami takardar shaida ba tare da na'urar kanku ko haɗin intanet ba.

Idan kun shirya ci gaba da koyo, ku binciki [Gabatarwa ga Bitcoin](https://myfirstbitcoin.org/learn/programs#intro) ko [tafiyar ɗalibi](https://journey.myfirstbitcoin.org/). Waɗannan ƙarin kayan koyo ne na zaɓi kuma ba sa cikin tantancewa ko bayar da takardar shaida. Ku kwatanta bayanan da wannan jagora, kuma ku tattauna duk abin da bai bayyana ba da malaminku. Kada ku taɓa shigar da [jimlolin dawo da walat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), [maɓallan sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key), ko bayanan kuɗinku na sirri cikin wani kayan aiki na intanet.

### Ku yi rajistar Difloma na Bitcoin

Malaminku na iya gayyatar ku don ku [yi rajistar Difloma na Bitcoin](https://forms.myfirstbitcoin.org/register-student/bd). Yin rajista yana taimaka wa My First Bitcoin wajen bayar da tallafi da damar koyo a nan gaba, da kuma inganta Diflomar ga ɗalibai irin ku.

### Dalilin samar da Difloma na Bitcoin

Yawancin mutane suna amfani da kuɗi kafin su koyi yadda kuɗi suke aiki. Za su iya samun su, ajiye su, karɓar su bashi, da kashe su ba tare da nazarin wanda ke fitar da su, yadda adadinsu ke sauyawa, ko dalilin da ya sa [ƙarfin sayensu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power) yake sauyawa ba.

Bitcoin yana gabatar da wani tsari dabam. Ba shi da wani [mai fitarwa na tsakiya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer), yana bin ƙa'idojin da suke a bayyane ga jama'a, kuma yana ba da damar mallaka kai tsaye ta hanyar dijital. Bayyana waɗannan ra'ayoyi a sarari na iya zama da wahala idan ba a fara nazarin kuɗi da matsalolin da za su iya tasowa a tsarin kuɗi na zamani ba.

Saboda haka, Diflomar Bitcoin tana bin hanya mai sauƙi:

1. Bayyana matsalar da aka ƙirƙiri Bitcoin domin magancewa.
1. Bayyana babban mafitar da Bitcoin yake bayarwa.
1. Bayyana tare da aiwatar da alhakin riƙewa da amfani da bitcoin.
1. Bincika yadda Bitcoin zai iya aiki a matsayin kuɗi a cikin al'ummarku.

Manufar ba ita ce a mayar da ku ƙwararru cikin sa'o'i takwas ba. Manufar ita ce a ba ku ingantaccen tushe, a taimaka muku ku guji kura-kuran da masu farawa suka saba yi, kuma a shirya ku don ci gaba da koyo.

### Yadda za ku iya amfani da wannan kwas a matsayin mafari

Ku ɗauki Diflomar Bitcoin a matsayin farkon dogon tsarin koyo. Zuwa ƙarshensa, ya kamata ku samu isasshen ilimin da zai sa ku yi tambayoyi masu zurfi kuma ku gane batutuwan da suke buƙatar ƙarin nazari.

Za ku iya amfani da kwas ɗin don:

* bayyana kalmomin da aka saba amfani da su game da kuɗi da Bitcoin
* kwatanta tsarin kuɗi na zamani da Bitcoin cikin harshe mai sauƙi
* tattauna iƙirari ta amfani da hujjoji da [abin da ake samu da abin da ake rasa wajen yin zaɓi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off)
* gano walat da [hanyoyin ajiya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) da za a iya zaɓa
* gane haɗarin tsaro da aka saba fuskanta
* zaɓar matakin koyo na gaba mai aminci
* haɗa kai da ƙungiyar masu koyo ta yankinku
* shirya don nazari mai zurfi


> **Callout – Ilimi yana zuwa kafin aiki**
>
> Ba sai kun saya ko kun mallaki bitcoin ta wata hanya ba kafin ku kammala Diflomar Bitcoin. Haka kuma, ba sai kun girka [walat na Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-wallet) ko kun yi amfani da kuɗinku na kanku ba. Ana iya tantance ƙwarewar aikace-aikacen da ake buƙata ta amfani da walat ɗin horo da aka tanada, kwaikwayo, muhallin gwaji, samfurin zahiri, ko wani aiki mai ƙarancin haɗarin asara da malami ya amince da shi.


Idan lokacinku yana da iyaka ko kuna son samun gabatarwa ta farko ga Bitcoin, ku fara da [Gabatarwa ga Bitcoin](https://myfirstbitcoin.org/learn/programs#intro). Wannan gajeren kwas ne da yake gabatar da muhimman abubuwan farko. Bayan Diflomar Bitcoin, za ku iya ci gaba zuwa [Shirin Horar da Malamai](https://etp.myfirstbitcoin.org/) idan kuna son haɓaka ƙwarewar koyarwa. Waɗannan hanyoyin koyo ne daban-daban da za a iya zaɓa: Gabatarwa ga Bitcoin taƙaitaccen kwas ne na farko, Diflomar Bitcoin tana gina tushe mai faɗi ga masu farawa, sannan Shirin Horar da Malamai yana haɓaka ƙwarewar koyarwa. Babu ɗayansu da ake buƙata don kammala Diflomar Bitcoin. Don Diflomar Bitcoin, kuna buƙatar wannan Jagorar Ɗalibai, malami, da kayayyaki da sauran abubuwan taimako da malaminku ya ayyana. Ku binciki [tafiyar ɗalibi](https://journey.myfirstbitcoin.org/) don ganin yadda waɗannan damammaki suke da alaƙa da juna kuma ku zaɓi matakinku na gaba.
