# Matakai na gaba na zaɓi

Kowane shiri da damar da ke wannan sashe ƙarin koyo ne na zaɓi bayan Difloma na Bitcoin. Ba sai kun bi ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan hanyoyin haɗin yanar gizo ba don kammala Difloma na Bitcoin.

### Ku ba da labarin gogewarku a Difloma

Bayan kwas ɗin, [ku cika takardar binciken ra'ayi ta kammala Difloma](https://forms.myfirstbitcoin.org/bd-exit-survey). Ra'ayoyinku suna taimaka wa My First Bitcoin ya fahimci gogewarku ta koyo, ya auna tasirin Difloma, kuma ya ci gaba da inganta ilimin Bitcoin.

### Ku taimaka wajen inganta wannan jagora

Kun gano kuskure, kalmomin da ba su da sauƙin fahimta, ko hanyar inganta fassara? Ku yi amfani da fom ɗin hukuma na [gyaran rubutu](https://forms.myfirstbitcoin.org/proofread) don sanar da mu. Don ba da ra'ayoyi kan kwas ɗin gaba ɗaya, ku yi amfani da [fom ɗin ba da ra'ayi](https://forms.myfirstbitcoin.org/feedback). Kada ku haɗa da [jimlolin kalmomin dawo da walat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), [maɓallan sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key), ragowar kuɗin walat, ko wasu bayanan sirri na mutum ko na tsaro.

### Ku ba da gudummawa ga My First Bitcoin da Cibiyar Albarkatun Koyo

Ku zaɓi hanyar taimakawa sannan ku yi amfani da fom ɗin hukuma da ya dace don farawa:

* **[Ku yi aikin sa kai](https://forms.myfirstbitcoin.org/volunteer):** ku ba da lokacinku ko ƙwarewarku don tallafa wa Difloma na Bitcoin, malamai, ko ƙungiyoyin koyo na yankinku.
* **[Ku ba da gudummawa ga Cibiyar Albarkatun Koyo](https://forms.myfirstbitcoin.org/submit-resource):** ku raba wani abu mai amfani wajen koyar da Bitcoin, kayan koyo na yankinku, ko wani gyara da wasu za su iya dubawa su kuma yi amfani da shi.
* **[Ku bincika Shirin Horar da Malamai](https://etp.myfirstbitcoin.org/):** ku koyi yadda My First Bitcoin yake shirya mutane don koyar da Difloma na Bitcoin.

Ku yi amfani da fom ɗin hukuma kawai. Kada ku haɗa da [jimlolin kalmomin dawo da walat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), [maɓallan sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key), ragowar kuɗin walat, ko wasu bayanan sirri na mutum ko na tsaro a cikin abin da za ku miƙa.

### Kuna son koyar da Difloma na Bitcoin?

Jagorar Malamai tana taimaka muku shirya darussa, gudanar da ayyuka, daidaita misalai da yanayin al'ummarku, tallafa wa atisaye cikin aminci, da tantance abin da ɗalibai za su iya yi. [Ku karanta Jagorar Malamai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/en/educator-guide/) don tsara koyarwarku.

### Ku shiga ƙungiyar koyo

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/8f3937993daec592440695eb585bf63089a75772-1840x722.png)

Ita [Cibiyar Haɗin Gwiwar Ilimin Bitcoin](https://myfirstbitcoin.org/about/network) tana haɗa malamai masu zaman kansu da ƙungiyoyin koyo na yankuna. Ku nemo ƙungiya a kusa da ku, ko wadda ayyukanta suke jan hankalinku, ku tuntuɓe ta don tambayar yadda za ku iya koyo, yin aikin sa kai, ko ba da gudummawa. Shiga na zaɓi ne kuma ba ya shafar kammala Difloma, tantancewa, ko samun takardar shaida.

### Tunatarwa kan tsaro


<!-- micrographic: final-safety-check-01 -->
[Jerin dubawar tsaro na ƙarshe da aka yi wa alamar kammalawa: ku kare sirruka, ku tabbatar da bayanan biyan kuɗi, ku yi gwaji da ƙaramin adadi, kuma ku dakata idan ana matsa muku. Idan wani abu bai bayyana ba, ku dakata ku ƙara koyo kafin ku ɗauki mataki.](/micrographics/final-safety-check-01/ha.md)


Ku tuna da waɗannan ƙa'idoji:

* Wata [jimlar kalmomin dawo da walat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) ko [maɓallin sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) na ba da ikon sarrafa kuɗi. Kada ku taɓa raba ta ko shi da wani.
* Ku tabbatar da mai karɓa, hanyar sadarwa, adireshi ko takardar neman biyan kuɗi, adadin kuɗi, da kuɗin caji kafin aikawa.
* Bitcoin [ma'amalolinsa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) galibi ba sa karɓar sokewa.
* Ku yi ƙaramin gwaji idan tsarin sabo ne a gare ku.
* Kada ku ɗauki mataki saboda matsin lamba, tsoro, ko alkawuran samun riba tabbatacciya.
* Ku tabbatar da sahihancin manhajar walat da hanyoyin tuntuɓar masu ba da tallafi ta majiyoyi masu zaman kansu.
* Ku gano wanda yake riƙe da maɓallan.
* Ku gwada hanyar dawo da walat cikin aminci kafin ku dogara da ita wajen kare kuɗi masu yawa.
* Ku ɓoye bayanan adadin kuɗaɗenku da shirye-shiryen tsaronku.
* Dokoki, haraji, caje-caje, da ayyukan hidima suna canzawa. Ku binciki bayanan yankinku na yanzu.

Ilimi yana rage haɗari amma ba zai iya kawar da shi gaba ɗaya ba. Idan wani yanayi bai bayyana ba, ku tsaya ku ƙara koyo kafin ku ɗauki mataki.

### Ku yi amfani da wannan manhajar karatu kuma ku daidaita ta

Difloma ta Bitcoin manhajar karatu ce mai buɗaɗɗen tushe wadda ake ci gaba da sabuntawa. Ana iya yin bitar gyare-gyare da ingantawa a bainar jama'a.

Asalin tushen manhajar karatun shi ne: [github.com/MyFirstBitcoin/mfb-curriculum](https://github.com/MyFirstBitcoin/mfb-curriculum)

Ku binciki bayanan bugu ko sigar da aka fitar kafin koyarwa, fassara, ko daidaita kayan karatun. Idan kun gano kuskure, ku bi tsarin ba da gudummawa ko kai rahoton matsala da ma'ajiyar take amfani da shi a yanzu.

Yayin da kuke daidaita wannan aiki:

* ku kiyaye ƙwarewar da ake buƙata don Difloma da ƙa'idodin aminci
* ku bayyana misalan yankinku a sarari
* ku tabbatar da sahihancin ikirari, ayyukan hidima, dokoki, da caje-cajen da ake da su a yanzu
* ku yi amfani da kalmomin da suka dace da masu farawa ba tare da sauya su barkatai ba
* ku bayyana abin da kuka canza
* ku ambaci My First Bitcoin a matsayin tushen aikin
* ku wallafa sigar da kuka daidaita ƙarƙashin lasisin buɗaɗɗen tushe da ake buƙata

### Ma'anar takardar shaida da mai bayar da ita

An wallafa manhajar karatu ta 26.10 da farko a matsayin kunshin koyarwa da tsarin tantancewa. Wallafawar ba ta nufin cewa kowane malami ko kowace hanyar koyarwa ta gida a shirye take ta bayar da takardun shaidar kammalawa ba. Malami yana bayar da takardar shaida ne kawai bayan ya shirya ayyukan da ake buƙata, hanyoyin da ɗalibai za su iya bi, kundin sakamakon tantancewa, da matakan kariya da suka dace da yankin, sannan ya tantance ɗalibi bisa buƙatun ilimin ka'ida da na aikace-aikace da aka amince da su.

Akwai nau'ikan takardar shaida guda biyu:

* Wata **takardar shaidar shiga** tana tabbatar da halarta ko shiga. Ba ta tabbatar da cewa ɗalibi ya ci tantancewar ba.
* Wata **takardar shaidar kammalawa** tana tabbatar da cewa malamin da aka ambata ya tantance ɗalibi kuma ya yanke hukuncin cewa duk ƙwarewar da ake buƙata ta cika ma'auni.

Takardar shaidar tabbaci ne daga malamin da aka ambata ko ƙungiyarsa. My First Bitcoin ba ya tantance ɗalibi a matakin cibiya, ko bayar da takardar shaida, ko samar da sabis na tabbatarwa na cibiya, ko bayar da garantin ƙwarewar ɗalibi. Takardar shaidar kammalawa tana wakiltar ƙwarewar mafari da aka tantance a yanayin da aka ba shi tallafi, ba ƙwarewar zuba jari, ko ƙwarewar harkokin kuɗi ta sana'a, ko shirin sarrafa kadarori masu daraja sosai ta hanyar [tsare su da kai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#self-custody).

Idan malamin da yake bayar da takardar shaida ya samar da hanyar tabbatarwa, ku yi amfani da umarnin da aka buga a takardar kawai ko waɗanda aka samar ta hanyar sadarwar malamin da aka tabbatar da sahihancinta ta wata majiya mai zaman kanta. Ku binciki sigar kwas, malami ko ƙungiyar da ta bayar da takardar, ranar kammalawa, da matsayinta, tare da raba bayanan kanku kaɗan kawai gwargwadon buƙata. Sunan My First Bitcoin ko tambarinsa ba ya mai da takardar shaidar da malami ya bayar ta zama takardar shaidar cibiya ta My First Bitcoin.

Mafi muhimmancin sakamakon da kuka samu ba takardar shaidar ba ce. Shi ne ikon bayyana kuɗi da Bitcoin a sarari, gane haɗari, tabbatarwa kafin ɗaukar mataki, da ci gaba da koyo da kanku.
