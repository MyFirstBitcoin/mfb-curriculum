# 3.5 Jumlolin dawo da walat

<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Jimlar farfaɗowa**
>
> Wata [jimlar farfaɗowa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), wadda kuma ake kira [jimlar dawo da walat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), jerin kalmomi ne masu takamaiman tsari waɗanda walat da yawa ke amfani da su don samarwa da dawo da rukunin [maɓallan sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key). Wani [maɓallin sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) yana ba da izinin kashe kuɗi; [jimlar farfaɗowa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) kuwa ita ce madadin da ake adanawa wanda zai iya sake samar da maɓallan walat. Yawanci tana da kalmomi 12 ko 24, ko da yake tsarin walat ya bambanta.


Tsarin kalmomin da rubutunsu daidai suna da muhimmanci. Idan mutum yana da [jimlar farfaɗowa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), sau da yawa zai iya dawo da walat a wata na'ura mai jituwa da ita, sannan ya kashe kuɗin. Saboda haka, [jimlar farfaɗowa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) ba madadin da ake adanawa kawai ba ce. Tana ba da cikakken ikon shiga da sarrafa walat.


<!-- card: callout -->
> **Kada ku taɓa raba bayanan farfaɗowa**
>
> Ana iya raba bayanan karɓar kuɗi na jama'a. Wani [maɓallin sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key), [jimlar farfaɗowa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), kalmar sirri, ko lambar farfaɗowa dole su kasance a ɓoye. Babu wani halastaccen malami, kamfanin walat, ma'aikacin dandalin musayar kuɗi, ma'aikacin tallafi, ko mai haɓaka Bitcoin da yake buƙatar waɗannan bayanan. Idan wata [jimlar farfaɗowa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) ta yiwu an gan ta, an kwafe ta, an shigar da ita a intanet, ko an adana ta a na'urar da ba a amince da ita ba, ku ɗauka cewa asirrinta ya tonu, sannan ku nemi taimako ba tare da bayyana ta ba.



<!-- micrographic: seed-phrase-on-paper-01 -->
[Jerin kalmomin farfaɗowa jerin kalmomi ne da aka tsara bisa ƙayyadadden tsari; a nan, kalmomin misali goma sha biyu ne masu lambobi da aka rubuta a takarda. Kalmomin da suka dace a tsarin da ya dace za su iya dawo da ikon sarrafawa a walat mai jituwa, don haka ana kiyaye jerin a ɓoye kuma ba tare da haɗa shi da intanet ba.](/micrographics/seed-phrase-on-paper-01/ha.md)


Ku kare [jimlar farfaɗowa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) ta bin waɗannan ƙa'idoji:

* ku rubuta ta a keɓe kuma daidai lokacin ƙirƙirar walat
* ku bi umarnin hukuma na walat game da adana madadin farfaɗowa
* ku adana ta ba tare da haɗa ta da intanet ba
* kada ku taɓa ɗaukar hotonta, adana ta a bayanan kula na girgije, aika ta ta imel, ko aika ta cikin saƙo
* kada ku taɓa shigar da ita a shafin yanar gizo ko fom na kwamfuta na yau da kullum
* ku nisanta ta daga kyamarori, makirufo, idanun wasu mutane, da wuraren da za ta iya lalacewa, ɓacewa, ko wani ya same ta

Wasu walat suna amfani da ƙarin jimlolin sirri, maɓallai da yawa, ko wasu tsare-tsaren adana madadin farfaɗowa. Waɗannan suna cikin [Babban Matakin Koyo](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/advanced-modules/). Kada ku ƙara sarƙaƙiyar da ba za ku iya bayyana ta da kuma farfaɗowa daga gare ta cikin aminci ba. Idan wata [jimlar farfaɗowa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) ta yiwu an gan ta, an kwafe ta, an shigar da ita a intanet, ko an adana ta a na'urar da ba a amince da ita ba, ku ɗauka cewa asirrinta ya tonu: ku nemi jagora ba tare da bayyana ta ba, sannan ku yi amfani da sabuwar hanyar farfaɗowa da aka amince da ita.
