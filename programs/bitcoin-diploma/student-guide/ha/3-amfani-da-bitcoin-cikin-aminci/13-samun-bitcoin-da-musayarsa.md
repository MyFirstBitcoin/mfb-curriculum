# 3.13 Samun bitcoin da musayarsa

#### Fadakarwa, ba shawarar kuɗi ba

Mutane na iya karɓar bitcoin ta hanyar aiki, biyan kuɗi a cikin al'umma, musayar mutum-da-mutum, ATM, ko wani sabis. Kowace hanya tana da nata batutuwan tsada, [riƙon kadarori](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), sirri, zamba, doka, da gudanar da aiki waɗanda ke buƙatar a auna [fa'idodi da rashin fa'idodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off). Shirin Difloma na Bitcoin ba ya ba da shawarar wani mai ba da sabis ko gaya muku hanyar da za ku yi amfani da ita.


> **Light – Kwatanta hanyar biyan kuɗi ta yankinku**
>
> A cikin wani yanayi da aka shirya na yankinku ko na ƙagaggen labari, ku kwatanta [riƙon kadarori](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), jimillar tsada, bayanan shaida, iyakokin cire kuɗi, amincin ɗayan ɓangaren mu'amala da lafiyar jiki, da dokokin yankin da ke aiki a yanzu. Babu wata hanya da take zama mafi kyau kai tsaye a ko'ina. Wannan kwas yana ba da tambayoyin kwatantawa, ba shawarar wani mai ba da sabis ko shawarar kuɗi ta keɓaɓɓu ba.


* **Samun kuɗi ko karɓa:** Ana iya karɓar Bitcoin a matsayin kuɗin aiki, kaya, ayyuka, kyauta, mayar da kuɗi, ko biyan kuɗi a cikin al'umma. Har yanzu ɓangarorin suna buƙatar yarda kan adadin kuɗin, lokacin biya, da hanyar biyan kuɗi.
* **Musayar mutum-da-mutum:** Mutane biyu na iya musanya [kuɗin hukuma](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money) ko kaya da bitcoin kai tsaye ko ta hanyar sabis da ke haɗa masu son yin musaya. Wannan na iya kawo haɗarin ɗayan ɓangaren mu'amala, cutarwar jiki, soke biyan kuɗi, da zamba.
* **Dandalin musaya, dillali, ATM, ko rumfar sabis:** Wani sabis ko na'ura na iya samar da hanyar da aka saba da ita, amma tana iya haɗawa da caji, tantance shaida, [riƙon kadarori](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), iyakokin cire kuɗi, ko rashin dacewar [farashin musaya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#exchange-rate).

Babu wata hanya da take zama mafi kyau kai tsaye a ko'ina. Misalai na yankinku da jagororin da suka shafi takamaiman masu ba da sabis suna cikin **Kashi na 4**.


> **Definition – Ku San Abokin Cinikinku (KYC)**
>
> [KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#kyc) yana bayyana tantance shaida da sabis da ke ƙarƙashin ƙa'idojin hukuma zai iya buƙata. Zai iya taimakawa wajen dawo da damar shiga asusu ko bin doka, amma kuma yana samar da bayanan da ke haɗa shaidar mutum da harkokinsa na kuɗi. Hanyoyin da ke tattara bayanan shaida kaɗan na iya ƙunsar haɗurra daban-daban da suka shafi ɗayan ɓangaren mu'amala, doka, tsaro, tsada, ko sarƙaƙiya.
