# 3.2 Jakunkunan Kuɗi

> **Definition – Jakar Bitcoin**
>
> Wata [jakar Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-wallet) kayan aiki ce da ke sarrafa bayanan da ake buƙata don karɓa da ba da izinin [ma'amalolin Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction). Tana iya zama manhaja, na'urar da aka keɓe don wannan aiki, ko wani sabis. Ana rubuta Bitcoin a matsayin bayanan [ma'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) na baya a kan [kundin lissafi na bai ɗaya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger); jaka tana sarrafa maɓallan da ke ba da ikon kashe kuɗi, maimakon adana bitcoin a waya ko wata na'ura.


Me zai faru idan wayarku ta daina aiki? Bayanan Bitcoin za su ci gaba da kasancewa, amma samun damar amfani da shi zai dogara ne da maɓallan da kuma hanyar dawo da damar da jakar take amfani da ita. Jaka tana taimakawa wajen samar da bayanan karɓa, nuna ragowar kuɗi da bayanan [ma'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) na baya, da ba da izinin kashe kuɗi. Ba ta kawar da buƙatar fahimtar wanda ke da ikon ba da damar amfani ba.

> **Dark – Jaka tana sarrafa damar amfani; ba ta riƙe bitcoin**
>
> Ana rubuta Bitcoin a kan [kundin lissafi na bai ɗaya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger), ba a cikin wayarku ba. Kafin ku yi amfani da jaka, ku san wanda ke iko da maɓallan, yadda ake dawo da damar amfani, da abin da zai faru idan na'urar ko sabis ɗin ya daina aiki.



<!-- micrographic: wallet-manages-keys-01 -->


Tambaya ta farko game da kowace jaka ita ce: **wa ke iko da maɓallan?** Kafin ku yi amfani da wani kayan aiki, ku bincika wanda zai iya ba da izinin biyan kuɗi, yadda ake dawo da damar amfani, da hanyoyin biyan kuɗi da yake tallafawa. [**Manhajar da lambar tushenta take a buɗe**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#open-source-software) tana wallafa lambar tushenta domin kowa ya iya dubawa da nazartawa. Wannan na iya ƙara bayyana yadda take aiki da sauƙaƙa gano kurakurai, amma abu ɗaya ne kawai mai amfani da za a yi la'akari da shi: ba ya tabbatar da cewa kayan aiki yana da aminci, ana kula da shi, an girka shi daidai, ko ya dace da buƙatunku.

Ku kwatanta [wanda ke riƙe da iko da maɓallan](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), dawo da damar amfani, ko jama'a za su iya duba lambar tushen, dacewa da sauran tsaruka, [sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy), farashi, kulawa, da taimakon da ake samu ba tare da ɗaukar wata jaka a matsayin zaɓin da ya dace da kowa a duniya ba. Ku fara da yanayin amfani da malami ya shirya; kada ku zaɓi wani kayan aiki don kawai wani baƙo ya ba da shawararsa.


> **Callout – Jakunkunan koyo da jakunkunan amfani na kai**
>
> A lokacin karatun Diploma, kuna iya ƙirƙirar jaka ko rubuta [kalmomin dawo da damar amfani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) a matsayin wani ɓangare na atisaye. Aji wuri ne na koyo: ana iya ganin bayanai, kwafe su, raba su, ko a kuskura a fallasa su. Saboda haka, a al'ada ya kamata jakar horo ta kasance babu komai a cikinta ko kuma ta kwaikwayo, kuma bai kamata a yi amfani da ita wajen kiyaye kuɗi masu yawa ko muhimmanci ba. Inda zai yiwu, ana rufe ta, a sake saita ta, ko a mayar da ita a ƙarshen Diploma. Malaminku na iya zaɓar wani atisaye na gaske da haɗarin asararsa ba shi da yawa, amma ya kamata ya bayyana manufarsa da ƙarin haɗarin da ke tattare da shi ga sirri, tsaro, da asarar kuɗi kafin kowa ya shiga. Jakar da aka nufa don kiyaye kuɗi masu yawa ko muhimmanci tana buƙatar sabon saiti da aka yi a keɓance da sabbin bayanan dawo da damar amfani. Malaminku zai iya bayyana bambancin, amma ba a taɓa wajabta muku saita jakar amfani ta kanku don kammala Diploma ba.
