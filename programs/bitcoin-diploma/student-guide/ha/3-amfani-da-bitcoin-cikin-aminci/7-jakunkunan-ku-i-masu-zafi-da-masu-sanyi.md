# 3.7 Jakunkunan kuɗi masu zafi da masu sanyi

<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Jakar kuɗi mai haɗin intanet**
>
> Wata [jakar kuɗi mai haɗin intanet](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#hot-wallet) tana sarrafa maɓallai a kan na'urar da ke da haɗin intanet, kamar waya ko kwamfuta. Tana sauƙaƙa biyan kuɗi na yau da kullum, amma tana da ƙarin hanyoyin da za a iya kai mata hari domin manhajoji masu cutarwa, sabuntawa marasa tsaro, saƙonnin damfara, samun damar taɓa na'urar kai tsaye, ko kutse cikin na'urar na iya jefa jakar kuɗin cikin haɗari.



<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Jakar kuɗi marar haɗin intanet**
>
> Wata [jakar kuɗi marar haɗin intanet](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cold-wallet) tana nufin tsarin da ke ware maɓallan sa hannu daga na'urar amfani iri-iri mai haɗin intanet.



<!-- micrographic: hot-and-cold-wallets-01 -->
[Jakar kuɗi mai zafi tana adana maɓallan sanya hannu a kan na'urar da ke da haɗin intanet kamar waya; tsarin jakar kuɗi mai sanyi yana adana su nesa da duk wata na'ura mai haɗin intanet. Batun wanda ke da iko da maɓallan tambaya ce dabam.](/micrographics/hot-and-cold-wallets-01/ha.md)



<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Jakar kuɗi ta na'ura**
>
> Wata [jakar kuɗi ta na'ura](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#hardware-wallet) tana nufin wata na'ura ta musamman da aka ƙera don samarwa, karewa, da amfani da maɓallai tare da ware su daga kwamfuta ko wayar da aka haɗa ta da ita. Wata [jakar kuɗi ta na'ura](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#hardware-wallet) na iya tallafa wa tsarin ajiya marar haɗin intanet, amma kalmomin ba su nufin abu ɗaya ba; [riƙon amana](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) da dawo da damar shiga batutuwa ne daban-daban.



<!-- micrographic: wallet-arrangements-table-01 -->
[Teburin tsare-tsaren jakar kuɗi guda uku (jakar kuɗi mai haɗin intanet, jakar kuɗi ta na'ura ko tsarin da ba ya haɗe da intanet, asusun da wani ke riƙewa a madadin mai shi), kowannensu da fa'idar da aka fi sani da kuma nauyin da ya kamata a tuna. Batun wanda ke iko da maɓallai da batun muhallin sanya hannu abubuwa ne daban.](/micrographics/wallet-arrangements-table-01/ha.md)


**Ku gwada:** Don wani ɗan ƙaramin biyan kuɗi na ƙirƙira da kuma wani biyan kuɗi na ƙirƙira da ba a yawan yi, ku bayyana wace [daidaitawa tsakanin fa'ida da rashin amfani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) ta fi muhimmanci. Kada ku zaɓi kayan aiki ko adadin kuɗi don kuɗaɗenku na kanku.
