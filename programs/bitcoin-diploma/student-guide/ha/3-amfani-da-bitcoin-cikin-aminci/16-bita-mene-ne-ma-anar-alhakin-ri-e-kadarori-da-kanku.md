Ku yi tunanin wani abokinku ya aiko muku da buƙatar biyan kuɗi, a daidai lokacin da wani asusun tallafi da ba ku sani ba yake cewa dole ku ɗauki mataki nan take. Me za ku iya bincikawa kafin yin komai? Za ku iya kare tsarin farfaɗowa, bambance bayanan karɓar kuɗi da bayanan sirri, sannan ku tabbatar da mutumin, hanyar biyan kuɗi, inda za a aika, adadin kuɗi, da abin da ke allon ƙarshe kafin aikawa? Ko sabis ne ke sarrafa maɓallan ko kuma ku ne, taƙaitaccen abin yi bai bambanta ba: ku kare bayanan sirri, ku tabbatar kafin amincewa, ku gane alamun zamba, sannan ku rage girman kurakurai yayin koyo.

#### Muhimman abubuwan tunawa

* Walat yana sarrafa maɓallai da bayanan da ake amfani da su wajen karɓa, dubawa, da kashe bitcoin; ba a adana bitcoin a waya ko wata na'ura ba.
* Ana iya raba adiresoshi da buƙatun biyan kuɗi domin karɓar bitcoin, amma [maɓallan sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) da [jimlolin kalmomin farfaɗowa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) dole su kasance sirri.
* Wata [jimlar kalmomin farfaɗowa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) na iya ba da cikakkiyar damar farfaɗowa, don haka dole a adana ta ba tare da haɗa ta da intanet ba, a ɓoye, kuma ba a shafukan yanar gizo ko saƙonni ba.
* Tsarin da wani ke riƙe muku da maɓallai da tsarin da kuke riƙe da su da kanku suna haifar da nauye-nauyen farfaɗowa da sarrafawa daban-daban. [Walat na na'ura](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#hardware-wallet) har yanzu suna dogara da kwafin ajiya mai aminci.
* Kafin karɓa ko aikawa, ku tabbatar da ingantacciyar hanyar biyan kuɗi, inda za a aika, adadin kuɗi, da matsayin ƙarshe; aikawa da aka yi daidai yawanci yana da wahalar juyawa ko ma ba zai yiwu a juyar da shi ba.
* Ku fara da atisaye mai aminci ko ƙananan adadin kuɗi da aka amince da su. Ku dakata idan wata buƙata ta haifar da gaggawa, ɓoye-ɓoye, ko matsin lamba.
