# 3.12 Jerin Tabbatar da Tsaro

**Atisaye na 3: gano haɗari kuma ku dakata cikin aminci**

Me zai sa ku dakata kafin biyan kuɗi ko girka manhaja? Ku ɗauki waɗannan a matsayin alamun haɗari: wani ya yi kwaikwayon ma'aikacin tallafi ko malami; ya matsa muku ku yi gaggawa ko ku ɓoye al'amari; ya yi alkawarin tabbatacciyar riba; ya nemi ku fara aika bitcoin; ya sauya inda za a aika kuɗi; ko ya nemi maɓallai, [kalmomin dawo da walat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), damar shiga na'urarku daga nesa, ko kuɗin da za a biya tun da farko.

[Alamomin gargaɗi guda biyar na zamba: gaggawa, yin amfani da sunan wani, sauya inda za a aika, tabbatacciyar riba da neman bayanan sirri.](/micrographics/scam-red-flags-01/ha.md)


Amsar da ta dace don tsaro a koyaushe ita ce: **Ku dakata. Kada ku raba komai. Kada ku aika komai. Ku rufe hanyar sadarwar. Ku tabbatar da kanku ta hanyar majiya sananniya. Ku nemi taimako ba tare da tona asirai ba.** Majiya sananniya ita ce shafin hukuma, bayanin manhaja a shagon manhajoji, ko hanyar tuntuɓa da kuka samo da kanku, ba ta hanyar talla, saƙon kai tsaye, ko mahaɗin da ba ku yi tsammani ba. Ku bincika mai wallafawa da adireshin yanar gizon, ku kwatanta su da bayanan hukuma, kuma ku nemo bayanan tuntuɓa ta wata hanya dabam. Idan majiyar, umarnin, ko bayanan biyan kuɗin sun bambanta da tsarin da aka nuna muku, ku dakata ku nemi taimako daga malaminku ko amintaccen mai ba da tallafi a yankinku ba tare da bayyana [jerin kalmomin dawo da walat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) ko [maɓallin sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key). Dama ta gaskiya ba za ta gushe saboda an bincika ta a hankali ba; zamba kuwa sau da yawa tana dogara ne ga hana irin wannan bincike.

Ku kare bayanan dawo da walat: [jerin kalmomin dawo da walat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) na iya sake samar wa walat da [maɓallan sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key). Ku ajiye shi ba tare da haɗin intanet ba kuma a ɓoye daga gani. Ku tabbatar da inda za a aika kuɗi ta hanyar duba hanyar biyan kuɗi, mai karɓa, adireshi ko daftarin neman biyan kuɗi, adadin kuɗi, da allon ƙarshe bayan kwafi ko sikanawa. Ku bi umarnin hukuma kuma ku yi amfani da majiyar da aka tabbatar da ingancinta don kowane walat ko sabis; ku yi hattara da sakamakon binciken da aka biya kuɗin tallatawa, mahaɗan saƙonnin kai tsaye, da shafukan da suke kama da na gaskiya. Ku fara da atisaye mai aminci, kwaikwayon aiki, ko ƙaramin adadin da aka amince da shi; kada ku taɓa cin bashi don yin gwaji.

**Ku gwada:** A cikin saƙo ko allon biyan kuɗi da aka tanada, ku gano alamar gargaɗi, abin da za a iya rasa, da matakin farko na kiyaye aminci.
