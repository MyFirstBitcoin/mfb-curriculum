# 3.4 Maɓallai

Ana tabbatar da mallakar Bitcoin ta hanyar mabuɗan ɓoye bayanai.


> **Definition – Mabuɗin sirri**
>
> Wani [mabuɗin sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) bayani ne na sirri da ke ba walat damar ƙirƙirar ingantattun [sa hannun dijital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-signature). Sa hannu yana ba da izinin kashe bitcoin da mabuɗin da ya dace yake sarrafawa. Duk wanda ya samu [mabuɗin sirrin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) yawanci zai iya kashe waɗannan kuɗaɗen.


Wani [**mabuɗin jama'a**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#public-key) ana samo shi ta hanyar lissafi daga [mabuɗin sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key). Yana taimakawa wajen tantance sa hannu ba tare da bayyana [mabuɗin sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key). Ana ƙirƙirar adiresoshi daga [mabuɗin jama'a](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#public-key) ta amfani da bayanansa.

Muhimmiyar ƙa'idar tana da sauƙi:

**Ana iya raba bayanan karɓar kuɗi na jama'a. Bayanan sirri na kashe kuɗi dole ne su kasance a ɓoye.**

Wani [sa hannun dijital](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-signature) yana tabbatar da cewa [mabuɗin sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) da ake buƙata ya ba da izinin [ma'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction). Ba ya sanya [mabuɗin sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) a cikin [ma'amalar](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction). [Nododi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) suna tantance sa hannun kafin amincewa da kashe kuɗin.

Yawancin walat na masu farawa suna amfani da [sa hannu guda ɗaya](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#single-signature): mabuɗin sirri guda ɗaya yana ba da izinin ma'amala. Walat na zamani suna sarrafa mabuɗai da yawa kai tsaye. Bai kamata masu farawa su kwafi ko sarrafa [mabuɗan sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) ɗaya bayan ɗaya a matsayin jerin haruffa ba. Ku kiyaye kwafin ajiyar walat da aka amince da shi, kuma ku bi tsarin dawo da shi.

Babu wani sahihin malami, kamfanin walat, ma'aikacin dandalin musayar kuɗi, ma'aikacin taimakon fasaha, ko mai haɓaka Bitcoin da ke buƙatar [mabuɗin sirrinku](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) ko [jimlar kalmomin dawo da walat ɗinku](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase). Ya kamata a ɗauki duk wanda ya nemi waɗannan bayanan a matsayin barazana.


> **Light**
>
> **Ku gwada:** Ku rarraba misalan da aka shirya zuwa rukuni uku: bayanan da za a iya rabawa cikin aminci don karɓar kuɗi, bayanan sirri na kashe kuɗi, da bayanan da za su iya zama na jama'a amma har yanzu ya kamata a ɓoye su idan suna da alaƙa da wani mutum.
