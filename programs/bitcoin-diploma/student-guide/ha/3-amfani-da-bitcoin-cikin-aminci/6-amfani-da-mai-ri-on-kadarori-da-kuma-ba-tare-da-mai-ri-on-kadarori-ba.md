# 3.6 Amfani da mai riƙon kadarori da kuma ba tare da mai riƙon kadarori ba

<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Riƙon maɓallai**
>
> [Riƙon maɓallai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) yana amsa wata tambaya ta zahiri: wa ke sarrafa maɓallan da ake buƙata don kashe bitcoin?


A cikin **tsarin riƙon maɓallai** na sabis, kamfani ko wani mutum ne ke sarrafa maɓallan. Ragowar kuɗin da aka nuna muku alkawari ne daga [mai riƙon maɓallai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custodian). Yawanci, kuna shiga da asusu sannan ku nemi sabis ɗin ya aiwatar da cire kuɗi ko biyan kuɗi.

Wannan bambanci yana da muhimmanci wajen tabbatarwa. Ana iya bincika ƙa'idojin Bitcoin da jimillar adadinsa da kanku, amma idan ba ku ne ke sarrafa maɓallan ba, ba za ku iya tabbatarwa da kanku cewa ragowar kuɗin da aka nuna a asusunku ya yi daidai da bitcoin da sabis ɗin yake riƙe da shi kuma zai ba ku damar amfani da shi ba. Sarkar bulok ta jama'a na iya nuna bitcoin da maɓallai ke sarrafawa; ba za ta iya nuna cewa wani sabis yana bin wani takamaiman abokin ciniki bashin ragowar kuɗin da aka nuna a asusu ba. Har sai kun iya cire kuɗin kuma ku sarrafa maɓallan, ragowar kuɗinku alkawarin sabis ɗin ne na samar muku da bitcoin, ba bitcoin da za ku iya tabbatarwa kai tsaye kuma ku kashe da kanku ba.

A cikin **tsarin da babu mai riƙon maɓallai a madadin mai amfani** ko **tsarin riƙon maɓallai da kai** na walat, KU NE ke sarrafa maɓallan. Walat ɗin yana sanya wa [ma'amaloli](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) hannu bisa izinin ku. Har yanzu ana iya dogaro da manhaja, kayan aiki, sabar, ko hanyar sadarwa, amma babu wani [mai riƙon maɓallai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custodian) da ake buƙatar amincewarsa don ingantacciyar ma'amalar kashe kuɗi a kan sarkar bulok.

[Riƙon maɓallai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) ba koyaushe yake bayyana daga fuskar manhajar ba. Idan ba za ku iya sarrafa hanyar dawo da damar shiga ba kuma kuna buƙatar kamfani ya amince da cire kuɗi, mai yiwuwa kuna amfani da [mai riƙon maɓallai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custodian). [Riƙon maɓallai da kai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#self-custody) ba gwajin kimar mutum ba ne; muhimmin mataki shi ne gano irin amincewa da haɗarin da ke tattare da hakan.

**Ku gwada:** Ku karanta bayanai guda biyu da aka shirya game da walat. Ga kowannensu, ku bayyana wanda ke sarrafa maɓallan, wanda zai iya dawo da damar shiga, da haɗari guda ɗaya da za a bincika kafin amfani da shi.
