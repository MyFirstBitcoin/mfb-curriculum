# 3.11 Tabbatarwa, kayan binciken bulogi, da ma’amalolin da ba za a iya soke su ba

> **Definition – ID na mu'amala da mai binciken tubalai**
>
> Wani [ID na mu'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction-id) ([_txid_](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction-id)) yana tantance, a kan sarkar, wata [mu'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction). Wani [mai binciken tubalai](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#block-explorer) yana nuna bayanan Bitcoin na jama'a, kamar bayanan [mu'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) game da matsayinta da adadin [tabbatarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#confirmation). Hanya ce mai amfani ta duba bayanai, ba hukuma mai iko a kan Bitcoin ba ce, kuma ba hujja ba ce ta ainihin wanda mai karɓa yake ko amincinsa.


Me waɗannan bayanan jama'a za su iya nunawa, kuma me ba za su iya nunawa ba? Za su iya nuna bayanan [mu'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) game da matsayinta, inda za ta je, adadin kuɗi, kuɗin hidima, da adadin [tabbatarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#confirmation); ba za su iya tabbatar da dalilin yin biyan kuɗi ko ko mutum amintacce ne ba. A kan sarkar, wata [mu'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) tana kasancewa ba a tabbatar da ita ba bayan an [yaɗa ta](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#broadcast) kuma tana samun [tabbatarwarta ta farko](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#first-confirmation) lokacin da aka haɗa ta cikin ingantaccen tubali. Tubalan da suka zo daga baya suna ƙara amincewa da matsayinta a cikin kundin bayanai, amma ba sa gyara kuskuren inda aka aika kuɗi ko zamba. Babu wani adadin [tabbatarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#confirmation) da ya dace da kowane biyan kuɗi; binciken da ya dace ya danganta da adadin kuɗin da haɗarin da ke tattare da shi.

Domin bincika shiryayyiyar [mu'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction), ku samo [_txid_](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction-id) ɗinta, ku buɗe amintaccen mai binciken tubalai na [cibiyar sadarwar Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-network) da ake nufi, sannan ku bincika matsayi, inda za ta je, adadin kuɗi, kuɗin hidima, da [tabbatarwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#confirmation). Mai binciken tubalai hanya ce kawai ta samun damar duba bayanai. Neman adireshi ko [_txid_](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction-id) na iya bayyana wa mai gudanar da shi cewa na'urarku tana sha'awar waɗannan bayanai. Don atisaye, ku yi amfani da [mu'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) da malami ya shirya ko hoton allo da aka buga a takarda. Kada ku nemi adireshin ɗalibi na kansa ko [mu'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) tasa a wani sabis na jama'a. Idan daga baya za ku binciki biyan kuɗin kanku, ku fahimci yadda [keɓantawa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy) da tsaro suke da [fa'idodi da rashin fa'idodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) dangane da haɗin sadarwarku kafin ku yi amfani da sabis na jama'a.


> **Callout – Bayanan jama'a ba hujja ba ne ta ainihi ko gaskiya**
>
> Wani [mai binciken bulok](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#block-explorer) na iya nuna bayanan jama'a na [ma'amala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction), amma ba zai iya tabbatar da wanda ke sarrafa wani adreshi ba, ko dalilin da ya sa aka yi wani biyan kuɗi, ko kuma ko wanda ya karɓi kuɗin abin amincewa ne. Ku yi amfani da misalan da aka shirya don atisaye kuma ku kare [sirri](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy) yayin bincike a ayyukan da jama'a ke amfani da su.
