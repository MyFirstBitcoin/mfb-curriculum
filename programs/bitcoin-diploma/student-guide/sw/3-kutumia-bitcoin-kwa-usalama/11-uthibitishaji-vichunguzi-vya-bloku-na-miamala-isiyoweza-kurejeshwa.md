# 3.11 **Uthibitishaji, vichunguzi vya bloku, na miamala isiyoweza kurejeshwa**

<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Kitambulisho cha Muamala na Block Explorer**
>
> Kila [kitambulisho cha muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction-id) ([_txid_](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction-id)) hutambulisha [muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) ulio kwenye mnyororo wa bloku (on-chain). [Block Explorer](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#block-explorer) huonyesha data za umma za Bitcoin, kama vile hali ya [muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) na idadi ya [uthibitisho](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#confirmation). Ni mtazamo muhimu, lakini si mamlaka juu ya Bitcoin wala si ushahidi wa utambulisho au uaminifu wa mpokeaji.


Taarifa hizi za umma zinaweza kuonyesha nini, na haziwezi kuonyesha nini? Zinaweza kuonyesha hali ya [muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction), anwani ya kupokea, kiasi, ada, na idadi ya [uthibitisho](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#confirmation); haziwezi kuthibitisha kwa nini malipo yalifanyika au kama mtu anaaminika. [Muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) ulio kwenye mnyororo wa bloku haujathibitishwa baada ya [kutangazwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#broadcast) na hupata [uthibitisho wake wa kwanza](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#first-confirmation) unapojumuishwa katika bloku halali. Bloku zinazofuata huongeza imani kuhusu nafasi yake katika rekodi, lakini hazirekebishi anwani isiyo sahihi wala utapeli. Hakuna idadi moja ya [uthibitisho](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#confirmation) inayofaa kila malipo; ukaguzi unaofaa hutegemea kiasi na hatari.

Ili kukagua [muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) uliotayarishwa, pata [_txid_](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction-id) yake, fungua explorer inayoaminika kwa ajili ya [Mtandao wa Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-network) unaokusudiwa, kisha ukague hali, anwani ya kupokea, kiasi, ada, na idadi ya [uthibitisho](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#confirmation). Explorer ni kiolesura tu. Kutafuta anwani au [_txid_](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction-id) kunaweza kumfichulia mwendeshaji wake kwamba kifaa chako kinavutiwa na taarifa hiyo. Kwa mazoezi, tumia [muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) uliotayarishwa na mwalimu au picha ya skrini iliyochapishwa. Tafadhali usitafute anwani binafsi ya mwanafunzi au [muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) wake kwenye huduma ya umma. Ikiwa baadaye utachunguza malipo binafsi, jifunze kuhusu [faragha](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy) na usalama, pamoja na [faida na hasara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) za muunganisho wako kabla ya kutumia huduma ya umma.


<!-- card: callout -->
> **Data za umma si uthibitisho wa utambulisho au uaminifu**
>
> Zana ya [block explorer](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#block-explorer) inaweza kuonyesha data za umma za [muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction), lakini haiwezi kuthibitisha nani anadhibiti anwani, kwa nini malipo yalifanyika, au kama mpokeaji ni mwaminifu. Inashauriwa kutumia mifano iliyoandaliwa kwa mazoezi na kulinda [faragha](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy) wakati wa kutafuta katika huduma za umma.
