# 3.5 Maneno ya Mbegu

<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Maneno ya mbegu**
>
> Kinachoitwa [maneno ya mbegu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), au kwa jina lingine [maneno ya urejeshaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), ni orodha ya maneno yaliyopangwa kwa mfuatano ambayo pochi nyingi huitumia kuzalisha na kurejesha seti ya [funguo za kibinafsi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key). [Ufunguo wa kibinafsi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) huidhinisha matumizi; [maneno ya mbegu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) kwa kawaida ndiyo nakala ya akiba inayoweza kuunda upya funguo za pochi. Urefu wa kawaida ni maneno 12 au 24, ingawa miundo ya pochi hutofautiana.


Mpangilio na tahajia ya maneno ni muhimu. Ikiwa mtu ana [maneno ya mbegu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), mara nyingi anaweza kurejesha pochi kwenye kifaa kingine kinachooana nayo na kutumia fedha. Kwa hiyo, [maneno ya mbegu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) si nakala ya akiba tu. Ni ufikiaji kamili.


<!-- card: callout -->
> **Msishiriki kamwe taarifa za urejeshaji**
>
> Taarifa za umma za kupokea zinaweza kushirikiwa. [Ufunguo wa kibinafsi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key), [maneno ya mbegu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), nenosiri au msimbo wa urejeshaji lazima viwe siri. Hakuna mwalimu halali, kampuni ya pochi, mfanyakazi wa soko la kubadilishia fedha, mhudumu wa usaidizi au msanidi wa Bitcoin anayevihitaji. Ikiwa [maneno ya mbegu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) huenda yameonwa, yamenakiliwa, yameingizwa mtandaoni au yamehifadhiwa kwenye kifaa kisichoaminika, yachukulieni kuwa yameathiriwa na tafuteni msaada bila kuyafichua.



<!-- micrographic: seed-phrase-on-paper-01 -->
[Kifungu cha maneno ya urejeshaji (seed phrase) ni orodha ya maneno yaliyopangwa kwa mpangilio maalum; hapa ni maneno kumi na mawili ya mfano yenye namba, yaliyoandikwa kwenye karatasi. Maneno sahihi kwa mpangilio sahihi yanaweza kurejesha udhibiti katika pochi inayooana, kwa hivyo kifungu hiki hutunzwa kwa siri na nje ya mtandao.](/micrographics/seed-phrase-on-paper-01/sw.md)


Lindeni [maneno ya mbegu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) kwa kufuata kanuni hizi:

* yaandikeni kwa faragha na kwa usahihi wakati wa kuunda pochi
* fuateni maelekezo rasmi ya pochi kuhusu nakala ya akiba
* yahifadhini nje ya mtandao
* kamwe msiyapige picha, msiyahifadhi katika madokezo ya wingu, msiyatume kwa barua pepe wala kwa ujumbe
* kamwe msiyaandike kwenye tovuti au fomu ya kawaida ya kompyuta
* yawekeni mbali na kamera, maikrofoni, macho ya watu wengine, na mahali ambapo yanaweza kuharibika, kupotea au kupatikana

Baadhi ya pochi hutumia kaulisiri za ziada, funguo nyingi, au mifumo mingine ya nakala za akiba. Haya ni [Mafunzo ya Juu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/advanced-modules/). Msiongeze utata ambao hamwezi kuueleza na kurejesha pochi kutokana nao kwa usalama. Ikiwa [maneno ya mbegu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) huenda yameonwa, yamenakiliwa, yameingizwa mtandaoni au yamehifadhiwa kwenye kifaa kisichoaminika, yachukulieni kuwa yameathiriwa: tafuteni mwongozo bila kuyafichua na tumieni mchakato mpya wa urejeshaji unaoaminika.
