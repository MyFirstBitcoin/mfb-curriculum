# 3.4 Funguo

Umiliki wa Bitcoin unalindwa kupitia funguo za kriptografia.


> **Definition – Ufunguo wa kibinafsi**
>
> Kila [ufunguo wa kibinafsi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) ni taarifa ya siri inayoruhusu pochi kuunda [sahihi za kidijitali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-signature) halali. Sahihi huidhinisha matumizi ya bitcoin zinazodhibitiwa na ufunguo husika. Mtu yeyote anayepata [ufunguo wa kibinafsi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) kwa kawaida anaweza kutumia fedha hizo.


Kila [**ufunguo wa umma**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#public-key) hutokana kihisabati na [ufunguo wa kibinafsi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key). Husaidia kuthibitisha sahihi bila kufichua [ufunguo wa kibinafsi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key). Anwani huundwa kutokana na taarifa za [ufunguo wa umma](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#public-key).

Kanuni ya msingi ni rahisi:

**Taarifa za umma za kupokea zinaweza kushirikiwa. Taarifa binafsi za kutumia lazima zibaki siri.**

Kila [sahihi ya kidijitali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-signature) huthibitisha kwamba [ufunguo wa kibinafsi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) unaohitajika uliidhinisha [muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction). Haiweki [ufunguo wa kibinafsi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) ndani ya [muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction). [Nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) huthibitisha sahihi kabla ya kukubali matumizi hayo.

Pochi nyingi za wanaoanza hutumia [sahihi moja](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#single-signature): ufunguo mmoja wa kibinafsi huidhinisha muamala. Pochi za kisasa husimamia funguo nyingi kiotomatiki. Wanaoanza hawapaswi kunakili au kushughulikia mifuatano binafsi ya [ufunguo wa kibinafsi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key). Lindeni nakala rudufu iliyoidhinishwa ya pochi na fuateni utaratibu wake wa urejeshaji.

Hakuna mwalimu halali, kampuni ya pochi, mfanyakazi wa soko la kubadilishana, mhudumu wa msaada wa kiufundi, wala msanidi wa Bitcoin anayehitaji [ufunguo wenu wa kibinafsi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) au [maneno yenu ya mbegu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase). Mtu yeyote anayeviomba anapaswa kuchukuliwa kuwa tishio.


> **Light**
>
> **Jaribuni:** Pangeni mifano iliyoandaliwa katika makundi matatu: taarifa salama kushiriki kwa ajili ya kupokea, taarifa za siri za kutumia, na taarifa ambazo zinaweza kuwa za umma lakini bado zinapaswa kuwekwa faragha zinapohusishwa na mtu.
