# 2.1 Kwa nini Bitcoin iliundwa

Malipo ya kidijitali yalikuwepo kabla ya Bitcoin. Watu waliweza kutuma pesa kupitia benki, mitandao ya kadi, na kampuni za malipo za mtandaoni. Mifumo hii ilirahisisha biashara, lakini kila mmoja ulitegemea taasisi inayoaminika kutunza [daftari la kumbukumbu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger), kuidhinisha akaunti, kukamilisha uhamisho, na kutatua migogoro.

Utegemezi huu huzalisha mamlaka na hatari. [Mpatanishi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#intermediary) anaweza kuchelewesha malipo, kutoza ada, kukusanya na kutumia vibaya data binafsi, kuzuia akaunti, kubatilisha uhamisho, au kushindwa kufanya kazi. [Mtoaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) wa [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) pia anaweza kubadilisha kiasi chake kilichopo katika mzunguko.

Kwa miaka mingi, wanasayansi wa kompyuta walijaribu kuunda pesa ya kidijitali isiyohitaji [mtoaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) mkuu au kampuni ya malipo. Changamoto hiyo ilikuwa ngumu. Taarifa za kidijitali ni rahisi kunakiliwa. Ikiwa sarafu ya kidijitali ingeweza kunakiliwa na kutumiwa mara mbili, isingeweza kufanya kazi kama pesa. Ikiwa kampuni moja ingezuia unakili, kampuni hiyo ingekuwa kitovu cha udhibiti na cha kushindwa.

Bitcoin iliundwa kutatua tatizo hili kwa kutumia [Mtandao wa Rika-kwa-Rika (P2P)](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#peer-to-peer-network). Muundo wake unawawezesha washiriki kukubaliana juu ya historia moja ya [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) bila mtunzaji mkuu wa [daftari la kumbukumbu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger). Inaunganisha [daftari la kumbukumbu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger) la umma ([Blockchain](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#blockchain)), [sahihi za kidijitali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-signature), mtandao wa [nodi zinazothibitisha](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#full-node), [Proof-of-Work](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work), na motisha za kiuchumi.

Bitcoin haiondoi hitaji la kuaminiana katika kila nyanja ya maisha. Inapunguza hitaji la kuamini mamlaka kuu kwa utoaji na ukamilishaji wa malipo ya pesa yake asilia.

Katika kitabu hiki, **Bitcoin** yenye herufi kubwa B kwa kawaida humaanisha mtandao, itifaki, au mfumo mpana. Na **bitcoin** yenye herufi ndogo b humaanisha kipimo cha fedha au [mali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset). **BTC** ni ufupisho wa kawaida wa kiasi cha bitcoin, hasa wakati wa kuandika [bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) au viwango mahususi. Pochi au soko la kubadilishia fedha ni huduma inayoweza kuunganishwa na Bitcoin; si [Mtandao wa Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-network) wenyewe.
