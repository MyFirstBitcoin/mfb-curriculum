# 2.6 Ugatuzi

Kila [mfumo wa kati](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#centralized-system) una kituo cha udhibiti. Benki hudhibiti [leja](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger) ya wateja wake. Kampuni ya malipo hudhibiti mtandao wake. Serikali hudhibiti kanuni za utoaji wa [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) yake.


> **Definition – Ugatuaji**
>
> Kila [mfumo uliogatuliwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#decentralized-system) hugawa majukumu na utekelezaji wa maamuzi miongoni mwa washiriki wengi huru. Bitcoin haina ofisi kuu inayoweza kufungua akaunti, kubadilisha salio, au kuidhinisha uanachama.


Kwa Bitcoin, [ugatuaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#decentralization) unatokana na vipengele kadhaa:

* mtu yeyote anaweza kupakua na kukagua [programu huria](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#open-source-software)
* watumiaji wanaweza kufanya miamala bila kufungua akaunti na [mtandao wa Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-network)
* washiriki huru wanaoendesha [nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) hukagua [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) na bloku
* [wachimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) hushindana kupendekeza bloku
* hakuna [mchimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) anayeweza kufanya bloku batili ikubalike na waendeshaji waaminifu wa [nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node)
* mabadiliko ya kanuni yanahitaji kukubaliwa kwa hiari na washiriki
* nakala za [leja](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger) zinahifadhiwa kwenye kompyuta na maeneo mengi

[Ugatuaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#decentralization) si sawa na kuwa na ushawishi sawa. Vifaa vya [uchimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work), maarifa ya kiufundi, pesa, upatikanaji wa intaneti, na uundaji wa programu havijagawanywa kwa usawa. Watoa huduma wakubwa wanaweza kuwa vituo vya utegemezi kwa watumiaji wao wenyewe. Dai la Bitcoin ni finyu zaidi: hakuna mshiriki mmoja aliye na mamlaka ya kubadilisha peke yake [leja](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger) au kanuni za fedha kwa kila mtu.

[Ugatuaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#decentralization) pia huleta gharama. Uratibu unaweza kuwa wa polepole zaidi. Watumiaji lazima wathibitishe taarifa na kubeba wajibu zaidi. Hakuna dawati kuu la usaidizi kwa [mtandao wa Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-network). [Mabadilishano haya ya faida na hasara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) ni sehemu ya muundo, si kasoro za bahati mbaya zinazoweza kuondolewa bila kubadilisha mfumo.
