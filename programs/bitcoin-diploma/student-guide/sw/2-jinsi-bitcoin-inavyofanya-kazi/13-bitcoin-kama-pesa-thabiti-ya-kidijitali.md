# 2.13 Bitcoin kama pesa thabiti ya kidijitali

Kama **Moduli ya 1** ilivyoeleza, [**pesa imara**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#sound-money) inaelezea pesa ambayo ugavi wake ni mgumu kuongezwa na ambayo sifa zake zinawezesha matumizi ya kuaminika kwa muda mrefu. Neno hili linaonyesha tathmini inayotegemea vigezo, kwa hivyo linapaswa kuchunguzwa badala ya kukubaliwa tu kama lebo.

Bitcoin inaunganisha sifa ambazo hapo awali hazikuwahi kuwepo pamoja katika [rasilimali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) moja:

| Sifa | Maelezo kwa lugha rahisi |
| --- | --- |
| Uimara | Leja inanakiliwa kwenye nodi nyingi. Ufikiaji unategemea kuhifadhi funguo halali. |
| Uwezo wa kubebeka | Muamala uliotiwa saini unaweza kuhamisha thamani kupitia mtandao wa mawasiliano. |
| Uwezo wa kugawanyika | Bitcoin moja (1 BTC) inagawanyika kuwa sats 100,000,000. |
| Ubadilishanaji sawa | Kiasi sawa kinaweza kutumika chini ya kanuni zilezile za itifaki, ingawa historia ya umma ya miamala inaweza kuathiri jinsi baadhi ya huduma zinavyoshughulikia fedha fulani. |
| Uhaba | Utoaji hufuata kanuni za umma zenye kiwango cha juu cha milioni 21. |
| Uwezo wa kuthibitishwa | Nodi inaweza kuthibitisha ugavi, kanuni za miamala, na uthibitisho wa umiliki. |
| Kukubalika | Kukubalika kunaongezeka katika baadhi ya maeneo, lakini bado ni kwa kiwango cha chini sana ikilinganishwa na sarafu za kitaifa katika jamii nyingi. |


Bitcoin inaweza kutekeleza kazi tatu za pesa, lakini si kwa usawa katika kila mahali:

* Kama [**hifadhi ya thamani**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#store-of-value), ugavi wake unatabirika, lakini [bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) yake sokoni hubadilika-badilika sana.
* Kama [**njia ya kubadilishana**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#medium-of-exchange), inaweza kuhamishwa bila benki, lakini kukubalika, ada, na zana za malipo hutofautiana.
* Kama [**kipimo cha thamani**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#unit-of-account), kiasi kinaweza kuelezwa kwa bitcoin au [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), lakini bidhaa nyingi bado zinapangiwa bei kwa [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) ya fiat.

Kwa hivyo, Bitcoin inaleta faida na pia [hasara zinazoambatana nazo](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off).

**Faida zinazowezekana** ni pamoja na umiliki wa moja kwa moja, ugavi unaotabirika, uhamishaji wa kimataifa, ushiriki ulio wazi kwa wote, kanuni zilizo wazi, na ukinzani dhidi ya udhibiti wa upande mmoja.

[**Hasara**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) ni pamoja na [bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) [yenye mabadiliko](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#volatility), jukumu la usalama binafsi, ukweli kwamba malipo halali kwa kawaida ni magumu au haiwezekani kurejeshwa, [ada za muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction-fee) zinazobadilika, utegemezi wa umeme na mawasiliano kwa uhamishaji wa kidijitali, rekodi za [muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) zilizo wazi kwa umma, na kukubalika kwa kiwango kidogo.

Iwapo mtaiita Bitcoin [pesa imara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#sound-money) kunapaswa kutokana na vigezo vyenu. Ilinganisheni na [pesa ya fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money), madini ya thamani, na [mali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) nyinginezo kwa kutumia viwango vilevile.

**Ukaguzi wa thamani ya ndani:** Je, bitcoin ina [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) katika jamii yenu? Tenganisheni maswali matatu katika jibu lenu: Ni [bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) gani ya soko inayoweza kuonekana? Inakubaliwa wapi, ikiwa inakubaliwa popote? Ni matakwa binafsi au [upendeleo](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bias) gani unaoathiri tathmini yenu? [Bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price), manufaa ya ndani, na idhini binafsi vinahusiana lakini si sawa.
