# 2.11 Ugavi wenye kikomo

Sheria za Bitcoin zinaweka [ratiba ya utoaji inayotabirika](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuance-schedule) na [kiwango cha juu cha ugavi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#maximum-supply) cha bitcoin milioni 21 (21,000,000 BTC). Bitcoin mpya huingia katika mzunguko kupitia [ruzuku ya bloku](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#block-subsidy) inayolipwa kwa [wachimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) wanaozalisha bloku halali.


<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Halving (kupunguzwa kwa nusu)**
>
> Kila baada ya bloku 210,000, ruzuku hupunguzwa kwa nusu. Tukio hili huitwa [halving](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#halving). Kwa kuwa bloku huzalishwa takriban kila baada ya dakika kumi kwa wastani, [halving](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#halving) hutokea takriban kila baada ya miaka minne. Tarehe kamili ya kalenda haijapangwa.



| Mwaka wa halving | Urefu wa bloku | Ruzuku baada ya halving |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2012 | 210,000 | 25 BTC |
| 2016 | 420,000 | 12.5 BTC |
| 2020 | 630,000 | 6.25 BTC |
| 2024 | 840,000 | 3.125 BTC |
| Inatarajiwa 2028 | 1,050,000 | 1.5625 BTC |


Ruzuku ilianza kwa 50 BTC kwa kila bloku. Hupungua kwa nusu katika hatua zinazojirudia hadi inapokuwa ndogo mno kuendelea. [Wachimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) pia hupokea [ada za muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction-fee). Kadiri muda unavyopita, ada zinatarajiwa kuwa sehemu muhimu zaidi ya mapato ya [wachimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner).

[Ruzuku ya bloku huanza kwa bitcoin 50 na hupunguzwa nusu kila baada ya bloku 210,000, huku jumla ya bitcoin zilizotolewa ikiongezeka kuelekea kikomo cha milioni 21. Ada za miamala hulipwa juu ya hizo na si bitcoin mpya; hakuna utabiri wa bei unaodokezwa.](/micrographics/bitcoin-issuance-schedule-01/sw.md)


Kikomo cha milioni 21 kinasimamiwa na [nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node). Ikiwa [mchimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) ataunda bitcoin zaidi ya zinazoruhusiwa na sheria, [nodi zinazothibitisha](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#full-node) huikataa bloku hiyo. Msanidi programu anaweza kuandika msimbo tofauti, lakini hawezi kuwalazimisha watu wengine kuuendesha.


<!-- card: dark -->
> **Ugavi usiobadilika ni kanuni ya mtandao, si ahadi kuhusu bei**
>
> Kanuni ya ugavi wa Bitcoin iko wazi kwa umma, inaweza kuthibitishwa kwa kujitegemea, na ni vigumu kwa upande mmoja kuibadilisha kwa sababu [nodi zinazothibitisha](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#full-node) huitekeleza. Si sheria ya kimaumbile: inaendelea kufanya kazi maadamu washiriki wanaendelea kuitekeleza. Ugavi wenye kikomo hauhakikishi kwamba soko litashuhudia kupanda kwa [bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price); [bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) pia hutegemea mahitaji.
>
> Nyingi kati ya [mali za kidijitali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-asset) zinaweza kudai kuwa na ugavi wenye kikomo. Jiulizeni kama kikomo hicho kinaaminika kiutendaji, si tu kama kimeandikwa kwenye hati au kuonyeshwa kwenye tovuti.


**Swali la papo hapo:** Je, kuna faida gani kwa Bitcoin kuwa na ugavi wenye kikomo? Je, kuongeza ugavi wa Bitcoin kungekuwa na athari gani kwa [nguvu ya ununuzi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power)? Nani angekataa ongezeko la ugavi?


<!-- card: light -->
> Ugavi wenye kikomo pia haumaanishi kwamba kila bitcoin inapatikana kwa ajili ya kuuzwa; baadhi zinaweza kushikiliwa kwa muda mrefu au kutofikika kwa sababu funguo zilipotea.
