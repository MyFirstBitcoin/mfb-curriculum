# 2.9 Watumiaji, nodi na wachimbaji

Bitcoin hufanya kazi kwa sababu washiriki tofauti hutekeleza majukumu tofauti.

* **Watumiaji** hupokea, huhifadhi, na kutuma bitcoin. Mtumiaji anaweza kudhibiti funguo zake mwenyewe zilizohifadhiwa kwenye pochi au kutumia [mtunzaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custodian). Watumiaji huupa mtandao maana ya kiuchumi kwa kuchagua iwapo watakubali bitcoin na ni sheria au huduma zipi wanazoziamini.
* [**Nodi**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) huendesha programu ya Bitcoin na huthibitisha sheria kwa kujitegemea. [Nodi kamili](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#full-node) hukagua kwamba [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) ina sahihi halali, kwamba bitcoin hazijatumiwa tayari, kwamba bloku zinatimiza [Proof-of-Work](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work), na kwamba bitcoin mpya zinafuata [ratiba ya utoaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuance-schedule). [Nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) hushiriki data halali na wenzao na hukataa data batili.
* [**Wachimbaji**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) hukusanya [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) inayosubiri, hujenga [bloku tarajiwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#candidate-block), na hushindana kupitia [Proof-of-Work](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work). [Mchimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) aliyefanikiwa [hutangaza](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#broadcast) bloku na anaweza kupokea [ruzuku ya bloku](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#block-subsidy) inayoruhusiwa na [ada za miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction-fee). [Wachimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) husaidia kupanga [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) na hufanya [leja](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger) kuwa ghali kuandikwa upya.
* **Wasanidi programu** hukagua, huandika, na kupendekeza mabadiliko ya programu. Wao hawamiliki sheria za Bitcoin. Mabadiliko ya programu huwa na maana tu iwapo washiriki watachagua kuendesha programu inayofuata sheria hizo.

Majukumu haya hudhibitiana:

* mtumiaji hawezi kutumia bitcoin bila sahihi halali inayohitajika
* kila [mchimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) anaweza kupendekeza bloku, lakini [nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) ndizo huamua iwapo inafuata sheria
* kila [nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) huthibitisha kwa kutumia sheria zake yenyewe, lakini [maafikiano](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#consensus) yanahitaji sheria zinazoendana katika mtandao wote
* wasanidi programu wanaweza kupendekeza programu, lakini washiriki huchagua kama wataiendesha au la

Baadhi ya watu hutekeleza majukumu kadhaa. [Mchimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) anaweza kuendesha [nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) na pia kuwa mtumiaji. Mwanafunzi anayeanza hahitaji kutekeleza kila jukumu ili kueleza jinsi majukumu hayo yanavyosawazishana.

**Swali la papo hapo:** Kwa nini [nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) huthibitisha [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction)? Taja angalau sheria moja ambayo [nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) huikagua.
