# 2.17 Uwezeshaji, hatari na uwajibikaji

Sheria za umma za Bitcoin, [mtandao wa Rika-kwa-Rika (P2P)](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#peer-to-peer-network), na [sahihi za kidijitali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-signature) huwapa washiriki majukumu yenye maana. Watumiaji wanaweza kuchagua **kupokea**, **kuhifadhi**, na **kutuma** bitcoin; [nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) zinaweza kuthibitisha sheria kwa kujitegemea; [wachimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) wanaweza kupendekeza bloku; na wasanidi programu wanaweza kupendekeza mabadiliko ya programu. Hakuna jukumu moja linalodhibiti mtandao kwa niaba ya kila mtu.



Sifa hizi zinaweza kuwawezesha washiriki, lakini pia zinawatwika jukumu kubwa zaidi. Bila mamlaka kuu ya kuidhinisha [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction), kurejesha ufikiaji, au kubatilisha makosa mengi, kila mtu lazima aweze kutambua jukumu na hatari anazokubali.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/046836e98fde3a839b4969cfce7f63bfaf59b23f-1536x1024.svg)

Kwa mtu anayechagua [kujihifadhia mwenyewe](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#self-custody), udhibiti wa funguo huleta udhibiti wa moja kwa moja wa bitcoin. Hakuna kampuni inayoweza kuzuia salio lililo kwenye mnyororo linalodhibitiwa ipasavyo. Wala hakuna kampuni inayoweza kurejesha [maneno ya mbegu (seed phrase)](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) yaliyopotea.

[Kujihifadhia mwenyewe](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#self-custody) kwa hiyo si kipengele cha kiufundi tu. Kunahitaji kulinda nakala za akiba, kuthibitisha anwani, kukagua tabia ya pochi, na kuandaa mpango wa urejeshaji. Moduli inayofuata inaeleza jinsi ya kuhifadhi na kutumia bitcoin kwa uangalifu, ikiwa ni pamoja na jinsi ya kudhibiti hatari na majukumu haya.


<!-- card: light -->
> Uwajibikaji binafsi haumaanishi kutenda peke yake au kukataa msaada. Mwanafunzi mwenye tahadhari anaweza kutumia elimu, mapitio ya wenzake, taratibu zilizoandikwa, na majaribio madogo bila kumpa mtu yeyote ufikiaji wa taarifa za siri.
