> **Definition – Proof-of-Work**
>
> [Proof-of-Work](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work) ni mchakato ambao [wachimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) hutumia kushindana kupata haki ya kupendekeza bloku inayofuata.


Kila [Kazi ya Hashi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#hash-function) hubadilisha data kuwa tokeo lenye urefu usiobadilika. Mabadiliko madogo kwenye ingizo huzalisha tokeo jipya lisilotabirika. [Mchimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) hubadilisha kipande kidogo cha data katika bloku inayopendekezwa na kuihashi mara kwa mara. Tokeo ni halali tu pale namba yake inapokuwa chini ya [lengo](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#target) la Mtandao, kizingiti kilichowekwa na kanuni; [lengo](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#target) la chini zaidi humaanisha [matokeo halali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#valid-result) machache zaidi na majaribio mengi zaidi. Hakuna njia ya mkato, hivyo [wachimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) wanaweza kuhitaji kufanya majaribio mengi.

Wakati [mchimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) anapopata [tokeo halali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#valid-result), [hutangaza](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#broadcast) bloku hiyo. [Nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) zinaweza kukagua ushahidi huo haraka, ingawa kuupata kulihitaji hesabu na nishati nyingi. Ikiwa bloku inajumuisha [muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction), [muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) huo hupata [uthibitisho wake wa kwanza](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#first-confirmation): rekodi yake imejumuishwa katika bloku moja halali. Kila bloku halali inayoongezwa baada yake huongeza [uthibitisho](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#confirmation) mwingine, hivyo kufanya nafasi ya [muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) katika [Blockchain](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#blockchain) kuwa ghali zaidi kuandikwa upya. Tofauti hii ni muhimu: kazi ni ghali kuzalisha na rahisi kuthibitisha.

[Proof-of-Work](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work) ina malengo kadhaa:

* inawapa [wachimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) huru njia ya kushindana bila mratibu mkuu
* inafanya mpangilio wa [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) kuwa ghali kuandikwa upya
* inaunganisha usalama wa Mtandao na vifaa na nishati halisi
* inasaidia njia ya wazi ya kuchagua kati ya historia halali zinazoshindana: mnyororo wenye kiasi kikubwa zaidi cha [kazi iliyolimbikizwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#accumulated-work)

[Uchimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work) hautatui milinganyo yenye manufaa kwa lengo la nje. Lengo lake ni kulinda mpangilio wa [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) ya Bitcoin. Mtandao hurekebisha lengo mara kwa mara: vitalu vinapofika haraka mno hushusha lengo, na vinapofika polepole mno hulipandisha, kwa nia ya kupata takriban kitalu kimoja kila dakika kumi kwa wastani kadiri nguvu ya kompyuta inavyobadilika.

[Proof-of-Work](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work) inahusisha gharama halisi ya nishati. Wafuasi wanadai kwamba gharama hii ndiyo inayoimarisha usalama wa Bitcoin na inaweza kuzalisha mahitaji ya nishati inayonyumbulika au ambayo vinginevyo isingetumika. Wakosoaji wanahoji kiasi na chanzo cha nishati inayotumika. Tathmini ya kuwajibika huuliza ni nishati gani inayotumika, inatoka wapi, inatoa usalama gani, na ni njia mbadala zipi zinazolinganishwa.
