# 2.14 Bitcoin, stablecoins na mali nyingine za kripto

[Mali za kidijitali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-asset) zinaweza kuonekana sawa kwenye skrini huku zikitegemea mifumo tofauti kabisa. Bitcoin ina sheria za umma kuhusu ugavi na haina [mtoaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) anayeweza kuunda bitcoin mpya nje ya sheria hizo. Kwa kawaida stablecoin hutegemea [mtoaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer), akiba, madai ya kisheria, au utaratibu unaokusudiwa kufuatilia [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) nyingine. Mali nyingine za kripto zinaweza kuwa na watu au mashirika tofauti yanayodhibiti utoaji, yanayobadilisha sheria, au yanayoendesha sehemu muhimu za mfumo.

Mnapolinganisha [mali yoyote ya kidijitali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-asset), jiulizeni: Nani anadhibiti utoaji au mabadiliko? Ninaweza kuthibitisha nini kwa kujitegemea? Nani anashikilia funguo? Ni kampuni gani, [mtoaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) gani, au mhusika mwingine yupi ninayepaswa kumwamini? Diploma hii inayohusu Bitcoin pekee inatumia ulinganisho huu ili kufafanua Bitcoin tu. Haipendekezi kununua, kufanya biashara, au kutangaza [mali yoyote](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset).

Ulinganisho ulio hapa chini unatofautisha makundi na machaguo ya muundo. Si uidhinishaji, pendekezo, wala dai kwamba [mali yoyote](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) ni salama, inaaminika, au inawafaa ninyi.


> **Dark**
>
> Ulaghai, madai ya kupotosha, na huduma zisizo salama zinaweza kuathiri [mali yoyote ya kidijitali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-asset) au njia yoyote ya malipo. Kagueni [mali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) mahususi, huduma, na mpangilio wa [uhifadhi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) husika badala ya kudhani kwamba kundi fulani lenyewe ni salama au si salama.


**Bitcoin (BTC)** ni [mali ya kidijitali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-asset) iliyo kwenye [Mtandao wa Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-network). [Ugavi wake wa juu kabisa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#maximum-supply) na [ratiba yake ya utoaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuance-schedule) ni sheria za umma. Watu wanaweza kumiliki bitcoin moja kwa moja kupitia funguo na wanaweza kuthibitisha sheria za Bitcoin kwa kujitegemea kwa kuendesha programu inayofaa. Inaweza kutumwa popote duniani bila kuhitaji [mtoaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) mkuu wa Bitcoin kuidhinisha kila [muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction). [Bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) yake ya soko inaweza kubadilika ghafla na kwa kiasi kikubwa, na kupoteza funguo au taarifa za urejeshaji kunaweza kumaanisha hasara ya kudumu; malipo yaliyofanywa kimakosa kwa kawaida ni magumu au haiwezekani kuyarudisha. Ada, muda wa [uthibitisho](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#confirmation), umeme, mawasiliano, rekodi za umma za [muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction), na kukubalika kwake katika eneo husika pia vinaweza kuathiri matumizi. Mtoa huduma wa pochi au soko la kubadilishana linaweza kuongeza hatari zake zinazohusiana na mhusika mwingine na [uhifadhi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) pia.

Baadhi ya miradi hutumia msimbo wa Bitcoin kama mahali pa kuanzia lakini hufanya kazi chini ya sheria au mitandao tofauti. Miradi hii mara nyingi huitwa forks. Historia ya pamoja ya msimbo haifanyi fork kuwa sehemu ya Bitcoin wala haiipi sheria, washiriki, au usalama wa Bitcoin.

**Stablecoins**, kama vile USD Coin (USDC) na Tether (USDT), ni tokeni za kidijitali zilizoundwa kufuatilia [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) ya marejeo, mara nyingi kipimo kimoja cha [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) ya kitaifa kama vile dola ya Marekani. Baadhi zinaweza kuwa na manufaa katika kuhamisha [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) ya tokeni kati ya huduma au mitandao inayooana. Muundo wake, akiba, haki za ukombozi, na udhibiti hutofautiana kwa kiasi kikubwa. Stablecoin inaweza kupoteza [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) iliyokusudiwa (“depeg”), na watumiaji wanaweza kutegemea [mtoaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer), [mhifadhi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custodian) wa akiba, benki, mchakato wa kisheria wa ukombozi, mwendeshaji wa [blockchain](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#blockchain), au daraja (bridge). [Watoaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) au majukwaa yanaweza kuzuia tokeni au kuwekea mipaka ufikiaji pale ambapo kanuni zao au sheria zinaruhusu. Ubora wa akiba, uwazi, ufilisi, udhibiti wa kisheria, makosa ya mikataba-erevu (smart contracts), ulaghai, na ada za mtandao ni hatari za ziada.

**Mali nyingine za kripto** ni kundi pana la [mali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) zinazotumiwa na mitandao au programu nyingine. Kanuni zake zinaweza kuwezesha kazi kama vile kulipa ada za mtandao, kuweka dhamana (staking), utawala, au matumizi ya programu. Utoaji, udhibiti, muundo wa kiufundi, na kile kinachoweza kuthibitishwa kwa kujitegemea hutofautiana kutoka [mali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) moja hadi nyingine. [Bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) na ukwasi vinaweza kubadilika haraka, na wasanidi, taasisi, wathibitishaji, wasimamizi, au wamiliki wakubwa wanaweza kuwa na ushawishi mkubwa, kutegemea mfumo. Mabadiliko ya kanuni, utoaji wa tokeni, hitilafu za mikataba-erevu, udukuzi, kushindwa kwa madaraja (bridges), ulaghai, kuanguka kwa majukwaa, na hali ya kisheria isiyo wazi vinaweza kusababisha hatari za hasara au za kupoteza ufikiaji. Kumiliki tokeni hakumpi mtumiaji moja kwa moja umiliki wa kampuni, mapato yaliyohakikishwa, au haki ya kuikomboa kwa [mali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) nyingine.


| Aina ya mali | Muundo mkuu | Tofauti kuu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bitcoin | Kanuni za umma za ugavi na hakuna mtoaji mkuu. | Kiwango chake cha juu cha milioni 21 kinatekelezwa na kanuni zinazothibitishwa kwa kujitegemea, si kwa akiba wala ahadi za ukombozi. |
| Stablecoins | Tokeni iliyoundwa kufuatilia thamani ya marejeo. | Thamani iliyokusudiwa inategemea kanuni zake mahususi, akiba, mchakato wa ukombozi, na wahusika wengine; mambo haya hutofautiana kati ya stablecoin moja na nyingine. |
| Mali nyingine za kripto | Mali za kidijitali zinazotumiwa kwenye mitandao au programu. | Kanuni, utoaji, na udhibiti hutofautiana kulingana na mali. |


Kabla ya kutumia [mali ya kidijitali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-asset) yoyote, tofautisheni [mali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) yenyewe na huduma inayowahifadhia. Alama ya biashara inayojulikana (ticker), [bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) inayoonekana thabiti, au programu iliyoundwa vizuri haiondoi haja ya kutambua na kulinganisha hatari za [uhifadhi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), za mhusika mwingine, za kiufundi, za kisheria, na za hasara.
