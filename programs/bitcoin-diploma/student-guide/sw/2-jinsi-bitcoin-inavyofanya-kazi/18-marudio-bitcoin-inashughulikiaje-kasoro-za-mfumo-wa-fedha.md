# 2.18 Marudio: Bitcoin Inashughulikiaje Kasoro za Mfumo wa Fedha?

Tafadhali sita kidogo na mtafakari jinsi kanuni za umma, utoaji wenye kikomo, uhamisho wa Rika-kwa-Rika (P2P), na [uthibitishaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#verification) huru vinavyojibu dosari zilizobainishwa katika **Moduli ya 1**: ugavi unaobadilika, udhibiti wa kitaasisi, upatikanaji uliozuiliwa, na kutegemea uaminifu. Kwa nini [nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) na [wachimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) wana majukumu tofauti, na kwa nini [matumizi maradufu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#double-spending) lazima yazuiwe? Bitcoin inabadilisha sehemu ya uaminifu kwa taasisi na kuweka [uthibitishaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#verification) na uwajibikaji binafsi; haiondoi hatari wala [mabadilishano ya faida na hasara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off).

**Wazo kuu:** Kwa kuzingatia mliyojifunza kuhusu Bitcoin katika moduli hii, je, ina [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) kwenu? Je, [bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) ya sasa ya bitcoin inawakilisha [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) yake?

#### Mambo muhimu ya kukumbuka

* Bitcoin iliundwa ili kuwezesha uhamisho wa kidijitali bila mtunzaji mkuu wa [leja](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger) huku ikizuia [matumizi maradufu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#double-spending).
* Vipengele vyake — [leja](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#ledger) ya umma, [sahihi za kidijitali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-signature), [mtandao wa Rika-kwa-Rika (P2P)](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#peer-to-peer-network), [Proof-of-Work](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work), na [uthibitishaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#verification) huru — hufanya kazi pamoja kudumisha historia ya pamoja ya [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction).
* Bitcoin imegatuliwa kwa maana kwamba hakuna mshiriki mmoja anayeweza, kwa uamuzi wake peke yake, kubadilisha salio au kanuni zinazomhusu kila mtu.
* Watumiaji huchagua kama watashikilia au kukubali bitcoin; [nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) huthibitisha kanuni; [wachimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) hupendekeza bloku; na wasanidi programu wanaweza kupendekeza, lakini si kulazimisha, mabadiliko ya programu.
* Bitcoin ina ratiba ya usambazaji iliyo wazi kwa umma yenye kiwango cha juu cha BTC milioni 21, na [nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) hukataa vitalu vinavyovunja kanuni hii.
* Bitcoin moja (1 BTC) hugawanyika kuwa vipande 100,000,000 vinavyoitwa [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), hivyo watu wanaweza kutumia vipimo vidogo bila kuhitaji bitcoin nzima.
* Bitcoin inaweza kutathminiwa kama [pesa imara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#sound-money) kwa kutumia sifa za kifedha, huku tukitambua pia [hasara zinazoambatana nayo](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off): [mabadiliko ya bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#volatility), ukubalikaji mdogo, malipo ambayo kwa kawaida ni magumu au hayawezekani kurejeshwa, na hitaji la kuongezeka kwa uwajibikaji binafsi.
