# 2.2 Utafutaji wa pesa ya kidijitali isiyo na udhibiti wa kati

Kukua kwa kompyuta binafsi na intaneti kulileta fursa mpya za mawasiliano. Pia kulileta aina mpya za ufuatiliaji na udhibiti. Mwishoni mwa karne ya ishirini, kikundi cha watengenezaji programu, wanahisabati na wanaharakati waliojulikana kama [**Cypherpunks**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cypherpunk) walichunguza jinsi [cryptography](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cryptography) inavyoweza kulinda [faragha](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy) na uhuru wa mtu binafsi katika mifumo ya kidijitali.


> **Definition – Cryptography**
>
> [Cryptography](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cryptography) hutumia mbinu za kihisabati kulinda taarifa na kuthibitisha kwamba kitendo ni halisi. [Usimbaji fiche](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#encryption) unaweza kuuweka ujumbe kuwa wa faragha. [Sahihi za kidijitali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-signature) zinaweza kuthibitisha kwamba ujumbe au agizo lilitoka kwa mmiliki wa ufunguo wa siri bila kuufichua ufunguo huo.


Kundi la [Cypherpunks](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cypherpunk) lilielewa kwamba mawasiliano ya faragha hayakutosha. Watu pia walihitaji njia ya kubadilishana [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) mtandaoni. Ikiwa kila malipo ya kidijitali yangehitaji benki au kampuni, [mpatanishi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#intermediary) huyo angeweza kuuchunguza, kuuruhusu au kuuzuia ubadilishanaji huo.

Lengo lao halikuwa tu pesa za kidijitali. [Amana za benki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bank-deposit) tayari zilikuwa za kidijitali. Lengo gumu zaidi lilikuwa **pesa za kidijitali zilizogatuliwa**: mfumo usio na [mtoaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) mmoja, seva moja wala msimamizi mmoja ambaye washiriki walilazimika kumwamini.

Bitcoin ilijengwa juu ya kazi ya miongo kadhaa ya watu wengi. Muundo wake uliunganisha mawazo ya awali kwa njia mpya na ukatoa motisha kwa mtandao ulio wazi kuendelea kufanya kazi.
