# 2.3 Majaribio ya Awali ya Pesa za Kidijitali na Tatizo la Kutumia Mara Mbili

Bitcoin haikuwa jaribio la kwanza la pesa za kidijitali. Ilijengwa juu ya miongo minne ya majaribio, ikitumia mawazo kutoka kwa miradi mingi ya awali iliyosaidia kuunda muundo wake uliofanikiwa.

* [**DigiCash**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digicash) ilitumia [cryptography](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cryptography) kuwezesha malipo ya kidijitali ya faragha. Ilitegemea kampuni kuu na mahusiano na benki. Kampuni hiyo ilipoanguka, mfumo haukuweza kuendelea kama mtandao huru wa pesa.
* [**Hashcash**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#hashcash) ilitumia [Proof-of-Work](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work) kufanya barua pepe zisizotakiwa ziwe ghali. Kompyuta ya mtumaji ililazimika kufanya kazi kabla ya kutuma ujumbe. Wazo hili lilionyesha jinsi gharama ya kikokotozi inavyoweza kuzuia matumizi mabaya.
* [**Bit Gold**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bit-gold) ulielezea mfumo uliopendekezwa ambapo kazi ya kikokotozi ingeweza kuunda rekodi adimu za kidijitali. Haukuzinduliwa kama mfumo kamili wa pesa uliogatuliwa, lakini ulikuza mawazo muhimu kuhusu [uadimu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#scarcity) wa kidijitali na uthibitisho.
* [**B-money**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#b-money) na [**Proof-of-Work inayoweza kutumika tena (RPoW)**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#reusable-proof-of-work) zilichunguza njia za washiriki kuunda au kuhamisha [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) adimu ya kidijitali bila kutegemea hifadhidata moja kuu pekee. Miradi hii haikuwa kamili, lakini ilisaidia kufafanua kile ambacho mfumo unaofanya kazi ungehitaji.
* Ingawa [**BitTorrent**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bittorrent) haikuwa pesa, ilionyesha kwamba [mtandao wa Rika-kwa-Rika (P2P)](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#peer-to-peer-network) unaweza kuruhusu kompyuta nyingi huru kushiriki taarifa bila seva moja kuu kudhibiti mfumo mzima.

Kwa pamoja, majaribio haya yalionyesha vipande muhimu vya fumbo. Pia yalifafanua tatizo lililobaki.

> **Definition – Matumizi maradufu**
>
> [Matumizi maradufu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#double-spending) ni jaribio la kutuma kitengo kilekile cha kidijitali kwa wapokeaji wawili. Data ya kidijitali inaweza kunakiliwa kikamilifu, hivyo mfumo wa pesa za kidijitali unahitaji njia ya kuthibitisha ni [muamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) upi ulio halali.


[Mifumo ya kati](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#centralized-system) huzuia hili kwa kuruhusu hifadhidata moja inayoaminika kuamua ni malipo yapi yalitangulia. Tarehe 31 Oktoba 2008, [Satoshi Nakamoto](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi-nakamoto), katika karatasi nyeupe yake, [_Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System_](https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf), alieleza mbinu tofauti: mfumo wa umma unaoweza kuthibitisha historia ya pamoja ya malipo bila benki, kampuni, au mamlaka nyingine kuu.

Karatasi nyeupe iliunganisha vipengele vilivyohitajika kwa mbinu hiyo:

1. **Mtandao wa [Rika-kwa-Rika (P2P)](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#peer-to-peer-network):** [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) hutangazwa moja kwa moja kwa mtandao wa washiriki.
1. **[Sahihi za kidijitali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-signature):** kila [ufunguo wa kibinafsi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) ni taarifa ya siri inayounda sahihi ya kuidhinisha matumizi; ni mmiliki wake pekee anayeweza kufanya hivi.
1. **Vitalu na [uchimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work):** [wachimbaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#miner) hukusanya [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) katika vitalu na hutumia [Proof-of-Work](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work) kushindana kuongeza kitalu kinachofuata.
1. **Uthibitishaji [huru](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#verification):** [nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) hukagua [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) na vitalu dhidi ya kanuni za Bitcoin.
1. **Historia ya pamoja yenye [Proof-of-Work](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work) iliyolimbikizwa:** washiriki hutambua [mnyororo halali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#valid-chain) wa [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) wenye [kazi iliyolimbikizwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#accumulated-work) zaidi kuwa ndio rekodi ya pamoja.

Kwa pamoja, dhana hizi zilitoa jibu la vitendo kwa [tatizo la matumizi mara mbili](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#double-spending): mtandao unaweza kukataa jaribio la pili la kutumia bitcoin ileile bila kutegemea mamlaka kuu. Bitcoin ilitoa mchanganyiko uliokosekana ambao majaribio ya pesa za kidijitali ya miongo kadhaa yalikuwa yakiutafuta.
