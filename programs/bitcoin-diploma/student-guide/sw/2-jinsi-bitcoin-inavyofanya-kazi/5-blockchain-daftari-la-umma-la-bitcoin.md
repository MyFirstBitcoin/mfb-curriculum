# 2.5 Blockchain: Daftari la Umma la Bitcoin

<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Blockchain**
>
> Katika mfumo wa Bitcoin, [blockchain](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#blockchain) ni rekodi ya umma ya [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) inayonakiliwa kwenye kompyuta nyingi zinazojitegemea. Bloku hukusanya kundi la [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) halali kuwa sehemu inayofuata inayopendekezwa ya rekodi hiyo. Kila bloku halali huunganishwa na ile iliyotangulia, hivyo kuupa mtandao historia ya pamoja iliyopangwa kwa mfuatano ili kuithibitisha.



<!-- card: light -->
> **Jinsi nodi zinavyofikia maafikiano**
>
> [Nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) hukagua kwa kujitegemea sheria zilezile na kuafikiana kuhusu historia halali yenye kiwango kikubwa zaidi cha [Proof-of-Work](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work) iliyolimbikizwa. Kubadilisha rekodi ya awali hubadilisha bloku yake na kuvunja viungo vya bloku zinazofuata, hivyo historia shindani lazima ifanye upya kazi hiyo na kufikia kiwango hicho kabla [nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) hazijaikubali. Mchakato huu wa gharama wa kupanga mfuatano husaidia mtandao kufikia [maafikiano](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#consensus) bila mtunza kumbukumbu wa kati. Nakala hazibadiliki kwa wakati mmoja; [nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) hupokea na kuthibitisha data kwa kujitegemea. Bitcoin inalenga bloku moja mpya takriban kila dakika kumi kwa wastani. Historia ni ngumu na ya gharama kubwa kuandikwa upya, lakini si kwamba haiwezekani kabisa kubadilishwa: historia shindani za muda na upangaji upya wa mnyororo zinaweza kutokea.
