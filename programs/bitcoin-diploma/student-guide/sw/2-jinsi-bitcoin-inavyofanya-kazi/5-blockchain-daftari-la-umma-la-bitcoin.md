# 2.5 Blockchain: Daftari la Umma la Bitcoin

> **Definition – Blockchain**
>
> Katika Bitcoin, [blockchain](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#blockchain) ni rekodi ya umma ya [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) inayonakiliwa kwenye kompyuta nyingi zinazojitegemea. Bloku hukusanya kundi la [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) halali na kuifanya kuwa sehemu inayofuata inayopendekezwa ya rekodi hiyo. Kila bloku halali huunganishwa na ile iliyotangulia, na hivyo kuupa mtandao historia ya pamoja iliyopangwa kwa mfuatano ambayo inaweza kuthibitishwa.



> **Definition – Nodi**
>
> [Nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) hukagua kanuni zilezile kila moja kivyake na hukubaliana juu ya historia halali yenye [Proof-of-Work](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work) nyingi zaidi iliyolimbikizwa. Kubadilisha rekodi ya awali hubadilisha bloku yake na kuvunja viungo vyake na bloku zinazofuata, hivyo historia shindani lazima irudie kazi hiyo na kufikia historia iliyopo kabla [nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) hazijaikubali. Mchakato huu wa gharama kubwa wa kupanga mfuatano husaidia mtandao kufikia [maafikiano](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#consensus) bila kuwepo kwa mtunza kumbukumbu mkuu. Nakala hazibadiliki kwa wakati mmoja; [nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) hupokea na kuthibitisha data kila moja kivyake. Bitcoin inalenga kuzalisha bloku moja mpya takriban kila dakika kumi kwa wastani. Historia hii ni ngumu na ina gharama kubwa kuandikwa upya, lakini haimaanishi kwamba haiwezekani kabisa kubadilishwa: historia shindani za muda na upangaji upya zinaweza kutokea.
