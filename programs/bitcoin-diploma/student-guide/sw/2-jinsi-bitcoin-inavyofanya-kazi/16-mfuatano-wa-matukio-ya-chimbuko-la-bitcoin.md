# 2.16 Mfuatano wa matukio ya chimbuko la Bitcoin

Bitcoin ilitokea kupitia mfululizo mfupi wa matukio ya hadharani.


<!-- micrographic: bitcoin-origin-timeline-01 -->


Chunguza rekodi za vyanzo asili: soma [Karatasi nyeupe ya Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-whitepaper) ([PDF ya chanzo](https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf)), kagua [Genesis Block](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#genesis-block) ([rekodi ya bloku](https://mempool.space/block/000000000019d6689c085ae165831e934ff763ae46a2a6c172b3f1b60a8ce26f)) na rekodi ya kwanza ya [muamala wa mtu kwa mtu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#person-to-person-transaction) ([rekodi ya muamala](https://mempool.space/tx/f4184fc596403b9d638783cf57adfe4c75c605f6356fbc91338530e9831e9e16)), na usome [ombi la awali la piza](https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=137.0). [Block Explorer](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#block-explorer) ni kiolesura rahisi cha kufikia data ya umma, si mamlaka kuu juu ya sheria za Bitcoin.

[**Satoshi Nakamoto**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi-nakamoto) ni jina bandia lililotumiwa na muundaji au waundaji wasiojulikana wa Bitcoin. [Satoshi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi-nakamoto) aliwasiliana mtandaoni, akatoa programu kama chanzo huria, na baadaye akaacha kushiriki.

Hakuna kampuni kuu inayomiliki Bitcoin. Programu na sheria ziliendelea kwa sababu watu wengine walichagua kuziendesha, kuzikagua na kuziendeleza. Asili hii ni muhimu: muundaji hakubaki kama kiongozi wa kudumu ambaye angeweza kutoa sarafu zaidi au kuidhinisha [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction).

Tukio la [Genesis Block](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#genesis-block) na msukosuko wa kifedha wa 2008 vinaipa Bitcoin muktadha muhimu wa kihistoria. Hata hivyo, tathmini Bitcoin kwa muundo na uendeshaji wake, si kwa hadithi kuhusu muundaji wake pekee.

Mnamo Oktoba 2008, [Satoshi Nakamoto](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi-nakamoto) alitangaza [Karatasi nyeupe ya Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-whitepaper). Swali lake kuu lilikuwa rahisi: **watu wanawezaje kufanya malipo mtandaoni bila kuruhusu kipimo kilekile cha kidijitali kutumiwa mara mbili au kumpa [mpatanishi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#intermediary) mmoja udhibiti?**
