# 2.4 Uhaba wa kidijitali na ugavi unaoweza kuthibitishwa kwa kujitegemea

Kwa kawaida, taarifa za kidijitali ni rahisi kunakiliwa.


<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Uhaba wa kidijitali**
>
> Katika ulimwengu wa kidijitali, [uhaba](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#scarcity) unamaanisha kwamba mfumo unaweza kutofautisha kipimo halali na nakala batili, na kuzuia kipimo kilekile kisitumike mara mbili.


Bitcoin huunda [uhaba](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#scarcity) wa kidijitali kwa kuchanganya saini, historia ya [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) iliyo wazi kwa umma, [Proof-of-Work](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#proof-of-work), na [uthibitishaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#verification) huru wa sheria. bitcoin moja inaweza kuhamishwa, lakini mtandao hukataa jaribio lolote la kutumia bitcoin hiyohiyo tena.

[Uhaba](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#scarcity) unaweza kuthibitishwa kwa kujitegemea pale watu wanapoweza kukagua sheria za umma wenyewe na hakuna [mtoaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) mmoja anayeweza kubadilisha ugavi kimyakimya kwa kila mtu. Hii ndiyo sababu kikomo cha ugavi wa Bitcoin lazima kichunguzwe pamoja na [ugatuaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#decentralization) na jinsi [nodi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#node) zinavyofanya [uthibitishaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#verification).
