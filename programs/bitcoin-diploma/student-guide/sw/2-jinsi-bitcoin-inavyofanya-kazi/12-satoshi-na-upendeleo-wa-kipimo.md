# 2.12 Satoshi na upendeleo wa kipimo

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/98c8cef9bc6e6d0955458f6fb4a71f1de5ed5225-1536x1024.svg)



Bitcoin moja (1 BTC) inaweza kugawanywa katika vipande **100,000,000 [vya satoshi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi)**. [Satoshi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), ambayo mara nyingi hufupishwa kuwa [**sat**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), ndicho kipimo kidogo zaidi kinachorekodiwa kwenye safu ya msingi ya Bitcoin.

**Hamhitaji kununua au kutumia bitcoin nzima.** Programu za Bitcoin zinaweza kuonyesha kiasi katika bitcoin au [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), kama vile [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) inavyoweza kutumia kipimo kikuu na vipimo vidogo. Watu wengi hutumia [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi) kwa kiasi cha kila siku kwa sababu namba kamili ni rahisi kusoma. Maombi ya malipo ya kwenye mnyororo (on-chain) na ya Lightning yanaweza kuonyeshwa kwa [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi).

**Upendeleo [wa kipimo (unit bias)](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bias)** ni hisia potofu kwamba [bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) ya chini kwa kila kipimo kizima hufanya [mali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) kuwa nafuu zaidi au rahisi zaidi kufikiwa. Idadi ya vipimo ni uamuzi wa muundo. Kumiliki 100,000 [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi) na kumiliki 0.001 BTC kunaelezea kiasi kilekile.

Mnapolinganisha [bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price), zingatieni sehemu ya jumla ya usambazaji, kiasi cha [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) kinachohusika, na hatari. Msidhani kwamba [mali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) yenye vipimo vingi vinavyoonekana kuwa vya bei nafuu inatoa [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) bora zaidi kuliko [mali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) iliyogawanywa katika vipimo vidogo zaidi.
