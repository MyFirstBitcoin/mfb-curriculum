# 1.6 Historia ya pesa kwa wanaoanza: kutoka kubadilishana bidhaa hadi pesa za kidijitali

Pesa hazikukua kwa mfuatano mmoja unaofanana kila mahali. Jamii tofauti zilitumia bidhaa, kumbukumbu na taasisi tofauti. Hata hivyo, mwelekeo mpana husaidia kueleza pesa za kisasa.

> “Pesa … kwa vyovyote si matokeo ya makubaliano … bali ni zao lisilokusudiwa la maendeleo ya kihistoria.”  
_Carl Menger, “Kuhusu Asili ya Pesa” (1892)_

* **Ubadilishanaji wa moja kwa moja na kumbukumbu za mahali husika:** Watu walitumia [biashara ya kubadilishana bidhaa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#barter), zawadi, wajibu, na [madeni](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#debt) yaliyoandikwa au yaliyokumbukwa. Mifumo hii ilitegemea sana mahusiano ya mahali husika.
* **[Pesa za bidhaa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#commodity-money):** Jamii zilichagua bidhaa zilizothaminiwa na wengi na zilizo rahisi zaidi kubadilishana. Chumvi, shanga, makombe, mifugo, shaba, fedha, na [dhahabu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold) zimetumika kama pesa katika maeneo tofauti. Bidhaa ina matumizi au [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) inayozidi ahadi ya [mtoaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer).
* **[Sarafu sanifu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#standard-coins):** Mamlaka zilipima madini na kuyagonga mihuri katika vipimo sanifu. Sarafu ziliboresha utambuzi na uhasibu. Mamlaka pia zingeweza kupunguza kiasi cha madini ya thamani katika sarafu hizi huku zikidumisha [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) ileile iliyotajwa. Hili linaitwa [udhalilishaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#debasement).
* **[Karatasi wakilishi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#representative-paper):** Kusafirisha na kulinda madini kulikuwa kugumu. Hifadhi na benki zilitoa [hati za madai za karatasi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#paper-claim) ambazo zingeweza kukombolewa kwa kiasi kilichotajwa cha madini. Karatasi hizo zilikuwa rahisi zaidi kusafirisha, lakini watumiaji walilazimika kumwamini [mtoaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) kuhifadhi madini ya kutosha na kuheshimu ukombozi. Chini ya matoleo tofauti ya [**kiwango cha dhahabu**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold-standard), kipimo cha [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) kiliunganishwa kisheria na kiasi kilichotajwa cha [dhahabu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold). Kanuni zilitofautiana kulingana na mahali na kipindi, na wakati mwingine serikali au benki zilisitisha au kukomesha [ubadilishaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#convertibility).
* **Pesa za benki na za serikali:** Benki zilitoa madai na mikopo zaidi. Serikali zilianzisha [benki kuu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank) na mifumo ya [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) ya kitaifa. Pesa ilizidi kutegemea kumbukumbu na ahadi za taasisi badala ya wananchi kumiliki bidhaa moja kwa moja. Katika nchi nyingi, [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) ya kitaifa baadaye haikuweza tena kubadilishwa kwa [mali](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) halisi kama vile [dhahabu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold). Kutenganishwa huku na dhamana ya bidhaa ni sifa bainifu ya [**pesa ya fiat**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money) ya leo (tazama hapa chini).
* **Pesa za kidijitali:** Sehemu kubwa ya pesa za leo husafiri kama kumbukumbu za kidijitali kupitia benki na huduma za malipo. Kadi au simu hutuma maagizo; haina pesa yenyewe. Kwa kawaida, maagizo hayo hubadilisha [amana ya benki](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bank-deposit), yaani wajibu wa benki uliorekodiwa—au dai—wa kumlipa mwenye akaunti kiasi fulani cha [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) ya kitaifa.

Historia hii inaonyesha [ubadilishanaji wa faida na hasara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) unaojirudia. Urahisi uliongezeka, lakini mara nyingi watumiaji walizidi kutegemea sera ya fedha, [watoaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer), benki, na [wapatanishi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#intermediary) wa malipo.
