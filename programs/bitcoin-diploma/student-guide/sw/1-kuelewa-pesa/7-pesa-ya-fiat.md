# 1.7 Pesa ya Fiat

<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Pesa ya fiat**
>
> [Pesa ya fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money) ni [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) inayotolewa chini ya mamlaka ya serikali, kwa kawaida kupitia [benki kuu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank), ambayo haiwezi kubadilishwa kwa kiasi maalum cha bidhaa kama vile [dhahabu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold). Serikali huunga mkono—na katika baadhi ya mazingira hulazimisha—matumizi yake kwa kuitaka itumike kulipa kodi na kwa kuweka sheria za fedha halali. Sheria kamili za kukubali malipo na kulipa [madeni](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#debt) hutofautiana kulingana na eneo la mamlaka.


[Pesa ya fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money) hufanya kazi kwa sababu watu, biashara, benki, na serikali huikubali. Pesa zote zinaweza kujenga [athari za mtandao](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#network-effect): watu huitumia kwa sababu watu wengine huitumia na [bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) hutajwa kwayo. Kwa [pesa ya fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money), wajibu wa kodi, sheria za fedha halali, na mifumo ya malipo ya kitaasisi vinaweza kuimarisha mtandao huo.

> **Chunguzeni malipo ya eneo lenu**
>
> Chagueni malipo moja ya kawaida katika jamii yenu, kama vile kulipia usafiri, chakula, au salio la simu; kupokea mshahara; au kulipa bili. Mnaweza kutumia hali ya kubuni inayowezekana kihalisia mkipenda. Kwa sentensi tatu au nne, elezeni:
>
> 1. ni nini kinacholipiwa na ni [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) gani inayotumika kama [kipimo cha thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#unit-of-account);
> 1. kwa nini muuzaji au mpokeaji yuko tayari kukubali [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) hiyo; na
> 1. ni taasisi, kumbukumbu, au sheria gani ambazo watu hutegemea ili kukamilisha malipo.
>
> Malizieni kwa kutaja njia moja ambayo malipo hayo yangekuwa magumu zaidi iwapo ukubalifu huo wa pamoja au imani hiyo ingevunjika.


Mifumo ya fiat inaweza kutoa malipo rahisi, mikopo inayonyumbulika, na zana za kukabiliana na migogoro ya kifedha. Unyumbufu huo huo huleta **hatari**. Idadi ndogo ya taasisi inaweza kubadilisha ugavi na gharama ya pesa, kuzuia upatikanaji, au kufanya maamuzi yanayopunguza [nguvu ya ununuzi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power).

[Pesa ya fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money) inahitaji tabaka za **imani**. Watumiaji huziamini benki kutunza kumbukumbu sahihi, huduma za malipo kushughulikia uhamisho, [benki kuu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank) kusimamia [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency), na serikali kudumisha taasisi zinazofuata sheria. Mifumo hii inaweza kufanya kazi kwa muda mrefu, lakini sheria zake si za kudumu na zinaweza kukabiliwa na uzembe au matumizi mabaya.
