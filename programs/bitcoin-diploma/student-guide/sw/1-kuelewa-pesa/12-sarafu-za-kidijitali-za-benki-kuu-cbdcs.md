# 1.12 Sarafu za Kidijitali za Benki Kuu (CBDCs)

<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Sarafu ya kidijitali ya benki kuu (CBDC)**
>
> Kimsingi, [sarafu ya kidijitali ya benki kuu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc), au [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc), ni aina ya kidijitali ya [pesa ya fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money) inayotolewa kama [dhima](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#liability) ya [benki kuu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank): ni dai ambalo [benki kuu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank) inadaiwa na mmiliki wake. Ni tofauti na pesa taslimu halisi na amana ya kawaida katika benki ya biashara, ingawa uzoefu wa mtumiaji unaweza kuonekana sawa.


[CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc) hutofautiana kimuundo. Baadhi zimekusudiwa hasa kwa taasisi za fedha; nyingine zinapendekezwa kwa matumizi ya umma. Kwanza ulizeni [mtoaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) ni nani: kwa [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc), [benki kuu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank) inabaki kuwa [mtoaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) na bado inaweza kutekeleza sera ya fedha. Kwa hiyo, [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc) haiundi ugavi maalum wa pesa wala kuondoa udhibiti wa kati; inabadilisha tu teknolojia na usambazaji wa [pesa ya fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money).

Kisha ulizeni muundo huo unaruhusu nini. Kulingana na kanuni, [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc) inaweza kuruhusu ukamilishaji wa haraka zaidi wa malipo, upatikanaji mpana zaidi, au vipengele vipya vya malipo. Inaweza pia kuzipa taasisi uwezo mkubwa zaidi wa kufuatilia [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction), kutekeleza kanuni, kuzuia matumizi, au kuunganisha utambulisho moja kwa moja na malipo. Huu ni udhibiti wa kati: taasisi, badala ya watumiaji wa kawaida kwa kujitegemea, ndiyo huweka au kubadilisha kanuni husika.

Kidijitali haimaanishi moja kwa moja kuwa imegatuliwa, ni adimu, ni ya faragha, au iko wazi kwa uthibitishaji huru. [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc) inaweza kutumia teknolojia ya kidijitali, lakini bado ni [pesa ya fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money) ikiwa [mtoaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer) wake, kanuni za ugavi, kanuni za upatikanaji, na ruhusa za malipo vinaendelea kusimamiwa kwa udhibiti wa kati.

Mnapojadili [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc), ulizeni:

* Nani hutoa pesa hii?
* Nani anaweza kuimiliki au kuitumia?
* Taarifa gani hukusanywa?
* Je, malipo yanaweza kuzuiwa, kuwekewa kikomo, au kubatilishwa?
* Je, kanuni zinaweza kubadilika?
* Je, watumiaji wa kawaida wanaweza kuthibitisha kanuni wenyewe kwa kujitegemea?
* Nini hutokea pasipo umeme au intaneti?
* Kuna kinga gani za kisheria?

Msidhani kwamba kila [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc) inayopendekezwa ina muundo uleule. Tumieni taarifa za sasa, za eneo lenu, na zinazoaminika.
