# 1.13 Marudio: Pesa inawezaje kuharibika?

Tafakarini kwa muda kuhusu pesa mnazotumia: ni nini kinachoifanya iwe na manufaa katika maisha ya kila siku, na ni kanuni zipi zake zinazotegemea taasisi ambazo mnalazimika kuziamini? [Biashara ya kubadilishana bidhaa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#barter) ni ngumu kwa sababu kulinganisha mahitaji hakuna ufanisi. [Pesa ya fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money) hurahisisha ubadilishanaji, lakini ugavi wake unaobadilika, udhibiti wa taasisi, ufikiaji uliowekewa vikwazo, na kushuka kwa [nguvu ya ununuzi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power) vinaweza kusababisha kasoro kubwa kwa watu wanaoitegemea. Watu wanaweza kutofautiana kuhusu uzito wa kila kasoro au jinsi ya kuishughulikia. Ndiyo sababu baadhi ya watu hutafuta njia mbadala za kifedha zenye [mabadilishano tofauti ya faida na hasara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) kuhusu ugavi, udhibiti, ufikiaji, na uaminifu.

#### Mambo muhimu ya kukumbuka

* Pesa huwasaidia watu kubadilishana, kulinganisha [bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price), kurekodi [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value), na kuhamisha [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) kupitia wakati.
* Kazi kuu za pesa ni kuwa [chombo cha kubadilishana](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#medium-of-exchange), [kipimo cha hesabu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#unit-of-account), na [hifadhi ya thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#store-of-value).
* Sifa za kifedha kama vile [uimara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#durability), [urahisi wa kubebeka](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#portability), [ugawanyikaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#divisibility), [uhaba](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#scarcity), [kukubalika](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#acceptability), na [uwezo wa kuthibitishwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#verifiability) hutusaidia kulinganisha aina tofauti za pesa.
* [Biashara ya kubadilishana bidhaa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#barter) ni ngumu kwa kiwango kikubwa kwa sababu watu wote wawili lazima wataka kile ambacho mwingine anatoa kwa wakati mmoja na kwa kiasi kinachofaa.
* Pesa imekua kwa njia tofauti katika jamii mbalimbali, mara nyingi ikiongeza urahisi huku ikileta [mabadilishano mapya ya faida na hasara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) katika uaminifu, udhibiti, na ufikiaji.
* [Pesa ya fiat](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money) inaweza kuwezesha malipo rahisi na mikopo inayonyumbulika, lakini ugavi wake na kanuni zake hutegemea taasisi.
* [Mfumuko wa bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#inflation), [mfumo wa benki wa akiba ya sehemu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fractional-reserve-banking), na muundo wa [CBDC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cbdc) vinaweza kuathiri [nguvu ya ununuzi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power), upatikanaji, [faragha](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy), na hitaji la kuamini taasisi.
* Baadhi ya watu hutafuta njia mbadala kama vile madini ya thamani wanapotaka [uwiano tofauti wa faida na hasara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) kuhusu [nguvu ya ununuzi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power), ugavi, au udhibiti; kila njia mbadala ina mipaka na hatari zake.

#### Muda wa kutafakari

Ni swali gani moja ambalo sasa mnauliza kuhusu pesa ambalo hamkuliuliza hapo awali?

Moduli inayofuata inachunguza jaribio la Bitcoin la kuunda mfumo tofauti wa kanuni za fedha.
