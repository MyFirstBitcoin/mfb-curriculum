# 1.10 Kwa nini dola ya Marekani ni sarafu ya akiba ya dunia?

Sarafu **ya akiba [(reserve currency)](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency)** ni [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) ambayo [benki kuu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank) na serikali huishikilia na kuitumia kwa malipo ya kimataifa. Kabla ya Vita vya Kwanza vya Dunia, pauni ya Uingereza ndiyo iliyotekeleza jukumu hili kwa nguvu zaidi. Vita vilidhoofisha [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) na vituo vya fedha vya Ulaya, huku Marekani ikisambaza bidhaa na kulimbikiza [dhahabu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold). Hili liliongeza matumizi ya kimataifa ya dola.

Baada ya Vita vya Pili vya Dunia, Marekani ilikuwa na uchumi mkubwa na sehemu kubwa ya uwezo wake wa kifedha ilibaki salama. Kisha, Mkataba wa [Bretton Woods](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bretton-woods) wa mwaka 1944 ulianzisha mfumo wa baada ya vita ambapo [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) nyingi ziliunganishwa na dola, na dola iliunganishwa rasmi na [dhahabu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold), hivyo kuiweka dola katikati ya malipo ya kimataifa.

Mwaka 1971, Rais Nixon alikomesha ubadilishaji rasmi wa dola kwa [dhahabu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold) uliofanywa na serikali za kigeni na [benki kuu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank). Hili lilikomesha uwezo rasmi wa dola wa kupatiwa [dhahabu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold) [kwa kubadilishwa](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#convertibility) na kuufikisha mwisho mfumo wa [Bretton Woods](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bretton-woods). Hata hivyo, dola iliendelea kutumika kwa wingi kwa sababu ya nafasi yake iliyojengeka katika biashara na fedha, ukubwa wa uchumi wa Marekani, na kina cha masoko ya fedha ya Marekani. Inaendelea kuwa [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) ya akiba inayoongoza leo, ingawa [benki kuu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#central-bank) pia hushikilia [sarafu](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) nyingine na sehemu yake inaweza kubadilika kadiri muda unavyopita.
