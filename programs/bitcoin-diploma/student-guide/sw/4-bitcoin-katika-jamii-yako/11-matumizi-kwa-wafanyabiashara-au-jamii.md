# 4.11 Matumizi kwa wafanyabiashara au jamii

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/9fd6b8ca04a295f860a0ab150d8f05823cbcea16-1536x1024.svg)


<!-- micrographic: merchant-payment-operations-01 -->
[Mfanyabiashara anayekubali Bitcoin hufanya kazi katika maeneo manne: masoko, mafunzo, uhasibu na mauzo.](/micrographics/merchant-payment-operations-01/sw.md)


Mfanyabiashara anahitaji zaidi ya kitufe cha kupokea malipo. Mchakato kamili wa malipo unaweza kujumuisha upangaji wa bei, mafunzo ya wafanyakazi, risiti, urejeshaji wa pesa, [uhifadhi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), ubadilishaji wa fedha, uhasibu, kodi, usalama wa vifaa, na huduma kwa wateja.

Kabla ya kukubali Bitcoin, mfanyabiashara anapaswa kuamua:

* kama [bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) zinaonyeshwa kwa [sarafu ya ndani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency), [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), au zote mbili
* kama malipo yanatumia Bitcoin ya on-chain, Lightning, au zote mbili
* kama pochi inahifadhiwa na mtu mwingine au inajihifadhi yenyewe
* nani anaweza kufikia kifaa cha kupokea malipo
* jinsi fedha zilizopokelewa zinavyolindwa au kuhamishwa
* kama baadhi ya fedha zinabadilishwa kuwa [sarafu ya ndani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency)
* jinsi malipo yanavyothibitishwa mahali pa mauzo
* jinsi urejeshaji wa pesa na makosa yanavyoshughulikiwa
* kumbukumbu zipi zinazotakiwa na sheria za ndani
* wafanyakazi wafanye nini mtandao au pochi inaposhindwa kufanya kazi

Matumizi ya kijamii yanaweza kujumuisha:

* kuwalipa wafanyakazi au wabunifu wa eneo husika
* kupokea michango kwa uwazi
* kuhamisha [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) kati ya vikundi vya jamii
* kusaidia kikundi cha akiba chenye utaratibu ulio wazi wa [uhifadhi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody)
* kumsaidia mfanyakazi wa mbali kupokea malipo ya kimataifa
* kuwaunganisha wazalishaji na wafanyabiashara wa eneo husika
* kufundisha matumizi salama ya pochi
* kutoa msaada wa dharura mahali ambapo mifumo ya kawaida ya malipo haipatikani

[Uhifadhi wa kijamii](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#community-custody) unahitaji uangalifu maalum. Ikiwa mratibu mmoja anashikilia fedha za watu wengi, mratibu huyo anakuwa [mhifadhi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custodian) na kitovu cha uaminifu. [Fedha za pamoja](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#shared-funds) zinahitaji sheria zilizo wazi, kumbukumbu, udhibiti wa ufikiaji, na mapitio ya kisheria inapofaa.

Kwa kila matumizi, mafanikio yanapaswa kupimwa kwa tatizo lililotatuliwa, si kwa idadi ya [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) iliyokamilika.
