# 4.6 Matumizi ya Bitcoin katika Maisha ya Kila Siku

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/b86058296c3087bf7e2280da71b62bd19050e17b-1536x1024.svg)


<!-- micrographic: daily-bitcoin-uses-01 -->


Bitcoin inaweza kufanya kazi kama pesa kwa njia kadhaa. Kutumia ni mojawapo tu ya njia hizo.

**Kupata mapato:** Mfanyakazi, mbunifu, biashara au mwanajamii hupokea bitcoin kwa [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) anayoitoa. Kupata mapato kunaweza kuepusha mchakato tofauti wa ununuzi, lakini mpokeaji lazima akubaliane kuhusu upangaji wa bei, [mabadiliko ya bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#volatility), kumbukumbu na [uhifadhi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody).

**Kuweka akiba:** Mtu hushikilia bitcoin kwa matumizi ya baadaye. Ugavi unaotabirika unaweza kuvutia, lakini [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) yake ya ubadilishaji kwa sarafu ya fiat inaweza kushuka kwa kasi, na usalama wa funguo unakuwa muhimu.

**Kupokea:** Mtu hupokea zawadi, mchango, marejesho ya pesa, pesa zinazotumwa na ndugu kutoka mbali au malipo ya deni. Mpokeaji lazima atoe ombi sahihi la malipo na kuthibitisha kwamba malipo yamekamilika.

**Kutuma:** Mtu huhamisha [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) kwa familia, marafiki, biashara au shirika. Lazima alinganishe njia ya malipo, kasi, ada, [faragha](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy) na uhakika wa kukamilika kwa malipo.

**Kutumia:** Mtu hubadilisha bitcoin kwa bidhaa au huduma. Anapaswa kubainisha [kiwango cha ubadilishaji](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#exchange-rate), sera ya mfanyabiashara, njia ya malipo na athari za kodi za eneo husika zinazohusika.

**Upangaji wa bei na uhasibu:** Mtu au biashara hurekodi [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) katika bitcoin au [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi). Katika maeneo mengi, [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) kwa sarafu ya fiat wakati wa malipo bado inahitajika kwa hesabu au kodi.

**Kuchangia:** Mtu husaidia shirika au mtu binafsi moja kwa moja. Anapaswa kuthibitisha ombi la malipo la mpokeaji kupitia chanzo kinachoaminika.

Kwa matumizi yoyote, jiulizeni maswali matatu:

1. Ni tatizo gani lililopo ambalo [Bitcoin inatatua](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-address) hapa?
1. Je, Bitcoin ni bora kuliko njia mbadala iliyopo katika hali hii?
1. Bitcoin inaleta wajibu au hatari gani mpya?

Epukeni kuanza na chombo. Anzeni na hitaji.
