# 4.9 Faida na hasara za KYC na bila KYC

[KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#kyc) na [no-KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#no-kyc) ni njia zinazoleta [faida na hasara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) tofauti. Hakuna lebo yoyote kati ya hizi inayoondoa haja ya kufanya tathmini makini.


> **Callout – Faragha haiondoi hatari za kisheria wala za usalama wa mwili**
>
> Si [KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#kyc) wala [no-KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#no-kyc) iliyo bora moja kwa moja. Linganisheni njia halisi, tumieni mwongozo wa sasa wa eneo lenu pale sheria zisipokuwa wazi, na msivunje sheria za eneo lenu wala kujiweka katika hatari ya kimwili kwa ajili ya kutafuta [faragha](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy).


![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/7af8a2eb79ede0b36361dc0d1d6fe8f7ff404da2-1536x1024.svg)

Huduma ya [KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#kyc) inaweza kuomba jina halali, hati ya utambulisho, anwani, picha, taarifa kuhusu chanzo cha fedha, au kumbukumbu nyingine. Hili linaweza kuiwezesha huduma kutimiza sheria za eneo husika, kuunganishwa na benki, kuweka viwango vya juu zaidi, au kusaidia kurejesha akaunti.

[KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#kyc) pia huleta hatari kwa data. Huduma inaweza kuhifadhi muunganisho wa kudumu kati ya utambulisho, njia za malipo, na [miamala](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) ya bitcoin. Uvujaji wa data, ombi la kisheria, matumizi mabaya ya ndani, au mabadiliko ya sera yanaweza kufichua au kuzuia taarifa hizo.

Njia ya [no-KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#no-kyc) inaweza kukusanya taarifa chache za utambulisho. Hili linaweza kuboresha [faragha](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy) ya kifedha. Inaweza pia kuhusisha wahusika wasiodhibitiwa sana, ukwasi mdogo, [bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) za juu zaidi, msaada mdogo, hatari kubwa zaidi ya ulaghai, au vikwazo vya kisheria.

Linganisheni njia halisi:

[Faragha](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy) si usiri dhidi ya kila hatari. Ni uwezo wa kudhibiti taarifa zinazowahusu. Msivunje sheria za eneo lenu wala kujiweka katika hatari ya kimwili kwa ajili ya kutafuta [faragha](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy). Tafuteni mwongozo wa sasa wa eneo lenu kutoka kwa mtu mwenye sifa stahiki pale sheria zisipokuwa wazi.
