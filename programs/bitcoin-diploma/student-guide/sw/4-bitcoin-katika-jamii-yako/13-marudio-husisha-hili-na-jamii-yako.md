Kamilisheni tathmini hii kila mmoja binafsi, kisha linganisheni hoja zenu na timu au kundi lenu. Tafakarini mfano wa ndani mliouchunguza: ulishughulikia hitaji gani halisi, ni kazi gani ya pesa iliyohusika, na je, [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) ilihamishwa on-chain, kupitia Lightning, au kwa njia zote mbili? Ulizeni ni nani aliyedhibiti funguo, faida kubwa zaidi na hatari kubwa zaidi zilikuwa zipi, na jinsi sheria, miundombinu, lugha au utamaduni wa eneo hilo ulivyoathiri matokeo. Hatua inayofuata yenye uwajibikaji ni ile inayoendana na muktadha wa ndani, inayoweka wazi gharama na [uhifadhi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), inayopunguza makosa na utapeli, na inayoweza kutathminiwa kwa kuzingatia ushahidi.

**Jibu la Moduli ya 4:** Bitcoin inaweza kufanya kazi kama pesa pale watu wanapoitumia kwa hiari kupata kipato, kuweka akiba, kupokea, kutuma, kutumia, [kupanga bei](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price), au kukamilisha malipo ya [thamani](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value). Hatua ya Connect inaanza na hitaji halisi, inalinganisha mbadala unaowezekana, inaweka wazi [uhifadhi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) na gharama, inaheshimu sheria za ndani na usalama, na inawapa wanafunzi maarifa ya kutosha kuchagua kwa uhuru.

#### Mambo makuu ya kukumbuka

* Bitcoin kama pesa ni suala la kienyeji: njia yenye manufaa inategemea mahitaji ya jamii, miundombinu, sheria, gharama, zana, lugha na hali za usalama.
* Shughuli ya Connect ya **Moduli ya 4** iliyoandaliwa na mwalimu inapaswa kuunganisha mafunzo ya Tatizo, Suluhisho na Hatua na tatizo au fursa halisi, mbadala unaowezekana, mazoezi salama, na ushahidi wa kujifunza.
* Warsha za ndani zinapaswa kufuata mfuatano salama: kuangalia mchakato, kufanya mazoezi kwa msaada, kisha kueleza au kukamilisha kazi iliyobainishwa bila kufichua siri.
* Kabla ya kutumia zana au huduma ya ndani, hakikisheni lengo lake, mfumo wake wa [uhifadhi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), chanzo, mchakato wa urejeshaji, [faragha](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy), gharama, utangamano, usaidizi, na kanuni za sasa za ndani.
* Malipo ya on-chain na ya Lightning yana [mabadilishano tofauti ya faida na hasara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) katika ukamilishaji wa malipo, kasi, ada, [uhifadhi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), na sehemu zinazoweza kushindwa; thibitisheni ni njia ipi ya malipo ambayo mpokeaji anatarajia.
* Njia za ndani za ubadilishaji, [KYC](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#kyc) kama chaguo, mtiririko wa malipo kwa wafanyabiashara, na matumizi ya kijamii yanapaswa kulinganishwa kwa kuzingatia gharama zake kamili, [uhifadhi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), faragha, usalama, na [mabadilishano ya kisheria ya faida na hasara](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off).
* Uchumi mzunguko imara au matumizi ya kijamii yenye nguvu ni ya hiari, hutatua tatizo halisi, huwalinda washiriki, na haiwafanyi wanafunzi kutegemea huduma moja, mratibu mmoja au ahadi moja.
