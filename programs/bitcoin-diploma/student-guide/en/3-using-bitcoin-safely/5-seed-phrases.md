# 3.5 Seed phrases

> **Definition – Seed phrase**
>
> A [seed phrase](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), also called a [recovery phrase](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), is an ordered list of words that many wallets use to derive and recover a set of [private keys](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key). A [private key](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key) authorizes a spend; the [seed phrase](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) is normally the backup that can recreate the wallet's keys. Common lengths are 12 or 24 words, although wallet designs differ.


The order and spelling of the words matter. If a person has the [seed phrase](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), they can often restore the wallet on another compatible device and spend the funds. The [seed phrase](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) is therefore not only a backup. It is full access.


> **Callout – Never share recovery information**
>
> Public receiving information can be shared. A [private key](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#private-key), [seed phrase](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase), password, or recovery code must remain secret. No legitimate educator, wallet company, exchange employee, support worker, or Bitcoin developer needs it. If a [seed phrase](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) may have been seen, copied, entered online, or stored on an untrusted device, treat it as compromised and seek help without revealing it.

Protect a [seed phrase](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) with these rules:

* write it down privately and accurately during wallet creation
* follow the wallet's official backup instructions
* keep it offline
* never photograph it, store it in cloud notes, email it, or send it in a message
* never type it into a website or ordinary computer form
* keep it out of cameras, microphones, other people's view, and places where it may be damaged, lost, or found

Some wallets use additional passphrases, multiple keys, or other backup systems. These are [Advanced Learning](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/advanced-modules/). Do not add complexity you cannot explain and recover from safely. If a [seed phrase](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase) may have been seen, copied, entered online, or stored on an untrusted device, treat it as compromised: seek guidance without revealing it and use a new trusted recovery process.
