# 3.7 Hot and cold wallets

> **Definition – Hot wallet**
>
> A [hot wallet](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#hot-wallet) manages keys on a device connected to the internet, such as a phone or computer. It is convenient for regular payments, but has a larger attack surface because malicious software, insecure updates, phishing, physical access, or device compromise may expose the wallet.



> **Definition – Cold wallet**
>
> A [cold wallet](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#cold-wallet) describes an arrangement that keeps signing keys away from an internet-connected general-purpose device.



<!-- micrographic: hot-and-cold-wallets-01 -->



> **Definition – Hardware wallet**
>
> A [hardware wallet](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#hardware-wallet) describes a dedicated device designed to generate, protect, and use keys while keeping them isolated from the connected computer or phone. A [hardware wallet](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#hardware-wallet) can support a cold arrangement, but the terms are not interchangeable; [custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) and recovery are separate questions.
