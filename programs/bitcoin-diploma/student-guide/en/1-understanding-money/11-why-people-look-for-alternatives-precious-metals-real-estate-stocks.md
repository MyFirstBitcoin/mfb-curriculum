# 1.11 Alternatives for saving and holding value

People often seek alternatives to holding cash when they believe it will lose [purchasing power](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power). Common choices include precious metals, real estate, and stocks. Precious metals have also served as money in many places and periods. Each option has benefits and risks.


<!-- micrographic: saving-alternatives-table-01 -->


An [asset](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) is something a person can own or claim that may have [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value); it does not automatically perform every function of money. A home may store [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) but is not practical for buying food. A share may be easy to sell in one country but unavailable to someone without a brokerage account. [Gold](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold) has served as money across long periods of history but can be difficult to verify or divide during a small exchange.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/73bd09407fd1c7ac48ccf9725918a1227c355533-1536x1024.svg)

People may also choose foreign [currency](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency), government [bonds](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bond), private businesses, land, tools, education, or other [assets](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset). Every choice includes [opportunity cost](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#opportunity-cost) and risk. There is no [asset](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) that guarantees [purchasing power](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power) in every period and situation.

**Note:** Tax treatment can also shape these decisions. Rules for income, gains, property, and [transactions](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) can change the cost or benefit of holding, selling, or using an [asset](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset). Tax rules vary by jurisdiction and can change over time; this course does not provide tax advice.

Bitcoin emerged as another proposed alternative: a scarce [digital asset](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#digital-asset) that can be held and transferred without a central [issuer](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#issuer). To evaluate that claim, we must study why Bitcoin was created and how its rules work.
