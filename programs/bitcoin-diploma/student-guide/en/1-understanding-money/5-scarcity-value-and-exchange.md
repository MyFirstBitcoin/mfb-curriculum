# 1.5 Scarcity, value, and exchange

Resources are limited, while human wants are not.


<!-- card: definition -->
> **Definition – Scarcity**
>
> [Scarcity](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#scarcity) means that choosing one use of a resource prevents another use. Time, land, energy, good health, tools, and [sound money](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#sound-money) are all scarce.


The [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) of a good is not contained inside the object. People [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) goods according to their needs, preferences, information, and circumstances. This is sometimes called [**subjective value**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#subjective-value). Clean water may be inexpensive near a safe public source and extremely valuable during a shortage.

Exchange happens when both sides prefer what they receive to what they give up. They do not need to agree that the two items have the same personal [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value). They only need to agree to the terms.

Three ideas help explain everyday choices:

* [**Opportunity cost**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#opportunity-cost) is the best alternative you give up when you make a choice. Money spent on transport cannot also be spent on food.
* [**Time preference**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#time-preference) describes how strongly a person prefers [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) now instead of later. Urgent needs can create a high preference for the present. Saving for a future goal shows a greater willingness to wait.
* [**Trade-off**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) means gaining one benefit while accepting a cost or giving up another benefit.


<!-- micrographic: one-choice-two-uses-01 -->
[A shopper with limited money looks at the options in front of them: food for today, transport, or saving for later. Choosing one gives up the others, which is the opportunity cost, and no option is marked as the right one.](/micrographics/one-choice-two-uses-01/en.md)


[Scarcity](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#scarcity) alone does not guarantee [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value). An item may be rare and still have no demand. A useful monetary [asset](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) combines limited supply with properties and demand that make people willing to hold or exchange it.
