# 1.1 Opening questions about money

Money shapes everyday choices about food, transport, work, saving, and helping people we care about. Discuss these questions with your cohort.

1. Why do people need money?
1. What makes something money?
1. Who can create or control the money you use?
1. Why does the money you use have [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value)?
1. Does your money buy the same amount today as it did in the past?

As you discuss these questions, notice the difference between a fact and an opinion. “This note is accepted for taxes” is a fact that can be checked. “This is the best form of money” is an opinion or conclusion that needs criteria and evidence.
