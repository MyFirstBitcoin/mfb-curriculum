# 1.3 Functions and properties of money

Money usually performs three main functions.

**[Medium of exchange](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#medium-of-exchange):** People accept it in payment for goods and services. This reduces the need for direct [barter](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#barter).

**[Unit of account](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#unit-of-account):** [Prices](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) are expressed in a shared unit. This allows people to compare the cost of different goods and keep financial records.

**[Store of value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#store-of-value):** [Value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) can be moved from the present into the future. Every form of money has risks to [purchasing power](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#purchasing-power), but people compare how well different forms preserve it across a chosen period and situation.

An item can perform one function better than another. In many current monetary systems, a national [currency](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) is widely accepted for daily payments while people may choose another [asset](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) for long-term saving.

No form of money scores perfectly.

People also compare money by its [**soundness**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#sound-money): whether its supply is difficult to expand and whether its properties support reliable use across time. “[Sound money](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#sound-money)” is a judgment, not a fact label. To use it well, state the criteria and compare different forms of money by the same standards.


<!-- micrographic: functions-and-properties-01 -->


[Gold](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#gold) is durable and scarce but difficult to divide and transport for small payments. [Paper cash](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#paper-cash) is portable and accepted locally, but it can be damaged and its supply can expand. The best comparison depends on the purpose, place, and time.

**Think critically:** Name a time when you chose one type of money or [asset](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) over another. What was the purpose? Compare the properties of the options you could have used. Which properties mattered most to you, and why?
