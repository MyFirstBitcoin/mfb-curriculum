# Quiz – Understanding Money: quick check

## Question 1

Why is money accepted?

* [x] Others are expected to accept it
* [ ] It makes goods equally valuable
* [ ] It removes all rules
* [ ] It turns services into goods

### Explanation

Money helps strangers coordinate exchange across time because people expect others to accept it later. It still depends on trust, rules, and social acceptance. Review Student Guide, Module 1, “Why money exists.”

## Question 2

Priced, paid, saved: which roles?

* [ ] Store; unit; medium
* [x] Unit; medium; store
* [ ] Medium; store; unit
* [ ] Scarcity; scarcity; scarcity

### Explanation

A unit of account expresses prices, a medium of exchange is accepted in payment, and a store of value moves value into the future. Review Student Guide, Module 1, “Functions and properties of money.”

## Question 3

Rice for shoes fails: why?

* [ ] Different prices
* [x] No matching wants
* [ ] No bank approval
* [ ] Rice cannot trade

### Explanation

Direct barter is difficult when each person does not want what the other offers at the same time. This is the double coincidence of wants. Review Student Guide, Module 1, “Barter and the double coincidence of wants.”

## Question 4

Prices +10%, income +5%: purchasing power?

* [ ] Rises
* [ ] Stays the same
* [x] Falls
* [ ] Cannot tell

### Explanation

Purchasing power is what money can buy. If prices rise faster than income, the income buys less. Review Student Guide, Module 1, “Inflation and purchasing power.”
