# 4.8 Local on-ramp and off-ramp pathways

An **on-ramp** is a path from local money, work, or another asset into bitcoin. An **off-ramp** is a path from bitcoin into local money, goods, or services. The same pathway may perform both roles.

Local pathways may include:

* earning bitcoin;
* buying or selling directly with another person;
* using an exchange or broker;
* using an ATM or kiosk;
* accepting or spending with a merchant;
* using a payment service that converts between bitcoin and fiat;
* participating in a local circular economy.

Compare the **complete route**, not only the first fee. A service may advertise a low purchase fee but use a poor exchange rate or charge for withdrawal. A peer-to-peer path may appear private but require time, trust, and physical safety.

Check whether the path allows withdrawal to a wallet you control. A displayed bitcoin balance inside a closed service may be an internal claim rather than an on-chain balance under your keys.

For in-person exchange, use a public and appropriate location, avoid carrying unnecessary cash, bring a trusted person where suitable, verify payment before leaving, and follow local law. Do not reveal where you store bitcoin or how much you own.
