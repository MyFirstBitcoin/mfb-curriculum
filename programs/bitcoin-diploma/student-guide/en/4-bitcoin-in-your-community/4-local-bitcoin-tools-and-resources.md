[Ten things to verify before using a local Bitcoin tool: purpose, custody, source, project continuity, recovery, privacy, cost, compatibility, support and law, each with its question. Note the date you checked.](/micrographics/tool-verification-checklist-01/en.md)


Local bitcoiners may use wallets, exchanges, peer-to-peer markets, merchant tools, maps, meetups, hardware devices, and education resources.


<!-- card: light -->
> **Check the local example**
>
> A local tool or service is a dated example for learning, not a promise of safety, availability, low cost, or suitability. Verify its source, [custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody) model, recovery process, [privacy](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy), costs, compatibility, support, and current local status before real use.
