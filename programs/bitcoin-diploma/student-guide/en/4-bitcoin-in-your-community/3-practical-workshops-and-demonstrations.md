# 4.3 Practical workshops and demonstrations

You will move through three stages: **Presentation**, where your educator shows the process and its risks; **Practice**, where you follow a guided process using safe training materials; and **Production**, where you complete a task or explain a decision independently.


<!-- micrographic: presentation-practice-production-01 -->


Your workshop may include:

* identifying whether a wallet is custodial or self-custodial
* locating the receive screen without creating or exposing a real [seed phrase](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#recovery-phrase)
* comparing an on-chain address with a [Lightning invoice](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#lightning-invoice)
* checking a [transaction](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) in a wallet or [block explorer](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#block-explorer)
* comparing fees and received amounts across local pathways
* identifying scam signals
* planning a merchant payment or community donation flow
* identifying and checking local resources for a defined purpose

Before any live demonstration, make sure you know:

* the tool and instructions were checked recently
* how the educator will explain recovery and what happens if something fails
* whether real funds are involved and the privacy, security, and loss risks
* that you will not be pressured to install software or disclose financial data
* that any live amount is small enough to make a loss a lesson, not a crisis
* that an offline or no-funds alternative is available
* how the class will stop if the result differs from the plan

The result of your workshop should be an explanation or skill, not only a completed [transaction](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction).
