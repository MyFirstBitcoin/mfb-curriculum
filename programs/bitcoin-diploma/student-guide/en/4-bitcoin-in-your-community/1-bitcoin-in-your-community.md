# 4.1 Bitcoin in Your Community

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/119ea5325cea83e0cdbf15651f5a19becfb07cdf-1536x1024.svg)

This is the **Connect** stage of the Bitcoin Diploma. You will connect the modern money-system flaws and [trade-offs](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off), Bitcoin's response, and safe-use responsibility from **Modules 1–3** to a local need or opportunity, a realistic alternative, and a safe practical task. You are not expected to adopt Bitcoin or conclude that it is the best option in every local situation.

Bitcoin is one global protocol, but people use it under different local conditions. Internet access, phones, laws, taxes, [currencies](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency), banks, merchants, fees, language, safety, and technical support all affect what is useful.

Your educator selects the learning activities in this module. The goal is not to demonstrate every tool. It is to connect concepts from **Modules 1–3** to real choices in your community. The educator may invite a local expert when specialized knowledge is needed.

Use the **Module 3** safety checklist throughout every local module. Using bitcoin or a named tool in a supervised exercise does not mean that My First Bitcoin or your educator recommends frequent, ongoing, or higher-value use. The purpose is to compare the option and its [trade-offs](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) well enough to make your own decision.
