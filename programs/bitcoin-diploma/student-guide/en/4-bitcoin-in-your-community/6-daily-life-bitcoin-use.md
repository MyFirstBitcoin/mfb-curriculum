# 4.6 Daily-life Bitcoin use

Bitcoin can function as money in several ways. Spending is only one of them.

**Earning:** A worker, creator, business, or community member receives bitcoin for value provided. Earning can avoid a separate purchase process, but the receiver must agree on pricing, volatility, records, and custody.

**Saving:** A person holds bitcoin for future use. Predictable supply may be attractive, but the fiat exchange value can fall sharply and key security becomes important.

**Receiving:** A person accepts a gift, donation, refund, remittance, or repayment. The receiver must provide the correct payment request and verify settlement.

**Sending:** A person transfers value to family, friends, a business, or an organization. They must compare payment path, speed, fee, privacy, and finality.

**Spending:** A person exchanges bitcoin for goods or services. They should identify the exchange rate, merchant policy, payment method, and local tax consequences that apply.

**Pricing and accounting:** A person or business records value in bitcoin or sats. In many places, the fiat value at the time of payment is still needed for accounts or taxes.

**Donating:** A person supports an organization or individual directly. They should verify the recipient's payment request through a trusted source.

For any use, ask three questions:

1. What existing problem does Bitcoin address here?
1. Is Bitcoin better than the available alternative for this case?
1. What new responsibility or risk does Bitcoin create?

Avoid starting with the tool. Start with the need.
