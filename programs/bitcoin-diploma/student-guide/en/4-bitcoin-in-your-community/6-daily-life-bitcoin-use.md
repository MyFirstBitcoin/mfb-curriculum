# 4.6 Daily-life Bitcoin use

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/b86058296c3087bf7e2280da71b62bd19050e17b-1536x1024.svg)


<!-- micrographic: daily-bitcoin-uses-01 -->


Bitcoin can function as money in several ways. Spending is only one of them.

**Earning:** A worker, creator, business, or community member receives bitcoin for [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) provided. Earning can avoid a separate purchase process, but the receiver must agree on pricing, [volatility](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#volatility), records, and [custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody).

**Saving:** A person holds bitcoin for future use. Predictable supply may be attractive, but the fiat exchange [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) can fall sharply and key security becomes important.

**Receiving:** A person accepts a gift, donation, refund, remittance, or repayment. The receiver must provide the correct payment request and verify settlement.

**Sending:** A person transfers [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) to family, friends, a business, or an organization. They must compare payment path, speed, fee, [privacy](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#privacy), and finality.

**Spending:** A person exchanges bitcoin for goods or services. They should identify the [exchange rate](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#exchange-rate), merchant policy, payment method, and local tax consequences that apply.

**Pricing and accounting:** A person or business records [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) in bitcoin or [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi). In many places, the fiat [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) at the time of payment is still needed for accounts or taxes.

**Donating:** A person supports an organization or individual directly. They should verify the recipient's payment request through a trusted source.

For any use, ask three questions:

1. What existing problem does [Bitcoin address](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bitcoin-address) here?
1. Is Bitcoin better than the available alternative for this case?
1. What new responsibility or risk does Bitcoin create?

Avoid starting with the tool. Start with the need.
