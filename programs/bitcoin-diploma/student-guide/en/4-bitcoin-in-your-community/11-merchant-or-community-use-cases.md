# 4.11 Merchant or community use cases

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/9fd6b8ca04a295f860a0ab150d8f05823cbcea16-1536x1024.svg)


<!-- micrographic: merchant-payment-operations-01 -->
[A merchant who accepts Bitcoin works across four areas: marketing, training, accounting and sales.](/micrographics/merchant-payment-operations-01/en.md)


A merchant needs more than a receive button. The full payment process may include pricing, staff training, receipts, refunds, [custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody), conversion, accounting, taxes, device security, and support.

Before accepting Bitcoin, a merchant should decide:

* whether [prices](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) are displayed in local [currency](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency), [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), or both
* whether payments use on-chain Bitcoin, Lightning, or both
* whether the wallet is custodial or self-custodial
* who can access the receiving device
* how received funds are secured or moved
* whether some funds are converted to local [currency](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency)
* how payment is confirmed at the point of sale
* how refunds and mistakes are handled
* what records local law requires
* what staff do when the network or wallet fails

Community use cases may include:

* paying local workers or creators
* receiving donations transparently
* transferring [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) between community groups
* supporting a savings group with clearly defined [custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custody)
* helping a remote worker receive international payment
* connecting local producers and merchants
* teaching safe wallet use
* providing emergency support where ordinary payment systems are unavailable

[Community custody](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#community-custody) requires special care. If one organizer holds funds for many people, that organizer becomes a [custodian](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#custodian) and a point of trust. [Shared funds](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#shared-funds) need transparent rules, records, access controls, and legal review where appropriate.

For each use case, define success in terms of the problem solved, not the number of [transactions](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) completed.
