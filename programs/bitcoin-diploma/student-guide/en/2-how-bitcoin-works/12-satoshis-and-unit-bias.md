# 2.12 Satoshis and unit bias

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/98c8cef9bc6e6d0955458f6fb4a71f1de5ed5225-1536x1024.svg)



One bitcoin (1 BTC) can be divided into **100,000,000 [satoshis](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi)**. A [satoshi](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), often shortened to [**sat**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), is the smallest unit recorded on Bitcoin's base layer.

**You do not need to buy or use a whole bitcoin.** Bitcoin software can display an amount in bitcoin or [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), just as a [currency](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency) can use a main unit and smaller units. Many people use [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi) for everyday amounts because whole numbers are easier to read. Both on-chain and Lightning payment requests can be shown in [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi).

**Unit [bias](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bias)** is the mistaken feeling that a lower [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) per whole unit makes an [asset](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) cheaper or more accessible. The number of units is a design choice. Owning 100,000 [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi) and owning 0.001 BTC describe the same amount.

When comparing [prices](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price), focus on the share of the total supply, the amount of [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) involved, and the risks. Do not assume that an [asset](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) with many cheap-looking units offers better [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) than an [asset](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) divided into smaller units.
