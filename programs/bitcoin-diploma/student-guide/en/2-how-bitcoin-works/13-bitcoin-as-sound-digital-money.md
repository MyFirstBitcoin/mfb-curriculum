# 2.13 Bitcoin as sound digital money

As **Module 1** introduced, [**sound money**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#sound-money) describes money whose supply is difficult to expand and whose properties support reliable use across time. The term expresses a judgment based on criteria, so it should be examined rather than accepted as a label.

Bitcoin combines properties that did not previously exist together in one [asset](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset):


<!-- micrographic: bitcoin-money-properties-01 -->
[Bitcoin combines seven properties of money in one asset: durability, portability, divisibility, fungibility, scarcity, verifiability and acceptability. Each has practical limits, such as keeping keys safe and acceptance that is still low in many communities.](/micrographics/bitcoin-money-properties-01/en.md)



| Property | Plain-language explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Durability | The ledger is copied across many nodes. Access depends on preserving valid keys. |
| Portability | A signed transaction can transfer value through a communications network. |
| Divisibility | One bitcoin (1 BTC) divides into 100,000,000 sats. |
| Fungibility | Equal amounts are spendable under the same protocol rules, although public transaction history can affect how some services treat particular funds. |
| Scarcity | Issuance follows public rules with a 21 million maximum. |
| Verifiability | A node can verify supply, transaction rules, and ownership proofs. |
| Acceptability | Acceptance is growing in some places but remains much lower than that of national currencies in many communities. |


Bitcoin can perform the three functions of money, but not equally in every place:

* As a [**store of value**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#store-of-value), its supply is predictable, but its market [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) is volatile.
* As a [**medium of exchange**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#medium-of-exchange), it can move without a bank, but acceptance, fees, and payment tools vary.
* As a [**unit of account**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#unit-of-account), amounts can be stated in bitcoin or [sats](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#satoshi), but most goods are still priced in fiat [currency](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#currency).

Bitcoin therefore presents both benefits and [trade-offs](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off).

**Potential benefits** include direct ownership, predictable supply, global transfer, open participation, transparent rules, and resistance to unilateral censorship.

[**Trade-offs**](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#trade-off) include [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) [volatility](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#volatility), personal security responsibility, the fact that a valid payment is usually difficult or impossible to reverse, changing [transaction fees](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction-fee), dependence on electricity and communications for digital transfer, public [transaction](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#transaction) records, and limited acceptance.

Whether you call Bitcoin [sound money](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#sound-money) should follow from your criteria. Compare it with [fiat money](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#fiat-money), precious metals, and other [assets](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#asset) using the same standards.

**Local-value check:** Does bitcoin have [value](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#value) in your community? Separate three questions in your answer: What market [price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price) can be observed? Where, if anywhere, is it accepted? What personal preference or [bias](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#bias) affects your judgment? [Price](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#price), local usefulness, and personal approval are related but not identical.
