# Ku ɗauki nauyin jagoranci

Wannan sabon tsari yana ba malamai ƙarin jagoranci a inda daidaito yake da muhimmanci, da ƙarin sassauci a inda hikimar al'umma take da muhimmanci. Jagorancinku cikin kulawa yana sauya Difloma na Bitcoin daga littafi zuwa ƙwarewar koyo ta haɗin gwiwa: wadda ke taimaka wa mutane su yi tunani mai zurfi da fahimta game da kuɗi, Bitcoin, ɗaukar alhaki, da al'ummarsu.

Don ƙarin koyo na zaɓi bayan Difloma na Bitcoin, ku binciki [Shirin Horar da Malamai](https://etp.myfirstbitcoin.org/) ko ku haɗu da wasu ta hanyar [Cibiyar Haɗin Gwiwar Ilimantarwa kan Bitcoin](https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org). Haka kuma za ku iya [raba ra'ayoyinku](https://forms.myfirstbitcoin.org/feedback), [aika gyare-gyare ko hanyoyin inganta fassara](https://forms.myfirstbitcoin.org/proofread), raba kayan koyo na yankinku ta hanyar [fom ɗin bayar da gudummawa na hukuma](https://forms.myfirstbitcoin.org/), ko ku yi amfani da buɗaɗɗiyar-tushe [ma'ajiyar manhajar karatu](https://github.com/MyFirstBitcoin/mfb-curriculum) don duba bugu na yanzu da kuma shiga cikin inganta aikin. Ba a buƙatar amfani da ko ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan hanyoyin haɗin yanar gizo don kammala Difloma na Bitcoin.
