# Ku tantance koyo cikin adalci

Samun takardar shaida yana buƙatar ɓangarori biyu **waɗanda nasara a ɗaya ba ta rama gazawa a ɗayan** waɗanda malami ne zai tantance su duka ya kuma rubuta sakamakonsu:


| Ɓangare | Abin da ake karɓa a matsayin shaida |
| --- | --- |
| Fahimtar ƙa'ida (50%) | Bayani, kwatantawa, amsa ga wani yanayi, ko wata mu'amala makamanciya daga ɗalibi, wadda aka amince da ita kuma ta dace da manufar koyo, ana tantance ta bisa ƙa'idojin da aka bayyana. |
| Ƙwarewa a aikace (50%) | Lura kai tsaye da ɗalibi yana kammala aikin da aka amince da shi, kwaikwayon aiki, ko wasan kwaikwayon wata rawa cikin aminci, tare da tantance shi bisa ƙa'idojin kimanta aikin. |


Halartar darasi, nuna sha'awa, yin bita da kai, ko tattaunawa ta yau da kullum kaɗai ba su zama shaidar samun takardar shaida ba. Ku bayyana manufar koyo, iyakokin kiyaye aminci, aikin da za a yi, da ƙa'idojin samun nasara a sarari; ku fara da koyarwa da atisaye; sannan ku lura da yadda ɗalibi yake aiki, ku ba da ra'ayin ingantawa, ku rubuta sakamakon, kuma ku ba da damar sake tantancewa cikin adalci idan an buƙata. Sassan karatu na 3 da 4 suna buƙatar lura kai tsaye da aikin ɗalibi.
