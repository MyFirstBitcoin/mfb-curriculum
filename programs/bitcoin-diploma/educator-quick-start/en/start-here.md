# Start here

Welcome. The new Bitcoin Diploma keeps the mission that matters—open-source, community-led, Bitcoin-only education—while making it clearer, more practical, and easier to adapt responsibly. You do not need to be a perfect lecturer or know every answer. Your role is to help learners ask good questions, practise safely, and explain what they understand in their own words.

Use this Quick Start to prepare your first route, then use the Educator Guide for complete facilitation, safety, adaptation, and assessment guidance.

The 26.10 curriculum is a framework-first teaching package. Before offering completion certificates, prepare the selected tasks and local variants, observation capacity, accessibility routes, Assessment Grid, and proportionate local privacy, correction, and appeals arrangements. You assess the learner and issue the certificate; My First Bitcoin does not centrally evaluate, issue, verify, or guarantee learner competency.

1. Choose a delivery shape: one full day, four two-hour sessions, eight one-hour sessions, or another schedule that protects the complete learning and assessment experience.
1. Read the relevant Student Guide module and its matching section in the [Educator Guide](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/educator-guide/). Choose one main activity and one low-tech backup.
1. Prepare a welcoming room, printed or PDF materials, and a way to record approved assessment evidence. Check local facts, examples, tools, and safety conditions before class.
1. Teach the simple rhythm: **explain → ask → practise → debrief → check**. Give learners supported practice before any assessment.
1. Close each session with a learner explanation, a next step, and a note about what you will revisit.

The Bitcoin Diploma works offline or online. No learner needs a personal device, connectivity, or prior Bitcoin experience to learn or complete the Bitcoin Diploma.
