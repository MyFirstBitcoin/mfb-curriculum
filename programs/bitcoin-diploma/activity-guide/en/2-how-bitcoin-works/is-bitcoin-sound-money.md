# 2.11 Is Bitcoin Sound Money?

_Discussion_

Participants return to the money comparison table and assess Bitcoin alongside cows, cigarettes, diamonds, and euros. Using characteristics such as durability, portability, uniformity, acceptability, scarcity, and divisibility, they complete the comparison and discuss whether Bitcoin meets the characteristics of sound money.

#### Objectives

* Participants compare Bitcoin with other forms of money using the characteristics of good money.

#### Materials

* Money comparison table.

#### Setup

Return to the money comparison table from Chapter 2.

#### Procedure

##### Step 1

Review the characteristics: durable, portable, uniform, acceptable, scarce, and divisible.

##### Step 2

Compare cows, cigarettes, diamonds, euros, and Bitcoin against each characteristic.

##### Step 3

Complete the totals in the comparison table.

#### Discussion questions

How does Bitcoin compare with other forms of money?
