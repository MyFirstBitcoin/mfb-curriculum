# 2.9 Watch: Why Every Bitcoiner Should Run Their Own Node

_Discussion · 5 min_

Participants watch the first five minutes of a video explaining what Bitcoin nodes are and why running one may matter. The educator stops before the setup instructions and emphasizes that nodes are neither miners nor wallets: they independently run the Bitcoin protocol and maintain an updated blockchain copy.

#### Objectives

* Participants distinguish the roles of a Bitcoin node, a miner, and a wallet.

#### Materials

* Internet access and video playback.

#### Procedure

##### Step 1

Open the video.

##### Step 2

Watch only the first five minutes, then stop when the speaker begins explaining setup.

##### Step 3

Emphasize that nodes are not miners or wallets: they run the Bitcoin protocol and maintain an updated copy of the blockchain.

#### Key points

Nodes help ensure the network is decentralized and secure.
