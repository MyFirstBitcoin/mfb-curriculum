# 2.6 Watch: Blockchain 101 – A Visual Demo

_Discussion_

Participants watch most or all of “Blockchain 101 – A Visual Demo” as a visual alternative or complement to generating a SHA-256 hash. The video illustrates how blocks are linked, allowing participants to observe how changes to data affect a chain and to connect the demonstration to the course discussion of blockchain verification.

#### Objectives

* Participants observe how linking blocks makes changes to earlier blockchain data visible.

#### Materials

* Internet access and video playback.

#### Procedure

##### Step 1

Open the video.

##### Step 2

Watch most or all of the video as time permits.

##### Step 3

Note how the video shows blocks being chained.

#### Notes

The full video is 17 minutes long and may be difficult to play in its entirety.

#### Additional resources

* [“Blockchain 101 – A Visual Demo” visually demonstrates how blocks are linked and how altering earlier data becomes visible, reinforcing the activity’s discussion of blockchain verification.](https://youtu.be/_160oMzblY8)
