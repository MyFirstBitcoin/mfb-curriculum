# 1.7 Alternative Time Preference Questions for Discussion

_Discussion_

Participants use guided questions to connect time preference with money, entrepreneurship, consumption, saving, and interest rates. The discussion treats money as a way people communicate value in a free market and considers how understanding present and future preferences can inform personal decisions.

#### Objectives

* Participants connect time preference with saving, consumption, and interest rates through discussion.

#### Procedure

##### Step 1

Ask why time preference is included in a chapter about money.

##### Step 2

Ask how time preference helps entrepreneurs decide whether to save or spend, and how understanding it can help people live better lives.

##### Step 3

Discuss money as a communicator of value, the choice between immediate consumption and future savings, and how time preference appears through interest rates.

#### Key points

Money communicates value in a free market. Time preference influences consumption and savings decisions.

#### Discussion questions

How can learning about time preference help you make better money decisions?
