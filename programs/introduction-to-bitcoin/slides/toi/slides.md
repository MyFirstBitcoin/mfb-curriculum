## Mutanabelesya Cikwama Cili Coonse

* **Kulipila:** pa blockchain, pa Lightning, na zyoonse zyobilo?

## Pa Blockchain a Lightning

* **Muketani:** kulembwa caantangalala muketani wamabbuuloko
* **Lightning:** netiweeki yakulipila iyakilidwe atala a Bitcoin kuti kulipila tusimbi tuniini kucitike cakufwambaana alimwi kanji-kanji, mbuli kuula kofi
* Cikwama ca Lightning cilakonzya alimwi kubambwa aumwi muntu naa kubambwa ndinywe nobeni

## Amwiime, Mutaabani Cintu, Mutatumizyi Cintu

Amwiime naa umwi wamukulwaizya kucita cintu cakufwambaana naa mubusisidwe, wamusyomezya mpindu iisinizidwe, wamulomba kuti mutume bitcoin kutanguna, naa wamulomba mazwi aakuvwuntulula cikwama canu. Amulindile. Mutapa pe cintu. Mutatumi pe cintu. Amane musinizye kwiinda kunsulo yamakani njimuzi kale.

## Zintu Zyone Zipati

* **Mali ayanzikide kumilawo akusyoma.** Bunji bwa mali aa fiat amilawo yaayo zibambwa amabungwe masyoonto.
* **Bitcoin ijisi milawo iipusene.** Bunji bwa mali buzibidwe kubantu boonse, bujisi mamanino, buzululidwe muntu uuli woonse, alimwi inga bwalolisisigwa kutali kubusyoma buyo.
* **Kukwabililwa kulatalika.** Amwiiye kusaanguna, amutalike azisyoonto, amusinizye zyoonse, mutaambizigwi muntu mabala aakuvwununa cikwama canu.
* **Aaya ngu matalikilo buyo.** Kutola ntaamu yomwe iikwabilidwe kulakkwana.

## Dipuloma ya Bitcoin

Intampu iitobela. Zipanzi zyayo zyone zilamutola kunembo:

* Kumvwisya mali
* Bitcoin mbwiibeleka
* Kubelesya Bitcoin munzila iikwabilila
* Bitcoin mucilawo canu

Amuzumanane kuyandisisya kuzyiba, amuzumanane kubuzya mibuzya, alimwi amwiiye zinji ku [**myfirstbitcoin.org**](http://myfirstbitcoin.org).

Tulalangila kumubona mugulu lyesu litobela.
