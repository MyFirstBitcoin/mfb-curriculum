## Imali ye-fiat

* 1971: iUnited States yaphelisa ukutshintshiselwa ngokusemthethweni kweedola ngegolide ngoorhulumente bamazwe angaphandle neebhanki ezingoovimba
* Namhlanje uninzi lweemali zamazwe yimali yefiat: ikhutshwa phantsi kwegunya likarhulumente, ayinakutshintshiselwa ngegolide okanye nayiphi na enye imveliso yorhwebo
* _Fiat_ ligama lesiLatini elithetha ukuthi "makwenziwe", elidla ngokuqondwa njengelithetha ukuthi "ngommiselo"
* Yamkelwa kuba abantu, amashishini, iibhanki noorhulumente bayayisebenzisa; imithetho iyenza ibe yimali esemthethweni yokuhlawula kwaye ifuna ukuba isetyenziswe ekuhlawuleni irhafu

Akukho nkampani inokumisa ukusetyenziswa kwe-bitcoin eniyilawulayo ngokwenu. Enye inkalo yaloo nkululeko luxanduva: intengiselwano esemthethweni idla ngokungakwazi ukurhoxiswa, kwaye akukho ziko loncedo lale nethiwekhi.
