# Wetin Be Money?

Money na tool wey people dey use take exchange things.

---

## Trade by Barter and Wen Two People Want Wetin Each Other Get

The two people must want wetin the other person get, for the same time and for quantity wey suit both of dem.

Di two people must want wetin di other person get, for di same time and for di right quantity.

## Money Na Tool for Exchange

Nobody want money just to get am. Money dey useful because other people dey accept am, as dem dey expect say other people go accept am later.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/11c8ef017ba2a817db5029a4902751e0814b4558-1774x887.svg)

<!-- notes: Banknote na just piece of paper. Money dey solve di problem of trade-by-barter by separating selling from buying: una fit sell una rice to one person, come buy shoes from another person. Na this dey make money different from trade-by-barter. -->

![bg right:40%](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/608140649b513680f20281ddc9a3b2f28b0f8883-1054x1493.png)

## Three Things Wey Money Dey Do

* **Wetin dem dey use do exchange:** people dey accept am as payment
* **Wetin dem dey use measure price:** dem dey use am state price
* **Wetin dey preserve value:** e dey carry value from today enter future

No money dey preserve value perfectly, but some kind money dey lose their power to buy things more slowly than others.

---

![bg right:40%](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/a9efb037f62e6bfc928c9192ce4e5178d285dc26-1942x809.svg)

## From Shell Dem Go Reach Paper

* Kauri, salt, beads, farm animals, kopa, silva, gold
* Metals wey get high value: dem no plenty, dem dey last, people for plenty places dey accept dem
* Paper wey give person right to collect the amount of metal wey dem write for am
* Gold standards: system wey law tie one unit of currency to gold

The rules no be the same for every place and every time.

---

## Fiat Moni

* 1971: United States stop the official arrangement wey allow foreign governments and central banks exchange dollars for gold
* Today, most countries their currencies na fiat money: government authorise how dem dey issue am, but person no fit exchange am for gold or any other commodity
* _Fiat_ na Latin for "make e happen", wey people often understand as "by government order"
* People accept am because people, businesses, banks, and governments dey use am; laws make am money wey dem recognise for payment and require people to use am pay tax

When people hear the word inflation, wetin dem dey usually think na say price of things dey go up.

## Plenty Reasons, One Choice

* Increase for money and credit
* Supply wey no dey flow well and changes for demand
* Currency wey dey lose value, taxes, cost to produce things

One cause na deliberate choice, no be accident: for fiat system, small number of institutions fit increase di amount of money and credit wey dey available and change di rules wey dey guide am.

<!-- notes: Economists dey debate how much each cause matter. Make una focus on the one cause wey be deliberate decision, no be something wey happen by chance. -->

---

## How E Scarce and Wetin E Worth

* Di original Mona Lisa get value pass any copy: na only one dey
* Scarcity alone no dey make something get value, but when people want something, how much of am dey available matter
* When di amount of money wey dey available dey increase faster than di things wey e fit buy, each unit of di money dey tend to buy less

---

## Inflation No Dey Affect Everybody the Same Way

* Cash and money wey person save fit lose power to buy things
* People wey own assets wey dia price dey rise fit get some protection
* Wages and prices dey change at different speeds

Money wey dey your bank account different from cash wey dey your hand: na promise wey the bank make to pay you. Most times, the bank dey keep that promise, but e depend on the bank and the rules wey dey guide am.

1. Abeg, ask person how much e cost some years ago, and check record wey person fit trust if una fit
1. Money wey people dey earn increase at the same rate? Wetin else fit explain the change?

# Wetin be Bitcoin?

Digital money wey no get one central body wey dey control am, wey get limit for the total amount wey fit dey, and wey person fit send directly to another person without bank permission.

---

## Di Problem of Spending di Same Money Two Times

**31 October 2008:** Satoshi Nakamoto publish _Bitcoin: Elektronik Cash Sistem Wey Dey Work From Person to Person_. **January 2009:** di network start.


<!-- micrographic: central-ledger-vs-bitcoin-01 -->
[For payment kompani, na one organization dey keep di rekod buk and approve every moni transfer. For Bitcoin, di person wey dey send moni dey sign di transakshon, independent node dem dey check am and miner dem dey add am to one rekod buk wey all of dem dey share, so di person wey dey receive moni no need to depend too much on one central authority.](/micrographics/central-ledger-vs-bitcoin-01/pcm.md)

<!-- notes: Before Bitcoin come, to send money online always depend on organization wey people trust: bank or payment company wey dey keep record of transactions, approve accounts, and fit delay, block, or reverse payment. Digital information easy to copy: if person fit spend one digital coin twice, e no go fit work as money. Na this be the problem of spending the same money twice. Satoshi Nakamoto na one person or group wey dey use that name. -->

---

## No Central Control · Supply Get Limit · Person to Person

* **E no get central control:** no central office dey open account, change account balance, or approve who fit join
* **Supply get limit:** bitcoin no go ever pass 21 million
* **Person to person:** send to anybody, anywhere, without asking permission from any central authority

No company fit freeze bitcoin wey una dey control by unaself. The other side of that freedom na responsibility: normally, nobody fit reverse transaction wey follow the rules, and no help desk dey for the network.

<!-- notes: Decentralized no mean say everybody get equal power to influence things. E mean say no single participant fit change the ledger or the money rules for everybody. -->

---

## Limit Wey Anybody Fit Check

<!-- micrographic: claimed-or-verified-supply-01 -->
[Supply limit wey dem write for website or inside di database of di body wey dey issue am fit change without anybody knowing, if di body decide to change am. Bitcoin supply rule dey open to everybody, and signature dem, history wey everybody fit see, proof say work don happen and checking by node dem dey work together to stop anybody from creating new coin anyhow or spending di same bitcoin twice.](/micrographics/claimed-or-verified-supply-01/pcm.md)

<!-- notes: Dem dey release new bitcoin according to timetable wey everybody fit see, and independent computers wey dem dey call nodes dey reject any block wey break that rule. Anybody fit trust the limit because anybody fit check am. E no be law of nature: e dey hold because people wey dey take part continue to enforce am. Supply wey get limit no guarantee say price go rise; price depend on demand as well as supply. -->

---

## You No Need One Full Bitcoin

One bitcoin fit divide into 100,000,000 smaller units wey dem dey call satoshis, or sats.

[Table wey get two column: 1 BTC na 100,000,000 sats, 0.1 BTC na 10,000,000 sats, 0.01 BTC na 1,000,000 sats, 0.001 BTC na 100,000 sats, and 0.00000001 BTC na 1 sat.](/micrographics/bitcoin-and-sats-01/pcm.md)

## Bitcoin Get Im Own Advantages and Disadvantages

* Im price fit change sharp sharp
* Most times, mistake wey happen no fit reverse
* For plenty places, people wey dey accept am still no plenty
* To use am safely, person need learn some things

Bitcoin dey replace some trust wey people get for institutions with checking to confirm things and taking responsibility by yourself. E no remove risk.

# How You Go Take Use Bitcoin?

Di first question about any wallet na: who dey control di keys?

---

## Wallet Dey Manage Key Dem

<!-- micrographic: wallet-manages-keys-01 -->
[Wallet dey hold di keys. With dem, una fit receive and send bitcoin, while di bitcoin itself dey recorded for di ledger wey everybody dey share, no be inside di phone or device.](/micrographics/wallet-manages-keys-01/pcm.md)

<!-- notes: Bitcoin wallet na tool, wey most times be app, wey dey allow una receive, check, and send bitcoin. Una bitcoin no dey stored inside una phone: na for blockchain dem record am. Di wallet dey manage di keys wey una fit use prove say di bitcoin na una own and give permission for payment. -->

---

## Na Who Really Dey Hold Your Bitcoin?

* **Una dey keep am by unaself:** na una dey control di key dem
* Nobody fit recover una bitcoin for una if una lose di recovery word dem and di device
* **Company dey keep am for una:** one company dey hold di key dem, small like bank
* Di balance wey una get na di company promise to pay una; e fit limit how much una fit withdraw, freeze account, suffer hacker attack, or close down

To choose who go keep una bitcoin no be test of una value. Wetin matter na to know which kind una dey use and wetin una dey put trust for.

## Your Seed Phrase Na Full Access

List of words wey dey arranged in order, wey often be 12 or 24 words, wey fit recreate your keys. Anybody wey get am fit take your bitcoin.


<!-- micrographic: seed-phrase-on-paper-01 -->
[Seed phrase na list of words wey follow correct order; for here, na twelve sample words wey dem number and write for paper. The correct words for the correct order fit restore control for wallet wey support am, so e dey stay secret and offline.](/micrographics/seed-phrase-on-paper-01/pcm.md)

<!-- notes: Seed phrase no be only backup: na wetin dey give full access. Abeg, write am correctly for paper when una create di wallet; keep am offline, keep am private, and put am for where e no go lost or spoil. No ever snap am, send am by email, save am for cloud notes, send am for message, or type am inside any website. Di words wey dem show na only example; no ever show or collect anybody real seed phrase. -->

## Before Una Use Any Wallet

* **Keys:** na una dey control dem, abi na company?
* **Recovery:** wetin go happen if una phone lost or di company disappear?
* **Where e come from:** na di official website or di app wey dey app store, no be link wey person send una?
* **Code:** e dey open for everybody to check, and dem still dey maintain am well? (Dis one alone no prove say e safe.)
* **Charges:** how much dem dey charge to send, receive, or withdraw?
* **Payments:** na for blockchain, Lightning, abi both?

<!-- notes: My First Bitcoin no dey recommend any particular wallet. Tools, fees, and who own dem dey change, and wetin fit one community fit no fit another. Make una direct learners to one teacher for their area wey dem fit trust. -->

---

## For Blockchain and Lightning

* **On-chain:** dem record am directly inside di blockchain
* **Lightning:** na payment network wey dem build on top Bitcoin for small payments wey dey happen fast and often, like to buy coffee
* Lightning wallet fit also be one wey another person dey keep di keys, or one wey di owner dey keep di keys by demself


<!-- micrographic: on-chain-and-lightning-01 -->
[Dem dey record payments wey happen on-chain straight for Bitcoin blockchain, while Lightning na payment network wey dem build on top Bitcoin for quick payments wey person fit make often, but wey fit still fail. For any of the two ways, na either another person dey keep the money for the owner, or the owner dey keep am by themself.](/micrographics/on-chain-and-lightning-01/pcm.md)

## Three Rules as Una Dey Learn

* **Learn first.** No take action because of excitement, fear, or pressure.
* **Start small.** Use amount wey una fit afford to lose take try tools wey una never sabi.
* **No Just Trust, Verify.** Check the person, the payment method, the address, and the amount.


<!-- card: callout -->
> **Share information for receiving payment, no ever share secrets**
>
> Una fit share address or payment request. Una no suppose ever share seed phrase or private key. No genuine teacher, wallet company, or support worker go ever ask una for am.

## Stop, No Share Anything, No Send Anything

Make una stop if person dey rush una or tell una to keep am secret, promise say una go surely make profit, ask una to send bitcoin first, or ask for una seed phrase. Make una pause. No share anything. No send anything. Then confirm am through source wey una already know.

## Four Main Points to Hold for Mind

* **Money dey depend on rules and trust.** Na small number of institutions dey set the amount of fiat money wey go dey and the rules.
* **Bitcoin get different rules.** The amount wey go dey get limit and dey open for everybody to see, anybody fit use am, and una fit check am instead of just to trust am.
* **Safety na first.** Make una learn first, start small, check everything well, and no ever share una seed phrase.
* **Na just the beginning be this.** Just one safe next step don do.
