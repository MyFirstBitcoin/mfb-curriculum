* **Uburyo bwo guhanahana:** abantu barayemera mu kwishura

Nta mafaranga azigama agaciro bimwe bitagira agahaze, ariko ubwoko bumwebumwe bw'amafaranga butakaza ubushobozi bwo kugura buhorobuhoro kuruta ubundi.

## Kuva ku bikonoshwa gushika ku mpapuro

* Ibikonoshwa, umunyu, ubunigi, ibitungwa, umuringa, ifeza, inzahabu
* Ivyuma vy'agaciro: biboneka gake, biramba, vyemerwa henshi
* Impapuro ziha uwuzifise uburenganzira bwo guhabwa urugero rw'icuma rwanditsweko
* Uburyo bwo gushingira amafaranga ku nzahabu: igice c'ifaranga gifitaniye isano n'inzahabu hisunzwe amategeko

Amategeko yarahinduka bivanye n'ahantu be n'ikiringo.

## Amafaranga ashingiye kw’itegeko rya Leta

* 1971: Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika zahagaritse uburyo bwemewe bwo kuvunjisha amadolari mu nzahabu bwakoreshwa na za Leta z'amahanga n'amabanki nkuru
* Muri iki gihe, amafaranga y'ibihugu vyinshi ni amafaranga ya fiat: asohorwa ku bubasha bwa Leta, ntashobora kuvunjishwa mu nzahabu canke mu kindi kidandazwa ico ari co cose
* _Fiat_ ni ijambo ry'ikilatini risobanura ngo "nibikorwe", akenshi rigatahuzwa n'insobanuro ngo "biciye kw'itegeko"
* Aremerwa kuko abantu, amashirahamwe y'ubudandaji, amabanki na za Leta bayakoresha; amategeko arayemeza nk'uburyo bwo kuriha kandi agasaba ko akoreshwa mu kuriha amakori

# Bitcoin ni iki?

Amafaranga yo mu buhinga bwa none atagenzurwa n’urwego rumwe, afise igitigiri ntarengwa, kandi ashobora kurungikwa ava ku muntu aja ku wundi ata ruhusha rwa banki.

**31 Gitugutu 2008:** Satoshi Nakamoto asohora _Bitcoin: Uburyo bwo guhanahana amafaranga yo mu buhinga bwa elegitoronike hagati y’abakoresha ata muhuza_. **Nzero 2009:** umuhora utangura gukora.

## Bidashingiye ku buyobozi bumwe · Igitigiri ntarengwa · Umuntu ku wundi

* **Bidatwarwa n'urwego rumwe:** nta biro bikuru vyugurura amakonti, bihindura amafaranga ari kuri yo, canke vyemeza abemerewe kwinjira
* **Igitigiri ntarengwa:** ntihazokwigera habaho bitcoin zirenga imiliyoni 21
* **Umuntu ku wundi:** murashobora kurungikira uwo ari we wese, aho ari hose, mutarinze gusaba uruhusha urwego rukuru

Nta shirahamwe rishobora guhagarika ikoreshwa rya bitcoin mwigenzurira ubwanyu. Uwo mwidegemvyo ujana n'ukubazwa ivyo mukora: ihanahanwa ryemewe akenshi ntirishobora gusubizwa inyuma, kandi nta biro vyo gufasha abakoresha uwo muhora biriho.

## Ni nde mu vy’ukuri abitse Bitcoin zanyu?

* **Kwibikira:** ni mwebwe mugenzura impfunguruzo
* Nta n'umwe ashobora kubagarurira bitcoin zanyu mutakaje urukurikirane rw'amajambo yo kugaruza ikotomoni n'igikoresho
* **Kubikizwa:** ishirahamwe ni ryo ribika impfunguruzo, nk'uko ibanki ibigenza
* Amafaranga mufise kuri konti ni isezerano iryo shirahamwe ribaha ryo kuzobariha; rirashobora kugabanya ayo mushobora kubikuza, guhagarika ibikorwa kuri konti, kwinjirirwa n'abasuma bo kuri internet, canke kwugara

Guhitamwo uburyo bwo kubika si ikigeragezo c'agaciro kanyu. Igihambaye ni ukumenya uburyo muriko murakoresha n'ico muriko murizigira.

Nimuhagarare nimba hari umuntu atuma ibintu bisa n’ivyihutirwa canke abasaba kubigira ibanga, abizeza inyungu ata kabuza, abasaba kubanza kurungika bitcoin, canke abasaba amajambo yanyu yo kugarura ikotomoni. ## Ivyigwa bine nyamukuru

* **Amahera ashingiye ku mategeko no ku kwizigirana.** Igitigiri c'amahera ashingiye ku ngingo ya Leta n'amategeko ayagenga bishingwa n'inzego nkeyi.
* **Bitcoin itanga amategeko atandukanye.** Igitigiri c'amahera kizwi na bose kandi gifise aho kigarukira, ashobora gukoreshwa na bose, kandi ico gitigiri gishobora kugenzurwa aho kucizigira gusa.
* **Umutekano uza imbere ya vyose.** Nimubanze mwige, mutangure n'udukeyi, mugenzure vyose, kandi ntimukigere musangiza abandi amajambo yo kugarukana ikotomoni yanyu.
* **Iyi ni intango gusa.** Intambwe imwe ikurikira itekanye irahagije.

## Impamyabumenyi ya Bitcoin

Intambwe ikurikira. Ibice vyayo bine bibafasha gutera iyindi ntambwe:

* Gutahura amafaranga
* Ingene Bitcoin ikora
* Gukoresha Bitcoin mu mutekano
* Bitcoin mu kibano canyu
