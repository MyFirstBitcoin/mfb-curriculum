## Kuchokera ku Zigoba za Nkhono Kupita ku Pepala

* Zigoba za nkhono, mchere, mikanda, ziweto, mkuwa, siliva, golide

## Ndalama za Fiat

* 1971: dziko la United States linathetsa kusinthanitsa mwalamulo madola ndi golide kwa maboma akunja ndi mabanki akuluakulu a mayikowo
* Masiku ano ndalama za mayiko ambiri ndi ndalama za fiat: zimaperekedwa ndi chilolezo cha boma, ndipo sizingawomboledwe ndi golide kapena chinthu china chilichonse chogulitsidwa
* _Fiat_ ndi liwu la Chilatini lotanthauza kuti "zichitike", ndipo nthawi zambiri limamveka ngati "mwa lamulo"
* Zimavomerezedwa chifukwa anthu, mabizinesi, mabanki, ndi maboma amazigwiritsa ntchito; malamulo amazipatsa udindo wa ndalama zovomerezeka zolipirira ngongole ndipo amafuna kuti zigwiritsidwe ntchito polipira misonkho

## Kukwera kwa Mitengo Sikukhudza Aliyense Mofanana

* Malipiro ndi mitengo zimasintha pa liwiro losiyanasiyana

Ndalama zimene zili mu akaunti yanu ya kubanki n'zosiyana ndi ndalama zimene muli nazo m'manja: ndi lonjezo la banki lakuti idzakulipirani. Nthawi zambiri lonjezo limeneli limakwaniritsidwa, koma zimadalira bankiyo ndi malamulo amene imatsatira.

# Kodi Bitcoin ndi chiyani?

Ndalama za digito zomwe sizilamulidwa ndi bungwe limodzi, zili ndi malire pa kuchuluka kwake, ndipo zingatumizidwe kuchokera kwa munthu wina kupita kwa wina popanda chilolezo cha banki.

## Vuto la Kugwiritsa Ntchito Ndalama Zomwezo Kawiri

**31 Okutobala 2008:** Satoshi Nakamoto amasindikiza _Bitcoin: Dongosolo la Ndalama Zamagetsi Lochokera kwa Munthu Kupita kwa Munthu_. **Januwale 2009:** netiweki imayamba kugwira ntchito.

Uthenga wa digito ndi wosavuta kukopera: ngati khobidi la digito likanatha kugwiritsidwa ntchito kawiri, silikanatha kugwira ntchito ngati ndalama. ## Yopanda Ulamuliro Wapakati · Kuchuluka Kwake Kuli ndi Malire · Munthu kwa Munthu

* **Zopanda ulamuliro wapakati:** palibe ofesi yapakati imene imatsegula maakaunti, kusintha ndalama zotsala m'maakaunti, kapena kuvomereza amene angalowe
* **Kuchuluka kuli ndi malire:** sipadzakhala bitcoin zoposa 21 miliyoni
* **Munthu ndi munthu:** tumizani kwa aliyense, kulikonse, popanda kupempha chilolezo ku ulamuliro wapakati

Palibe kampani imene ingaletse kugwiritsa ntchito bitcoin zimene mukuzilamulira nokha. Ufulu umenewu umabweranso ndi udindo: kutumiza ndalama kovomerezeka nthawi zambiri sikungabwezedwe, ndipo netiwekiyi ilibe malo opempherako thandizo.

<!-- notes: Kusakhala ndi ulamuliro wapakati sikutanthauza kuti aliyense ali ndi mphamvu zofanana zokhudza zisankho. Kumatanthauza kuti palibe wotenga nawo mbali mmodzi amene angasinthe kaundula wa zochitika za ndalama kapena malamulo a ndalama kwa aliyense. -->

## Malamulo Atatu Pamene Mukuphunzira

* **Phunzirani kaye.** Musachite zinthu chifukwa cha chisangalalo, mantha, kapena kukakamizidwa.
* **Yambani ndi zochepa.** Yesani zida zomwe simunazolowere ndi ndalama zomwe kutaya kwake sikungakuvutitseni.
* **Musangokhulupirira, Tsimikizirani.** Yang'anani munthuyo, njira yolipirira, adiresi, ndi kuchuluka kwa ndalama.


> **Callout – Gawani uthenga wolandirira ndalama, osagawana zinsinsi**
>
> Adiresi kapena pempho lolipira zingagawidwe. Mawu obwezeretsera chikwama kapena kiyi wachinsinsi siziyenera kugawidwa konse. Palibe mphunzitsi wovomerezeka, kampani ya zikwama zadijito, kapena wothandiza makasitomala amene angakufunseni zimenezi.

## Imani, Musagawane Chilichonse, Musatumize Chilichonse

Imani ngati wina akukukakamizani kuchita zinthu mwachangu kapena mwachinsinsi, akulonjeza phindu lotsimikizika, akukupemphani kuti mutumize bitcoin kaye, kapena akupempha mawu anu obwezeretsera chikwama. Dikirani kaye. Musagawane chilichonse. Musatumize chilichonse. Kenako tsimikizirani kudzera ku gwero limene mumalidziwa kale.

## Mfundo Zinayi Zofunika Kukumbukira

* **Ndalama zimadalira malamulo ndi kukhulupirirana.** Kuchuluka kwa ndalama za fiat ndi malamulo ake zimakhazikitsidwa ndi mabungwe ochepa.
* **Bitcoin imapereka malamulo osiyana.** Kuchuluka kwa ndalama kodziwika kwa anthu onse, kokhala ndi malire, komwe aliyense angagwiritse ntchito ndipo kungatsimikizidwe m'malo mongokukhulupirira.
* **Chitetezo chimabwera poyamba.** Phunzirani kaye, yambani ndi zochepa, tsimikizirani chilichonse, ndipo musauze aliyense mawu anu obwezeretsera chikwama.
* **Ichi ndi chiyambi chabe.** Sitepe imodzi yotsatira yotetezeka ndi yokwanira.


> **Callout – Nthawi yosinkhasinkha**
>
> Bwererani ku mafunso asanu oyambirira aja. Kodi mungayankhe bwanji tsopano?

## Dipuloma ya Bitcoin

Gawo lotsatira. Magawo ake anayi akukuthandizani kupita patsogolo:

* Kumvetsetsa ndalama
* Mmene Bitcoin imagwirira ntchito
* Kugwiritsa ntchito Bitcoin mosamala
* Bitcoin m’dera lanu

Pitirizani kukhala ndi chidwi chofuna kudziwa, kufunsa mafunso, ndi kuphunzira zambiri pa [**myfirstbitcoin.org**](http://myfirstbitcoin.org).

Tikuyembekezera kukuonani m’gulu lathu lotsatira la ophunzira.
