# Iyini i-Bitcoin?

## Inkinga Yokusebenzisa Imali Kabili

**31 kuMfumfu 2008:** Satoshi Nakamoto ushicilela _Bitcoin: Uhlelo Lwemali Ye-elekthronikhi Lontanga Ngontanga_. **UMasingana 2009:** inethiwekhi iyaqala.

Ulwazi lwedijithali kulula ukulukopisha: uma uhlamvu lwemali lwedijithali belungasetshenziswa kabili, belungeke lusebenze njengemali. Lena yinkinga yokusebenzisa imali efanayo kabili. ## Okungalawulwa Yisikhungo Esisodwa · Inani Elilinganiselwe · Kusuka Kumuntu Kuya Kumuntu

* **Ukungabi ngaphansi kwesikhungo esisodwa:** alikho ihhovisi eliyinhloko elivula ama-akhawunti, elishintsha amabhalansi, noma eligunyaza ukuthi ubani ongajoyina
* **Inani elilinganiselwe:** ngeke kube nama-bitcoin angaphezu kwezigidi ezingu-21
* **Phakathi kontanga:** thumelani kunoma ubani, noma kuphi, ngaphandle kokucela imvume esikhungweni esiyinhloko

Ayikho inkampani engamisa ukusetshenziswa kwe-bitcoin enizilawulela yona. Olunye uhlangothi lwaleyo nkululeko luwukuzibophezela: ngokuvamile akunakwenzeka ukuhlehlisa ukuthengiselana okuvumelekile, futhi alikho ihhovisi losizo lenethiwekhi.

Yimani uma othile eniphoqa ukuba niphuthume noma nigcine imfihlo, ethembisa inzuzo eqinisekisiwe, enicela ukuba nithumele i-bitcoin kuqala, noma ecela amagama enu okubuyisela isikhwama. Ake nithi ukuma. Ningabelani ngalutho. Ningathumeli lutho. Bese niqinisekisa ngomthombo enivele niwazi.

## Amaphuzu Amane Abalulekile

* **Imali incike emithethweni nasekwethembaneni.** Inani lemali ye-fiat nemithetho yayo kunqunywa yizikhungo ezimbalwa.
* **I-Bitcoin inikeza imithetho ehlukile.** Inani lemali elibekwe obala, elinomkhawulo, elifinyeleleka kunoma ubani, elingaqinisekiswa esikhundleni sokumane lithenjwe.
* **Ukuphepha kuza kuqala.** Fundani kuqala, qalani ngokuncane, qinisekisani konke, ningalokothi nabelane ngamagama enu okubuyisela isikhwama.
* **Lokhu kumane kuyisiqalo.** Isinyathelo esisodwa esilandelayo esiphephile sanele.


> **Callout – Isikhathi sokuzindla**

## Idiploma ye-Bitcoin

Isinyathelo esilandelayo. Amamojuli ayo amane aniqhubekisela phambili:

* Ukuqonda imali
* Indlela i-Bitcoin esebenza ngayo
* Ukusebenzisa i-Bitcoin ngokuphepha
* I-Bitcoin emphakathini wenu

Hlalani ninelukuluku lokwazi, qhubekani nibuza imibuzo, futhi nifunde okwengeziwe ku-[**myfirstbitcoin.org**](http://myfirstbitcoin.org).

Sibheke ngabomvu ukunibona eqenjini lethu elilandelayo labafundi.
