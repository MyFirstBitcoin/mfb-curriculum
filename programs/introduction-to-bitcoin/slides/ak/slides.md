## Nokwasɛm anaa Adwene?

* **Nokwasɛm:** "Wɔgye saa krataa sika yi de tua ɛtoɔ." Wobetumi ahwɛ sɛ ɛyɛ nokorɛ anaa.
* **Adwene:** "Yei ne sika a ɛyɛ papa pa ara." Ɛhia gyinapɛn ne adanseɛ.

## Nneɛma Mmiɛnsa a Sika Yɛ

* **Nsesayɛ adeɛ:** nkurɔfoɔ gye tom sɛ adeɛ a wɔde tua ka
* **Akontaabu susudeɛ:** wɔde kyerɛ nneɛma boɔ
* **Boɔ akoraeɛ:** ɛde boɔ firi ɛnnɛ kɔ daakye

Sika biara ntumi nkora boɔ so pɛpɛɛpɛ, nanso tumi a sika ahodoɔ bi wɔ de tɔ nneɛma no so te brɛoo sene afoforɔ deɛ.

## Efi Sedie So Kɔ Krataa So

* Ɛpo mu nkoradeɛ, nkyene, nhene, mmoa a wɔyɛn wɔn, kɔbere, dwetɛ, sika kɔkɔɔ
* Nnadeɛ a ɛsom bo: ɛho yɛ na, ɛkyɛ, wɔgye tom wɔ mmeaeɛ pii
* Nkrataa a wɔtumi de kɔgye dadeɛ dodorɔ bi a wɔakyerɛ
* Sika kɔkɔɔ gyinapɛn: sika susudua a mmara de abɔ sika kɔkɔɔ ho

Na ɛsono baabiara ne berɛ biara mmara.

## Aban Mmara Sika

* 1971: United States gyaee nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛma amannɔne aban ne wɔn sikakorabea titire tumi de dɔla sesa sikakɔkɔɔ wɔ aban kwan so no
* Ɛnnɛ, aman mu sika dodow no ara yɛ fiat sika: wɔde ba wɔ aban tumi ase, na wɔrentumi mfa nsesa sikakɔkɔɔ anaa aguadeɛ foforɔ biara
* _Fiat_ yɛ Latin asɛmfua a ɛkyerɛ "ma wɔnyɛ no saa", na wɔtaa te aseɛ sɛ "ɛnam ɔhyɛ so"
* Wɔgye tom ɛfiri sɛ nnipa, nnwuma, sikakorabea ne aban de di dwuma; mmara ma ɛyɛ sika a wɔgye tom de tua ka na ɛhwehwɛ sɛ wɔde tua ɛtoɔ

## Nnyinasoɔ Pii, Ɔpaw Baako

* Sika ne bosea dodoɔ a ɛkɔ soro
* Nneɛma a wɔde ma no mu ntwitwiram ne nneɛma a nkurɔfoɔ hwehwɛ no mu nsakraeɛ
* Sika a ɛsom bo so retew, ɛtoɔ, nneɛma a wɔyɛ ho ka

Adeɛ baako a ɛde ba no yɛ gyinaeɛ a wɔsi, na ɛnyɛ biribi a ɛba kwa: wɔ fiat sika nhyehyɛeɛ mu no, ahyehyɛdeɛ kakraa bi tumi ma sika ne bosea dodoɔ kɔ soro na wɔsesa nhyehyɛeɛ no mmara.

## Ahoyɛna ne Boɔ

* Mona Lisa mfonini ankasa no boɔ sen biara a wɔayɛ no sɛso: ɛno nko ara na ɛwɔ hɔ
* Ɛnyɛ adeɛ ho a ɛyɛ na nko ara na ɛma ɛnya boɔ, nanso sɛ nkurɔfoɔ pɛ adeɛ bi a, ɛho hia sɛ wɔhwɛ dodoɔ a ɛwɔ hɔ
* Sɛ sika dodoɔ kɔ soro ntɛmntɛm sen nneɛma a wɔde bɛtumi atɔ a, sika no mu baako biara taa tumi tɔ nneɛma kakra sen dada

## Nneɛma Boɔ Nkɔanim Nnya Obiara So Nkɛntɛnsoɔ Pɛ

* Sika a ɛwɔ nsam ne sika a wɔakora no tumi a ɛde tɔ nneɛma betumi so ate
* Wɔn a wɔwɔ agyapadeɛ a ne bo kɔ soro no betumi anya ahobammɔ bi
* Akatua ne nneɛma bo nsakraeɛ mma ntɛmntɛm pɛ

Sika a ɛwɔ mo sikakorabea akonta mu yɛ soronko wɔ sika a ɛhyɛ mo nsam ho: ɛyɛ sikakorabea no bɔhyɛ sɛ ɛbɛtua mo sika no. Mpɛn pii no, wɔdi saa bɔhyɛ no so, nanso ɛgyina sikakorabea no ne mmara a ɛkyerɛ sɛdeɛ ɛyɛ adwuma no so.

# Dɛn ne Bitcoin?

Dijitaal sika a ɛnhyɛ tumi baako ase, ne dodoɔ wɔ ano, na wɔtumi de firi obi hɔ kɔ obi nkyɛn tẽẽ a wɔnhia sikakorabea bi kwan.

## Tumi a wɔakyekyɛ mu · Dodow a ɛwɔ ano hyeɛ · Obi ne ne yɔnko ntam

* **Tumi nni beaeɛ baako:** adwumayɛbea titire biara nni hɔ a ɛbue akonta, sesa sika dodoɔ a ɛwɔ akonta mu, anaa ɛma kwan ma obi bɛka ho
* **Ne dodoɔ wɔ ano:** bitcoin dodoɔ remmoro ɔpepem 21 da
* **Onipa ne onipa ntam:** momfa nkɔma obiara, wɔ baabiara, a monsrɛ tumi titire bi kwan

Adwumakuo biara ntumi nsi bitcoin a mo ankasa modi so no ano. Saa ahofadie no de asɛdeɛ nso ba: mpɛn pii no, wɔrentumi nnan sika nkitahodie a ɛdi mmara so no akyi, na ntwamutam no nni mmoa dwumadibea biara.

## Bitcoin Wɔ Mfasoɔ ne Sintɔ

* Ne boɔ tumi sesa ntɛmntɛm
* Mpɛn pii no, wɔntumi nsiesie mfomsoɔ a wɔayɛ no bio
* Mmeaeɛ bebree no, wɔda so ara nnye ntom kɛseɛ
* Sɛ wɔde bedi dwuma ahobammɔ mu a, ɛhia sɛ wɔsua ho adeɛ kakra

Bitcoin de nneɛma mu a wɔhwehwɛ de si so dua ne obiara ankasa asɛdeɛ si ahotosoɔ a wɔwɔ wɔ ahyehyɛdeɛ mu no bi ananmu. Ɛnyi asiane mfiri hɔ.

## Hwan na Nokorɛm Ɔkura Mo Bitcoin?

* **Mo ankasa na mokora so:** mo na modi nsafeɛ no so

## Mmara Mmiɛnsa Bere a Moresua Adeɛ

* **Monsua adeɛ kan.** Mommma anigyeɛ, ehu anaa nhyɛsoɔ mma monnyɛ biribi.

## Asuadeɛ Anan

* **Sika gyina mmara ne ahotosoɔ so.** Ahyehyɛdeɛ kakraa bi na ɛkyerɛ fiat sika dodoɔ a ɛwɔ hɔ ne ɛho mmara.
