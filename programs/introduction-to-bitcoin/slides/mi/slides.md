## I mua i tō whakamahi i tētahi pūkoro

* **Ngā kī:** mā wai e whakahaere, māu, mā tētahi kamupene rānei?
* **Whakaora:** ka aha mēnā ka ngaro tō waea, ka ngaro rānei te kamupene?
* **Pūtake:** ko te paetukutuku whaimana, ko te rārangi rānei i te toa taupānga, kaua ko tētahi hononga i tukuna mai ki a koe e tētahi atu?
* **Waehere:** he tuwhera te pūtake, ā, e tiakina tonutia ana? (Ehara tēnei anake i te taunakitanga o te haumaru.)
* **Ngā utu:** he utu mō te tuku, te whiwhi, te tango moni rānei?
* **Ngā utunga:** mā te mekameka, mā Lightning, mā ngā mea e rua rānei?

## Ngā Akoranga Matua e Whā

* **E whakawhirinaki ana te moni ki ngā ture me te whakaponotanga.** Ka whakatauria te rahinga me ngā ture o te moni fiat e ētahi whakahaere ruarua.
* **He rerekē ngā ture ka tukuna e Bitcoin.** He rahinga moni tūmatanui, he mea whakawhāiti, e wātea ana ki te katoa, ā, ka taea te manatoko, kāore e mate ki te whakawhirinaki noa.
* **Ko te haumaru te mea tuatahi.** Me ako i te tuatahi, me tīmata ki te iti, me manatoko i ngā mea katoa, kaua rawa e tuari i tō koutou kīanga kākano.
* **He tīmatanga noa iho tēnei.** E ranea ana kia kotahi noa te hipanga haumaru e whai ake nei.


> **Callout – He wā hei huritao**
>
> Hoki atu ki ngā pātai e rima i te tīmatanga. He aha ā koutou whakautu ināianei?
