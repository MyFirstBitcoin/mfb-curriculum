# Yintoni Elandelayo?

###### Imali ixhomekeke kwimithetho nasekuthembaneni

Imali ye-fiat yenza kube lula ukuhlawula, kodwa ubungakanani bayo nemithetho yayo zimiselwa linani elincinci lamaziko, yaye amandla ayo okuthenga anokuhla ekuhambeni kwexesha.

###### IBitcoin ibonelela ngemithetho eyahlukileyo

Ukususela ngo-2009, iBitcoin ibonelele ngemali enobungakanani obaziwa luluntu nobulinganiselweyo, efumaneka kuye nabani na, nenokuqinisekiswa endaweni yokuyithemba nje. Ikwanazo nezinto ezifuna ukuqwalaselwa: ukuguquguquka kwexabiso, iimpazamo ezingenakulungiswa, noxanduva lomntu ngamnye.

###### Ukhuseleko luza kuqala

Fundani kuqala, qalani kancinci, nize niqinisekise yonke into. Ningaze nabelane nabani na ngebinzana lenu lembewu. Akuyomfuneko ukuba nenze yonke into ngaxeshanye: inyathelo elinye elilandelayo nelikhuselekileyo lanele.

###### Esi sisiqalo nje

Buyelani kwimibuzo emihlanu esekuqaleni. Beninokuphendula nithini ngoku?

Le [Diploma yeBitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/) linyathelo elilandelayo. Iimodyuli zayo ezine zinisa phambili, ukusuka ekuqondeni imali, ukuya kwindlela esebenza ngayo iBitcoin, ukuya ekusebenziseni iBitcoin ngokukhuselekileyo, ukuya kwiBitcoin ekuhlaleni kwenu. Hlalani ninomdla wokwazi, qhubekani nibuza imibuzo, nize nifunde ngakumbi ku- [myfirstbitcoin.org](https://myfirstbitcoin.org).
