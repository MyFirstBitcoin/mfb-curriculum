# Yintoni Engalunganga Ngemali Yethu?

Xa abantu besiva igama elithi ukunyuka kwamaxabiso, badla ngokucinga ngamaxabiso anyukayo.

**Ukunyuka kwamaxabiso** kukunyuka okuqhubekayo komgangatho wamaxabiso ngokubanzi. Elinye icala lako **ngamandla okuthenga**: oko kukuthi, ubungakanani bezinto ezinokuthengwa ngeyunithi yemali. Ukuba ixabiso lebhaskithi yezinto ezisisiseko linyuka lisuka kwiiyunithi zemali eziyi-100 liye kweziyi-110, ezo yunithi ziyi-100 azisakwazi ukuthenga yonke ibhaskithi.

Ukunyuka kwamaxabiso kunezizathu ezininzi, yaye izazi ngezoqoqosho ziyaxoxa ngokuba ngasinye sinagalelo elingakanani: ukwanda kwemali namatyala, ukuphazamiseka konikezelo lweempahla, utshintsho kwimfuno, ukwehla kwamandla emali, iirhafu, neendleko zemveliso. Esinye isizathu sifanele siqwalaselwe kuba sisigqibo esithathwayo, kungekhona into eyenzeka ngengozi: kwinkqubo yemali yefiat, amaziko ambalwa anokwandisa ubungakanani bemali namatyala aze atshintshe imithetho yaloo nkqubo.

Cingani ngento eyenza into ethile ixabiseke.

Umzobo wokuqala weMona Lisa uxabiseke ngaphezu kwayo nayiphi na ikopi yawo, kungekhona kuba ukhangeleka ubhetele, kodwa kuba ukho mnye kuphela. Ukunqaba kwento kukodwa akuyenzi ixabiseke, kodwa xa abantu befuna into ethile, ubungakanani obukhoyo bayo bubalulekile.

**Xa ubungakanani bemali busanda ngokukhawuleza kunobungakanani bezinto enokuzithenga, iyunithi nganye idla ngokuthenga okuncinane.**

Ukunyuka kwamaxabiso akuchaphazeli wonke umntu ngokulinganayo. Umntu ogcina ubutyebi bakhe ubukhulu becala njengemali ezinkozo okanye imali ayongileyo usenokuphulukana namandla okuthenga. Umntu onee-asethi ezinyuka ngexabiso usenokukhuseleka ngokuyinxenye. Imivuzo namaxabiso nazo zitshintsha ngezantya ezahlukileyo.

Imali ekwiakhawunti yenu yebhanki nayo yahlukile kwimali ezinkozo eniyiphetheyo. Ibhanki ayigcini yonke imali efakiweyo njengemali ezinkozo: ibolekisa ize ityale uninzi lwayo. **Ibhalansi yeakhawunti yebhanki sisithembiso sebhanki sokunihlawula.** Eso sithembiso sidla ngokuzalisekiswa, kodwa oko kuxhomekeke kwibhanki nakwimithetho eyilawulayo.

###### Kuzameni: ukuhlola amandla okuthenga

Khethani into enye eqhelekileyo ekuhlaleni kwenu, njengesonka, isithuthi sokuhamba, okanye i-airtime yefowuni. Bhalani phantsi ixabiso layo lanamhlanje. Buzani umntu ukuba yayixabisa malini kwiminyaka eliqela eyadlulayo, nize nijonge ingxelo ethembekileyo ukuba ninako. Ngaba ingeniso yenyuka ngesantya esifanayo? Yintoni enye enokuchaza olo tshintsho?

Akukho nanye kwezi zinto ethetha ukuba imali yefiat ayisebenzi. Yenza kube lula ukuhlawula, ibonelela ngeendlela zokuboleka imali ezibhetyebhetye, nangezixhobo zokujongana neentlekele. Kodwa oko kubhetyebhetye kukwadala umngcipheko: ubungakanani bayo nemithetho yayo zinokutshintsha, yaye abantu abayisebenzisayo abanalo ilizwi elikhulu kwezo zigqibo.
