# Yintoni imali?

**Imali sisixhobo sotshintshiselwano.**

Akukho mntu ufuna imali ngenxa yayo nje. Siyifuna ngenxa yezinto enokuzithenga. Iphepha lemali lisisiqwenga sephepha nje. Into eyenza libe luncedo kukuba abanye abantu baya kulamkela ukuze basinike izinto esizifunayo ngokwenene, kuba belindele ukuba nabanye baya kulamkela kamva.

Yile nto eyahlula imali kutshintshiselwano lweempahla.

Kwinkqubo yotshintshiselwano lweempahla, ukurhweba kusebenza kuphela xa abantu bobabini befuna oko omnye anako, ngexesha elinye nangemilinganiselo efanelekileyo. Iingcali zoqoqosho zikubiza oku ngokuba **kukungqamana kweminqweno yamacala omabini**. Ukuba nilima irayisi yaye nifuna izihlangu, umenzi wezihlangu usenokufuna ioli yokupheka, hayi irayisi. Imali iyayisombulula le ngxaki ngokwahlula ukuthengisa ekuthengeni: nithengisa irayisi yenu komnye umntu nize nithenge izihlangu komnye.

**Imali idla ngokwenza izinto ezintathu.**

Yona **sisixhobo sotshintshiselwano**: abantu bayayamkela njengentlawulo. Kwakhona, yona **yiyunithi yokubala**: amaxabiso achazwa ngayo, ngoko sinokuthelekisa ixabiso lezinto ezahlukeneyo. Kwaye yona **yindawo yokugcina ixabiso**: idlulisela ixabiso lanamhlanje kwixesha elizayo. Akukho mali igcina ixabiso ngokugqibeleleyo, kodwa ezinye iintlobo zemali ziphulukana namandla okuthenga ngokucotha ngakumbi kunezinye.

Ukutyhubela imbali, izinto ezininzi ziye zasetyenziswa njengemali: amaqokobhe, ityuwa, amaso, imfuyo, ubhedu, isilivere negolide. Izinyithi ezixabisekileyo zasetyenziswa ngokubanzi kuba zazinqabile, zihlala ixesha elide yaye zamkelwa ngokubanzi. Kodwa ukuthwala nokukhusela izinyithi kunzima, ngoko iibhanki zaqalisa ukukhupha amaxwebhu ephepha awayenokutshintshwa ngesixa sesinyithi esichaziweyo. Phantsi kweentlobo ezahlukeneyo **zenkqubo yomgangatho wegolide**, iyunithi yemali yayinxulunyaniswa ngokusemthethweni nesixa esithile segolide. Imithetho yayisahluka ngokwendawo nangokwexesha.

Ekuhambeni kwexesha, imali yaqalisa ukuxhomekeka ngakumbi kumaziko nakwizithembiso kunakwizinyithi. Ngo-1971, iUnited States yaphelisa ukutshintshwa ngokusemthethweni kweedola ngegolide ngoorhulumente bamanye amazwe neebhanki ezingoovimba. Namhlanje, iimali ezininzi zamazwe **yimali yefiat**: imali ekhutshwa phantsi kwegunya likarhulumente, ngokuqhelekileyo ngebhanki enguvimba, engenakutshintshwa ngesixa esimiselweyo segolide okanye sayo nayiphi na enye impahla yorhwebo. Igama elithi fiat lelesiLatini yaye lithetha ukuthi "makwenziwe", nto leyo edla ngokuqondwa njengokuthi "ngomyalelo".

Imali yefiat iyasebenza kuba abantu, amashishini, iibhanki noorhulumente bayayamkela. Imithetho idla ngokuyenza ibe yimali yokuhlawula eyamkelwa ngokusemthethweni ize ifune ukuba isetyenziswe ekuhlawuleni irhafu, yaye abantu bayayifuna kuba wonke umntu obangqongileyo uyayisebenzisa.

**Sasuka kwimali exhaswa yinto ethile saya kwimali exhaswa kukuthembela kumntu othile.**
