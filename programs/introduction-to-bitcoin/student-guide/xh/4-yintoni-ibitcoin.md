# Yintoni iBitcoin?

Ngaphambi kweBitcoin, ukuthumela imali kwi-intanethi kwakusoloko kuxhomekeke kumbutho othenjiweyo: ibhanki okanye inkampani yeentlawulo eyayigcina incwadi yeerekhodi zemali, ivume iiakhawunti, yaye yayinokulibazisa, ithintele okanye irhoxise intlawulo. Kangangamashumi eminyaka, iingcali zenzululwazi yeekhompyutha zazama ukwenza imali yedijithali engenagunya linye liyikhuphayo. Eyona nto yayinzima yayikukuba kulula ukukopa ulwazi lwedijithali. Ukuba ingqekembe yedijithali yayinokukotshwa ize isetyenziswe kabini, yayingenakusebenza njengemali. Oku kubizwa ngokuba **yingxaki yokuchitha imali enye kabini**.

Ngomhla wama-31 kuOktobha 2008, umntu okanye iqela elalisebenzisa igama elithi Satoshi Nakamoto lapapasha uxwebhu oluchaza isisombululo: _Bitcoin: Inkqubo Yemali Ye-elektroniki Ethunyelwa Ngokuthe Ngqo Phakathi Kwabantu_. Inethiwekhi yaqala ngoJanuwari 2009.

Bitcoin yimali yedijithali **enolawulo olusasaziweyo**, ibe **nonikezelo olulinganiselweyo**, kwaye inokuthunyelwa **ngokuthe ngqo phakathi kwabantu** ngaphandle kwemvume yebhanki.

Ukuba **nolawulo olusasaziweyo** kuthetha ukuba akukho ofisi esembindini enokwenza iiakhawunti, itshintshe imali eseleyo kuzo, okanye ivume ukuba ngubani ojoyinayo. Nabani na unokukhuphela aze ahlole isoftware enekhowudi evulelekileyo, yaye iikhompyutha ezininzi ezizimeleyo zigcina ikopi yerekhodi. Oku akuthethi ukuba wonke umntu unempembelelo elinganayo. Kuthetha ukuba akukho mthathi-nxaxheba omnye onokutshintsha incwadi yeerekhodi zemali okanye imithetho yemali yomntu wonke.

Ukuba **nonikezelo olulinganiselweyo** kuthetha ukuba akusayi kuze kubekho ngaphezu kwezigidi ezingama-21 ze-bitcoin. Ii-bitcoin ezintsha zikhutshwa ngokweshedyuli eyaziwa luluntu, yaye iikhompyutha ezizimeleyo ezibizwa ngokuba ziinodi ziyala nayiphi na ibhloko eyaphula loo mthetho. Lo mda uyathembeka kuba nabani na unokuwuqinisekisa. Noko ke, awungomthetho wendalo: uhlala usebenza kuba abathathi-nxaxheba beqhubeka bewunyanzelisa. Yaye unikezelo olulinganiselweyo aluqinisekisi ukuba ixabiso liza kunyuka. Ixabiso lixhomekeke kwimfuno nakunikezelo.

Ukusebenza **ngokuthe ngqo phakathi kwabantu** kuthetha ukuba ningathumela i-bitcoin kuye nabani na, naphi na, ngaphandle kokuvula iakhawunti kwinethiwekhi yeBitcoin okanye nicele imvume kwigunya elisembindini. Akukho nkampani inokuthintela ukusetyenziswa kwe-bitcoin eniyilawulayo ngokwenu. Kwelinye icala laloo nkululeko kukho uxanduva: ngokuqhelekileyo akunakwenzeka ukurhoxisa intengiselwano esemthethweni, yaye akukho ziko loncedo lenethiwekhi.

Enye ingcamango ephosakeleyo yeyokuba Bitcoin ayifikeleleki kuba i-bitcoin enye iyabiza. **Akuyomfuneko ukuba nithenge okanye nisebenzise i-bitcoin epheleleyo.** I-bitcoin enye yahlulahlulwa ibe ziiyunithi ezincinane ezili-100 000 000 ezibizwa ngokuba zii-**satoshi**, okanye ii-**sat**. Ukuba nee-sat ezili-100 000 nokuba ne-bitcoin eyi-0,001 kuchaza isixa esifanayo.

Inethiwekhi ixhomekeke kubathathi-nxaxheba abohlukeneyo: **abasebenzisi** abathumela, bamkele baze bagcine i-bitcoin; **iinodi** ezihlola yonke intengiselwano nebhloko ukuba iyahambelana na nemithetho; kunye **nabasebenzi basemgodini** abakhuphisanayo ukongeza ibhloko entsha kwirekhodi malunga nemizuzu elishumi nganye. Loo rekhodi, ebizwa ngokuba **yityathanga leebhloko**, yeyoluntu yaye ibonakala kuye nabani na, kwaye kubiza kakhulu ukuyibhala ngokutsha.

Inqaku malunga nopelo: **Bitcoin** eno-B omkhulu idla ngokuthetha inethiwekhi okanye inkqubo. **bitcoin** eno-b omncinane ithetha imali ngokwayo.

Bitcoin ikwanazo izinto ekufuneka nizincamile ukuze nifumane iinzuzo zayo. Ixabiso layo linokutshintsha ngokukhawuleza. Iimpazamo zidla ngokuba zezisisigxina. Ukwamkelwa kwayo kusalinganiselwe kwiindawo ezininzi, yaye ukuyisebenzisa ngokukhuselekileyo kufuna ukufunda okuthile. Bitcoin ithabathelisa inxalenye yokuthembela kumaziko ngokuqinisekisa nangokuthwala uxanduva lomntu ngamnye. Ayiwususi umngcipheko.
