# Niyisebenzisa njani iBitcoin?

Le **walethi yeBitcoin** sisixhobo, ngokuqhelekileyo esiyi-app, esinivumela ukuba namkele, nijonge, nize nithumele i-bitcoin. I-bitcoin yenu ayigcinwanga ngaphakathi kwifowuni yenu: ibhalwe kwi-blockchain. Iwalethi ilawula **izitshixo** ezinivumela ukuba ningqine ukuba yeyenu nize nigunyazise intlawulo.

Ngoko umbuzo wokuqala ngayo nayiphi na iwalethi ngulo: **ngubani olawula izitshixo?**

###### Ngubani kanye kanye ogcina i-bitcoin yenu?

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/f93df0cc4a2ad82b3410a9ad4c3273d1b760f8ea-1774x887.svg)

Xa nisebenzisa **iwalethi enizigcinela ngokwenu izitshixo zayo**, nini abalawula izitshixo.

Xa niyiseta, inibonisa **ibinzana lembewu** (elikwabizwa ngokuba libinzana lokubuyisela): uluhlu lwamagama alandelelaniswe ngendlela ethile, adla ngokuba li-12 okanye angama-24. Linokuphinda lenze izitshixo zenu kwesinye isixhobo. Nabani na onalo unokuthatha i-bitcoin yenu. Ibinzana lembewu aliyondlela yokugcina ikopi yogcino kuphela: linika ufikelelo olupheleleyo.

Likhuseleni:

* libhaleni ngokuchanekileyo ephepheni xa nisenza iwalethi;
* ligcineni lingekho kwi-intanethi, liyimfihlo, nakwindawo elingayi kulahleka okanye lonakale kuyo;
* ningaze nilifote, nilithumele nge-imeyile, niligcine kumanqaku agcinwe efini, okanye nilithumele kumyalezo;
* ningaze nilichwetheze kwiwebhusayithi.

Ukuba nilahlekelwa libinzana lenu lembewu kunye nesixhobo senu, akukho mntu unokunibuyisela i-bitcoin yenu.

Xa nisebenzisa **iwalethi enezitshixo ezigcinwa yinkampani**, inkampani inigcinela izitshixo, ngendlela efana neyebhanki. Ibhalansi yenu sisithembiso senkampani sokunihlawula. Indlela yokuyiseta iqhelekile yaye nisenokufumana inkxaso, kodwa nixhomekeke kuloo nkampani: inokumisela imida ekutsaleni imali, imise ukusetyenziswa kweakhawunti, ihlaselwe ngabarhwaphilizi beekhompyutha, okanye ivale.


| Umbuzo | Izitshixo zigcinwa yinkampani | Izitshixo nizigcinela ngokwenu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ngubani ogcina izitshixo? | Yinkampani | Nini |
| Ngubani ojongene nokubuyisela? | Yinkampani, ngeakhawunti yenu | Nini, nisebenzisa ibinzana lenu lembewu |
| Eyona nzuzo noko kuncanywayo ngenxa yayo | Ukusebenziseka lula nenkxaso, kodwa nixhomekeke kwinkampani | Ulawulo oluthe ngqo, kodwa ninoxanduva lweekopi zogcino neempazamo |


Ibinzana eliluncedo kwihlabathi leBitcoin: **ukuba izitshixo ayizozenu, iingqekembe ayizozenu.** Ukukhetha indlela yokugcina izitshixo akulovavanyo lokufaneleka kwenu. Okubalulekileyo kukwazi ukuba nisebenzisa oluphi uhlobo nokuba yintoni eniyithembileyo.

I-My First Bitcoin ayicebisi ziwalethi zithile. Izixhobo, imirhumo nabanini ziyatshintsha, yaye oko kulungele olunye uluntu kusenokungalulungeli olunye. Buzani utitshala omthembileyo ukuba abantu basekuhlaleni basebenzisa ntoni, nize nizihlolele la manqaku ngaphambi kokusebenzisa nayiphi na iwalethi:

* **Izitshixo:** ngubani olawula izitshixo, nini okanye yinkampani?
* **Ukubuyisela:** kwenzeka ntoni xa ifowuni yenu ilahlekile okanye inkampani ingasekho?
* **Umthombo:** niyikhuphele kwiwebhusayithi esemthethweni okanye kwiphepha layo kwivenkile yee-app, kungekhona kwikhonkco enilithunyelelwe ngomnye umntu?
* **Ikhowudi:** ingaba ikhowudi yayo ivulelekile yaye ihlaziywa rhoqo? (Ukuvuleka kwekhowudi kuphela akungqini ukuba isixhobo sikhuselekile.)
* **Iindleko:** yeyiphi imirhumo ehlawulwayo xa kuthunyelwa, kusamkelwa, okanye kutsalwa imali?
* **Iintlawulo:** ingaba iyazixhasa iintlawulo enizifunayo, ezenziwa kwi-blockchain okanye nge-Lightning?

I-Bitcoin ineendlela ezimbini eziqhelekileyo zokuhlawula. **Kwi-blockchain** iintlawulo zirekhodwa ngqo kwi-blockchain. **I-Lightning** yinethiwekhi yeentlawulo eyakhelwe phezu kwe-Bitcoin ukwenzela iintlawulo ezikhawulezayo, ezincinci nezenziwa rhoqo, njengokuthenga ikofu. Isipaji se-Lightning naso sinokugcinelwa omnye umntu okanye silawulwe ngumnini waso.

Imithetho emithathu iya kunigcina nikhuselekile ngoxa nifunda:

1. **Fundani kuqala.** Musani ukwenza izinto ngenxa yemincili, uloyiko okanye uxinzelelo.
1. **Qalani ngemali encinci.** Zamani izixhobo eningaziqhelanga ngemali eninokukwazi ukumelana nokulahlekelwa yiyo.
1. **Musani Ukuthemba, Qinisekisani.** Jongani umntu, indlela yokuhlawula, idilesi nemali ngaphambi kokuba nigunyazise intlawulo.

**Yabelanani ngeenkcukacha zokwamkela imali, ningaze nabelane ngeemfihlo.** Idilesi okanye isicelo sentlawulo sinokwabelwana ngaso. Ibinzana lembewu okanye isitshixo sabucala masingaze kwabelwane ngaso. Akukho titshala, nkampani yezipaji okanye msebenzi wenkxaso osemthethweni oya kuze acele loo nkcazelo.

Yekani ukuba umntu wenza ngathi kufuneka nikhawuleze okanye nigcine imfihlo, uthembisa imbuyekezo eqinisekisiweyo, unicela ukuba nithumele i-bitcoin kuqala, okanye ucela ibinzana lenu lembewu. **Nqumamani. Musani ukwabelana nangantoni na. Musani ukuthumela nantoni na.** Emva koko qinisekisani ngomthombo enisele niwazi.
