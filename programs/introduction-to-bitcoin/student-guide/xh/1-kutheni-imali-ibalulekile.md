# Kutheni Imali Ibalulekile?

Abantu abaninzi abakhe banqumame ukuze bacinge ngemali.

Bengacingi ngokuyifumana okanye ngokuyisebenzisa, koko becinga ngokwenene ngokuba iyintoni na nendlela esebenza ngayo.

**Sisebenzisa imali yonke imihla, kodwa asifane sibuze ukuba isebenza njani.**

Phambi kokuba niqhubeke nifunda, khanithathe umzuzwana nicinge ngale mibuzo mihlanu. Bhalani impendulo emfutshane kumbuzo ngamnye ukuba ninako:

1. Kutheni abantu befuna imali?
1. Yintoni eyenza into ibe yimali?
1. Ngubani onokudala okanye alawule imali eniyisebenzisayo?
1. Kutheni imali eniyisebenzisayo inexabiso?
1. Ngaba imali yenu ithenga umlinganiselo ofanayo wezinto namhlanje nalowo yayiwuthenga ngaphambili?

Zigcineni iimpendulo zenu. Buyelani kuzo ekugqibeleni. Esi sikhokelo sisenokomeleza ezinye zeembono zenu size sitshintshe ezinye.

Njengoko nifunda, qaphelani umahluko phakathi kwenyani noluvo. "Eli phepha lemali lamkelwa ukuhlawula irhafu" yinyani enokuqinisekiswa. "Olu lolona hlobo lwemali lubalaseleyo" sisigqibo esifuna imilinganiselo yokugweba nobungqina.

Esi sikhokelo sisekelwe kwimibuzo emithandathu: kutheni imali ibalulekile, iyintoni na, yintoni enokonakala ngayo, yintoni iBitcoin, ingasetyenziswa njani ngokukhuselekileyo, nokuba yintoni elandelayo.

Senzelwe ukufundisa kuphela. Asiniki ngcebiso ngezemali, ngotyalo-mali, ngezomthetho okanye ngerhafu, yaye asinixeleli ukuba nithenge, nithengise okanye nigcine nantoni na.
