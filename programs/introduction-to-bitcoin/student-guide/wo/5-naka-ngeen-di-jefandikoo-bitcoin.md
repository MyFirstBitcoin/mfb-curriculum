# Naka ngeen di jëfandikoo Bitcoin?

Ab **dencukaayu Bitcoin** jumtukaay la, lu ëpp ab jëfekaay, buy tax ngeen man a jot, seet ak yónnee bitcoin. Deesu denc seen bitcoin ci biir seen telefon: dañu koy bind ci blockchain bi. Dencukaay bi mooy saytu **caabi yi** leen di may ngeen firndeel ne yéen a ko moom te maye ndigal ngir fay.

Kon laaj bu jëkk ci bépp dencukaay mooy: **kan moo yor caabi yi?**

###### Kan moo yor seen bitcoin ci dëgg?

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/f93df0cc4a2ad82b3410a9ad4c3273d1b760f8ea-1774x887.svg)

Ak ab **dencukaay bu ngeen yor caabi yi seen bopp**, yéen a yor caabi yi.

Bu ngeen koy samp, dafay leen won ab **waxyu cosaan** (ñu koy woowe itam waxyu delloo): ab limu baat yu toftaloo, lu ëpp 12 walla 24. Man na sosaat seen caabi yi ci beneen jumtukaay. Képp ku ko am man na jël seen bitcoin. Waxyu cosaan wi du sotti bu ñuy denc rekk: dafay may sañ-sañ bu mat.

Sàmmleen ko:

* bindleen ko bu baax ci kayit bi ngeen di sos dencukaay bi;
* dencleen ko fu dul jokkoo ak internet, te sutura, ci barab bu mu dul réer walla yàqu;
* buleen ko mukk nataal, yónnee ci imeel, denc ci kàdduy xalaat ci niir wi, walla yónnee ci ab bataaxal;
* buleen ko mukk bind ci ab dalub web.

Su seen waxyu cosaan ak seen jumtukaay réeree, kenn du man a delloo seen bitcoin ngir yéen.

Ak ab **dencukaay bu ab antarpiriis yor caabi yi**, ab antarpiriis moo leen di yoreel caabi yi, ni ab bànk tuuti. Xaalis bi feeñ ci seen kont mooy dige bi antarpiriis bi def ne dina leen fay. Samp gi dafa nirook li ngeen miin, te man ngeen am ndimbal, waaye antarpiriis boobu ngeen wéeru: man na yamale génne xaalis, tëj kont, ñu song ko ci wàllu informatik, walla mu taxaw liggéey.


| Laaj | Antarpiriis bi moo yor caabi yi | Yéen a yor caabi yi |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kan moo yor caabi yi? | Antarpiriis bi | Yéen |
| Kan mooy saytu delloo gi? | Antarpiriis bi, jaarale ko ci seen kont | Yéen, jaarale ko ci seen waxyu cosaan |
| Njariñ ak wareef yi gën a am solo | Yombal ak ndimbal, waaye antarpiriis bi ngeen wéeru | Yéen ci seen bopp a yor lépp, waaye yéen a war a saytu sotti yi ngeen denc ak njuumte yi |


Ab wax ju am njariñ ci àddinay Bitcoin: **su caabi yi du yu ngeen moom, kon xaalis bi du bu ngeen moom.** Tànn anam gi ñuy yore seen alal du natt seen njariñ. Li am solo mooy xam ban xeet ngeen di jëfandikoo ak li ngeen wóolu.

My First Bitcoin du digle ay dencukaay yu mu tudd. Jumtukaay yi, fay yi ak boroom yi dañuy soppeeku, te li méngoo ak ab mbooloo man na bañ a méngoo ak beneen. Laajleen ab jàngalekat bu ñu wóolu li nit ñi di jëfandikoo ci seen gox, te seetleen yéen ci seen bopp mbir yii bala ngeen di jëfandikoo benn dencukaay:

* **Caabi yi:** kan moo yor caabi yi, yéen walla ab antarpiriis?
* **Delloo:** lan mooy xew su seen telefon réeree walla antarpiriis bi nekkatul?
* **Cosaan:** ndax ci dalub web bu ofisyeel bi walla ci xëtu jëfekaay bi ci jaayukaayu jëfekaay yi ngeen ko yebee, te du ci lëkkalekaay bu leen kenn yónnee?
* **Kod:** ndax kod bi ubbeeku na ñépp te ñuy ko toppatoo bu baax? (Kod bu ubbeeku ñépp rekk du firndeel ne ab jumtukaay wóor na.)
* **Fay yi:** ban fay lañuy laaj ngir yónnee, jot walla génne xaalis?
* **Fey yi:** ndax dafay nangu fey yi ngeen soxla, ci blokseen bi walla ci Lightning?

Bitcoin am na ñaari anam yu ñu faral di jëfandikoo ngir fey. **Ci blokseen bi** lañuy bind fey yi te jaarul fenn. **Lightning** ab lëkkalekaayu fey la bu ñu tabax ci kaw Bitcoin ngir fey yu gaaw, yu tuuti te bari yoon, niki jënd benn kaas kafe. Dencukaayu Lightning itam, kenn mën na ko sàmmal boroom bi, walla boroom bi di ko sàmmal boppam.

Ñetti sàrt yii dinañu leen aar bi ngeen di jàng:

1. **Jëkkleen a jàng.** Buleen jëf ndax bànneex bu ëpp, ragal walla ñu di leen gàllankoor.
1. **Tambalileen ci lu tuuti.** Nattu leen jumtukaay yi ngeen xamul ak xaalis bu su leen réeree, du leen lor.
1. **Buleen gëm rekk, seetleen ba wóor.** Seetleen nit ki, anamu fey gi, màkkaay bi ak limu xaalis bi ba wóor, bala ngeen di nangu ab fey.

**Séddooleen xibaar yi ñuy jëfandikoo ngir ngeen jot xaalis, waaye buleen séddoo mukk ay kumpa.** Mën ngeen séddoo ab màkkaay walla ab laaju fey. Warulees a séddoo mukk ab mbooloom baati njàlbéen walla ab caabi bu sutura. Benn jàngalekat, këru dencukaay walla liggéeykatu ndimbal bu jub du leen ko laaj mukk.

Taxawleen su kenn di leen gaawal walla di leen sant ngeen nëbb li ngeen di def, di leen dig dugal xaalis gu wóor, di leen laaj ngeen jëkk a yónnee bitcoin, walla di leen laaj seen mbooloom baati njàlbéen. **Dal-leen. Buleen séddoo dara. Buleen yónnee dara.** Ba noppi, seetleen ba wóor ci benn bëtu xibaar bu ngeen xamoon ba noppi.
