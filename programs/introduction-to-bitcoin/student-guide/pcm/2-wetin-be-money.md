# Wetin Be Money?

**Money na tool for exchange.**

Nobody want money just to get money. We want am because of wetin e fit buy. Paper money na just piece of paper. Wetin make am useful na say other people go accept am in exchange for things wey we really want, because dem expect say other people go accept am later.

Na this one make money different from trade by barter.

For trade by barter, trading dey work only when both people want wetin the other person get, for the same time and for quantity wey suit both of dem. Economists dey call this one **when wetin two people want match wetin each of dem get**. If una dey grow rice and una need shoes, the shoemaker fit want cooking oil, no be rice. Money dey solve this problem by separating selling from buying: una go sell una rice to one person and buy shoes from another person.

**Money normally dey do three things.**

E na **wetin people dey use for exchange**: people dey accept am as payment. E na **unit wey people dey use measure value**: dem dey state prices with am, so we fit compare the value of different things. And e na **wetin people dey use store value**: e dey carry value from today enter future. No money dey store value perfectly, but the buying power of some kinds of money dey reduce more slowly than others.

Throughout history, plenty things don serve as money: shells, salt, beads, farm animals, copper, silver, and gold. People begin use precious metals for plenty places because dem scarce, dem dey last, and plenty people dey accept dem. But to carry and protect metal hard, so banks begin issue papers wey give the holder right to collect one stated quantity of metal. Under different versions of **sistem wey tie money value to gold**, law tie one unit of currency to one quantity of gold. The rules differ from place to place and from one period to another.

As time dey go, money begin depend more on institutions and promises than on metal. For 1971, the United States stop the official exchange wey allow foreign governments and central banks change dollars for gold. Today, most countries their currencies na **money wey government declare as money**: currency wey dem issue under government authority, normally through central bank, wey person no fit exchange for one fixed quantity of gold or any other commodity. The word fiat na Latin for "make dem do am", wey people often understand as "by decree".

Money wey government declare as money dey work because people, businesses, banks, and governments dey accept am. Laws normally dey make am money wey dem legally recognise for payment and require people to use am pay tax, and people want am because everybody around dem dey use am.

**We move from money wey something dey back, to money wey trust for somebody dey back.**
