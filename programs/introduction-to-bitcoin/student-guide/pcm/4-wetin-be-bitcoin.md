# Wetin be Bitcoin?

Before Bitcoin, to send money online always depend on organization wey people trust: bank or payment company wey dey keep record of transactions, approve accounts, and fit delay, block, or reverse payment. For many decades, computer scientists try to create digital money wey no get one central body wey dey issue am. The hard part na say digital information easy to copy. If person fit copy digital coin and spend am two times, e no go fit work as money. Dem dey call this one **problem of spending the same money two times**.

On 31 October 2008, one person or group wey use the name Satoshi Nakamoto publish one paper wey explain solution: _Bitcoin: Electronic Cash System from Person to Person_. The network start for January 2009.

Bitcoin na digital money wey **no dey under one central body's control**, wey get **supply wey get limit**, and wey person fit send **directly to another person** without bank permission.

As e **no dey under one central body's control** mean say no central office dey wey fit create accounts, change how much dey inside accounts, or approve who fit join. Anybody fit download and check the software wey its source code open to everybody, and plenty independent computers dey keep copy of the record. This one no mean say everybody get equal power to influence things. E mean say no single participant fit change the transaction record or the money rules for everybody.

To get **supply wey get limit** mean say bitcoin no go ever pass 21 million. Dem dey release new bitcoin according to timetable wey everybody fit see, and independent computers wey dem dey call nodes dey reject any block wey break that rule. People fit trust the limit because anybody fit check am. But no be law of nature: e dey hold because participants continue to enforce am. And supply wey get limit no guarantee say price go rise. Price depend on how people want buy am and how much dey available.

As e dey work **directly from person to person** mean say una fit send bitcoin to anybody, anywhere, without opening account with the Bitcoin network or asking any central authority for approval. No company fit freeze bitcoin wey una control by unaself. The other side of that freedom na responsibility: normally, person no fit reverse valid transaction, and no help desk dey for the network.

One wrong idea na say Bitcoin no dey within reach because one bitcoin cost plenty. **Una no need to buy or use one whole bitcoin.** One bitcoin fit divide into 100,000,000 smaller units wey dem dey call **satoshis**, or **sats**. To get 100,000 sats and to get 0.001 bitcoin na the same amount.

The network depend on different participants: **people wey dey use am** wey dey send, receive, and hold bitcoin; **nodes** wey dey check every transaction and block to make sure say dem follow the rules; and **miners** wey dey compete to add new block to the record about every ten minutes. That record, wey be the **blockchain**, dey open for anybody to see, and to rewrite am dey cost plenty.

Small note about spelling: **Bitcoin** wey start with capital B normally mean the network or the system. **bitcoin** wey start with small b mean the money itself.

Bitcoin still get advantages and disadvantages wey person need to weigh. Its price fit change quick. Normally, mistake wey person make no fit reverse. Plenty places never dey accept am well, and to use am safely, person need to learn some things. Bitcoin replace some of the trust wey people put for institutions with checking to confirm things and taking personal responsibility. E no remove risk.
