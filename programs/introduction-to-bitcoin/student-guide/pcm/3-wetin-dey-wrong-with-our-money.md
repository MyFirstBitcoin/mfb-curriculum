# Wetin Dey Wrong With Our Money?

When people hear the word inflation, na price wey dey go up dem usually dey think of.

**Inflation for price** na when the general level of prices dey rise steadily. The other side na **power to buy things**: how much one unit of money fit buy. If the price of one basket of basic goods rise from 100 unit of money to 110, that same 100 unit no go fit buy the full basket again.

Plenty things dey cause inflation, and economists dey debate how much each one dey contribute: increase for money and credit, wahala wey disrupt supply, changes for demand, currency wey dey lose value, taxes, and cost to produce things. One cause need special attention because na choice, no be accident: for fiat system, small number of institutions fit increase the supply of money and credit and change the rules wey guide am.

Think about wetin dey make something get value.

The original Mona Lisa get more value pass any copy of am, no be because e fine pass, but because na only one original dey. Scarcity alone no dey give something value, but when people want something, how much of am dey available matter.

**When the supply of money dey grow faster than the things wey e fit buy, each unit go tend to buy less.**

Inflation no dey affect everybody the same way. Person wey keep most of im money as cash or savings fit lose power to buy things. Person wey get assets wey their price dey rise fit get some protection. Wages and prices no dey adjust at the same speed too.

The money for your bank account still different from cash wey dey your hand. Bank no dey keep every deposit as cash: e dey lend out and invest most of am. **The balance for your bank account na the bank promise to pay you.** The bank usually dey keep that promise, but e depend on the bank and the rules wey guide am.

###### Try am: check your power to buy things

Choose one everyday thing for your community, like bread, transport, or phone credit. Write down how much e cost today. Ask somebody how much e cost some years ago, then check record wey you fit trust if you fit. Income rise at the same rate? Wetin else fit explain the change?

All this no mean say fiat money no dey work. E dey make payment easy, allow credit to adjust as need change, and provide ways to respond when serious wahala happen. But that same ability to adjust dey bring risk: the supply and rules fit change, and the people wey dey use am no get much say.

**Na why people dey look for other options, and na why dem create Bitcoin.**
