# Why Money Dey Important?

Plenty people no dey ever stop to think about money.

No be how to earn am or spend am, but to really think about wetin e be and how e dey work.

**We dey use money every day, but we no dey often ask how e dey work.**

Before you continue to read, take small time think about these five questions. Write short answer for each one if you fit:

1. Why people need money?
1. Wetin dey make something become money?
1. Who fit create or control the money wey you dey use?
1. Why the money wey you dey use get value?
1. Your money fit buy the same quantity of things today as e fit buy before?

Keep your answers. Come back to them when you reach the end. This guide fit make some of your views stronger and change others.

As you dey read, take note of the difference between fact and opinion. "Dem dey accept this money note to pay tax" na fact wey person fit check. "This na the best kind of money" na conclusion wey need clear standards and evidence.

This guide focus on six questions: why money matter, wetin e be, wetin fit go wrong with am, wetin Bitcoin be, how to use am safely, and wetin go come next.

Na only for learning. E no dey give advice about money matters, investment, law, or tax, and e no dey tell you to buy, sell, or keep anything.
