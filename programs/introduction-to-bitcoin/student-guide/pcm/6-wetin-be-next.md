# Wetin Be Next?

###### Money dey depend on rules and trust

Fiat money dey make payment easy, but na small number of institutions dey decide how much go dey and di rules wey e go follow, and wetin e fit buy fit reduce as time dey go.

###### Bitcoin get different rules

Since 2009, Bitcoin don provide money wey get supply limit wey everybody fit see, wey anybody fit use, and wey dem fit check instead of just to trust am. E still get things wey person must weigh: price wey dey go up and down, mistakes wey person no fit reverse, and responsibility wey person must carry by demself.

###### Safety na di first thing

Make una learn first, start small, and check everything well. Make una never share una seed phrase. Una no need to do everything at once: one safe next step don do.

###### Na just di beginning be this

Make una go back to di five questions for di beginning. Wetin una go answer now?

Di [Bitcoin Diploma](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/) na di next step. Di four modules inside am go carry una further, from understanding money, to how Bitcoin dey work, to how to use Bitcoin safely, to Bitcoin for una community. Make una continue to get mind to know more, continue to ask questions, and learn more for [myfirstbitcoin.org](https://myfirstbitcoin.org).
