# How You Go Take Use Bitcoin?

One **Bitcoin wallet** na tool, wey mostly be app, wey una fit use receive, check, and send bitcoin. Una bitcoin no dey inside una phone: na for blockchain dem record am. Di wallet dey manage di **key dem** wey una fit use prove say di bitcoin na una own and give permission for payment.

So di first question about any wallet na: **who dey control di key dem?**

###### Who really dey hold una bitcoin?

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/f93df0cc4a2ad82b3410a9ad4c3273d1b760f8ea-1774x887.svg)

With one **wallet wey una dey hold di key dem by unaself**, na una dey control di key dem.

When una set am up, e go show una one **seed phrase** (wey dem still dey call recovery phrase): na list of words wey follow particular order, mostly 12 or 24 words. E fit create una key dem again for another device. Anybody wey get am fit take una bitcoin. Di seed phrase no be just backup: e dey give full access.

Protect am:

* write am down correctly for paper when una create di wallet;
* keep am away from internet, keep am private, and put am where e no go lost or spoil;
* no ever snap am, send am through email, save am for cloud notes, or send am inside message;
* no ever type am inside any website.

If una lose una seed phrase and una device, nobody fit recover una bitcoin for una.

With one **wallet wey company dey hold di key dem**, na company dey hold di key dem for una, small like bank. Di balance wey una see na di company promise to pay una. Di setup na something wey una fit don sabi, and help fit dey available, but una dey depend on dat company: e fit limit how much una fit withdraw, freeze account, suffer hacker attack, or close down.


| Question | Company dey hold di key dem | Una dey hold di key dem by unaself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who dey hold di key dem? | Di company | Una |
| Who dey handle recovery? | Di company, through una account | Una, through una seed phrase |
| Di main gain and wetin una give up | Ease of use and help, but una dey depend on di company | Direct control, but na una responsible for backup dem and any mistake |


One useful saying for di Bitcoin world: **if di key dem no be una own, di coin dem no be una own.** To choose who go hold di key dem no be test of una worth. Wetin matter na to know which kind una dey use and wetin una dey put una trust in.

My First Bitcoin no dey recommend any particular wallet. Tool dem, fee dem, and owner dem dey change, and wetin fit one community fit no fit another one. Ask teacher wey una trust wetin people dey use for una area, and check these things by unaself before una use any wallet:

* **Key dem:** who dey control di key dem, na una or na company?
* **Recovery:** wetin go happen if una phone lost or di company disappear?
* **Where e come from:** una download am from di official website or di app page for app store, no be from link wey somebody send una?
* **Code:** di source code dey open for everybody to see, and dem dey maintain am regularly? (Say source code dey open alone no prove say tool safe.)
* **Cost dem:** which fee dem una go pay to send, receive, or withdraw?
* **Payment:** e dey support di kind payment wey you need, on-chain or Lightning?

Bitcoin get two common ways to pay. **On-chain** payment dey enter record directly for blockchain. **Lightning** na payment network wey dem build on top Bitcoin for payment wey fast, small, and dey happen often, like to buy coffee. Lightning wallet fit also be di kind wey another person dey control for you or di kind wey you dey control by yourself.

Three rules go keep you safe as you dey learn:

1. **Learn first.** No take action because of excitement, fear, or pressure.
1. **Start small.** Use amount wey you fit afford to lose take try tools wey you never sabi.
1. **No Just Trust, Verify.** Check di person, di payment method, di address, and di amount before you approve payment.

**Share information for receiving money, no ever share secrets.** You fit share address or payment request. No ever share your seed phrase or private key. No genuine teacher, wallet company, or support worker go ever ask you for am.

Stop if anybody dey rush you or tell you to keep things secret, promise profit wey dem say must surely come, ask you to send bitcoin first, or ask for your seed phrase. **Pause. No share anything. No send anything.** Then use source wey you already sabi take check.
