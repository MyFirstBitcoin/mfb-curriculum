# Gịnị na-esote?

###### Ego na-adabere n'iwu na ntụkwasị obi

Ego fiat na-eme ka ịkwụ ụgwọ dị mfe, mana ụlọ ọrụ ole na ole na-ekpebi ego ole a na-ewepụta na iwu ya, ike ya ịzụta ihe nwekwara ike ibelata ka oge na-aga.

###### Bitcoin na-enye iwu dị iche

Kemgbe 2009, Bitcoin enyela ego nke ọnụ ọgụgụ ya bụ ihe ọha maara ma nwee oke, nke onye ọ bụla nwere ike iji, nke a pụkwara inyocha kama ịtụkwasị ya obi naanị. Ọ nwekwara ihe ndị a ga-anabata: mgbanwe ọnụ ahịa, mmejọ a na-apụghị ịgbanwe, na ibu ọrụ nke onwe.

###### Nchekwa bụ ihe mbụ

Mụtanụ ihe mbụ, malitenụ n'obere ihe, ma nyochaanụ ihe niile. Enyela onye ọ bụla usoro okwu mgbake unu. Ọ dịghị mkpa ka unu mee ihe niile n'otu oge: otu nzọụkwụ ọzọ dị nchebe ezuola.

###### Nke a bụ naanị mmalite

Laghachinụ n'ajụjụ ise dị na mmalite. Gịnị ka unu ga-aza ugbu a?

Ihe a na-akpọ [Diplọma Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/) bụ nzọụkwụ ọzọ. Akụkụ anọ nke ihe ọmụmụ ya na-eme ka unu mụtakwuo ihe, site n'ịghọta ego, ruo n'otú Bitcoin si arụ ọrụ, ruo n'iji Bitcoin eme ihe n'ụzọ dị nchebe, ruo na Bitcoin n'obodo unu. Nọgidenụ na-achọ ịmata ihe, nọgidenụ na-ajụ ajụjụ, ma mụtakwuonụ ihe na [myfirstbitcoin.org](https://myfirstbitcoin.org).
