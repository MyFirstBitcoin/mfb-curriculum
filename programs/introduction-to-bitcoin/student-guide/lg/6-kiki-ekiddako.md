# Kiki Ekiddako?

###### Ssente zeesigama ku mateeka n'obwesige

Ssente za fiat zifuula okusasula okwangu, naye omuwendo gwazo n'amateeka agazifuga biteekebwawo ebitongole bitono, era obusobozi bwazo obw'okugula ebintu buyinza okukendeera ekiseera bwe kigenda kiyitawo.

###### Bitcoin ereeta amateeka ag'enjawulo

Okuva mu 2009, Bitcoin ereese ssente ezirina omuwendo ogumanyiddwa abantu bonna era ogukoma, nga buli muntu asobola okuzikozesa, era nga zisobola okukakasibwa mu kifo ky'okuzesiga obwesizi. Naye era erina ebisoomooza: ebbeeyi okulinnya n'okukka, ensobi ezitasobola kutereezebwa, n'obuvunaanyizibwa obw'omuntu kinnoomu.

###### Obukuumi bwe busooka

Musooke muyige, mutandike n'ebitono, era mukakase buli kimu. Temugabanangako na muntu yenna ebigambo byammwe eby'okuzzaawo waleti. Temwetaaga kukola buli kimu omulundi gumu: eddaala limu eriddako eritaliimu kabi limala.

###### Eno ntandikwa yokka

Mudde ku bibuuzo ebitaano ebiri ku ntandikwa. Kati mwandizzeemu mutya?

Eno [Dipulooma ya Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/) y'eddaala eriddako. Ebitundu byayo ebina bibongerayo, okuva ku kutegeera ssente, okutuuka ku ngeri Bitcoin gy'ekolamu, ku kukozesa Bitcoin mu ngeri ey'obukuumi, n'okutuuka ku Bitcoin mu kitundu kyammwe. Musigale nga mwagala okumanya, mweyongere okubuuza ebibuuzo, era muyige ebisingawo ku [myfirstbitcoin.org](https://myfirstbitcoin.org).
