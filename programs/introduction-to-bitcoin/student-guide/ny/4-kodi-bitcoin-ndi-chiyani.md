# Kodi Bitcoin ndi chiyani?

Bitcoin isanabwere, kutumiza ndalama pa intaneti nthawi zonse kunkadalira bungwe lodalirika: banki kapena kampani yolipiritsa yomwe inkasunga buku la zolemba za ndalama, kuvomereza maakaunti, ndipo inkatha kuchedwetsa, kuletsa, kapena kubweza malipiro. Kwa zaka makumi ambiri, asayansi a makompyuta anayesa kupanga ndalama zadijito zopanda wozitulutsa wapakati. Vuto lalikulu linali lakuti n’zosavuta kukopera zinthu zadijito. Ngati ndalama yadijito ikanatha kukopedwa ndi kugwiritsidwa ntchito kawiri, siikanatha kugwira ntchito ngati ndalama. Izi zimatchedwa **vuto la Kugwiritsa Ntchito Kawiri**.

Pa 31 Okutobala 2008, munthu kapena gulu logwiritsa ntchito dzina lakuti Satoshi Nakamoto linasindikiza chikalata chofotokoza njira yothetsera vutoli: _Bitcoin: Dongosolo la Ndalama Zamagetsi Zotumizidwa Pakati pa Anthu Mwachindunji_. Netiwekiyi inayamba mu Januwale 2009.

Bitcoin ndi ndalama zadijito zomwe ndi **zopanda ulamuliro wapakati**, zili ndi **kuchuluka kokhala ndi malire**, ndipo zingatumizidwe **pakati pa anthu mwachindunji** popanda chilolezo cha banki.

Kukhala **kopanda ulamuliro wapakati** kumatanthauza kuti palibe ofesi yapakati yomwe ingatsegule maakaunti, kusintha ndalama zomwe zili m’maakaunti, kapena kuvomereza amene angalowe. Aliyense angatsitse ndi kuunika pulogalamu yomwe khodi yake ndi yotseguka kwa onse, ndipo makompyuta ambiri odziimira pawokha amasunga zolemba zofanana. Izi sizikutanthauza kuti aliyense ali ndi mphamvu zofanana. Zikutanthauza kuti palibe wotenga nawo mbali mmodzi amene angasinthe buku la zolemba za ndalama kapena malamulo a ndalama kwa aliyense.

Kukhala ndi **kuchuluka kokhala ndi malire** kumatanthauza kuti sipadzakhala bitcoin zoposa 21 miliyoni. bitcoin zatsopano zimatulutsidwa motsatira ndandanda yodziwika kwa onse, ndipo makompyuta odziimira pawokha otchedwa ma node amakana buloko iliyonse yoswa lamulolo. Malirewa ndi odalirika chifukwa aliyense angawatsimikizire. Komabe, si lamulo la chilengedwe: amakhalapobe chifukwa otenga nawo mbali amapitiriza kuonetsetsa kuti akutsatiridwa. Ndipo kuchuluka kokhala ndi malire sikutsimikizira kuti mtengo udzakwera. Mtengo umadalira kufunika kwa ndalamazo komanso kuchuluka kwake.

Kukhala **pakati pa anthu mwachindunji** kumatanthauza kuti mungatumize bitcoin kwa aliyense, kulikonse, popanda kutsegula akaunti ku netiweki ya Bitcoin kapena kupempha chilolezo kwa ulamuliro wapakati. Palibe kampani yomwe ingaletse kugwiritsa ntchito bitcoin zomwe mumazilamulira nokha. Ufulu umenewu umabweranso ndi udindo: kutumiza ndalama kovomerezeka nthawi zambiri sikungathe kubwezedwa, ndipo netiwekiyi ilibe ofesi yothandiza ogwiritsa ntchito.

Maganizo amodzi olakwika ndi akuti Bitcoin ndi yosatheka kuipeza chifukwa bitcoin imodzi ndi yokwera mtengo. **Simufunika kugula kapena kugwiritsa ntchito bitcoin yathunthu.** bitcoin imodzi imagawika m’timagawo ting’onoting’ono 100,000,000 totchedwa **satoshis**, kapena **sats**. Kukhala ndi 100,000 sats ndi kukhala ndi 0.001 bitcoin zikutanthauza kuchuluka kofanana.

Netiwekiyi imadalira otenga nawo mbali osiyanasiyana: **ogwiritsa ntchito** omwe amatumiza, kulandira, ndi kusunga bitcoin; **ma node** omwe amaunika kutumiza ndalama kulikonse ndi buloko iliyonse kuti aone ngati zikugwirizana ndi malamulo; komanso **okumba** omwe amapikisana kuti awonjezere buloko yatsopano pa zolembazo pafupifupi mphindi khumi zilizonse. Zolemba zimenezo, zotchedwa **Blockchain**, ndi za poyera ndipo aliyense angazione, ndipo kuzilembanso n’kokwera mtengo kwambiri.

Mfundo yokhudza kalembedwe: **Bitcoin** yokhala ndi B wamkulu nthawi zambiri imatanthauza netiweki kapena dongosolo lake. **bitcoin** yokhala ndi b wamng’ono imatanthauza ndalama zenizenizo.

Bitcoin ilinso ndi zinthu zofunika kuziganizira posankha kuigwiritsa ntchito. Mtengo wake ukhoza kusintha mofulumira. Zolakwa nthawi zambiri sizingakonzedwe. M’madera ambiri, si anthu kapena mabizinesi ambiri amene amaivomereza, ndipo kuigwiritsa ntchito mosamala kumafuna kuphunzira. Bitcoin imalowetsa kutsimikizira ndi udindo wa munthu payekha m’malo mwa kukhulupirira mabungwe pa zinthu zina. Sichotsa chiopsezo.
