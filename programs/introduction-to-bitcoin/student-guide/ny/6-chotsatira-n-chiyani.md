# Chotsatira N’chiyani?

###### Ndalama zimadalira malamulo ndi kukhulupirirana

Ndalama zovomerezedwa ndi boma zimapangitsa kuti kulipira kukhale kosavuta, koma kuchuluka kwake ndi malamulo ake zimakhazikitsidwa ndi mabungwe ochepa, ndipo mphamvu yake yogulira zinthu ingachepe pakapita nthawi.

###### Bitcoin imapereka malamulo osiyana

Kuyambira mu 2009, Bitcoin yakhala ikupereka ndalama zomwe aliyense angagwiritse ntchito, zomwe kuchuluka kwake kumadziwika kwa onse ndipo kuli ndi malire, komanso zomwe zingatsimikiziridwe m'malo mongozikhulupirira. Ilinso ndi zovuta zake: kusinthasintha kwa mtengo, zolakwa zomwe sizingakonzedwe, ndi udindo wa munthu payekha.

###### Chitetezo chimabwera poyamba

Phunzirani kaye, yambani ndi zochepa, ndipo tsimikizirani zonse. Musauze aliyense mawu anu obwezeretsera chikwama. Simuyenera kuchita zonse nthawi imodzi: sitepe imodzi yotsatira yotetezeka ndi yokwanira.

###### Ichi ndi chiyambi chabe

Bwererani ku mafunso asanu omwe ali pachiyambi. Kodi mungayankhe bwanji tsopano?

Maphunziro a [Dipuloma ya Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/) ndi gawo lotsatira. Magawo ake anayi amakuthandizani kupita patsogolo, kuyambira pakumvetsetsa ndalama, kufika pa mmene Bitcoin imagwirira ntchito, kugwiritsa ntchito Bitcoin mosamala, ndi Bitcoin m'dera lanu. Pitirizani kukhala ndi chidwi chofuna kudziwa, pitirizani kufunsa mafunso, ndipo phunzirani zambiri pa [myfirstbitcoin.org](https://myfirstbitcoin.org).
