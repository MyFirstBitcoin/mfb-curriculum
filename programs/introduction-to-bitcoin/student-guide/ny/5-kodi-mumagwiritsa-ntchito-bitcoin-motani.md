# Kodi mumagwiritsa ntchito Bitcoin motani?

Chikwama cha **Bitcoin** ndi chida, nthawi zambiri pulogalamu, chomwe chimakulolani kulandira, kuona, ndi kutumiza bitcoin. Bitcoin yanu sisungidwa mufoni yanu: imalembedwa pa Blockchain. Chikwamacho chimayang'anira **makiyi** omwe amakulolani kutsimikizira kuti ndi yanu ndi kuvomereza kulipira.

Choncho funso loyamba lokhudza chikwama chilichonse ndi lakuti: **ndani amalamulira makiyi?**

###### Kodi ndani kwenikweni amasunga bitcoin yanu?

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/f93df0cc4a2ad82b3410a9ad4c3273d1b760f8ea-1774x887.svg)

Mukamagwiritsa ntchito **chikwama chodzisungira nokha**, ndinu amene mumalamulira makiyi.

Mukachikhazikitsa, chimakusonyezani **mndandanda wa mawu obwezeretsera** (womwe umatchedwanso mawu obwezeretsa chikwama): mawu olembedwa motsatira dongosolo, nthawi zambiri 12 kapena 24. Mawu amenewa angapangitsenso makiyi anu pa chipangizo china. Aliyense amene ali nawo akhoza kutenga bitcoin yanu. Mndandanda wa mawu obwezeretsera si wongothandiza pakatayika zinthu zokha: umapereka mwayi wonse wogwiritsa ntchito bitcoin yanu.

Utetezeni:

* ule mbeni molondola papepala pamene mukupanga chikwamacho;
* usungeni popanda kuulumikiza ku intaneti, mwachinsinsi, ndiponso pamalo pomwe sungatayike kapena kuwonongeka;
* musaujambule chithunzi, kuutumiza pa imelo, kuusunga m'zolemba zosungidwa pa intaneti, kapena kuutumiza mu uthenga;
* musaulembepo pa webusaiti.

Mukataya mndandanda wanu wa mawu obwezeretsera komanso chipangizo chanu, palibe amene angakubwezeretsereni bitcoin yanu.

Mukamagwiritsa ntchito **chikwama chosungiridwa ndi kampani**, kampani imakusungirani makiyi, mofanana pang'ono ndi banki. Ndalama zomwe akaunti yanu ikusonyeza ndi lonjezo la kampaniyo loti idzakulipirani. Njira yokhazikitsira chikwamacho ndi yodziwika bwino ndipo thandizo lingapezeke, koma mumadalira kampaniyo: ikhoza kuchepetsa kuchuluka kwa ndalama zomwe mungatulutse, kuletsa kugwiritsa ntchito akaunti, kulowereredwa ndi obera pa intaneti, kapena kutseka.


| Funso | Chosungiridwa ndi kampani | Chodzisungira nokha |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ndani amasunga makiyi? | Kampani | Inuyo |
| Ndani amayang'anira kubwezeretsa chikwama? | Kampani, kudzera mu akaunti yanu | Inuyo, kudzera mu mndandanda wanu wa mawu obwezeretsera |
| Phindu ndi udindo wake waukulu | Kusavuta kugwiritsa ntchito ndi thandizo, koma mumadalira kampani | Kulamulira mwachindunji, koma muli ndi udindo wosunga makope obwezeretsera ndi zolakwa zomwe mungachite |


Mawu othandiza m'dziko la Bitcoin ndi akuti: **ngati makiyi si anu, ndalama si zanunso.** Kusankha amene azikusungirani si mayeso a kufunika kwanu. Chofunika ndi kudziwa mtundu wa chikwama chomwe mukugwiritsa ntchito ndi zimene mukudalira.

My First Bitcoin sivomereza chikwama china chapadera. Zida, zolipiritsa, ndi eni ake zimasintha, ndipo zomwe zili zoyenera kwa gulu lina sizingakhale zoyenera kwa linzake. Funsani mphunzitsi wodalirika zomwe anthu amagwiritsa ntchito m'dera lanu, ndipo fufuzani nokha mfundo izi musanagwiritse ntchito chikwama chilichonse:

* **Makiyi:** ndani amalamulira makiyi, inuyo kapena kampani?
* **Kubwezeretsa:** chimachitika n'chiyani ngati foni yanu yatayika kapena kampani yasiya kupezeka?
* **Kochokera:** kodi munaitsitsa kuchokera pa webusaiti yovomerezeka kapena pa tsamba lake m'sitolo ya mapulogalamu, osati kudzera pa ulalo womwe munthu wina anakutumizirani?
* **Khodi:** kodi khodi yake ndi yotseguka kwa aliyense ndipo ikusamalidwa nthawi zonse? (Kukhala ndi khodi yotseguka kokha sikutsimikizira kuti chida n'chotetezeka.)
* **Zolipiritsa:** ndi zolipiritsa ziti zomwe zimafunika potumiza, polandira, kapena potulutsa ndalama?
* **Malipiro:** kodi chimathandiza pa malipiro amene mukufuna, a pa Blockchain kapena a Lightning?

Bitcoin ili ndi njira ziwiri zofala zolipirira. **Pa Blockchain** malipiro amalembedwa mwachindunji mu Blockchain. **Lightning** ndi netiweki yolipirira yomangidwa pamwamba pa Bitcoin kuti muzitha kulipira ndalama zochepa mwachangu komanso pafupipafupi, monga pogula khofi. Chikwama cha Lightning chingakhalenso choyang'aniridwa ndi wina kapena choyang'aniridwa ndi inu nokha.

Malamulo atatu adzakuthandizani kukhala otetezeka pamene mukuphunzira:

1. **Phunzirani kaye.** Musachite zinthu chifukwa cha kutengeka mtima, mantha, kapena kukakamizidwa.
1. **Yambani ndi zochepa.** Yesani zida zimene simukuzidziwa bwino ndi ndalama zochepa zimene mungakwanitse kutaya.
1. **Musamangokhulupirira, Tsimikizirani.** Onetsetsani munthu, njira yolipirira, adilesi, ndi kuchuluka kwa ndalama musanavomereze kulipira.

**Gawanani uthenga wolandirira ndalama, osati zinsinsi.** Adilesi kapena pempho lolipira zingathe kugawidwa. Mawu obwezeretsera chikwama kapena kiyi yachinsinsi siziyenera kugawidwa konse. Palibe mphunzitsi, kampani ya zikwama, kapena wogwira ntchito yothandiza makasitomala wovomerezeka amene angakufunseni zimenezi.

Imani ngati wina akukufulumizitsani kapena akufuna kuti musunge chinsinsi, akulonjeza phindu lotsimikizika, akukupemphani kuti mutumize bitcoin kaye, kapena akukufunsani mawu anu obwezeretsera chikwama. **Dikirani kaye. Musagawane chilichonse. Musatumize chilichonse.** Kenako tsimikizirani kudzera ku gwero limene mukulidziwa kale.
