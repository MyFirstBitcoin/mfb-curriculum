# Yini Engahambi Kahle Ngemali Yethu?

Lapho abantu bezwa igama elithi ukwehla kwamandla emali, ngokuvamile bacabanga ngokukhuphuka kwamanani.

**Ukukhuphuka kwamanani** kuwukwenyuka okuqhubekayo kwezinga lamanani lilonke. Olunye uhlangothi lwakho **amandla okuthenga**: ukuthi iyunithi lemali lingathenga okungakanani. Uma intengo kabhasikidi wezimpahla eziyisisekelo ikhuphuka isuka kumayunithi emali angu-100 iya kwangu-110, lawo mayunithi angu-100 awasakwazi ukuthenga wonke ubhasikidi.

Ukwehla kwamandla emali kunezimbangela eziningana, futhi izazi zezomnotho ziyaphikisana ngokuthi ngayinye inomthelela ongakanani: ukwanda kwemali nezikweletu, ukuphazamiseka kokutholakala kwezimpahla, izinguquko esidingweni sezimpahla nezinsizakalo, ukuba buthaka kohlobo lwemali, izintela, nezindleko zokukhiqiza. Enye imbangela idinga ukunakwa ngoba iwukukhetha, hhayi into ezenzakalelayo: ohlelweni lwemali ye-fiat, izikhungo ezimbalwa zingandisa inani lemali nezikweletu futhi ziguqule imithetho yalolu hlelo.

Cabangani ngokuthi yini eyenza into ibe yigugu.

Umdwebo wokuqala we-Mona Lisa ubaluleke ngaphezu kwanoma iyiphi ikhophi yawo, hhayi ngoba ubukeka kangcono, kodwa ngoba yiwo wodwa ongowokuqala. Ukuntuleka kwento kukodwa akuyenzi ibe yigugu, kodwa lapho abantu befuna into, inani layo elitholakalayo libalulekile.

**Lapho inani lemali likhula ngokushesha kunokwanda kwezinto engazithenga, iyunithi ngalinye livame ukuthenga okuncane.**

Ukwehla kwamandla emali akubathinti bonke abantu ngokulinganayo. Umuntu ogcina ingxenye enkulu yemali yakhe iwukheshi noma iyimali eyongiwe angase alahlekelwe amandla okuthenga. Umuntu onezimpahla ezikhuphuka ngentengo angase avikeleke ngokwengxenye. Amaholo namanani nawo aguquka ngesivinini esihlukene.

Imali eku-akhawunti yenu yasebhange nayo ihlukile kukheshi eniwuphethe. Ibhange aliyigcini yonke imali efakiwe iwukheshi: iningi layo liyalibolekisa futhi liyitshale. **Ibhalansi ye-akhawunti yasebhange iyisithembiso sebhange sokunikhokhela.** Leso sithembiso sivame ukufezeka, kodwa kuncike ebhange nasemithethweni elilawulayo.

###### Zamani lokhu: ukuhlola amandla okuthenga

Khethani into eyodwa evamile emphakathini wakini, njengesinkwa, ezokuthutha, noma i-airtime yocingo. Bhalani phansi intengo yayo yanamuhla. Buzani othile ukuthi yayibiza malini eminyakeni ethile edlule, bese nihlola irekhodi elithembekile uma nikwazi. Ingabe imali abantu abayitholayo yenyuka ngezinga elifanayo? Yini enye engachaza lolu shintsho?

Akukho kulokhu okusho ukuthi imali ye-fiat ayisebenzi. Yenza ukukhokha kube lula, ivumela ukuguquguquka ekunikezeni izikweletu, futhi inikeza amathuluzi okubhekana nezimo ezibucayi. Kodwa lokho kuguquguquka kudala ubungozi: inani layo nemithetho yayo kungashintsha, kanti abantu abayisebenzisayo banezwi elincane kulezo zinqumo.

**Yingakho abantu befuna ezinye izindlela, futhi yingakho kwasungulwa i-Bitcoin.**
