# Yini Elandelayo?

###### Imali incike emithethweni nasekwethembaneni

Imali ye-fiat yenza ukukhokha kube lula, kodwa inani layo elitholakalayo nemithetho yayo kunqunywa yizikhungo ezimbalwa, futhi amandla ayo okuthenga angehla ngokuhamba kwesikhathi.

###### I-Bitcoin inikeza imithetho ehlukile

Kusukela ngo-2009, i-Bitcoin inikeze imali enenani elitholakalayo elisobala nelinomkhawulo, evulekele wonke umuntu, engaqinisekiswa kunokumane yethenjwe. Iphinde ibe nezinselele: ukuguquguquka kwentengo, amaphutha angenakulungiseka, nokuzibophezela komuntu siqu.

###### Ukuphepha kuza kuqala

Fundani kuqala, qalani ngokuncane, futhi niqinisekise konke. Ningalokothi nabelane ngamagama enu okubuyisela isikhwama. Akudingeki nenze konke ngesikhathi esisodwa: isinyathelo esisodwa esilandelayo esiphephile sanele.

###### Lokhu kuseyisiqalo nje

Buyelani emibuzweni emihlanu esekuqaleni. Ningaphendula nithini manje?

Le [diploma ye-Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/) iyisinyathelo esilandelayo. Amamojuli ayo amane aniqhubekisela phambili, kusukela ekuqondeni imali, kuye endleleni i-Bitcoin esebenza ngayo, ekusebenziseni i-Bitcoin ngokuphepha, nase-Bitcoin emphakathini wenu. Hlalani nilangazelela ukwazi, qhubekani nibuza imibuzo, futhi nifunde kabanzi ku- [myfirstbitcoin.org](https://myfirstbitcoin.org).
