# Iyini Imali?

**Imali iyithuluzi lokushintshiselana.**

Akekho ofuna imali ngenxa yayo uqobo. Siyifuna ngenxa yalokho engakuthenga. Imali eyiphepha imane iyisiqephu sephepha. Okwenza ibe wusizo ukuthi abanye abantu bayayamukela ukuze basinike izinto esizifunayo ngempela, ngoba balindele ukuthi nabanye bazoyamukela kamuva.

Yilokhu okuhlukanisa imali nokuhwebelana ngezimpahla.

Ohlelweni lokuhwebelana ngezimpahla, ukuhweba kusebenza kuphela lapho bobabili abantu befuna lokho omunye anakho, ngesikhathi esifanayo nangamanani afanele. Izazi zezomnotho lokhu zikubiza ngokuthi **ukuqondana kwezidingo zabo bobabili**. Uma nitshala irayisi futhi nidinga izicathulo, umenzi wezicathulo angase afune amafutha okupheka, hhayi irayisi. Imali ixazulula lokhu ngokwehlukanisa ukuthengisa nokuthenga: nithengisela umuntu oyedwa irayisi lenu bese nithenga izicathulo komunye.

**Imali ivame ukwenza izinto ezintathu.**

Iyona **ndlela yokushintshiselana**: abantu bayayamukela njengenkokhelo. Iphinde ibe **iyunithi yokubala**: amanani entengo abekwa ngayo, ukuze sikwazi ukuqhathanisa inani lezinto ezahlukene. Futhi iyona **ndawo yokugcina inani**: idlulisa inani lisuke namuhla liye esikhathini esizayo. Ayikho imali egcina inani ngokuphelele, kodwa ezinye izinhlobo zilahlekelwa amandla okuthenga kancane kunezinye.

Kuwo wonke umlando, izinto eziningi ziye zasebenza njengemali: amagobolondo, usawoti, ubuhlalu, imfuyo, ithusi, isiliva negolide. Izinsimbi eziyigugu zaqala ukusetshenziswa kabanzi ngoba zaziyivelakancane, zihlala isikhathi eside futhi zamukelwa kabanzi. Kodwa ukuthwala nokuvikela insimbi kunzima, ngakho amabhange aqala ukukhipha amaphepha ayenikeza umnikazi ilungelo lokuwashintsha athole inani elishiwo lensimbi. Kwakunezinhlobo ezahlukene zalolu **hlelo lokusekela imali ngegolide**, lapho iyunithi yemali yayixhunyaniswe ngokomthetho nenani elithile legolide. Imithetho yayehluka ngokwendawo nangenkathi.

Ngokuhamba kwesikhathi, imali yaqala ukuncika kakhulu ezikhungweni nasezithembisweni kunasensimbini. Ngo-1971, i-United States yaqeda ukushintshaniswa okusemthethweni kwamadola ngegolide okwakwenziwa ohulumeni bamanye amazwe namabhange amakhulu. Namuhla, izinhlobo eziningi zemali yamazwe ziyi **mali ye-fiat**: imali ekhishwa ngaphansi kwegunya likahulumeni, ngokuvamile ngebhange elikhulu, engeke ishintshwe ukuze kutholakale inani elimisiwe legolide noma lanoma iyiphi enye impahla. Igama elithi fiat liyisiLatini elisho ukuthi "makwenziwe", okuvame ukuqondwa njengokuthi "ngomyalelo".

Imali ye-fiat isebenza ngoba abantu, amabhizinisi, amabhange nohulumeni bayayamukela. Ngokuvamile imithetho iyenza ibe yimali eyamukelwa ngokomthetho ekukhokheleni izikweletu futhi ifune ukuba kusetshenziswe yona ekukhokheni izintela, futhi abantu bayayifuna ngoba wonke umuntu obazungezile uyayisebenzisa.

**Sasuka emalini esekelwa okuthile, saya emalini esekelwa ukwethemba othile.**
