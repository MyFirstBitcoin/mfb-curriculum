# Niyisebenzisa Kanjani i-Bitcoin?

Le **walethi ye-Bitcoin** iyithuluzi, ngokuvamile eliwuhlelo lokusebenza, elinivumela ukuba namukele, nihlole futhi nithumele i-bitcoin. I-bitcoin yenu ayigcinwa ngaphakathi ocingweni lwenu: ibhalwa ku-blockchain. Iwalethi ilawula **okhiye** abanivumela ukuba nifakazele ukuthi ingeyenu futhi nigunyaze inkokhelo.

Ngakho umbuzo wokuqala nganoma iyiphi iwalethi uthi: **ngubani olawula okhiye?**

###### Ngubani ngempela ophethe i-bitcoin yenu?

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/f93df0cc4a2ad82b3410a9ad4c3273d1b760f8ea-1774x887.svg)

Uma nisebenzisa **iwalethi enizinakekela yona**, yinina enilawula okhiye.

Lapho niyisetha, inibonisa **ibinzana lembewu** (elibizwa nangokuthi ibinzana lokubuyisela): uhlu lwamagama ahlelwe ngokulandelana, ngokuvamile angu-12 noma angu-24. Lingaphinda lidale okhiye benu kwenye idivayisi. Noma ubani onalo angathatha i-bitcoin yenu. Ibinzana lembewu aligcini nje ngokuba ikhophi eyisipele: linikeza ukufinyelela okuphelele.

Livikeleni:

* libhaleni ngokunembile ephepheni lapho nakha iwalethi;
* ligcineni lingaxhunyiwe ku-inthanethi, liyimfihlo, futhi lisendaweni lapho lingeke lilahleke noma lonakale khona;
* ningalokothi nilithwebule isithombe, nilithumele nge-imeyili, niligcine kumanothi agcinwe efwini, noma nilithumele ngomlayezo;
* ningalokothi nilibhale kuwebhusayithi.

Uma nilahlekelwa yibinzana lembewu kanye nedivayisi yenu, akekho onganibuyisela i-bitcoin yenu.

Uma nisebenzisa **iwalethi eninakekelwa yona**, inkampani inigcinela okhiye, kucishe kufane nebhange. Ibhalansi yenu iyisithembiso senkampani sokunikhokhela. Indlela yokuyisetha ijwayelekile futhi usizo lungase lutholakale, kodwa nincike kuleyo nkampani: ingakhawulela ukukhipha imali, imise ukusebenza kwe-akhawunti, ihlaselwe ngabaduni, noma ivalwe.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Ngubani ophethe okhiye? | Inkampani | Yinina |
| Ngubani obhekele ukubuyisela? | Inkampani, nge-akhawunti yenu | Yinina, ngebinzana lembewu lenu |
| Inzuzo eyinhloko kanye nokuzidela okuhambisana nayo | Ukusebenziseka kalula nosizo, kodwa nincike enkampanini | Ukulawula ngokuqondile, kodwa ninomthwalo wokugcina amakhophi ayisipele namaphutha eniwenzayo |


Isisho esiwusizo emhlabeni we-Bitcoin sithi: **uma okhiye bengebona abenu, izinhlamvu zemali nazo akuzona ezenu.** Ukukhetha ukuthi ngubani ozonigcinela okhiye akusona isivivinyo sokubaluleka kwenu. Okubalulekile ukwazi ukuthi yiluphi uhlobo enilusebenzisayo nokuthi yini eniyethembayo.

I-My First Bitcoin ayincomi amawalethi athile. Amathuluzi, izimali ezikhokhiswayo nabanikazi kuyashintsha, futhi okulungele umphakathi othile kungase kungawufaneli omunye. Buzani uthisha omethembayo ukuthi abantu basebenzisa ini endaweni yakini, bese nizihlolela la maphuzu ngaphambi kokusebenzisa noma iyiphi iwalethi:

* **Okhiye:** ngubani olawula okhiye, yinina noma yinkampani?
* **Ukubuyisela:** kwenzekani uma ucingo lwenu lulahleka noma inkampani inyamalala?
* **Umthombo:** niyilande kuwebhusayithi esemthethweni noma ekhasini layo esitolo sezinhlelo zokusebenza, hhayi ngesixhumanisi enisithunyelelwe othile?
* **Ikhodi:** ingabe ikhodi yayo ivulekele umphakathi futhi inakekelwa ngokuqhubekayo? (Ukuthi ikhodi ivulekele umphakathi kukodwa akufakazeli ukuthi ithuluzi liphephile.)
* **Izindleko:** yiziphi izimali ezikhokhiswayo lapho nithumela, namukela noma nikhipha imali?
* **Izinkokhelo:** ingabe iyazisekela izinkokhelo enizidingayo, ezenziwa ku-blockchain noma nge-Lightning?

I-Bitcoin inezindlela ezimbili ezivamile zokukhokha. **Ku-blockchain** izinkokhelo ziqoshwa ngokuqondile ku-blockchain. **I-Lightning** iyinethiwekhi yokukhokha eyakhelwe phezu kwe-Bitcoin ukuze kwenziwe izinkokhelo ezisheshayo, ezincane nezivamile, njengokuthenga ikhofi. Isikhwama se-Lightning naso singaphathwa omunye umuntu noma siziphathwe yinina.

Imithetho emithathu izonigcina niphephile ngenkathi nifunda:

1. **Fundani kuqala.** Ningenzi lutho ngenxa yenjabulo, ukwesaba noma ingcindezi.
1. **Qalani ngokuncane.** Zama amathuluzi eningawajwayele ngenani eningakwazi ukubhekana nokulahlekelwa yilo.
1. **Ningathembi Nje, Qinisekisani.** Hlolani umuntu, indlela yokukhokha, ikheli nenani ngaphambi kokuba nivume inkokhelo.

**Yabelanani ngemininingwane yokwamukela izinkokhelo, ningalokothi nabelane ngezimfihlo.** Ikheli noma isicelo senkokhelo kungabiwa nabanye. Ibinzana lokubuyisela noma ukhiye oyimfihlo akumele neze kwabelwane ngakho. Akekho uthisha osemthethweni, inkampani yezikhwama noma umsebenzi wokweseka oyoke akucele lokho.

Yimani uma othile enenza nizwe sengathi kufanele niphuthume noma nigcine imfihlo, ethembisa inzuzo eqinisekisiwe, enicela ukuba nithumele i-bitcoin kuqala, noma ecela ibinzana lenu lokubuyisela. **Phumani kancane kulokho. Ningabelani ngalutho. Ningathumeli lutho.** Bese niqinisekisa ngomthombo eseniwazi kakade.
