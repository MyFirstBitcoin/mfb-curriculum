# Iyini i-Bitcoin?

Ngaphambi kwe-Bitcoin, ukuthumela imali nge-inthanethi kwakuhlale kuncike enhlanganweni ethenjwayo: ibhange noma inkampani yezinkokhelo eyayigcina incwadi yamarekhodi, igunyaze ama-akhawunti, futhi ikwazi ukubambezela, ukuvimba noma ukuhlehlisa inkokhelo. Kwaphela amashumi eminyaka ososayensi bamakhompyutha bezama ukwakha imali yedijithali engenamkhiphi oyisikhungo esisodwa. Ingxenye eyayinzima kwakuwukuthi ukwaziswa kwedijithali kulula ukukukopisha. Uma uhlamvu lwemali lwedijithali lwalungakopishwa futhi lusetshenziswe kabili, lwalungeke lusebenze njengemali. Lokhu kubizwa ngokuthi **inkinga yokusebenzisa imali efanayo kabili**.

Ngomhla ka-31 kuMfumfu 2008, umuntu noma iqembu elalisebenzisa igama elithi Satoshi Nakamoto lashicilela iphepha elalichaza isixazululo: _Bitcoin: Uhlelo Lwemali Ye-elekthronikhi Oluxhumanisa Abantu Ngokuqondile_. Inethiwekhi yaqala ngoMasingana 2009.

I-Bitcoin yimali yedijithali **engalawulwa yisikhungo esisodwa**, etholakala **ngenani elilinganiselwe**, futhi engathunyelwa **ngokuqondile isuka kumuntu iye komunye** ngaphandle kwemvume yebhange.

Ukuthi i-Bitcoin **ayilawulwa yisikhungo esisodwa** kusho ukuthi alikho ihhovisi eliyinhloko elingavula ama-akhawunti, lishintshe izilinganiso zemali ezisele, noma ligunyaze ukuthi ubani ojoyinayo. Noma ubani angadawuniloda futhi ahlole isofthiwe enekhodi yomthombo evulelekile, futhi amakhompyutha amaningi azimele agcina ikhophi yerekhodi. Lokhu akusho ukuthi wonke umuntu unethonya elilinganayo. Kusho ukuthi akekho umhlanganyeli oyedwa ongashintshela wonke umuntu incwadi yamarekhodi noma imithetho yemali.

Ukutholakala **ngenani elilinganiselwe** kusho ukuthi akusoze kwaba nama-bitcoin angaphezu kwezigidi ezingu-21. Ama-bitcoin amasha akhishwa ngokwesimiso sesikhathi esaziwa umphakathi, futhi amakhompyutha azimele abizwa ngokuthi ama-node enqaba noma yiliphi ibhulokhi elephula lowo mthetho. Lo mkhawulo uyakholakala ngoba noma ubani angawuhlola. Nokho, awuwona umthetho wemvelo: uyaqhubeka usebenza ngoba abahlanganyeli bayaqhubeka bewuphoqelela. Futhi inani elilinganiselwe aliqinisekisi ukuthi intengo izokhuphuka. Intengo incike esidingweni kanye nasenanini elitholakalayo.

Ukuthi i-Bitcoin isebenza **ngokuqondile isuka kumuntu iye komunye** kusho ukuthi ningathumela i-bitcoin kunoma ubani, noma kuphi, ngaphandle kokuvula i-akhawunti kunethiwekhi ye-Bitcoin noma ukucela imvume esikhungweni esiyinhloko. Ayikho inkampani engavimba ukusetshenziswa kwe-bitcoin enizilawulela yona. Olunye uhlangothi lwaleyo nkululeko luwukuzibophezela: ngokuvamile akunakwenzeka ukuhlehlisa ukuthengiselana okuvumelekile, futhi alikho ideski losizo lenethiwekhi.

Omunye umbono oyiphutha uthi i-Bitcoin ayifinyeleleki ngoba i-bitcoin eyodwa iyabiza. **Akudingeki nithenge noma nisebenzise i-bitcoin ephelele.** I-bitcoin eyodwa ihlukaniseka ibe amayunithi amancane angu-100 000 000 abizwa ngokuthi **ama-satoshi**, noma **ama-sat**. Ukuba nama-sat angu-100 000 nokuba ne-bitcoin engu-0,001 kuchaza inani elifanayo.

Inethiwekhi incike kubahlanganyeli abahlukene: **abasebenzisi** abathumela, abamukela futhi abagcina i-bitcoin; **ama-node** ahlola konke ukuthengiselana nawo wonke amabhulokhi ukuthi kuhambisana yini nemithetho; kanye **nabavukuzi** abancintisana ngokwengeza ibhulokhi elisha erekhodini cishe njalo ngemizuzu eyishumi. Lelo rekhodi, **i-blockchain**, livulelekile emphakathini futhi libonakala kunoma ubani, futhi kubiza kakhulu ukulibhala kabusha.

Okuphathelene nokubhala: **Bitcoin** ebhalwa ngo-B omkhulu ngokuvamile isho inethiwekhi noma uhlelo. **bitcoin** ebhalwa ngo-b omncane isho imali uqobo.

I-Bitcoin nayo inezinzuzo ezihambisana nokulahlekelwa okuthile. Intengo yayo ingashintsha ngokushesha. Ngokuvamile amaphutha awalungiseki. Ukwamukelwa kwayo kusalinganiselwe ezindaweni eziningi, futhi ukuyisebenzisa ngokuphepha kudinga ukufunda okuthile. I-Bitcoin ithatha indawo yokunye ukwethemba izikhungo ngokuqinisekisa nangokuzibophezela komuntu siqu. Ayibuqedi ubungozi.
