# He aha te Bitcoin?

I mua i a Bitcoin, i whakawhirinaki tonu te tuku moni mā te ipurangi ki tētahi whakahaere pono: he pēke, he kamupene utu rānei nāna i pupuri te pukapuka kaute, i whakamana ngā pūkete, ā, i āhei hoki ki te whakaroa, te aukati, te whakahoki rānei i tētahi utunga. Mō ngā tekau tau, i ngana ngā kaipūtaiao rorohiko ki te hanga moni matihiko kāore he kaiwhakaputa matua. Ko te uauatanga, he māmā te tārua i ngā mōhiohio matihiko. Mehemea ka taea tētahi moni matihiko te tārua me te whakapau kia rua ngā wā, e kore e whai hua hei moni. E kīia ana tēnei ko te **raruraru whakapau rua**.

I te 31 o Whiringa-ā-nuku 2008, i whakaputa tētahi tangata, tētahi rōpū rānei e whakamahi ana i te ingoa Satoshi Nakamoto i tētahi tuhinga e whakamārama ana i tētahi rongoā: _Bitcoin: He Pūnaha Moni Hiko mō te Whakawhiti Tōtika i Waenga i ngā Tāngata_. I tīmata te whatunga i te Hānuere 2009.

He moni matihiko a Bitcoin, ā, kua **tuarihia tōna mana whakahaere**, he **rahinga whāiti tōna**, ā, ka taea te tuku **tōtika i waenga i ngā tāngata** me te kore whakaaetanga a tētahi pēke.

Ko te tikanga o te **tuaritanga o te mana whakahaere**, kāore he tari matua e āhei ana ki te hanga pūkete, ki te whakarerekē i ngā toenga moni, ki te whakaae rānei ko wai ka uru mai. Ka taea e te tangata ahakoa ko wai te tikiake me te āta tirotiro i te pūmanawa waehere-tuwhera, ā, he maha ngā rorohiko motuhake e pupuri ana i tētahi tārua o te pūkete. Ehara tēnei i te mea he ōrite te mana whakaawe o ia tangata. Ko tōna tikanga, kāore e taea e te kaiuru kotahi te whakarerekē te pukapuka kaute, ngā ture moni rānei mō te katoa.

Ko te tikanga o te whai **rahinga whāiti**, e kore rawa te rahinga e hipa ake i te 21 miriona bitcoin. Ka tukuna ngā bitcoin hou i runga i tētahi hōtaka tūmatanui, ā, ka whakahē ngā rorohiko motuhake, e kīia ana he kōpuku, i ngā poraka katoa e takahi ana i taua ture. He pono taua tepe nā te mea ka taea e te tangata ahakoa ko wai te manatoko. Heoi, ehara tēnei i te ture o te taiao: ka mau tonu nā te ū tonu o ngā kaiuru ki te whakaū. Waihoki, ehara te rahinga whāiti i te kī taurangi ka piki te utu. E whakawhirinaki ana te utu ki te hiahia hoko me te rahinga e wātea ana.

Ko te tikanga o te whakawhiti **tōtika i waenga i ngā tāngata**, ka taea e koutou te tuku bitcoin ki te tangata ahakoa ko wai, ahakoa kei hea, me te kore whakatuwhera pūkete ki te whatunga Bitcoin, me te kore tono whakaaetanga hoki i tētahi mana whakahaere matua. Kāore tētahi kamupene e āhei ki te aukati i te whakamahinga o ngā bitcoin kei raro i tō koutou ake mana whakahaere. Ko tērā atu taha o taua herekoretanga ko te kawenga: i te nuinga o te wā kāore e taea te whakahoki i tētahi tauwhitinga whaimana, ā, kāore he tari āwhina mō te whatunga.

Ko tētahi pōhēhētanga, kāore e taea te whai wāhi ki Bitcoin nā te nui o te utu o te bitcoin kotahi. **Kāore koutou e mate ki te hoko, ki te whakamahi rānei i te bitcoin kotahi katoa.** Ka wehea te bitcoin kotahi ki ngā waeine iti ake 100,000,000, e kīia ana he **satoshi**, he **sats rānei**. He ōrite te rahinga o te whai 100,000 sats me te whai 0.001 bitcoin.

E whakawhirinaki ana te whatunga ki ngā momo kaiuru rerekē: ngā **kaiwhakamahi** e tuku ana, e whiwhi ana, e pupuri ana hoki i te bitcoin; ngā **kōpuku** e manatoko ana i ia tauwhitinga me ia poraka e ai ki ngā ture; me ngā **kaikeri** e whakataetae ana ki te tāpiri poraka hou ki te pūkete i ia tekau meneti, tata nei. Ko taua pūkete, arā, te **mekameka poraka**, he mea tūmatanui e kitea ana e te tangata ahakoa ko wai, ā, he tino nui te utu ki te tuhi anō.

He kōrero mō te tuhi kupu: **Bitcoin** me te B pūmatua, ko tōna tikanga i te nuinga o te wā ko te whatunga, ko te pūnaha rānei. **bitcoin** me te b pūriki, ko tōna tikanga ko te moni tonu.

He painga, he ngoikoretanga anō hoki tō Bitcoin. Ka tere te panoni o tōna utu. I te nuinga o te wā, kāore e taea te whakatika ngā hapa. He iti tonu ngā wāhi e whakaae ana ki te whakamahi, ā, me ako ētahi āhuatanga kia haumaru ai te whakamahi. Ka whakakapi a Bitcoin i tētahi wāhanga o te whakawhirinaki ki ngā whakahaere ki te manatoko me te kawenga whaiaro. Kāore e whakakore i te tūraru.
