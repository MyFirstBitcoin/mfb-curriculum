# Me pēhea te whakamahi i te Bitcoin?

Ko te **pūkoro Bitcoin** he taputapu, he taupānga te nuinga, e āhei ai koe ki te whiwhi, ki te tirotiro, ki te tuku hoki i te bitcoin. Kāore ō bitcoin e penapenahia ki roto i tō waea: ka tuhia kē ki te mekameka poraka. Mā te pūkoro e whakahaere ngā **kī** e āhei ai koe ki te whakamana nōu aua bitcoin, me te whakamana hoki i tētahi utunga.

Nō reira, ko te pātai tuatahi mō tētahi pūkoro: **mā wai ngā kī e whakahaere?**

###### Ko wai tonu e pupuri ana i ō bitcoin?

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/f93df0cc4a2ad82b3410a9ad4c3273d1b760f8ea-1774x887.svg)

Mā te whakamahi i tētahi **pūkoro māu anō e tiaki**, māu ngā kī e whakahaere.

Ina whakaritea e koe, ka whakaatu mai i tētahi **kīanga kākano** (e kīia ana hoki he kīanga whakaora): he rārangi kupu kua raupapahia, he 12, he 24 rānei i te nuinga o te wā. Ka taea te waihanga anō i ō kī ki tētahi atu pūrere mā taua kīanga. Ka taea e te tangata kei a ia taua kīanga te tango i ō bitcoin. Ehara te kīanga kākano i te tārua pūrua noa iho: mā reira ka whai urunga katoa.

Tiakina:

* tuhia tikatia ki te pepa i a koe e waihanga ana i te pūkoro;
* puritia tuimotutia, kia noho muna, ki tētahi wāhi e kore ai e ngaro, e tūkinotia rānei;
* kaua rawa e whakaahua, e tuku mā te īmēra, e penapena ki ngā tuhipoka kapua, e tuku rānei mā tētahi karere;
* kaua rawa e patopato ki tētahi pae tukutuku.

Ki te ngaro tō kīanga kākano me tō pūrere, kāore e taea e tētahi te whakaora ō bitcoin māu.

Mā te whakamahi i tētahi **pūkoro mā tētahi atu e tiaki**, mā tētahi kamupene ngā kī e pupuri māu, he āhua rite ki te pēke. Ko tō toenga te oati a te kamupene kia utua koe. He waia te tukanga whakarite, ā, tērā pea he tautoko e wātea ana, engari ka whakawhirinaki koe ki taua kamupene: ka taea e ia te whakawhāiti i ngā tangohanga, te whakatio i tētahi pūkete, ā, tērā pea ka urutomokia, ka kati rānei.


| Pātai | Mā tētahi atu e tiaki | Māu anō e tiaki |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mā wai ngā kī e pupuri? | Mā te kamupene | Māu |
| Mā wai te whakaora e whakahaere? | Mā te kamupene, mā tō pūkete | Māu, mā tō kīanga kākano |
| Te painga me tōna utu matua | He māmā, he tautoko hoki, engari ka whakawhirinaki koe ki te kamupene | Kei a koe tonu te mana whakahaere, engari kei a koe te kawenga mō ngā tārua pūrua me ngā hapa |


He kīanga whaihua i te ao Bitcoin: **ki te kore nōu ngā kī, ehara ngā moni i a koe.** Ehara te kōwhiri ko wai hei kaitiaki i te whakamātautau i tō mana. Ko te mea nui kia mōhio koe ko tēhea momo e whakamahia ana e koe, ā, he aha tāu e whakawhirinaki atu ana.

Kāore a My First Bitcoin e taunaki ana i ētahi pūkoro motuhake. Ka panoni ngā taputapu, ngā utu, me ngā kaipupuri, ā, ko te mea e pai ana mō tētahi hapori, kāore pea e pai mō tētahi atu. Pātai atu ki tētahi kaiwhakaako pono he aha ngā mea e whakamahia ana e te tangata i tō rohe, ā, māu anō ēnei āhuatanga e tirotiro i mua i te whakamahi i tētahi pūkoro:

* **Ngā kī:** mā wai ngā kī e whakahaere, māu, mā tētahi kamupene rānei?
* **Te whakaora:** ka aha mēnā ka ngaro tō waea, ka ngaro atu rānei te kamupene?
* **Te pūtakenga:** i tikiake koe i taua pūkoro mai i te pae tukutuku mana, i te rārangi mana rānei o te toa taupānga, kaua i tētahi hononga i tukuna mai e tētahi tangata?
* **Te waehere:** he puna tuwhera, ā, e āta tiakina tonutia ana? (Ehara te noho hei puna tuwhera anake i te taunakitanga he haumaru tētahi taputapu.)
* **Ngā utu:** he aha ngā utu mō te tuku, te whiwhi, te tango rānei?
* **Ngā utunga:** e tautoko ana rānei i ngā utunga e hiahiatia ana e koe, ahakoa mā te mekameka, mā Lightning rānei?

E rua ngā huarahi utu e whakamahia whānuitia ana i Bitcoin. **Ā-mekameka** te āhua o ngā utunga ka tuhia tikatia ki te mekameka poraka. **Ko Lightning** he whatunga utu kua hangaia ki runga i Bitcoin mō ngā utunga tere, iti, auau hoki, pērā i te hoko kawhe. Ka taea hoki te whai pūkoro Lightning e tiakina ana e tētahi atu, e tiakina ana rānei e koe anake.

Mā ēnei ture e toru koe e tiaki i a koe e ako ana:

1. **Me ako i te tuatahi.** Kaua e mahi nā te hiamo, nā te mataku, nā te pēhitanga rānei.
1. **Me tīmata ki te iti.** Whakamātauria ngā taputapu tauhou ki tētahi rahinga moni e taea ana e koe te ngaro me te kore e raru.
1. **Kaua e Whakapono Noa, Me Manatoko.** Āta tirohia te tangata, te tikanga utu, te wāhitau, me te rahinga moni i mua i tō whakaae ki tētahi utunga.

**Tohaina ngā mōhiohio whiwhi utu, kaua rawa ngā kōrero muna.** Ka taea te tohatoha i tētahi wāhitau, i tētahi tono utu rānei. Kaua rawa e tohatoha i tētahi kīanga kākano, i tētahi kī tūmataiti rānei. E kore rawa tētahi kaiwhakaako pono, kamupene pūkoro pono, kaimahi tautoko pono rānei e tono i ēnei.

Me mutu mēnā ka whakatupu tētahi i te kōhukihuki, i te mahi huna rānei, ka kī taurangi ka whai hua moni koe, ka tono rānei kia tuku bitcoin koe i te tuatahi, kia hoatu rānei i tō kīanga kākano. **Me tatari. Kaua e tohatoha i tētahi mea. Kaua e tuku i tētahi mea.** Kātahi ka manatoko mā tētahi puna mōhiohio e mōhio kē ana koe.
