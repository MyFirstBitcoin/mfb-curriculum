# He aha ka whai ake?

###### E whakawhirinaki ana te moni ki ngā ture me te whakawhirinakitanga

Mā te moni fiat e māmā ai ngā utu, engari mā tētahi rōpū iti o ngā whakahaere e whakatau tōna rahinga me ōna ture, ā, ka heke pea tōna mana hoko i te takanga o te wā.

###### He ture rerekē tā Bitcoin e tuku nei

Mai i te tau 2009, kua tukuna e Bitcoin he moni e wātea ana ki te katoa, he rahinga whāiti tōna e kitea ana e te katoa, ā, ka taea te manatoko, kaua ko te whakawhirinaki noa. Heoi, he āhuatanga hei āta whakaaro: ko te piki me te heke o te utu, ko ngā hapa e kore e taea te whakatika, me te kawenga whaiaro.

###### Ko te haumaru te mea tuatahi

Me ako i te tuatahi, me tīmata ki te iti, ā, me manatoko ngā mea katoa. Kaua rawa e tohatoha i tō koutou kīanga kākano. Kāore e hiahiatia kia mahia ngā mea katoa i te wā kotahi: kotahi noa te hīkoi haumaru e whai ake nei, kua ranea.

###### He tīmatanga noa tēnei

Hoki atu ki ngā pātai e rima i te tīmatanga. He aha ā koutou whakautu ināianei?

Ko te [Pōkairua Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/) te hīkoi e whai ake nei. Mā ōna kōwae e whā koutou e kawe whakamua, mai i te mārama ki te moni, ki te āhua o te mahi a Bitcoin, ki te whakamahi haumaru i a Bitcoin, ki a Bitcoin i tō koutou hapori. Kia pākiki tonu, me pātai tonu, ā, me ako tonu i [myfirstbitcoin.org](https://myfirstbitcoin.org).
