# He Aha te Moni?

**He taputapu te moni mō te whakawhiti.**

Kāore he tangata e hiahia ana ki te moni mō te moni noa iho. E hiahia ana tātou ki te moni mō ngā mea ka taea te hoko ki taua moni. He pepa noa iho te moni pepa. Ko te mea e whai hua ai, ko te whakaae a ētahi atu ki taua moni hei utu mō ngā mea e tino hiahiatia ana e tātou, nā te mea e tūmanako ana rātou ka whakaae anō ētahi atu ki taua moni ā muri ake.

Koinei te rerekētanga i waenga i te moni me te whakawhiti rawa.

I roto i te pūnaha whakawhiti rawa, ka whai hua noa te tauhokohoko ina hiahia ngā tāngata e rua ki ngā rawa a tētahi, a tētahi, i te wā kotahi, ā, kia tika hoki te rahinga. Ka kīia tēnei e ngā mātanga ohaoha ko te **tauritetanga takirua o ngā hiahia**. Ki te whakatipu raihi koutou, ā, e hiahia ana ki te hū, tērā pea ka hiahia te kaihanga hū ki te hinu tunu kai, kaua ki te raihi. Ka whakatika te moni i tēnei mā te wehe i te hoko atu i te hoko mai: ka hoko atu koutou i ā koutou raihi ki tētahi tangata, ka hoko mai ai i ngā hū i tētahi atu.

**E toru ngā mahi a te moni i te nuinga o te wā.**

He **ara whakawhiti**: ka whakaae te tangata ki te moni hei utu. He **waeine kaute**: ka whakaaturia ngā utu ki taua waeine, kia taea ai e tātou te whakataurite te uara o ngā mea rerekē. He **pupuri uara** anō hoki: ka kawe i te uara mai i tēnei rā ki anamata. Kāore he momo moni e tino pupuri pai ana i te uara i ngā wā katoa, engari he pōturi ake te heke o te kaha hoko o ētahi momo i ētahi atu.

Mai rā anō, he maha ngā mea kua whakamahia hei moni: ko te angaanga, te tote, te pirepire, te kararehe pāmu, te konukura, te hiriwa me te kōura. I whakamahia whānuitia ngā konganuku utu nui nā te mea he onge, he roa te mau, ā, i whakaaetia whānuitia. Engari he uaua te kawe me te tiaki konganuku, nō reira ka tīmata ngā pēke ki te tuku pepa taunaki e taea ana te whakawhiti mō tētahi rahinga konganuku kua tohua. I raro i ngā momo rerekē o te **paerewa kōura**, i herea ā-ture tētahi waeine moni ki tētahi rahinga kōura. I rerekē ngā ture i tēnā wāhi, i tēnā wāhi, i tēnā wā, i tēnā wā.

Ka hipa te wā, ka nui ake te whakawhirinaki o te moni ki ngā whakahaere me ngā kupu taurangi, tēnā i tōna whakawhirinaki ki te konganuku. I te tau 1971, ka whakamutua e te Hononga o Amerika te whakawhitinga mana o ngā tāra ki te kōura mā ngā kāwanatanga o tāwāhi me ngā pēke matua. I ēnei rā, ko te nuinga o ngā moni ā-motu he **moni ā-ture**: he moni ka tukuna i raro i te mana o te kāwanatanga, mā tētahi pēke matua i te nuinga o te wā, ā, kāore e taea te whakawhiti mō tētahi rahinga pūmau o te kōura, o tētahi atu rawa hokohoko rānei. Ko te kupu fiat he kupu Rātini mō te "kia meatia", ā, ko te tikanga e mōhiotia whānuitia ana ko te "mā te whakatau mana".

Ka whai hua te moni ā-ture nā te whakaae a ngā tāngata, ngā pakihi, ngā pēke me ngā kāwanatanga ki taua moni. I te nuinga o te wā, ka whakamanahia taua moni e te ture hei moni utu ā-ture, ā, ka whakahaua kia whakamahia hei utu tāke. E hiahia ana hoki te tangata ki taua moni nā te mea ka whakamahia e te katoa e noho tata ana ki a ia.

**I neke tātou i te moni e tautokona ana e tētahi mea, ki te moni e tautokona ana e te whakawhirinaki ki tētahi tangata.**
