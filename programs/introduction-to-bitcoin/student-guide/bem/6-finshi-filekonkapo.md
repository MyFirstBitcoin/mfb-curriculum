# Finshi filekonkapo?

###### Indalama shashintilila pa mafunde na pa kucetekela

Indalama sha fiat shalenga ukulipila ukuba ukwayanguka, lelo ubwingi bwasho na mafunde yashitungulula fipangwa na mabungwe ayanono, kabili amaka yasho aya kushita ifintu kuti yaceepa ilyo inshita ilepita.

###### Bitcoin ipeela amafunde ayapusana

Ukufuma mu 2009, Bitcoin yapeela indalama ishingabomfiwa na onse, isha bwingi ubwishibikwa ku bantu bonse kabili ubukwata impela, ishingasuzumwa mu ncende ya kucetekelafye. Lelo na yo ilikwata amafya: ukulunduluka kwa mutengo, amalubo ayashingakonkololwa, na kusenda ubwasuko pa ficitwa fyenu.

###### Ubucingililo e bufwile ukutangila

Mubalilepo ukusambilila, mutendeke na finono, kabili musuzume fyonse. Mwilaabana amashiwi ya kubweseshapo icikwama cenu na muntu nangu umo. Tamufwile ukucita fyonse pa muku umo: ukucita icintu cimo icakonkapo mu kucingililwa kwalilinga.

###### Uku ni ku tendekafye

Mubwelele ku mepusho yasano yali ku ntendekelo. Mwingasuka shani nomba?

Iyi [Diploma ya Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/) e ntampulo yakonkapo. Ifiputulwa fyayo fine filemutwala pantanshi, ukufuma pa kwiluka indalama, ukuya pa musango Bitcoin ibomba, pa kubomfya Bitcoin mu kucingililwa, na pa Bitcoin mu bwikashi bwenu. Mutwalilile ukukabila ukwishiba, mutwalilile ukwipusha amepusho, kabili musambililepo na fimbi pa [myfirstbitcoin.org](https://myfirstbitcoin.org).
