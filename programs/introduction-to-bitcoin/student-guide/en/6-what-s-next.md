# What's Next?

###### Money depends on rules and trust

Fiat money makes payments convenient, but its supply and rules are set by a small number of institutions, and its purchasing power can fall over time.

###### Bitcoin offers different rules

Since 2009, Bitcoin has offered money with a public, limited supply, open to anyone, that can be checked rather than trusted. It also brings trade-offs: price swings, permanent mistakes, and personal responsibility.

###### Safety comes first

Learn first, start small, and verify everything. Never share your seed phrase. You don't need to do everything at once: one safe next step is enough.
