# Nu ka nye ga?

**Ga nye nuwo tɔtrɔ ƒe mɔnu.**

Ame aɖeke medi ga le ga la ŋutɔ ta o. Míedi ga le nusiwo míate ŋu atsɔe aƒle ta. Ga-agbalẽ nye agbalẽ kakɛ aɖe ko. Nusi naa wòwɔa dɔe nye be ame bubuwo alɔ̃ axɔe ɖe nusiwo míedi ŋutɔ la teƒe, elabena wole mɔ kpɔm be ame bubuwo hã axɔe emegbe.

Esiae ɖea ga to vovo tso nuwo tɔtrɔ ga manɔ eme la gbɔ.

Le nuwo tɔtrɔ ga manɔ eme ƒe ɖoɖo me la, asitsatsa ate ŋu adze edzi ne ame eveawo dometɔ ɖe sia ɖe di nusi le evelia si, le ɣeyiɣi ɖeka me eye le agbɔsɔsɔ si sɔ me ko. Ganyawo ŋuti nunyalawo yɔa esia be **ame eveawo ƒe nuhiãwo ƒe gododo**. Ne miele mɔli dem eye afɔkpa hiã mi la, dzeɖoeƒe ami ate ŋu ahiã afɔkpalɔla la, ke menye mɔli o. Ga kpɔa nyawo sia gbɔ to nudzadzra kple nuƒeƒle mama me: miedzraa miaƒe mɔli na ame aɖe eye mieƒlea afɔkpa le ame bubu gbɔ.

**Ga wɔa nu etɔ̃ zi geɖe.**

Enye **nuwo tɔtrɔ ƒe mɔnu**: amewo xɔnɛ abe fexexe ene. Enye **akɔntabubu ƒe dzidzemɔnu**: wotsɔa ga ɖea asiwo fia, eya ta míate ŋu atsɔ nu vovovowo ƒe asixɔxɔ asɔ kple wo nɔewo. Eye enye **asixɔxɔ dzraɖoƒe**: ekɔa asixɔxɔ tso egbe yi etsɔme. Ga aɖeke medzraa asixɔxɔ ɖo bliboe o, gake ga ƒomevi aɖewo ƒe nuƒeƒleŋusẽ doa ɖe megbe blewu wu bubuwo tɔ.

Le amegbetɔƒomea ƒe ŋutinya katã me la, wozã nu geɖewo abe ga ene: ƒudɔmenulawo ƒe akpakpawo, edze, dzonuwo, lãnyinyɔwo, akɔbli, klosalo kple sika. Wozã gayibɔ xɔasiwo le teƒe geɖewo elabena womenɔ bɔbɔe o, wohea ɣeyiɣi didi eye amewo xɔ wo le teƒe geɖewo. Gake gayibɔ ƒe kɔkɔ kple edzi kpɔkpɔ sesẽ, eya ta gadzraɖoƒewo dze agbalẽɖaseɖiwo nana gɔme, siwo woate ŋu atsɔ axe gayibɔ agbɔsɔsɔ aɖe si woɖo ɖi. Le **sika-nuti gaɖoɖo** ƒe ɖoɖo vovovowo me la, se bla ga ƒe dzidzemɔnu aɖe ɖe sika agbɔsɔsɔ aɖe ŋu. Seawo nɔ vovovom ɖe teƒe kple ɣeyiɣi nu.

Esi ɣeyiɣi nɔ yiyim la, ga va nɔ te ɖe dɔwɔƒewo kple ŋugbedodowo dzi wu gayibɔ. Le 1971 me la, United States tsi dollarwo xexe ɖe sika teƒe le duta dziɖuɖuwo kple gadzraɖoƒe vevitɔwo gbɔ le se nu la nu. Egbe la, dukɔwo ƒe ga akpa gãtɔ nye **gbeɖeɖe ga**: ga si wotsɔa do go le dziɖuɖu ƒe ŋusẽ te, zi geɖe to gadzraɖoƒe vevitɔ me, si womate ŋu atsɔ axe sika alo asinu bubu aɖeke ƒe agbɔsɔsɔ tɔxɛ aɖe o. Nya fiat nye Latin-gbe me nya si gɔmee nye "na woawɔe", eye zi geɖe la, wose egɔme be "ɖe gbeɖeɖe nu".

Gbeɖeɖe ga wɔa dɔ elabena amewo, asitsatsadɔwɔƒewo, gadzraɖoƒewo kple dziɖuɖuwo lɔ̃a xɔnɛ. Zi geɖe la, seawo naa wònye ga si woxɔna le se nu eye wobiana be woatsɔe axe adzɔ, eye amewo dinɛ elabena ame sia ame si le wo gbɔ la zãnɛ.

**Míetso ga si nane le megbe na la gbɔ, va ga si dzi ka ɖe ame aɖe dzi le megbe na la gbɔ.**
