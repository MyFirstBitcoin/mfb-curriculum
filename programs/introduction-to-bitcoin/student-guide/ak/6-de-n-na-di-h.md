# Ɛdeɛn na ɛdi hɔ?

###### Sika gyina mmara ne ahotosoɔ so

Sika a aban de tumi ma no ma ɛyɛ mmerɛ sɛ wɔbɛtua sika, nanso ahyehyɛdeɛ kakraa bi na ɛkyerɛ ne dodoɔ ne ɛho mmara, na nneɛma a wɔde bɛtumi atɔ no so bɛtumi ate berɛ rekɔ so.

###### Bitcoin de mmara foforɔ ba

Ɛfiri 2009 no, Bitcoin de sika a obiara bɛtumi de adi dwuma aba. Ne dodoɔ wɔ anohyetoɔ a obiara bɛtumi ahunu, na wɔbɛtumi ahwehwɛ mu de asi so dua sen sɛ wɔbɛgye adi kɛkɛ. Nanso, ɔhaw bi nso ka ho: ne boɔ sakra, mfomsoɔ bi ntumi nsiesie, na ɛsɛ sɛ obiara gye n'ankasa dwumadie ho asodie.

###### Ahobanbɔ di kan

Monni kan nsua adeɛ, momfa kakraa bi nhyɛ aseɛ, na monhwehwɛ biribiara mu nsi so dua. Mommfa mo safoa nsɛmfua mma obiara da. Ɛnhia sɛ moyɛ biribiara prɛko pɛ: anammɔn baako a ahobanbɔ wom a mobɛtu akɔ anim no ara dɔɔso.

###### Yei yɛ mfitiaseɛ no ara kwa

Monsan nkɔ nsɛmmisa nnum a ɛwɔ mfitiaseɛ no so. Mmuaeɛ bɛn na anka mobɛma seesei?

Saa [Bitcoin ho Diploma](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/) yi ne anammɔn a ɛdi hɔ. N'adesua afanan no de mo kɔ akyiri, ɛfiri sika ho nteaseɛ so, kɔ sɛdeɛ Bitcoin yɛ adwuma so, afei sɛdeɛ wɔde Bitcoin di dwuma ahobanbɔ mu, ne Bitcoin a wɔde di dwuma wɔ mo mpɔtam. Monkɔ so mpɛ sɛ mohunu nneɛma, monkɔ so mmisa nsɛm, na monkɔsua pii wɔ [myfirstbitcoin.org](https://myfirstbitcoin.org).
