# Dɛn Na Ɛhaw Yɛn Sika?

Sɛ nkurɔfoɔ te sɛ wɔreka nneɛma boɔ a ɛkɔ soro ho asɛm a, wɔtaa dwene sɛ nneɛma boɔ rekɔ soro.

**Nneɛma boɔ a ɛkɔ soro** ne sɛ nneɛma boɔ nyinaa mu nkyekyɛmu kɔ soro berɛ tenten. N'afã foforɔ ne **tumi a sika wɔ de tɔ nneɛma**: nneɛma dodoɔ a sika baako tumi tɔ. Sɛ nneɛma a wɔhia daa a ɛwɔ kɛntɛn baako mu boɔ firi sika 100 kɔ sika 110 a, sika 100 korɔ no ara ntumi ntɔ nneɛma a ɛwɔ kɛntɛn no mu nyinaa bio.

Nneɛma pii na ɛma nneɛma boɔ kɔ soro, na sikasɛm ho abenfoɔ gye nea emu biara ho hia ho akyinnyeɛ: sika ne sika a wɔde fɛm dodoɔ a ɛkɔ soro, akwansideɛ a ɛma nneɛma a wɔde ma no so te, nsakraeɛ wɔ nneɛma a nkurɔfoɔ pɛ sɛ wɔtɔ mu, ɔman sika a ne boɔ rete, ɛtoɔ, ne nneɛma a wɔyɛ ho ka. Ɛfata sɛ yɛde yɛn adwene si nea ɛde ba no mu baako so, ɛfiri sɛ ɛyɛ nea wɔhyɛ da paw, na ɛnyɛ akwanhyia: wɔ fiat sika nhyehyɛeɛ mu no, ahyehyɛdeɛ kakraa bi tumi ma sika ne sika a wɔde fɛm dodoɔ kɔ soro, na wɔsesa ho mmara.

Monnwene nea ɛma biribi som bo ho.

Mona Lisa mfonini ankasa no som bo sene nea wɔasua ayɛ no biara, ɛnyɛ sɛ ɛyɛ fɛ sene wɔn deɛ nti, na mmom sɛ ɛno nko ara na ɛwɔ hɔ nti. Biribi ho a ɛyɛ na nko ara mma ɛnsom bo, nanso sɛ nkurɔfoɔ pɛ biribi a, ne dodoɔ a ɛwɔ hɔ ho hia.

**Sɛ sika dodoɔ a ɛwɔ hɔ kɔ soro ntɛmntɛm sene nneɛma a wɔtumi de tɔ a, sika baako biara taa tɔ nneɛma kakraa bi.**

Nneɛma boɔ a ɛkɔ soro nka obiara pɛpɛɛpɛ. Obi a n'ahodeɛ mu fa kɛseɛ yɛ sika a ɛwɔ ne nsam anaa sika a wakora no bɛtumi ahwere tumi a ne sika wɔ de tɔ nneɛma no bi. Obi a ɔwɔ agyapadeɛ a ne boɔ kɔ soro no bɛtumi anya ahobammɔ bi. Afei nso, akatua ne nneɛma boɔ nsesa ntɛmntɛm pɛpɛɛpɛ.

Sika a ɛwɔ mo bɛnke akonta mu nso yɛ soronko wɔ sika a ɛwɔ mo nsam ho. Bɛnke nkora sika a nkurɔfoɔ de gu hɔ nyinaa sɛ sika a ɛwɔ ne nsam: ɛde emu fa kɛseɛ fɛm nkurɔfoɔ na ɛde yɛ adwuma de hwehwɛ mfasoɔ. **Sika dodoɔ a ɛda mo bɛnke akonta mu no yɛ bɔ a bɛnke no ahyɛ sɛ ɛbɛtua mo.** Wɔtaa di saa bɔhyɛ no so, nanso ɛgyina bɛnke no ne mmara a ɛkyerɛ sɛdeɛ ɛyɛ n'adwuma no so.

###### Monsɔ nhwɛ: monhwehwɛ tumi a sika wɔ de tɔ nneɛma mu

Mompaw adeɛ baako a nkurɔfoɔ taa tɔ wɔ mo mpɔtam, te sɛ paanoo, akwantuo ho ka, anaa sika a wɔde gu fon so. Montwerɛ ne boɔ a ɛyɛ nnɛ no nto hɔ. Mommisa obi ne boɔ a na ɛyɛ mfeɛ pii a atwam no, na sɛ mobɛtumi a, monkɔhwɛ ho kyerɛwtohɔ a moyɛ ho ahotosoɔ. Sika a nkurɔfoɔ nyaeɛ no kɔɔ soro saa ara anaa? Ɛdeɛn foforɔ na ɛbɛtumi akyerɛ nea enti a saa nsakraeɛ no baeɛ?

Yeinom mu biara nkyerɛ sɛ fiat sika nyɛ adwuma. Ɛma sika tua yɛ mmerɛ, ɛma wɔtumi sesa sika a wɔde fɛm ho nhyehyɛeɛ mu, na ɛma wɔnya akwan a wɔfa so di ahokyere ho dwuma. Nanso saa tumi a wɔde sesa nneɛma mu no ara de asiane ba: wɔtumi sesa ne dodoɔ ne ho mmara, na nnipa a wɔde di dwuma no nni ho tumi ahe biara.

**Yei nti na nkurɔfoɔ hwehwɛ akwan foforɔ, na yei nti nso na wɔyɛɛ Bitcoin.**
