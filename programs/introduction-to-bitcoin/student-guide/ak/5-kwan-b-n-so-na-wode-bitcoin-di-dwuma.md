# Ɔkwan Bɛn So Na Wode Bitcoin Di Dwuma?

Saa **Bitcoin sika kotoku yi** yɛ adwinnadeɛ, a ɛtaa yɛ apu, a ɛma motumi gye bitcoin, hwɛ mu, na mosoma. Wɔnkora mo bitcoin wɔ mo fon mu: wɔkyerɛw no wɔ blɔk ahoma no so. Sika kotoku no hwɛ **safoa** a ɛma motumi kyerɛ sɛ ɛyɛ mo dea na moma kwan ma wotua sika no so.

Enti asɛmmisa a ɛdi kan fa sika kotoku biara ho ne sɛ: **hwan na ɔwɔ safoa no so tumi?**

###### Hwan na ɔkura mo bitcoin ankasa?

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/f93df0cc4a2ad82b3410a9ad4c3273d1b760f8ea-1774x887.svg)

Sɛ mode **sika kotoku a mo ara mokura safoa no** di dwuma a, mo na mowɔ safoa no so tumi.

Sɛ mohyehyɛ no a, ɛkyerɛ mo **safoa nsɛmfua** (a wɔsan frɛ no nsɛmfua a wɔde san nya safoa): nsɛmfua a wɔahyehyɛ no nnidisoɔ nnidisoɔ, a ɛtaa yɛ 12 anaa 24. Ɛtumi san yɛ mo safoa wɔ afidie foforɔ so. Obiara a ɔwɔ saa nsɛmfua no tumi fa mo bitcoin. Safoa nsɛmfua no nyɛ nea wɔde sie sɛ biribi sɛe a wɔde bɛsi ananmu nko ara: ɛma kwan ma obi tumi di biribiara so.

Mommɔ ho ban:

* monkyerɛw no pɛpɛɛpɛ wɔ krataa so bere a moyɛ sika kotoku no;
* monkora no wɔ baabi a ɛnni intanɛt so, ɛyɛ kokoam, na ɛrenyera anaa ɛrensɛe;
* monntwa ho mfonini da, mommfa ɛmɛɛl so nsoma, monnkora no wɔ intanɛt so nsɛm a wɔakyerɛw agu hɔ mu, anaa mommfa nkra mu nsoma;
* monnkyerɛw no ngu wɛbsaet biara so da.

Sɛ mo safoa nsɛmfua ne mo afidie yera a, obiara ntumi nsan mfa mo bitcoin mma mo.

Sɛ mode **sika kotoku a adwumakuo bi kura safoa no** di dwuma a, adwumakuo bi na ɛkura safoa no ma mo, te sɛ nea sikakorabea yɛ no kakra. Sika a ɛwɔ mo akonta mu no yɛ bɔ a adwumakuo no ahyɛ sɛ ɛbɛtua mo. Sɛnea wɔhyehyɛ no yɛ nea monim dedaw, na ebia mmoa bɛba, nanso mode mo ho to saa adwumakuo no so: ɛtumi to sika a moyi ano hyeɛ, si akonta bi ho kwan, kɔmputa so nsɛmmɔnedifoɔ tumi wura mu, anaa ɛtumi to mu.


| Asɛmmisa | Adwumakuo na ɛkura safoa | Mo ara na mokura safoa |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hwan na ɔkura safoa no? | Adwumakuo no | Mo |
| Hwan na ɔhwɛ ma wɔsan nya kwan kɔ mu? | Adwumakuo no, ɛnam mo akonta so | Mo, ɛnam mo safoa nsɛmfua so |
| Mfasoɔ ne asɛdeɛ titire | Ɛyɛ mmerɛ na mmoa wɔ hɔ, nanso mode mo ho to adwumakuo no so | Mowɔ so tumi tẽẽ, nanso mo na ɛsɛ sɛ mokora nneɛma a mode bɛsi ananmu no so, na mfomsoɔ ho asɛdeɛ da mo so |


Asɛm bi a ɛho wɔ mfasoɔ wɔ Bitcoin wiase ne sɛ: **sɛ safoa no nyɛ mo dea a, sika no nso nyɛ mo dea.** Sɛ mopaw nea ɔbɛkura safoa no a, ɛnyɛ sɔhwɛ a ɛkyerɛ mo boɔ. Nea ɛho hia ne sɛ mobɛhunu ɔkwan ko a mode redi dwuma ne nea mode mo ho reto so.

My First Bitcoin nkamfo sika kotoku pɔtee bi nkyerɛ. Nnwinnadeɛ, ɛka ne wɔn a wɔyɛ nneɛma no wuranom sesa, na nea ɛfata mpɔtam bi no renfata foforɔ. Mommisa ɔkyerɛkyerɛfoɔ a mowɔ ne mu ahotosoɔ nea nkurɔfoɔ de di dwuma wɔ mo mpɔtam, na mo ankasa monhwɛ saa nneɛma yi mu ansa na mode sika kotoku biara adi dwuma:

* **Safoa:** hwan na ɔwɔ safoa no so tumi, mo anaa adwumakuo bi?
* **Kwan a wɔfa so san nya kwan kɔ mu:** sɛ mo fon yera anaa adwumakuo no nni hɔ bio a, ɛdeɛn na ɛbɛsi?
* **Baabi a ɛfiri:** motwee no firii ɛho wɛbsaet ankasa anaa apu sotɔɔ no so, na ɛnyɛ link bi a obi de brɛɛ mo so?
* **Kɔmputa mmara:** ɛyɛ nea obiara tumi hwɛ ne kɔmputa mmara no mu na wɔkɔ so hwɛ siesie no? (Sɛ obiara tumi hwɛ kɔmputa mmara no mu nko ara nkyerɛ sɛ adwinnadeɛ bi yɛ nea asiane nni ho.)
* **Ɛka:** ɛka bɛn na wɔgye wɔ sika a mosoma, mogye anaa moyi ho?
* **Sikatua:** ɛboa sikatua a mohia, wɔ blɔk nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn no so anaa Lightning so?

Bitcoin wɔ akwan mmienu a wɔtaa fa so tua sika. **Blɔk nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn so** sikatua no, wɔkyere gu blɔk nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn no mu tẽẽ. **Lightning** yɛ sikatua ntam nkitahodie a wɔasi wɔ Bitcoin so de tua sika nketenkete ntɛmntɛm ne mpɛn pii, te sɛ kɔfe a wɔtɔ. Lightning sikakotoku nso, obi foforɔ bɛtumi ahwɛ so ama mo, anaa mo ara mobɛhwɛ so.

Mmara mmiɛnsa bɛbɔ mo ho ban berɛ a moresua no:

1. **Monsua adeɛ ansa.** Mommma anigyeɛ, ehu anaa nhyɛsoɔ mma monnyɛ biribi.
1. **Momfa kakra nfi aseɛ.** Momfa sika kakra a sɛ ɛyera a, mobɛtumi agyina ano nsɔ nnwinnadeɛ a monnim ho hwee no nhwɛ.
1. **Mommfa mo ho nto so kɛkɛ, monhwehwɛ mu nni ho adanseɛ.** Monhwɛ onipa no, ɔkwan a wɔfa so tua sika no, adrɛs no ne sika dodoɔ no mu ansa na moapene sikatua bi so.

**Momfa nsɛm a wɔde gye sika nkyɛ, na monnkyɛ ahintasɛm da.** Mobɛtumi de adrɛs anaa abisadeɛ a ɛfa sikatua ho akyɛ. Ɛnsɛ sɛ mode nsɛmfua a wɔde san bue sikakotoku anaa kokoam safoa kyɛ da. Ɔkyerɛkyerɛfoɔ, sikakotoku adwumakuo anaa mmoa adwumayɛni a ɔdi nokorɛ biara remmisa mo saa nsɛm no da.

Monnyae sɛ obi hyɛ mo sɛ monyɛ biribi ntɛm anaa kokoam, ɔhyɛ mo bɔ sɛ akyinnyeɛ biara nni ho sɛ mobɛnya mfasoɔ, ɔka sɛ monni kan mfa bitcoin mmrɛ no, anaa ɔbisa mo nsɛmfua a wɔde san bue sikakotoku no a. **Montwɛn kakra. Monnkyɛ biribiara. Mommfa biribiara nkɔma obiara.** Afei momfa baabi a monim dada sɛ monya nsɛm firi hɔ no so nhwehwɛ mu.
