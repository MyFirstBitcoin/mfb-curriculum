# Ncinzi Citobela?

###### Mali aayima amilawo akusyomana

Mali aa fiat aapa kuti kubbadala kube kuuba, pele bunji bwawo amilawo yawo zibikkwa amabungwe masyoonto, alimwi nguzu zyaawo zyakugula zilakonzya kuceya mukweenda kwaciindi.

###### Bitcoin iipa milawo iiyandene

Kuzwa mu 2009, Bitcoin yapa mali aajisi bunji buli aamamanino alimwi buzibidwe abantu boonse, aajanika kuli uuli woonse, aakonzya kusinizigwa kutali kusyomwa buyo. Pele ilaleta azintu ziyandika kuyeeyesya: kucinca-cinca kwamuulo, kuphonya kutakonzyi kululamikwa, alimwi mulandu wakulisunga nobeni.

###### Bukwabilizi bulaamba

Amwiile ntaanzi, amutaangile atuntu tusyoonto, alimwi amusinizye zyoonse. Mutapa muntu uumbi majwi aanu aakujulusya cikwama. Tamweelede kucita zyoonse aciindi comwe: ntampu yomwe iitobela iikwabilidwe ilakwana.

###### Aaku nkutaanguna buyo

Amujokele kumibuzyo iisanu yakutaanguna. Munga mwaingula buti lino?

Iyi [Diploma ya Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/) ndiyo ntampu iitobela. Zibeela zyayo zyakwiiya zyone zilamutola kunembo, kuzwa kukumvwisisisya mali, kuya kukuti Bitcoin ibeleka buti, kuya kukubelesya Bitcoin munzila iikwabilidwe, kusikila kuli Bitcoin mucooko canu. Amuzumanane kuyandisisya kuzyiba, kubuzya mibuzyo, alimwi amwiiye zinji ku [myfirstbitcoin.org](https://myfirstbitcoin.org).
