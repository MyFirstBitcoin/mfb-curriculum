# Mubelesya Buti Bitcoin?

Cimwi **cikwama ca Bitcoin** ncibelesyo, kanji cipulogilamu ca mufwuunwa, cimuzumizya kutambula, kulanganya akutumizya bitcoin. Bitcoin yanu tiibikkwi mukati ka mufwuunwa wanu: ilembedwe pa blockchain. Cikwama cilanganya **makiyi** aamuzumizya kusinizya kuti njiyanu akuzumizya kubbadela.

Aboobo, muzyo wakusaanguna kujatikizya cikwama cili coonse nguyu: **ngwani uulanganya makiyi?**

###### Ngwani ncobeni uujisi bitcoin yanu?

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/f93df0cc4a2ad82b3410a9ad4c3273d1b760f8ea-1774x887.svg)

Kwiinda mukubelesya **cikwama ncomulilanganizya nobeni**, ndinywe mulanganya makiyi.

Ciindi nomucibamba, cimutondezya **majwi aambuto** (aabitwa alimwi kuti majwi aakubweedezya): mulongo wamajwi aabikkidwe munzila yeelede, kanji 12 naa 24. Alakonzya kubamba alimwi makiyi aanu mucibelesyo cimbi. Uuli woonse uujisi majwi aaya ulakonzya kubweza bitcoin yanu. Majwi aambuto taali aakubweedezya buyo: alapa nguzu zyoonse zyakunjila akubelesya bitcoin yanu.

Aakwabilileni:

* aalembeni kabotu-kabotu apepa ciindi nomubamba cikwama;
* aabikkani kutali a intaneti, mucisisi, abusena mpataakajayi naa kunyonyoonwa;
* mutajisi cifwanikiso camajwi aaya, mutaatumizyi mu imeyili, mutaabikki mumalembe aabambwa pa intaneti, naa kuatuma mumulumbe;
* mutalembi majwi aaya awebusayiti.

Na mwajaya majwi aanu aambuto acibelesyo canu, taakwe uukonzya kumubweedezya bitcoin yanu.

Kwiinda mukubelesya **cikwama cilanganizyigwa akkampani**, kkampani njimubambila makiyi, mbuli mbicita bbange. Mali aatondezyegwa mucikwama canu ncisyomezyo cakkampani cakuti ilamubbadela. Nzila yakucibamba njimuuzyi alimwi mulakonzya kujana lugwasyo, pele muyeeme akk upampani eeyo: ilakonzya kubikka mipaka yakugwisya mali, kwimik aakaunti, kunjililwa aabantu babba, naa kujalwa.


| Muzyo | Cilanganizyigwa akkampani | Mucilanganizya nobeni |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ngwani uujisi makiyi? | Kkampani | Nywebo |
| Ngwani uulanganya kubweedezya? | Kkampani, kwiinda mu aakaunti yanu | Nywebo, kwiinda mumajwi aanu aambuto |
| Cintu cipati ncomujana ancomusiya | Kuuba cuuba akugwasyigwa, pele muyeeme akkampani | Kulanganizya nobeni, pele mulijisi mulimo wakubamba zyakwiiya kubweedezya akulilemeka pazilubizyo |


Majwi aagwasya munyika ya Bitcoin ngaaya: **na makiyi tali aanu, amali tali aanu.** Kusala uulanganya makiyi tacili cilenganyo cabulemu bwanu. Ciyandika nkuzyiba musyobo ngomubelesya acintu ncomusyoma.

My First Bitcoin taisalili bantu zikwama zimwi. Zibelesyo, mali aakubelesya zikwama, abene bazyo zilacinja, alimwi ceelela umwi musyobo wabantu cilakonzya kutayelela umbi. Buz y ani muyiisyi uusyomeka kuti bantu bamucilawo canu bab elesya nzi, alimwi langanyani zintu eezi nobeni kamutanabelesya cikwama cili coonse:

* **Makiyi:** ngwani uulanganya makiyi, ndinywe naa nikkampani?
* **Kubweedezya:** ncinzi cicitika na mufwuunwa wanu wajaya naa kk ampani yamana?
* **Nkokizwa:** mwakacigwisya awebusayiti yacigaminina naa abusena bwacigaminina mucitolo camapulogilamu, kutali kwiinda acijatikizyo ncomwakatumizigwa amuntu?
* **Mabambidwe aacipulogilamu:** mabambidwe aaco alavwulidwe kuti bantu boonse baabone akua langanya, alimwi cilalanganizyigwa lyoonse? (Kuvwula mabambidwe aacipulogilamu buyo takusinizyi kuti cibelesyo cila lukwabililo.)
* **Mali aakubelesya:** mbubotu nzi bwamali buleelede kubbadelwa pakutumizya, kutambula, naa kugwisya mali?
* **Kubbadala:** cilazumizya kubbadala munzila njomuyanda, mukati ka blockchain na kwiinda mu Lightning?

Bitcoin ilijisi nzila zyobilo zyibelesyegwa kapati kubbadala. **Mukati ka blockchain** kubbadala kulalembwa cakutalika mu blockchain. **Lightning** ngumukwatano wakubbadala uuyakidwe a Bitcoin kuti kubbadala mali masyoonto, cakufwambaana alimwi kanji-kanji, nkokuli kuula kofi. Cikwama ca Lightning cilakonzya kubikkilwa amwi mali aayanganilwa amuntu umwi naa aayanganilwa andinywe nobeni.

Milawo yotatwe iyoomukwabilila ciindi nomwiiya:

1. **Amwiiye kutanga.** Mutaciti cintu akaambo kakukkomana kapati, kuyoowa naa kuninizyigwa.
1. **Amutalike amasyoonto.** Amusunke zyakubelesya nzyomutanaziba amali ngomuzyiba kuti naa mwaluza, tamukoocisa kapati.
1. **Mutasyomi Buyo, Amusinizye.** Amubone kuti muntu, nzila yakubbadala, adilesi alimwi amali zyiluzi antoomwe kamutanazumizya kubbadala.

**Amwaabane makani aakutambula mali, kutali zintu zyacisisi.** Adilesi naa cikumbiyo cakubbadalwa cilakonzya kwaabanwa. Mabala aakuvwusya cikwama naa kiyi yacisisi tazyeelede kwaabanwa pe. Taakwe muyiisi uusyomeka, kampani yacikwama naa mubeleki ugwasya uuyoomulomba zintu eezyi.

Amuleke naa umwi wamuninilizya kuti mufwambaane naa musise, wamulaya kuti kuyooboola mali cakutadooneka, wamulomba kuti mutumizye bitcoin kutanga, naa wamulomba mabala aanu aakuvwusya cikwama. **Amuyoomane. Mutaaabani cintu. Mutatumizyi cintu.** Mpawo amusinizye kwiinda kumuntu naa kubusena bwamakani nkomuzi kale.
