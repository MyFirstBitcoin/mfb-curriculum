# Nkaambo Nzi Mali Ngaayandika?

Bantu banji tabaimiki kuti bayeeye atala a mali.

Kutali kusyoma mali, kutali kwaabelesya, pele kuyeeya ncobeni kuti mali ncinzi alimwi kuti asebenza buti.

**Tubelesya mali buzuba abuzuba, pele tatuzyi kubuzya kuti asebenza buti.**

Amutanatalika kubala kuya kunembo, amubikke kaindi kuswiiyo zyosanwe. Amulembe bwiinguzi bufwaafwi kucili coonse naa mulakonzya:

1. Nkaambo nzi bantu bayandila mali?
1. Ncinzi ciinda kuti cintu cibe mali?
1. Nguni uukonzya kulenga naa kweendelezya mali ngomubelesya?
1. Nkaambo nzi mali ngomubelesya ajisi bulemu?
1. Sena mali aanu alakonzya kuula zintu ziingi mbubonya sunu mbuli mbwaakacita kaindi?

Amukoye bwiinguzi bwanu. Amubupilukile kumamanino. Bbuku eeli lilakonzya kuyumya imwi mizeezo yanu akucinca imwi.

Nomubala, amubone kusiyana akati ka ncobeni amizeezo yamuntu. "Mali aaya aapepa alatambulwa kubbadala mitelo" ncobeni cikonzya kusyomezyegwa. "Aaya ngoomali mabotu kwiinda oonse" mbubambe bwamuzeezo buuyanda zintu zyakulinganya amboni.

Bbuku eeli lyakabambwa kwiinda mukwiingula swiiyo zyosanwe acimwi: nkaambo nzi mali ayandikana, mali ncinzi, ncinzi cikonzya kubiipa aali ngawo, Bitcoin ncinzi, mbomukonzya kuibelesya cakukwabilila, aziboola kunembo.

Nlyakuyiisya buyo. Talipe lulayo atala a mali, kubikka mali mumulimo kuti avwule, milawo, naa mitelo, alimwi talimwambii kuti muule, muuzye, naa mukoye cintu cili coonse.
