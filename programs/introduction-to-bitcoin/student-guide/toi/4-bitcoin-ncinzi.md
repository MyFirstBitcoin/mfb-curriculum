# Bitcoin ncinzi?

Bitcoin kaitanaba, kutuma mali kwi intaneti lyoonse kwakali kusyomeka kubunga busyomeka: bbanga na kampani yakulipilana yakali kubamba bbuku lyamali, kuzumizya maakaunti, alimwi yakali kukonzya kucelezya, kukasya, na kujokozya kulipila. Kwamyaka minji, basyaabusongo bwamakompyuta bakeezya kupanga mali aama kompyuta akutali mubambisi omwe uusalala. Cintu cakali kuyumya ncakuti twaambo twamu kompyuta tulakonzya kukopololwa cakufwambaana. Ikuti mali aamu kompyuta akakonzya kukopololwa akubelesegwa ziindi zyobilo, taakali kukonzya kubeleka mbuli mali. Eeci ciitwa kuti **penzi lyakubelesya mali omwe ziindi zyobilo**.

Mu 31 Oktoba 2008, muntu na kabunga kakali kubelesya zina lyakuti Satoshi Nakamoto wakazwayisya lugwalo lwakali kupandulula nzila yakumanizya penzi eeli: _Bitcoin: Nzila Yamali Aamakompyuta Aatumwa Kuzwa Kumuntu Kuya Kumuntu_. Mutanda wamakompyuta wakatalika mu Janwali 2009.

Bitcoin ni mali aamakompyuta aa **tabuswi abusolozi bomwe**, aajisi **mweelwe uujisi mpesi**, alimwi alakonzya kutumwa **kuzwa kumuntu kuya kumuntu** akutali kuzumizyigwa abbanga.

Kuba **kutabuswi abusolozi bomwe** caamba kuti taakwe ofesi iisalala iikonzya kupanga maakaunti, kucinca mali aali mumaakaunti, na kuzumizya bayandika kunjila. Muntu uuli woonse ulakonzya kubweza akutololesya pulogilamu iili akodi iijulikide boonse, alimwi makompyuta manji aaliiminina alikke alabweza makopi aamagwalo aalembwa. Eeci tacaambi kuti boonse bajisi nguzu zyeelene. Caamba kuti taakwe muntu omwe uunjizigwa uukonzya kucincila boonse bbuku lyamali na milawo yamali.

Kujana **mweelwe uujisi mpesi** caamba kuti takukabi bitcoin ziinda ku 21 miliyoni. bitcoin mpya zilazwayisyigwa kweendelana aciindi cizibidwe abantu boonse, alimwi makompyuta aaliiminina alikke aaitwa kuti ma node alakaka bbulooko lyoonse lityola mulawo ooyu. Mpesi eeyi ilasyomeka nkaambo muntu uuli woonse ulakonzya kuisinizya. Pele tauli mulawo wacilengwa: ulazumanana nkaambo bantu banjizigwa balazumanana kuusunga. Alimwi mweelwe uujisi mpesi taupi cisyomezya cakuti muulo unootanta. Muulo usyomeka akuyandwa kwamali alimwi amweelwe wawo uulipo.

Kuba **kuzwa kumuntu kuya kumuntu** caamba kuti mulakonzya kutuma bitcoin kumuntu uuli woonse, kulikoonse, akutali kujula akaunti kumutanda wa Bitcoin na kulomba kuzumizyigwa kubusolozi bomwe. Taakwe kampani iikonzya kukasya kubelesegwa kwa bitcoin njomulibambila nobeni. Lubazu lumbi lwaluumuno oolu nkulilemeka mulimo: kutumizya mali kuzumizyidwe kanji-kanji takukonzyi kujokozigwa, alimwi taakwe ofesi yakupa lugwasyo kumutanda ooyu.

Kumvwisya kutali kabotu kumwi nkukwaamba kuti Bitcoin tayikonzyi kujanwa nkaambo bitcoin yomwe iladula. **Tamweelede kuula na kubelesya bitcoin yomwe iili yoonse.** bitcoin yomwe ilaabanyigwa kuzwa mutupa tusyoonto tuli 100,000,000 twiitwa kuti **satoshis**, na kuti **sats**. Kujana 100,000 sats akujana 0.001 bitcoin kwaamba mweelwe omwe.

Mutanda ooyu usyomeka kubantu basiyene-siyene banjizigwa: **babelesya** batuma, batambula, akubamba bitcoin; **ma node** asinizya kutumizya mali koonse abbulooko lyoonse kuti zilatobela milawo; alimwi **basimi** bapalisana kuyoongela bbulooko mpya kumagwalo aalembwa mu ciindi ciinda afwaafwi maminiti aali kkumi. Magwalo aayo, aaitwa kuti **ciketani camabbulooko**, alijulikide boonse alimwi muntu uuli woonse ulakonzya kwaabona, pele kwaalemba lubo kuladula kapati.

Kaambo kajatikizya malembedwe: **Bitcoin** iilembedwe a B mupati kanji-kanji yaamba mutanda na nzila yoonse iibeleka. **bitcoin** iilembedwe a b musyoonto yaamba mali aayo ncobeni.

Bitcoin ilijisi zintu zibotu azitali kabotu. Muulo wayo ulakonzya kucinca cakufwambaana. Zilubizyo kanji-kanji tazikonzyi kululamisyigwa. Kutambulwa kwayo kucili kusyoonto mumaanze manji, alimwi kuibelesya cakukwabilila kuyandika kwiiya zimwi zintu. Bitcoin ilabwezya cisyomezyo cimwi cili kubunga akucibikka mukusinizya akulilemeka mulimo. Taigusyi ngozi.
