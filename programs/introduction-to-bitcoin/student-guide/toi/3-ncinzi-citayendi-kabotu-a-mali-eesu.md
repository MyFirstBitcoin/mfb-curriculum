# Ncinzi Citayendi Kabotu a Mali Eesu?

Bantu nibamvwa bbala lyakuti kusumpuka kwamitengo, kanji bayeeya kuti mitengo ilakwela.

**Kusumpuka kwamitengo** nkukwela kwamitengo yazintu zyoonse kutazumini. Lubazu lumbi ndwa **nguzu yakugula**: bungi bwazintu nzyokonzya kugula cibeela comwe camali. Ikuti mutengo wacisambwe cazintu ziyandika wabantu wakwela kuzwa kuzibeela zyamali ziinda ku 100 kusika ku 110, zibeela eezyo ziinda ku 100 tazikonzyi kugula cisambwe coonse pe.

Kusumpuka kwamitengo kuli azintu zyinji zikupa kuti kucitike, alimwi basikwiiya makani aabukkale bwabusambazi balazyubuzya kuti cimwi acimwi cilagwasya buti: kuyungizika kwamali azikwelete, kunyongana kwakusandulula zintu, kucinca kwakuyandwa kwazintu, kuceya kwanguzu yamali, misonko, amali aabelesegwa mukupanga zintu. Cimwi cintu ceelede kulangisisyigwa nkaambo cilasalwa, tacicitiki buyo: mububambe bwamali aafiat, mabunga masyoonto alakonzya kuyungizya mali azikwelete akucinca milawo yazyo.

Amuyeeye ncecakupa kuti cintu cibe amulimu.

Mufwanikiso wakusaanguna wa Mona Lisa ulijisi mulimu mupati kwiinda mufwanikiso uuli woonse wakopololwa kulinguwe, kutali nkaambo ulaboneka kabotu kwiinda imbi, pele nkaambo nguwe alike. Kuceya kwacintu kulike takucipi mulimu pe, pele bantu nibayanda cintu, bungi bwaco bulijisi mulimu.

**Bungi bwamali nibuyungizika cakufwambaana kwiinda zintu nzyakonzya kugula, cibeela comwe acomwe camali cilatandika kugula zintu zisyoonto.**

Kusumpuka kwamitengo takukwati bantu boonse munzila iikozyenye pe. Muntu uujisi mali manji mumaboko naakuti mali aabikkide ulakonzya kusweekelwa nguzu yakugula. Muntu uujisi zintu zyabusambazi zikwelwa mitengo ulakonzya kukwabililwa kulubazu lumwi. Miholo amitengo azyalo zilacinca cakufwambaana kutakozyenye.

Mali aali muakaunti yanu yakubbanka alaindene amali aali mumaboko aanu. Bbanki taibambi mali oonse aabikkwamo kuti abe mumaboko pe: ilakopesya mali manji akuaabelesya mumilimo iizyalusya mali. **Mali aali muakaunti yakubbanka ncisyomezyo cabbanki cakuti ilamupa mali aanu.** Kanji cisyomezyo eeci cilazuzikizyigwa, pele ciyeme kubbanki akumilawo iilanga milimo yayo.

###### Amusoleke: kulanga nguzu yakugula

Amusale cintu comwe cibeleka mazuba oonse mucilawo canu, mbuli cinkwa, kweenda, naakuti eya tayimu yafoni. Amulembe mutengo waco wanyamwaca. Amubuzye muntu kuti cali mali anga mumyaka yakumane, mpoona amulange mulugwalo lusyomeka ikuti mulakonzya. Sena mali abantu ngibajana aakayungizika munzila iikozyenye? Ncinzi cimbi cikonzya kupandulula kucinca ooku?

Zyoonse eezyi tazyaambi kuti mali aafiat taabeleseki pe. Alapa nzila ziubauba zyakulipila, zikwelete zikonzya kucincwa kweelana azileelede, anzila zyakulwana mapenzi mapati. Pele kukonzya kucinca ooku kulaleta ntenda: bungi bwamali aaya amilawo yaako zilakonzya kucinca, alimwi bantu baabelesya mali aaya balijisi nguzu zisyoonto zyakupa mizeezo yabo.

**Eeci ncecakupa kuti bantu bayandaule nzila zimbi, alimwi ncecakupa kuti Bitcoin ipangwe.**
