# Mali Ncinzi?

**Mali ncibelesyo cakusanduluzya zintu.**

Kunyina muntu uuyanda mali akaambo kamali buyo. Tulayanda mali akaambo kazintu nzyakonzya kuula. Mali aapepa mbuli cipanzi capepa buyo. Aabeleseka akaambo kakuti bamwi bantu balazumina kwaatambula kutegwa batupe zintu nzyotuyanda ncobeni, nkaambo balasyoma kuti bamwi bayakuyazumina kumbele.

Eeci ncecilesya mali kwaambana akusanduluzya zintu azintu zimwi.

Muburyo bwakusanduluzya zintu azintu zimwi, kusambala kulacitika buyo kuti bantu bobilo bayanda zintu nzyajisi umwi, aciindi comwe alimwi mumyeelwe yeelede. Basyaazibwene mubukwebo bacita eeci kuti **kuyandana zintu kwa bantu bobilo aciindi comwe**. Na mulima mpunga alimwi muyanda nsangu, uupanga nsangu ulakonzya kuyanda mafuta aakujika, kutali mpunga. Mali alamanizya penzi eeli kwiinda mukwaanzya kusambala akuula: mulasambalila muntu omwe mpunga wanu, mpoona mwaula nsangu kuli umwi.

**Mali kanji-kanji alacita zintu zitatu.**

Mali ndi **nzila yakusanduluzya zintu**: bantu balazumina kwaatambula mbubonya bwakubbadelwa. Alimwi ndi **cipimo cakubalila mitengo**: mitengo ilatalikwa mumali, eelyo tulakonzya kweezyanisya bulemu bwazintu zyaandeene. Alimwi ndi **nzila yakubamba bulemu**: alanyaamuna bulemu kuzwa sunu kuya kumbele. Kunyina mali aabamba bulemu cakumaninina, pele misyo imwi yamali ilaluza nguzu zyakugula asyoonto-asyoonto kwiinda imwi.

Kufumbwa mucaano, zintu zinji zyakabelesegwa mbuli mali: zikumba, munyo, bulungu, banyama bakumunzi, mukuba, siliva, angolida. Zisyano ziyandikana zyakatalika kubelesegwa mumasena manji nkaambo zyakasyoonto, zyakali kukkala ciindi cilamfwu, alimwi zyakali kuzuminwa abantu banji. Pele kunyamuna akukwabilila zisyano kulayuma, aboobo mabbanki akatalika kupa mapepa aapa nguzu zyakutambula mweelwe wazisyano uutalidwe. Mumisyo yaandeene ya **nzila yakuswaanganya mali angolida**, cipimo camali cakali kuswaanganyizyigwa amweelwe wangolida kweelana amulawo. Milawo yakali kwaambana kweelana abusena aciindi.

Kwinda kwaciindi, mali akatalika kusyomeka kapati kuzwa kumabungwe azisyomezyo kwiinda kuzisyano. Mu 1971, United States yakalekezya kusanduluzya madola angolida munzila yazumizyidwe kuli balelo bamasi amwi amabbanki aakati. Sunu, mali aamasiyabunji ndi **mali aa fiat**: mali aapegwa ansi panguzu zabulaleli, kanji-kanji kwiinda kubbanki lyakati, aatakonzya kusandululwa angolida mumweelwe uutalidwe naaciba acintu cimbi. Ijwi lyakuti fiat lizwa mu Latin, caamba kuti "kacitwe", kanji-kanji alimvwa mbuli "amulawo".

Mali aa fiat alabeleseka nkaambo bantu, makwebo, mabbanki, abalelo balazumina kwaatambula. Milawo kanji-kanji ilapa mali aaya nguzu zyakubelesegwa mukubbadala alimwi ilayanda kuti abelesegwe mukubbadala mitelo, eelyo bantu balayaayanda nkaambo boonse babazingulukide balayaabelesya.

**Twakazwa kumali aakali kujatikizigwa acintu, kuya kumali aajatikizigwa akusyoma muntu.**
