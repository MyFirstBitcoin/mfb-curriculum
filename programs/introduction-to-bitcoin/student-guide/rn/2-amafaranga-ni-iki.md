# Amafaranga ni iki?

**Amahera ni igikoresho co guhanahana.**

Nta muntu yipfuza amahera kubera yo ubwayo. Tuyipfuza kubera ivyo ashobora kugura. Inoti ni urupapuro gusa. Igituma iba ngirakamaro ni uko abandi bantu bazoyemera mu kuyiguza ibintu twipfuza vy'ukuri, kuko biteze ko n'abandi bazoyemera mu nyuma.

Ivyo ni vyo bitandukanya amahera no kugurana ibintu ku bindi.

Mu buryo bwo kugurana ibintu ku bindi, uguhanahana gushoboka gusa igihe abantu bompi bipfuza ivyo uwundi afise, ku mwanya umwe kandi ku rugero rubereye. Abahinga mu vy'ubutunzi ivyo bavyita **uguhura kw'ivyipfuzo vya bompi**. Nimba murima umuceri kandi mukeneye ibirato, uwukora ibirato ashobora kuba yipfuza amavuta yo guteka, atari umuceri. Amahera atorera umuti iyo ngorane mu gutandukanya ukudandaza no kugura: mudandariza umuceri wanyu umuntu umwe, mukagura ibirato ku wundi.

**Amahera asanzwe arangura ibikorwa bitatu.**

Ni **uburyo bwo guhanahana**: abantu barayemera mu kwishurwa. Ni **igipimo co guharura agaciro**: ibiciro bigaragazwa hakoreshejwe amahera, bigatuma dushobora kugereranya agaciro k'ibintu bitandukanye. Kandi ni **uburyo bwo kubika agaciro**: abika agaciro k'uyu musi kugira kazokoreshwe muri kazoza. Nta mahera abika agaciro ata gahaze, ariko ubwoko bumwebumwe bw'amahera butakaza ubushobozi bwo kugura buhorobuhoro kurusha ubundi.

Kuva kera, ibintu vyinshi vyarakoreshejwe nk'amahera: ibishishwa vy'ibikoko vyo mu mazi, umunyu, amasaro, ibitungwa, umujumpu, ifeza n'inzahabu. Ivyuma vy'agaciro vyaratanguye gukoreshwa cane kubera vyari imbonekarimwe, bikaramba kandi bikemerwa na benshi. Ariko gutwara no gukingira ivyuma biragoye, ni co gituma amabanki yatanguye gutanga impapuro zemeza uburenganzira bwo kuronka urugero rw'icuma rwanditse kuri zo. Mu buryo butandukanye bwa **gahunda yo gushingira agaciro k'amahera ku nzahabu**, igice kimwe c'ifaranga cari gifatanijwe n'urugero rw'inzahabu hakurikijwe amategeko. Amategeko yarahinduka bivanye n'ahantu n'igihe.

Uko igihe cagenda kirarengana, amahera yarushirije gushingira ku nzego no ku mihango aho gushingira ku vyuma. Mu 1971, Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika zarahagaritse ku mugaragaro uguhindurira amadolari mu nzahabu kw'amaleta y'amahanga n'amabanki nkuru. Uyu musi, amahera y'ibihugu vyinshi ni **amahera ashingiye kw'itegeko rya Leta**: amahera asohorwa ku bubasha bwa Leta, akenshi biciye mw'ibanki nkuru, adashobora guhindurirwa ku rugero rwashinzwe rw'inzahabu canke rw'ikindi kidandazwa ico ari co cose. Ijambo fiat ni iry'ikilatini risobanura "nibikoreke", rikaba akenshi ritahurwa nk'irisobanura "kw'itegeko".

Amahera ashingiye kw'itegeko rya Leta arakora kubera abantu, amashirahamwe y'ubudandaji, amabanki n'amaleta bayemera. Amategeko akenshi atuma aba amahera yemewe yo kwishura kandi agasaba ko akoreshwa mu kuriha amakori; abantu na bo bakayipfuza kubera abo babana bose bayakoresha.

**Twavuye ku mahera ashingiye ku kintu, tuja ku mahera ashingiye ku kwizigira umuntu.**
