# Ni igiki gikurikira?

###### Amahera ashingiye ku mategeko no ku kwizigirana

Amahera ya fiat arorosha kwishura, ariko igitigiri cayo n'amategeko ayagenga bishingwa n'inzego nkeyi, kandi ubushobozi bwayo bwo kugura burashobora kugabanuka uko igihe kirengana.

###### Bitcoin itanga amategeko atandukanye

Kuva mu 2009, Bitcoin itanga amahera afise igitigiri kizwi na bose kandi gifise aho kigarukira, ashobora gukoreshwa n'uwo ari we wese, kandi ashobora kugenzurwa aho gupfa kwizigirwa gusa. Ariko kandi, izana ingorane zimwezimwe: ihindagurika ry'igiciro, amakosa adashobora gukosorwa, n'ukwiyitwararika ku giti canyu.

###### Umutekano uza imbere

Nimubanze mwige, mutangure n'ibike, kandi mugenzure vyose. Ntimukigere musangiza abandi amajambo yo kugarukana ikotomoni yanyu. Si ngombwa ko mukora vyose icarimwe: intambwe imwe ikurikira itekanye irahagije.

###### Iyi ni intango gusa

Nimusubire kuri vya bibazo bitanu vyo mu ntango. Ubu mwokwishura iki?

Iyo [Impamyabumenyi ya Bitcoin](https://curriculum.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/) ni yo ntambwe ikurikira. Ibice vyayo bine vy'inyigisho birabafasha gutera imbere, kuva ku gutahura amahera, gushika ku kuntu Bitcoin ikora, ku gukoresha Bitcoin mu mutekano, no kuri Bitcoin mu kibano canyu. Nimugumane inyota yo kumenya, mubandanye kubaza ibibazo, kandi mwige vyinshi kuri [myfirstbitcoin.org](https://myfirstbitcoin.org).
