# Bitcoin ni iki?

Imbere y'uko Bitcoin ibaho, kurungika amafaranga kuri internet vyama bisaba kwisunga ishirahamwe ryizigiwe: ibanki canke ishirahamwe ryo kwishurana ryabika igitabu c'ivyanditswe vy'amafaranga, ryemeza amakonti, kandi ryashobora gutebesha, guhagarika canke gusubiza inyuma ukwishura. Mu myaka mirongo, abahinga mu vya mudasobwa baragerageje kurema amafaranga yo mu buhinga bwa none ata rwego rumwe ruyasohora. Ingorane yari uko amakuru yo mu buhinga bwa none yoroshe gukoporora. Iyo igiceri co mu buhinga bwa none gishobora gukopororwa kigakoreshwa kabiri, nticoshobora gukora nk'amafaranga. Ivyo vyitwa **ingorane yo gukoresha amafaranga amwe kabiri**.

Ku wa 31 Gitugutu 2008, umuntu canke umugwi wakoresha izina Satoshi Nakamoto warasohoye inyandiko yasigura umuti w'iyo ngorane: _Bitcoin: Uburyo bw'amafaranga yo mu buhinga bwa none akoreshwa hagati y'abantu ata muhuza_. Uwo muhora watanguye gukora muri Nzero 2009.

Bitcoin ni amafaranga yo mu buhinga bwa none **atagengwa n'urwego rumwe**, afise **igitigiri ntarengwa**, kandi ashobora kurungikwa **umuntu ku wundi ata muhuza** ata ruhusha rw'ibanki.

Kuba **atagengwa n'urwego rumwe** bisigura ko ata biro bikuru bishobora kwugurura amakonti, guhindura amafaranga ari kuri yo, canke kwemeza uwemerewe kwinjira. Uwo ari we wese arashobora kuvoma no gusuzuma porogarama ifise kode yuguruye, kandi mudasobwa nyinshi zigenga zirabika ikopi y'ivyanditswe. Ivyo ntibisigura ko bose bafise ububasha bungana. Bisigura ko ata muntu umwe mu bayikoresha ashobora guhindura igitabu c'ivyanditswe vy'amafaranga canke amategeko agenga amafaranga kuri bose.

Kugira **igitigiri ntarengwa** bisigura ko hatazokwigera habaho bitcoin zirenga imiliyoni 21. Bitcoin nshasha zisohorwa hakurikijwe ingengabihe izwi na bose, kandi mudasobwa zigenga zitwa amapfundo y'umuhora ziranka ikigwi cose kirenga kuri iryo tegeko. Urwo rugero ntarengwa rurizigiwe kuko uwo ari we wese ashobora kurugenzura. Ariko si itegeko ry'ibidukikije: rugumaho kubera ko abari muri uwo muhora babandanya kurwubahiriza. Kandi igitigiri ntarengwa nticemeza ko igiciro kizoduga. Igiciro kivana n'igitigiri c'abashaka kugura hamwe n'igitigiri c'ibiriho.

Gukorana **umuntu ku wundi ata muhuza** bisigura ko mushobora kurungikira bitcoin uwo ari we wese, aho ari ho hose, mutarinze kwugurura ikonti ku muhora wa Bitcoin canke gusaba uruhusha urwego rukuru. Nta shirahamwe rishobora guhagarika ikoreshwa rya bitcoin mwigenzurira ubwanyu. Ariko uwo mwidegemvyo ujana n'ibanga ryo kwitwararika ivyo mukora: irungikwa ry'amafaranga ryemewe akenshi ntirishobora gusubizwa inyuma, kandi uwo muhora nta biro bifasha abawukoresha ufise.

Kimwe mu vyiyumviro bitari vyo ni uko Bitcoin idashobora gushikirwa na bose kubera ko bitcoin imwe izimvye. **Ntimukeneye kugura canke gukoresha bitcoin imwe yose.** Bitcoin imwe igaburwamwo udufaranga dutoduto 100 000 000 twitwa **satoshis**, canke **sats**. Gutunga sats 100 000 no gutunga bitcoin 0,001 bisigura igitigiri kimwe c'amafaranga.

Uwo muhora wisunga abawugiramwo uruhara batandukanye: **abawukoresha** barungika, bakakira kandi bakabika bitcoin; **amapfundo y'umuhora** asuzuma ko irungikwa ryose ry'amafaranga n'ikigwi cose vyubahiriza amategeko; hamwe n'**abacukuzi** bahiganwa mu kwongera ikigwi gishasha ku vyanditswe hafi buri minota cumi. Ivyo vyanditswe, ari vyo **urunani rw'ibigwi**, biri ku mugaragaro kandi birashobora kubonwa n'uwo ari we wese, kandi kubisubira kwandika bisaba uburyo bwinshi cane.

Icitonderwa ku nyandiko: **Bitcoin** yanditswe na B nkuru akenshi isobanura umuhora canke uburyo bukora. **bitcoin** yanditswe na b ntoyi isobanura amafaranga ubwayo.

Bitcoin na yo ifise ivyiza n'ibibi bijana. Igiciro cayo kirashobora guhinduka ningoga. Amakosa akenshi ntashobora gukosorwa. Ahantu henshi iracemerwa n'abantu bake gusa, kandi kuyikoresha ata nkomanzi bisaba kwiga bimwe. Bitcoin isubiriza igice c'ukwizigira inzego n'ukugenzura hamwe n'ukwiyitwararika kw'umuntu ku giti ciwe. Ntikuraho ingorane zo guhomba.
