# Mukoresha Bitcoin gute?

Iyo **kofi ya Bitcoin** ni igikoresho, akenshi kikaba ari porogarama, kibafasha kwakira, kugenzura no kurungika bitcoin. Bitcoin zanyu ntizibikwa muri telefone yanyu: zandikwa kuri blockchain. Ikofi icungera **impfunguruzo** zibafasha kwemeza ko ari izanyu no gutanga uruhusha rwo kuriha.

Ku bw’ivyo, ikibazo ca mbere ku bijanye n’ikofi iyo ari yo yose ni iki: **ni nde agenzura impfunguruzo?**

###### Ni nde mu vy’ukuri abitse bitcoin zanyu?

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/production/f93df0cc4a2ad82b3410a9ad4c3273d1b760f8ea-1774x887.svg)

Mukoresheje **ikofi mwicungera**, ni mwebwe mugenzura impfunguruzo.

Igihe muyitunganya, irabereka **amajambo y’urufatiro** (yitwa kandi amajambo yo gusubirana ikofi): urutonde rw’amajambo akurikirana ku rutonde rwashinzwe, akenshi akaba ari 12 canke 24. Arashobora gusubira kurema impfunguruzo zanyu ku kindi gikoresho. Umuntu wese ayafise arashobora gutwara bitcoin zanyu. Amajambo y’urufatiro si ayo gufasha gusubirana ikofi gusa: atanga uburenganzira bwose bwo kuyikoresha.

Muyakingire:

* muyandike neza ku rupapuro igihe murema ikofi;
* muyabike ahatari kuri interineti, mw’ibanga, kandi ahantu atazotakara canke ngo yononekare;
* ntimukigere muyafotora, muyarungika kuri imeyili, muyabika mu vyanditswe bibikwa kuri interineti, canke muyarungika mu butumwa;
* ntimukigere muyandika ku rubuga rwa interineti.

Mutakaje amajambo yanyu y’urufatiro n’igikoresho canyu, nta n’umwe ashobora kubasubiza bitcoin zanyu.

Mukoresheje **ikofi icungerwa n’ishirahamwe**, ishirahamwe rirababitse impfunguruzo, nk’uko ibanki ibigenza. Amahera aboneka kuri konti yanyu ni umuhango iryo shirahamwe ribaha wo kuzobariha. Kuyitunganya bikorwa mu buryo mumenyereye kandi mushobora kuronka imfashanyo, ariko muba mwishimikije iryo shirahamwe: rirashobora kugabanya amahera mushobora kubikuza, guhagarika ikoreshwa rya konti, kwinjirirwa n’abagizi ba nabi bo kuri interineti, canke kwugara.


| Ikibazo | Icungerwa n’ishirahamwe | Mwicungera |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ni nde abitse impfunguruzo? | Ishirahamwe | Mwebwe |
| Ni nde afasha gusubirana ikofi? | Ishirahamwe, biciye kuri konti yanyu | Mwebwe, mukoresheje amajambo yanyu y’urufatiro |
| Inyungu nyamukuru n’ivyo zisaba kwemera | Ukworoherwa n’imfashanyo, ariko mwishimikiza ishirahamwe | Kugenzura ikofi ubwanyu, ariko ni mwebwe mujejwe kubika ivyobafasha kuyisubirana kandi mukabazwa amakosa mukora |


Hari imvugo ngirakamaro mu bakoresha Bitcoin: **impfunguruzo zitari izanyu, ibiceri na vyo si ivyanyu.** Guhitamwo uwubabikira si ikigeragezo c’agaciro kanyu. Igihambaye ni ukumenya ubwoko bw’ikofi mukoresha n’ico mwizigiye.

My First Bitcoin ntihimiriza gukoresha amakofi amwamwe yihariye. Ibikoresho, amafaranga y’ivyo bikorwa n’abavyegukira birahinduka, kandi ibibereye umugwi umwe w’abantu bishobora kutabera uwundi. Mubaze umwigisha mwizigiye ivyo abantu bakoresha aho muba, kandi mwigenzurire ibi bintu imbere yo gukoresha ikofi iyo ari yo yose:

* **Impfunguruzo:** ni nde agenzura impfunguruzo, ni mwebwe canke ni ishirahamwe?
* **Gusubirana ikofi:** bigenda gute iyo telefone yanyu itakaye canke ishirahamwe ritakiriho?
* **Aho iva:** mwayikuye ku rubuga rwemewe canke ku rupapuro rwayo rwo mw’iduka rya porogarama, atari ku muhora umuntu yabarungikiye?
* **Kode:** kode yayo iruguruye kuri bose kandi yoba yama yitwararikwa ikongera igatunganywa? (Kuba kode yuguruye kuri bose vyonyene ntivyemeza ko igikoresho gitekanye.)
* **Amafaranga asabwa:** ni amafaranga ayahe asabwa mu kurungika, mu kwakira canke mu kubikuza?
* **Ukwishura:** yoba ishigikira uburyo bwo kwishura mukeneye, kuri blockchain canke kuri Lightning?

Bitcoin ifise uburyo bubiri bukunze gukoreshwa mu kwishura. **Kuri blockchain**, ukwishura kwandikwa muri blockchain nyene. **Lightning** ni umuhora wo kwishura wubakiye kuri Bitcoin, utuma abantu bishura ningoga amahera makeyi kandi kenshi, nko mu kugura ikawa. Ikotomoni ya Lightning na yo nyene irashobora kuba icungiwe n'uwundi muntu canke iyo mwicungira.

Amategeko atatu azobakingira mu gihe mwiga:

1. **Mubanze mwige.** Ntimugire ico mukoze kubera akanyamuneza karenze, ubwoba canke uguhatirwa.
1. **Mutangure n'amahera makeyi.** Mugerageze ibikoresho mutamenyereye mukoresheje amahera mushobora kwemera guhomba.
1. **Ntimupfe kwizera, musuzume.** Musuzume uwo muntu, uburyo bwo kwishura, aderesi n'igitigiri c'amahera imbere yo kwemeza ukwishura.

**Mutange amakuru yo kwakira amahera, ntimwigere mutanga amabanga.** Aderesi canke ubusabe bwo kwishurwa birashobora gutangwa. Amajambo yo kugarura ikotomoni canke urufunguzo rw'ibanga ntibikwiye kwigera bitangwa. Nta mwigisha, ishirahamwe ry'amakotomoni canke umukozi ajejwe gufasha abakoresha akora mu buryo bwemewe azokwigera abibasaba.

Muhagarike nimba hari uwubihutisha canke abasaba kugira ibanga, abasezeranira inyungu ata kabuza, abasaba kubanza kurungika bitcoin, canke abasaba amajambo yo kugarura ikotomoni yanyu. **Mubanze muhagarare. Ntimutange na kimwe. Ntimurungike na kimwe.** Muheze musuzume mwifashishije isoko ry'amakuru musanzwe muzi.
