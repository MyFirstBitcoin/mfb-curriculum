# 8.1 Visibility & Reach

Making your project visible is essential to reaching new students, collaborators, and supporters. The Education Network provides several open tools to help your classes and community be discovered globally.

For new Communities, this is a powerful way to increase reach and enroll students beyond your local community.

## BTCMap

[BTCMap](https://btcmap.org/) is an open-source map created by the Bitcoin community. It shows Bitcoin-accepting businesses, but also Bitcoin communities around the world.

The My First Bitcoin Education Network [is listed on BTCMap](https://btcmap.org/communities/mi-primer-bitcoin), and by adding your project you:

* Increase visibility for your Community
* Show real proof-of-work
* Make it easier for visitors and supporters to find you

After adding your community, we strongly encourage you to also add local merchants that accept Bitcoin.

To get started, follow this [BTCMap setup video](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1U1EkANGSPFE2-vs4kS9a4H9Hjpvip2YN/view?usp=sharing).

### Add Your Community to BTCMap and Earn Points for Merchants

Adding your Community to BTCMap is quite straightforward:

1. Check whether your Community is already listed on the [BTCMap communities leaderboard](https://btcmap.org/communities/leaderboard).
1. If it is not, [add your Community to BTCMap](https://btcmap.org/communities/add).
1. Once it is listed, link your BTCMap community to your profile in the **My First Bitcoin App**.

From then on, every Bitcoin-accepting merchant you onboard on BTCMap in your area earns your Community points on the Education Network Leaderboard: **5 points per merchant** (see the [Transparency page](https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org/transparency)).
