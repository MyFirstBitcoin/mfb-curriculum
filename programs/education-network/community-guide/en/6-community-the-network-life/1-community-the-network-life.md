# 13.1 Community: The Network Life

The General Assembly (GA) is where the Education Network comes together. It’s the main space for coordination, collaboration, and shared decision-making across the global network.

## General Assembly (GA)

The General Assembly takes place every month, on the last Thursday, via our virtual office, at 10:00 a.m. Central America time.

This is where much of the “network magic” happens.

During each General Assembly, Communities:

* Share experiences and achievements
* Discuss challenges and opportunities
* Exchange best practices
* Ask for help and support
* Receive updates and announcements
* Review results of monthly and quarterly voting sessions

## Agenda & Participation

Communities who wish to speak during the GA must claim a slot in advance by filling out the GA Agenda.

Key details:

* The agenda is shared by email and in the Education Network channels two weeks before the GA
* Speaking slots are assigned on a first-come, first-served basis
* This structure helps keep calls organized as the network continues to grow

You can find past and upcoming agendas in the shared GA folder.

## Your First General Assembly

As a new Community, your first General Assembly is special. You’ll be:

* Welcomed by the network
* Given time to introduce yourself and your project

We strongly encourage you to attend. It’s one of the best ways to connect with other Communities and feel part of the global community.

The General Assembly is not just a meeting. It’s a key mechanism that keeps the Education Network aligned, transparent, and decentralized.
