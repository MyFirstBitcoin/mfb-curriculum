# 14.1 Funding & Sustainability

As your project grows, you may need funding to sustain operations or responsibly expand your impact. Within the Education Network, funding is approached with care and intention, prioritizing Bitcoin-native, ethical, and transparent models aligned with independent education.

Funding is not the goal. Sustainability without capture is.

Independent Bitcoin education only works when funding reinforces the mission rather than compromising it. Funding choices should protect:

* Independence
* Impartiality
* Community trust

Bitcoin teaches us to lower time preference and think long term. Applied to funding, this means:

* Building diverse sources of support
* Maintaining clear separation between funding and education
* Establishing firm boundaries
* Treating funding as a tool for sovereignty, not dependency

Who funds your project matters, because over time funding relationships shape incentives and behavior.

## When to Say No to Funding

Just as Bitcoin resists capture, educational projects must remain vigilant. Be cautious when funding comes with:

* Attempts to influence what or how you teach
* Pressure to promote products or narratives
* Restrictions on critical thinking
* Growth incentives that prioritize quantity over quality

Declining misaligned funding is not a setback. It is an opportunity to protect your mission and lead by example.

Before accepting any funding, take time to reflect:

* What are their interests?
* Who else do they fund?
* What do they expect in return?
* How might this relationship evolve over time?
* Does it strengthen or threaten your independence?

If the answers are unclear, it is worth pausing.

## Grassroots and Bitcoin-Native Funding

For most early-stage projects, grassroots support and Bitcoin-native tools are the natural place to start.

[Geyser](http://geyser.fund/) is a Bitcoin-native crowdfunding platform that allows projects to:

* Receive donations directly in Bitcoin
* Operate without a legal entity
* Accept Bitcoin and Lightning
* Launch campaigns quickly and transparently

This makes Geyser well suited for early-stage projects covering operational costs while maintaining independence.

To get started, follow the [Geyser guide](https://guide.geyser.fund/geyser-docs).

### Other Bitcoin-Native Options

* Direct Bitcoin or Lightning addresses
* My First Bitcoin’s BTCPay Server for Education Network members
* Self-hosted BTCPay instances for maximum independence

## Building a Strong Foundation

Sustainable funding follows strong foundations. Projects with:

* A clear vision
* Defined roles
* Measurable objectives
* A realistic budget

build trust and reduce the risk of mission drift.

## Transparency, Growth, and Sustainability

Funding tends to follow proof of work. Consistently documenting activities, sharing impact, and engaging your local community builds credibility and attracts aligned support.

Transparency is essential.

A simple and sustainable practice is to publish regular updates that combine:

* A brief financial overview
* A short impact summary

## Institutional and Grant-Based Funding

Institutional or grant-based funding may be considered if grassroots support is no longer sufficient. This step is optional and should only be pursued if it aligns with your values, capacity, and long-term vision.

If grassroots tools like Geyser already cover your needs, institutional funding is not required.

Although many fundraising resources are written with the United States in mind, their principles and steps can often be adapted to other jurisdictions.

* My First Bitcoin does not fund Education Network projects directly.
* In some cases, My First Bitcoin may connect or recommend projects to grant facilities when appropriate, but funding decisions always remain independent.

Most grant facilities require a 501(c)(3) status or equivalent legal structure. Grant funding should reinforce your mission, not reshape it.

We explain how to set this up in the [Startup Guide](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/education-network/en/community-guide/module/10/).

### Grant Facilities

## Build Sustainably

Funding should enable your work, not control it.

Grow at a pace your community can sustain, protect trust, and remain grounded in independence.
