# 7.1 Set up your Profile in Leaderboard

The [**Education Network Leaderboard**](https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org/) is a public record of the local communities teaching Bitcoin in the My First Bitcoin Education Network, and of the work each of them has done.

Joining is free. It takes about an hour, and most of your first points come from things you already have: a name, a logo, a website, and your social accounts.

The My First Bitcoin team reads your email with the signed Charter, sets up your community profile, and adds you to the leaderboard. In the future, this will go fully automated, but we’re still testing our systems.

Your community then has its own page, and it appears in the list of communities on the reporting form. **You cannot report points before this step:** the reporting form only lists communities that are already on the leaderboard.

Every rule and its point value is public on the [**Transparency page**](https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org/transparency): [https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org/transparency](https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org/transparency)

## What to Include in Your Community Profile

* Community name
* Country / location
* Team members (names and roles)
* Languages you teach in
* Website and social accounts
* Relevant links (past events, media coverage, anything else worth a look)
* What you are teaching and where you are headed next

## Profile Points: One Email, Well Over 100 Points

These are one-time points for having a complete profile. They count in every time window on the leaderboard (marked **"Always"** on the Transparency page), so they never expire.


| What | Points | What to send |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign the Community Charter | 25 | The signed PDF |
| Publish a website about your community | 15 | Your website address |
| Write a short bio about your community | 10 | A few sentences about who you are |
| Add a social media handle | 10 each | Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Nostr (npub), X/Twitter |
| Add a team member | 10 each | Names and roles of your team |
| Link a Bitcoin donation address | 10 | A Lightning address |
| Add a fundraising link (e.g. Geyser) | 10 | Your fundraising page |
| Add a logo | 5 | Your logo file |
| Every month your community stays active | 1 per month | Nothing: this grows by itself |


### Worked Example

A community that sends the Charter, a website, a bio, a logo, a Lightning address, a fundraising link, three social accounts, and three team members earns:

**25 + 15 + 10 + 5 + 10 + 10 + (3 × 10) + (3 × 10) = 135 points**

That is already past a **reputation score of 100** before you have run a single class.

### Perks

As your reputation score grows, your Community becomes eligible for **perks**: support, visibility and tools from My First Bitcoin. Some perks are open to everyone; others come within reach at a reputation score of 100, 500 or 3,000. See them all on the [**Perks page**](https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org/perks): [https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org/perks](https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org/perks)

### Two Things to Know

1. The website and social accounts need to exist before we can list them.
1. Perks are awarded at the discretion of the My First Bitcoin team.

Climbing the leaderboard puts you on the radar; it does **not** unlock a perk automatically.

## Next Quick Wins: One Document or One Form Each

Once you are listed, report these through the form at [forms.myfirstbitcoin.org/report-reputation](https://forms.myfirstbitcoin.org/report-reputation), also available through the **"Report your score"** button on the Transparency page.


| What | Points | Effort |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Publish a Vision + Mission statement | 50 | One page, shared as a link |
| Publish an Executive Summary of your community | 100 | One or two pages, shared as a link |
| Register a physical community space | 100 | A link or photo of the space |
| Run an Intro to Bitcoin cohort in person | 20 per cohort, plus 4 per graduate | Report the cohort on the form |
| Run an Intro to Bitcoin cohort online | 10 per cohort, plus 2 per graduate | Report the cohort on the form |
| Feature in a podcast, article, or public appearance | 1 each | A link to it |


## Bigger Steps, When You Are Ready

Once your profile is complete and you have reported your first quick wins, these are the steps that move your Community up the leaderboard the most. They take more time and preparation: publishing your finances, running full Bitcoin Diploma cohorts, hosting an Unconference, or training your team as educators. There is no rush. Pick the one that fits where your Community is today, and come back to the others as you grow:


| What | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Publish your budget (where your community stands today) | 400 |
| Publish your forecast (where your community is heading) | 400 |
| Publish your accounting (what your community has already spent) | 400 |
| Host a My First Bitcoin Unconference in your community | 500 |
| Run a Bitcoin Diploma cohort in person / online | 300 / 175 per cohort |
| Proofread a Bitcoin Diploma translation | 250 |
| Certify a team member through the Educator Training Program | 100 each |
| Hold a mentorship call with another community | 100 |
