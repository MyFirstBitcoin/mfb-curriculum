# 1.1 Welcome to the Bitcoin Education Network

Welcome to the **My First Bitcoin Education Network**. You are now part of a global, decentralized movement focused on independent, impartial, and community-led Bitcoin Education. We’re glad you’re here!

## A Message from John Dennehy, Founder of My First Bitcoin

> _My First Bitcoin began in 2021 as El Salvador’s first Bitcoin non-profit. Our very first class had just one student, taught in a borrowed yoga studio in San Salvador. Since then, we’ve taught tens of thousands of students, launched the world’s first Bitcoin Diploma used for public school credit, and grown into the largest Bitcoin-only education organization, all without government funding and without compromising our independence._

> _This was Step One of our vision: Create an example in El Salvador._

> _Step Two is to make that example easy to copy around the world. Everything we do is open source. In 2023, we launched a network of independent Bitcoin educators that now spans 40+ countries. And we’re just getting started. This is not a business. This is a movement._

## How the Bitcoin Education Network Works

The Bitcoin Education Network is modeled after Bitcoin itself. My First Bitcoin in El Salvador is the genesis block. From there, a global, decentralized network grows. Every Community follows a shared consensus of values and standards, but operates locally and independently.

## Your Mission as a Community

Your mission is simple and powerful:

**Deliver the highest quality Bitcoin education to your local community.**

You’re not doing this alone. We’ve assembled a **Start-up Guide** with tools and resources to help you get started.

The following wikis will guide you through:

* Teaching online or in-person
* Organizing classes and materials
* Increasing visibility
* Coordinating with the network
* Sustaining and scaling your work
