# 1.1 Learning Materials: Educational Resources

**Start with the Educator Guide.** The _Bitcoin Diploma Educator Guide_ is the most complete guide to teaching with My First Bitcoin materials: your role as an educator, planning the course, facilitation methods, a companion for every module, adapting to your language and context, closing the Diploma well, and what comes after. Read it before you teach your first class: [https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/en/educator-guide/](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/en/educator-guide/)

_This chapter is coming soon. Until then, here is a short map of what is available._

## All Programs

Every program is free, open source, and available in many languages: [https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/)

* **Introduction to Bitcoin:** a short, beginner-friendly introduction. No prior knowledge needed. [https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/introduction-to-bitcoin/](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/introduction-to-bitcoin/)
* **Bitcoin Diploma:** our flagship curriculum in four modules (Understanding Money, How Bitcoin Works, Using Bitcoin Safely, Bitcoin in Your Community), with a student guide, the Educator Guide and a Quick Start. [https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/)
* **Educator Training Program (ETP):** prepares you to teach the Bitcoin Diploma and become a certified educator. [https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/educator-training-program/](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/educator-training-program/)
* **Bitcoin for Juniors:** the Diploma reworked for younger learners, with its own educator guide. [https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-for-juniors/](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-for-juniors/)
* **Bitcoin for Organizations:** for NGOs, cooperatives and mission-driven organisations. [https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-for-organizations/](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-for-organizations/)
* **Advanced Modules:** [https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/advanced-modules/](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/advanced-modules/)

## Resources, Lexicon and Activities

* **Resources Library:** slide decks, handouts, videos and other material from communities across the network. You can submit your own. [https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/resources/](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/resources/)
* **Lexicon:** Bitcoin terms explained in plain words. [https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/)
* **Activity Library:** classroom activities you can use alongside any program. [https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/activities/en/](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/activities/en/)

## Certificates

Students who complete the Bitcoin Diploma receive a certificate. The Educator Guide explains how to close the course and when a certificate can be issued: only once the assessment is complete.

## The Student Journey

The Student Journey shows everything My First Bitcoin offers, level by level, from a first introduction to becoming a Community Leader. Share it with your students so they always know their next step: [https://journey.myfirstbitcoin.org/](https://journey.myfirstbitcoin.org/)
