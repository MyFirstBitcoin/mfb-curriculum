# 6.1 Overview

#### Foreword

This chapter gathers the ideas from the course—value, scarcity, time and energy, technology, privacy, and shared rules—and connects them to Bitcoin. Learners will meet Bitcoin as a human response to problems with money and as a network maintained by people working together.

#### Overarching objectives

* Connect prior learning to Bitcoin’s origin, rules, and network.
* Describe Bitcoin with age-appropriate vocabulary and concrete examples.
* Ask thoughtful questions about money, technology, security, and participation.

#### What to look forward to

Students will hear the story of Satoshi and the Genesis Block, create stories about an unresolved mystery, and simulate mining by finding card pairs and confirming transactions. Educators can look forward to moments of synthesis as students use familiar ideas and new vocabulary to explain a complex system in their own words.

#### Why it matters for Bitcoin

Young people will grow up in a world shaped by digital money and networks. Encountering Bitcoin now does not ask them to adopt it; it gives them a framework for asking how a monetary system works, who sets its rules, how it is secured, and what responsibilities come with taking part.
