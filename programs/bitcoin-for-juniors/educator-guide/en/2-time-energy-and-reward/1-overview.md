# 2.1 Overview

#### Foreword

This chapter helps learners notice that time and energy are valuable resources. Students will explore how effort, rest, luck, rules, and patience can shape the rewards people receive, while developing language for fairness and for choices that affect the future.

#### Overarching objectives

* Notice how time, energy, effort, and rest affect everyday decisions.
* Compare fairness, chance, and effort-based reward systems.
* Consider short-term choices alongside longer-term outcomes.

#### What to look forward to

Learners will map their routines, track energy across a day, play games with different reward systems, discuss a story about fairness, revisit _The Three Little Pigs_, and design a smart-choice superhero. Educators can expect honest reflections and useful conversations about balance, effort, chance, and long-term thinking.

#### Why it matters for Bitcoin

Bitcoin is built around time, energy, and shared rules: proof of work connects effort to the security of the network. Young learners do not need to master its technical details yet; they need opportunities to reason about why work has value, why rules matter, and why a patient choice can sometimes lead to a stronger result.
