# 3.1 Overview

#### Foreword

This chapter invites students to become curious observers of the tools around them. By comparing analogue and digital technologies, learners will see that innovation responds to people’s needs, brings trade-offs, and keeps changing the ways we communicate, create, and solve problems.

#### Overarching objectives

* Identify and compare analogue and digital technologies.
* Discuss how a tool’s benefits and trade-offs affect people’s choices.
* Use creativity and clear communication to imagine solutions for the future.

#### What to look forward to

Students will match old and new technologies, vote with their feet and explain their preferences, then imagine and pitch an invention for the future. Educators can look forward to active debate, surprising family connections, and creative problem-solving rooted in students’ everyday experiences.

#### Why it matters for Bitcoin

Bitcoin is a digital form of money, so understanding the difference between physical and digital systems helps learners ask better questions about how information and value move. Encountering these ideas young builds the confidence to examine new technologies critically, appreciate their possibilities, and consider the responsibilities that come with using them.
