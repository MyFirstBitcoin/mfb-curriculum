# 1.1 Overview

#### Foreword

This chapter gives young learners a practical starting point for thinking about why people trade, why things matter to different people, and how prices emerge. Students will discover that value is personal, scarcity matters, and a price is not the same thing as what something means to us.

#### Overarching objectives

* Explore exchange, scarcity, value, and price through direct experience.
* Recognize that people can value the same thing differently.
* Practice explaining choices, listening to others, and weighing trade-offs.

#### What to look forward to

Through a shipwreck trading challenge, a lively shell auction, sorting and discussion activities, and a shared price line, students will negotiate, make choices, compare ideas, and see prices form in real time. Educators can look forward to rich conversations about wants, needs, fairness, and differing points of view.

#### Why it matters for Bitcoin

Bitcoin invites people to ask fundamental questions about money: What gives something value? Why does scarcity matter? How do people agree to exchange? Meeting these ideas early helps learners become thoughtful participants in economic life, able to question prices, understand trade-offs, and recognize that money is a tool people use to coordinate with one another.
