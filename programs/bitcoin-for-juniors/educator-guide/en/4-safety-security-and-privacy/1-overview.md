# 4.1 Overview

#### Foreword

This chapter introduces the habits of careful digital citizenship: deciding what to share, recognizing who controls access to information, and understanding why private messages deserve protection. Learners will practice making thoughtful choices without being asked to reveal anything personal.

#### Overarching objectives

* Distinguish between public and private information.
* Explore trust, access, and the ways information can be protected.
* Practice respectful, responsible decisions about sharing.

#### What to look forward to

Through public-or-private sorting, message-passing games, cipher-wheel making, and a relay challenge, students will experience how information can be changed, overheard, or protected. Educators can look forward to practical conversations about boundaries, trust, consent, and responsible communication.

#### Why it matters for Bitcoin

Bitcoin depends on people protecting important information and understanding the difference between a public record and a private key. These early, age-appropriate ideas prepare learners to navigate connected systems safely and to value privacy as a choice and a responsibility—not as something to fear.
