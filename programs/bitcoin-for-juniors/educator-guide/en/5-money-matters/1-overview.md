# 5.1 Overview

#### Foreword

This chapter brings earlier learning about value, time, energy, choice, and fairness into one shared classroom economy. Students will see that money decisions involve planning, opportunity, risk, and cooperation—and that the same rules can still lead people to different outcomes.

#### Overarching objectives

* Apply ideas about earning, saving, investing, borrowing, and trade.
* Make and reflect on decisions under shared rules, constraints, and uncertainty.
* Connect individual choices to outcomes in a wider economy.

#### What to look forward to

In an extended role-play, learners set personal goals and move among home, farm, factory, market, and bank stations to earn, save, invest, borrow, and trade. Educators can look forward to observing strategic thinking, negotiation, and reflective discussion as students test their decisions in a low-stakes setting.

#### Why it matters for Bitcoin

Bitcoin opens a wider conversation about how money systems work and who participates in them. Giving young learners a concrete experience of economic choices helps them approach that conversation with agency: they can recognize incentives, ask whether rules are fair, and understand that financial tools shape real lives.
