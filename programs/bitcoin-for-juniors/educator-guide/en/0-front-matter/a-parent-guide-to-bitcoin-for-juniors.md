# A Parent Guide to Bitcoin for Juniors

Welcome! Bitcoin for Juniors is a playful, discussion-led course from My First Bitcoin. We create open educational materials that help young people explore important ideas with curiosity, confidence, and care.

This is not a course about buying Bitcoin, trading, or investing. It is a first, enjoyable introduction to the ideas behind it: what makes something valuable, what is fair, how we use our time and energy, how we make choices, and why trust and responsibility matter.

Through games, crafts, movement, stories, and guided discussion, students build a foundation for future learning—about Bitcoin and about the world around them.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Will my child handle money or Bitcoin?

No. No funds, money, wallets, accounts, or transactions are used in this course. Nothing is bought, sold, or exchanged. Students simply arrive, participate, and learn through activities.

#### Is this a financial or investment course?

No. We do not teach children to invest, trade, or promote Bitcoin. Most of the course focuses on the questions adults often wish they had explored earlier: What matters to us? What is fair? What makes something valuable? How do we spend our time and energy? Bitcoin appears as a later example that helps connect these ideas.

#### Does my child need a phone, computer, or previous knowledge?

No. The course is intentionally low-tech and designed to work first through real-world play and conversation. No mobile devices are required, and no prior knowledge of Bitcoin is expected.

#### Will there be tests or “right answers”?

No. There is no pass or fail, and students are not expected to agree with a particular point of view. We encourage questions, respectful discussion, imagination, and independent thinking. The goal is a positive experience of exploring new ideas.

### Want to Know More?

Parents are warmly invited to speak with the responsible educator before or during the course.

You may also review the complete, open-source course material in advance: [Bitcoin for Juniors course materials](https://github.com/MyFirstBitcoin/curriculum/tree/main/programs/bitcoin-for-juniors/en).

These materials are created on a non-profit, open-source basis. They are freely available to read, adapt, and improve, helping remove conflicts of interest and keep the focus on high-quality, enjoyable education.

We hope your child leaves the course with more questions, more confidence, and a positive first experience of thinking critically about Bitcoin and the ideas that surround it.
