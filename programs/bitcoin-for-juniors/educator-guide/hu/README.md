**Activities** carry a facts line (types, participation, duration), a **Materials**
list and an **Additional resources** list. Those three are a view of structured
fields, so edits to them are not applied from a pull request: a material line holds
a printable, a reference letter and a nice-to-have flag that a line of text cannot
carry. Everything else in an activity is prose and edits are welcome: the title, the
summary, Objectives, Preparation notes, Setup, the Procedure and its steps, Key
points, Discussion questions and Notes.
